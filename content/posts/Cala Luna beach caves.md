# Cala Luna beach caves

Group of shallow sea caves at Cala Luna beach, Sardinia. These are recorded separately from the submerged cave described in [Grotte di Cala Luna]({{< relref "posts/Grotte di Cala Luna.md" >}}).

## Research notes

Sardinia's trail authority describes multiple caves and recesses at the beach formed by marine erosion in limestone. The saved Facebook post does not identify which opening it depicts.
