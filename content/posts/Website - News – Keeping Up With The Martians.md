As regular readers are well aware, prolonged silence here is usually a telltale sign that things have been pretty busy in my neck of the woods – this latest gap is no different. Let me catch you up on what’s been going on!

- [“Josh Richards – Mars Needs Guitars”](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/josh-richards-mars-needs-guitars-5/) was a stunning success at National Science Week. While there were some serious concerns on numbers in the weeks leading up to the shows, the people of Perth turned out in droves, with 150+ taking part in the three performances in the Scitech LotteryWest Theatre. Special thanks to all of the wonderful staff at Scitech who helped make it happen (particularly WA’s National Science Week Coordinator Kerry Mazzotti), and to my venue manager/door staff Sian Choyce – you’ll always have a spot on my team to Mars if you want it ![:P](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-includes/images/smilies/icon_razz.gif)

- For those that missed the show during National Science Week, it will soon be available online! The final show was filmed from several angles, the footage cut together and has been uploaded to Mars One – they’re currently assessing if they can share it on their [official Youtube channel](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/http://www.youtube.com/user/MarsOneProject)! In the unlikely event they can’t use it, I’ll be sure to share it on my own YouTube channel in the coming week. Either way keep an eye out for the full-length recording of “Josh Richards – Mars Needs Guitars!” online very soon!

- Keith the Anger Management Koala put together his own Mars One application video, but unfortunately it was submitted AFTER the September 5th deadline. But if you’d like to know why a 5’6″ ginger koala with a raft of anger management issues thinks he belongs on Mars, then watch the video below – it’s some of Keith’s finest work.

[Watch archived video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=be5PVIlphCw)- Mars One is continuing to hit the headlines all over the world, particularly since the closure of the 2013 Astronaut Application Round on September 5th. Since then I’ve conducted interviews with [The Guardian in the UK](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/http://www.theguardian.com/science/2013/sep/10/mars-one-mission-space-travel-applicants); radio interviews with RTRFM in Perth, [3AW in Melbourne](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/2013/09/10/mars-project-3aw-interview-with-tom-elliott/?fb_source=pubv1) and [2SER in Sydney](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/http://www.2ser.com/component/k2/item/5051-space-oddity-josh-richards-wants-a-one-way-ticket-to-mars); and I’m appearing on prime-time national television tonight (Thursday September 12th) to discuss Mars One and my application with [The Project](theprojecttv.com.au) on Channel 10 It’s incredibly exciting to be able to promote and share such an amazing endeavor with so many people in Australia and across the globe, and I can only hope the press continues to show an interest as we progress through Round 2′s Medical & Selection Board phase!

And in one final bit of great news, I’ve submitted my application to the [International Space University’s “Southern Hemisphere Summer Space Program”](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/http://www.isunet.edu/programs/southern-hemisphere-program); a 5-week post-graduate course starting in Janurary 2014 at the University of South Australia. Participants take part in an intensive course learning about the science, engineering and law of space, studying and working with top space professionals to tackle the challenges facing in the space industry today. I can wait to take part, hopefully bringing my experience in comedy and science communication to the traditionally conservative space industry. Keith the Anger Management Koala will ofcourse be joining me in Adelaide if I’m accepted ![:D](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-includes/images/smilies/icon_biggrin.gif)

The coming months have plenty of surprises in-store as I change gears: moving from show writing/performing to studying and website writing. That’s not a PROMISE there will be more regular posts here, only that there is an increased PROBABILITY of posts. But it’s all looking up Ginge fans – keep an eye on the [Facebook Fanpage](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/https://www.facebook.com/joshrichardsthemightyginge) and my [Twitter feed](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023403/https://twitter.com/Mighty_Ginge) for daily updates, and looking forward to sharing more great news here on the Mighty Ginge in the coming months.
