# Gua Tok Sheik

Cave recorded near Baling, Kedah, Malaysia. The register spells the name ‘Tok Sheik’; the saved lead says ‘Tok Sheikh’.

## Research notes

Grotto Map lists it as Gua 9 and attributes its description to the Malaysia–Swiss Caving Expedition. The saved Facebook links are retained for comparison with that record.
