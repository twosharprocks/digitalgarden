Don’t get your hopes up sports fans:regular posts on the Mighty Ginge won’t be back for another week. Between waiting to hear about a job offer and finding somewhere to live –since the pair of retirees I’ve been leeching off and suffering at the same time for the last 3 weeks have decided they don’t trust me in their house anymore –new posts will have to wait till the 17th.

But in the meantime,we have some fairly major site updates. If you’re reading this direct from the website –and not from an RSS feed –then you’ve probably already noticed the Mighty Ginge of 2011 has changed slightly. If you **are** reading this from an RSS feed clearly no one told you [RSS is dead](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530064209/http://techcrunch.com/2010/09/13/rss-is-not-not-not-not-not-dead/),and the only people who still use it are obstinate arseholes like me.

In an act of monumental self absorption my real-life author Josh Richards is stepping out from behind the curtain,adding sections on his upcoming stand-up gigs,contact details if you’re stupid enough to want to book him,that “101 Things To Do Before You Die”bullshit he’s always going on about,and a bunch of other crap I can’t be bothered listing –seriously,it’s easier for you to find it yourself. Oh and to add insult to injury he’s plastered pictures of his rude head all over the site too –because having a face like a warthog isn’t enough,he needs to advertise it too.

2011 is going to be an interesting one for the Mighty Ginge:I’m being joined here by my good friend and long-time threat to pre-pubescent children –Lenny the Lemur –with his monthly “Letters from Lenny”;regular Mighty Ginge posts start next week,and my monthly podcast will be starting as soon as I have a home and don’t feel like dropping the toaster in the bath every morning.

And ofcourse our “creator”Josh will be pretending to be funny in new ways,so keep an eye out for the gallery section coming soon with my podcast as well as videos and photos of his ridiculous face.

Here’s to 2011 Ginge-worshippers - may your awesomeness inspire fear in others and cause their cats to burst into flames.

**The Mighty Ginge**
