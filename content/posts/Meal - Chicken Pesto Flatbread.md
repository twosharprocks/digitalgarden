# Ingredients
- Flatbread - 2
- Chicken breast - 300g
- Pesto - 2 tbsp
- Mozzarella - 1/2 cup
- Spinach - 1 cup

# Directions
1. Cook chicken and slice
2. Spread pesto on flatbread
3. Add chicken, cheese and spinach
4. Bake until crispy
