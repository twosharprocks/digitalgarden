Fear not Mighty Ginge fans –I’m still here. I’ve been left behind with the rest of you by the prophesied May 21st rapture,and I’ve been busy with two other major comedy projects that have been dragging me away from my regular Mighty Ginge duties. But since both projects are coming to fruition,there will be a whole lot going on here in the coming months. So what’s been happening?

First up I didn’t get through the Laughing Horse Quarter Finals. Not because the audience didn’t LOVE my justification that if they move slow enough to have moss grow on them,then maybe sloths deserve to be extinct. No –I didn’t get through because the organisers had 18 comedians on the same night,but only put 17 of them on the ballot paper. Guess who didn’t get their name on the voting sheet? On the upside though,Alex Bothwell (who won our Quarter Final) is an amazingly funny,geeky and gorgeous lady who deserved to go much further than the semi finals,and who a) I pray doesn’t read this,and b) I fully intend to get drunk with and clumsily hit on next time on I’m in London. SHAZAM!

In the meantime my one-man show at the 2011 Edinburgh Fringe Festival is nearing completion. “Apocalypse Meow with Josh Richards”will be playing for just two shows in Edinburgh this year:Iman’s Grill at 4:30pm August 17th,and The Banshee Labyrinth (Cinema) at 3:30 August 18th. Keep an eye here,the [Mighty Ginge Facebook](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133503/http://www.facebook.com/pages/Josh-Richards-The-Mighty-Ginge/97584028460 "Josh Richards - The Mighty Ginge (Facebook Fan Page)") page,and the “[Upcoming Gigs](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133503/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/upcoming-gigs/ "Upcoming Gigs - Josh Richards - The Mighty Ginge")”section (Sweet Monkey Jesus,that’s THREE eyes!) for announcements about preview shows around the UK before August.

And finally,my obnoxious Australian voice can now be regularly heard violating the radio airwaves by tuning into [Stroud FM](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133503/http://www.stroudfm.co.uk/ "StroudFM") on 107.9 MHz,or listen online if you’re not confused by the interwebs. Again,check the “Upcoming Gigs”page for a schedule of upcoming radio shows. Currently I’ve got 8pm till 9 every second Wednesday night,but expect that to change as the station managers start to realise just how completely badass I am. Or,more probably,they realise just how incompetent I am and decide to give me more practice…

It’s all looking up folks. Keep your eyes peeled,your ears tuned and your shotgun loaded –the Mighty Ginge is about to kick in your door,eat your cheese and crackers,then violate your pets. And he’ll be cracking jokes about Buddah’s fat ass reincarnating while he does it.
