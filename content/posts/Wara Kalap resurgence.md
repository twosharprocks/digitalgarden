# Wara Kalap resurgence

Resurgence and exploration catchment in the Nakanai Mountains, Papua New Guinea. Wara Kalap is used for the resurgence and drainage objective, not necessarily one cave entrance.

## Research notes

Petzl's account of the 2015 expedition says the team reached the Wara Kalap collector through a cave they named Philip Pato. Keep Philip Pato distinct if it receives its own record.
