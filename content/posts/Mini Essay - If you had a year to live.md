If you knew you were going to die in a year, what would you do? Would you stay at work? Fuck no. Would you stay here with Chloe? Maybe, but only if it were to write as much as you possibly could with your final year. If you were going to die before you turned 41, there's no question you'd travel and write constantly. And you certainly wouldn't be diving in Mt Gambier - you'd be going straight to Spain to cave dive, maybe France for more of the same. 

You'd burn fucking everything too - sharing all that you had and making sure there was as little left behind as you could. You'd leave as neat a pile as possible, with nothing that others have to sort through - all your files and notes and snippets filed away so someone else could use them if they wanted, but not taking up any more space than needed. 

You'd want to experience weightlessness and go to space if possible. You'd want to see Antarctica and get close to Leopard Seals if you could, maybe swim with orca and sleep in the snow in Norway. You'd want to experience [Skeleton](https://www.whistlerslidingcentre.com/public-sliding/public-skeleton) too, and see the Northern lights again. Would you go on a long walk? Maybe - something to disappear and be with your own thoughts for awhile as you contemplate existence. Ayahuasca in the Amazon might be on the agenda, but I think spending time alone will provide the clarity you seek.

Would you try to help people with your last year? You probably would. There's no one you feel you need to settle things with or try to make right with, but you'd want to make sure that you made life better for those around you even as your life ended. Above all you'd want to write though - to put together books that make people laugh and think. That's been your greatest sense of achievement so far - writing and publishing Cosmic Nomad. You want to have the incredible experiences and learn the incredible things that it takes to be able to sit down and write a book about it. You have to have lived those experiences, to have walked the talk.

So if I had a year to live, I'd probably write a book about it while travelling. I'd shed all my excess shit, travel on adventures, and write about it every step of the way. That's what's most important to you, and that's what you'd like to spend your days doing - ***writing books that make people laugh and think.***
