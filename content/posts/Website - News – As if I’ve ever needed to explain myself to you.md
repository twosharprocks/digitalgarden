I’m not even going to try to justify a 3 month absence – you should all be used it by now. Especially since I went even longer before the last set of posts. This year on the Mighty Ginge has been pretty poor actually… So rather than apologising (because we’d all know I wouldn’t mean it anyway) I’m going to tell you what I’ve been up to **instead** of tormenting you all with regular posts this year.

- I wrote the one-man show from hell, previewed it in London, then took it to the Edinburgh Fringe for two performances, with plans to return with it next year for a full run. The show, “Apocalypse Meow”, is about doomsday/the end of all life on Earth, and was inspired by the end of an “educational” relationship around Christmas last year. *(Mighty Ginge Life Tip #72 – If you want to be friends after a break up, it’s best not to end it by throwing holy water on her and screaming “THE POWER OF CHRIST COMPELS YOU!”)*
- “Mighty Ginge Radio” (MGRadio) on StroudFM ran along unevenly for several months while I tried to sort my life out, and then went on hiatus while “Apocalypse Meow” was being completed/taken to Edinburgh. Followers of the [Mighty Ginge Facebook page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140320160036/http://www.facebook.com/pages/Josh-Richards-The-Mighty-Ginge/97584028460 "Mighty Ginge Facebook fan page") would have seen the updates on MGRadio were about as regular as updates here. MGRadio returns in a new, shiny and better smelling format this Wednesday night on StroudFM at 8pm-9pm however (see below for more details)
- I started learning to play the ukulele, also know as the guitar for hobbits. I plan on learning the banjo next, and should be re-enacting scenes from Deliverance with pretty-mouthed city slickers by Christmas

As always a whole range of different things have been going on, but I won’t bore you with all the details – needless to say, a fair bit of real life crap has been happening when I should have been updating here being deliberately offensive and obtuse. So while I know I’ll get overwhelmed with more soon enough, for now I can assure you the Ginge is back and the updates are about to start flowing.

As mentioned before, Mighty Ginge Radio returns to StroudFM this Wednesday night – tune on 107.9FM if you’re close enough to the gaping vortex of despair that is Stroud, or alternatively listen from a safe distance [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140320160036/http://www.stroudfm.co.uk/radioplayer.php "StroudFM - 107.9"). Martin Hughes returns to be mercilessly tormented by puppets, I’ll be giving away signed “Apocalypse Meow” posters in a competition that will probably end in legal action, and we’ll be playing music picked by a 9-year-old – it’s going to be wild.

Stay tuned!
