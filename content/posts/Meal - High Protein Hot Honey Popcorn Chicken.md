# Ingredients
- Chicken breast - 600g (cubed)
- Potatoes - 400–500g (diced or frozen cubes)
- Greek yogurt - 150g
- Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
- Oil spray

## Hot Honey Sauce
- Honey - 2 tbsp
- Hot sauce - 1 tbsp
- Chilli flakes (optional)

# Directions
1. Coat diced chicken in Greek yogurt and seasoning
2. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
3. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
4. Cook potatoes until golden and crispy (air fryer or oven)
5. Mix honey and hot sauce to create hot honey glaze
6. Toss cooked chicken in hot honey sauce
7. Serve with crispy potatoes
