For years now there's been something drawing you to the quiet darkness of an otherwise empty forest outpost. There are critical elements to this interest though which are non-negotiable - it they're not present, then the entire vibe is thrown off and it doesn't work.

First and foremost is rain sounds. You knew the moment you heard the sounds of rain as the ship passed Saturn in *Interstellar* that it's the sound that relaxes you most - the sounds that drowns out the other noise, any intrusive thoughts you might have, and never distracts you in itself. Right now you've got the [Dark Forest Ambient Focus Music](https://youtu.be/_sJUHQUcemE?si=GXFuwF0QRZJ_6DZ3) from YouTube on the other screen as you type, and the ambient synths are a fraction distracting... but the rain sounds that run underneath help make up for it. 

It also needs to be dark. This current video is perfect because there's a little bit of ambient light to see the surroundings, but not so much that your vision is restricted to direct lighting. The lighting is enough to leave plenty of shadows, but never long ones. It's also enough to offset the displays that are around - the ideal would be to have the display brightness and surrounding environment both dark but always balanced so that you can look at any of it without a transition. If anything it's be better to have "dark mode" on the screens so they're potentially *darker* than the already dark surroundings. You've just switched to a new, green Obsidian theme which will be interesting - we'll see if it helps or not

The forest aspect is important too, but I wonder if that's less important after the combination of rain and darkness? Being out in a forest in the rain and dark isn't fun, but being inside a secure outpost looking out on a dark forest is one of the most amazing things ever. Being here is calming because the environment is dark, cool, and I have a sense of purpose in being here. It's like being a lighthouse keeper, or manning a radio - you're a sentinel in a secure environment, ready to respond as necessary but mostly there to just keep watch. 

That's what you're really after here - a dry little box with a beautiful, dark & wet view where you can be the human-in-the-loop, reading & writing books, thinking about the universe, and generally being alone with your thoughts.
