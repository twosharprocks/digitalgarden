# Grotte Sainte-Anne

Cave at Tilff, Liège, Belgium. Réseau Lemaire is a section of this cave, not a separate cave record.

## Research notes

The Belgian cave guide describes Réseau Lemaire as an upper level discovered in the 1960s with calcite pools and a lake. It describes controlled access to the cave. The saved post's phrase ‘Lac du Réseau Lemaire’ belongs under this cave.
