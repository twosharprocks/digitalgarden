Short piece of writing on some thing you find interesting
All about retention, learning & reflection
Write fast and often - over time it provides **depth**
Mini-essays make learning more addictive by being **novel**
Like a sketchbook for an artist

---
Three rules of Mini-essays
1. Only cover one idea
2. Keep them short (fit on monitor without scrolling)
3. Keep them organised (Obsidian)

Elements of mini-essay
- 100-300 words (no scrolling within Obsidian, focus on being brief)
- Storytelling approach? Prose? Make sure this serves your goals
- Write it as if you're teaching it (Feynmann technique)
- Restrict to single concept (one distinct idea) or idea (save connection/blending for larger writing)

---
Intro
- Make a strong statement - be bold
- Inspire emotion
- Ask a question
- Tell a story
Body
- Just express idea - get the idea across without rambling
- If asking "How do I say this?" then step away for 10 secs and come back as a fresh reader
Conclusion
- Condense the core idea
