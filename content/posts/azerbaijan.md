# About
**Moderate Potential** 
Areas of Interest: Talysh and Caucasus Foothills
### **Geological Context**
- Azerbaijan has extensive limestone outcrops in the **Greater Caucasus**, **Lesser Caucasus**, and **Talysh Mountains**. 
- Karst formations are concentrated in **Nakhchivan**, **Ismailli**, and **Lankaran** areas.
### **Known Caves**
- Dry caves like **Ashabi-Kahf**, **Karst caves of Gobustan**, and others have archaeological and geological significance.
- **Thermal karst springs** exist in Nakhchivan and other mountainous zones.
### **Cave Diving Status**
- No documented cave diving.
- However, **karst springs near Lankaran and Lerik** may contain submerged sections suitable for exploration.
### **Exploration Potential**
- Greater logistical challenges and less existing infrastructure than Armenia.
- Some springs in **south-eastern Azerbaijan (Talysh Range)** appear strong enough to support substantial submerged conduits.
