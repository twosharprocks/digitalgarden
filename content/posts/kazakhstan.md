# About
[Karatau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karatau,_Kazakhstan) Karst
- **Why:** Vast, underexplored limestone regions with documented spring systems and karst topography.
- **Status:** Negligible caving infrastructure or documentation; excellent for first exploration.
- **Potential:** Large-scale karst mapping with possibility of diving major resurgences.
- **Best Season:** May to August.
