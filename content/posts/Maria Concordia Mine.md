# Additional Notes

"Maria Concordia, an old, flooded Magnesite mine with its irregular corridors, beautiful magnesite veins and crystal clear water seemed to be the perfect site to conduct the survey. Our goal is to create a complete photogrammetry scan of all 4 corridor levels, along with the 40m deep shaft."
- **GoogleMaps:** https://goo.gl/maps/egfnCc5R76Zpa9zE9

- **Website:** https://mariaconcordia.pl/

"The underwater part of the "Maria Concordia" Mine offers over 2 hours of diving in beautiful corridors with still visible magnesite veins, at depths from 40 m to 6 m (4 levels: 38 m, 26 m, 15 m and 7 m). Divers can only move in places made available for diving, which are marked with handrails. While diving, photography and filming for private and commercial purposes is allowed, as well as the use of a scooter.

At the moment, we do not have a compressor room, but we help in organizing the filling of the cylinder at a friendly diving center in Wrocław (0.5 h from Sobótka)."
