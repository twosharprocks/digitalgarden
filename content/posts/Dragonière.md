# Additional Notes

La Dragonnière is a small but beautiful cave in the Lot. The entrance along the road is not really appealing but once inside the colors and the shapes of the rocks are just amazing. During that dive the visibility was not optimal, but good enough for the divers to enjoy the dive and bring back some pictures.
- **Facebook:** https://www.facebook.com/groups/LotCavesConditions/permalink/2848906718638748/

# References

This cave is located on the lands of the Nature Reserve of the Gorges of Ardeche, access is regulated and limited to a few months in the year. It houses the isopod crustacean Sphaeromides raymond, endemic species known less than ten stations in southern France, for which no withdrawals are permitted.
