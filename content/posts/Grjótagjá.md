# About

Grjótagjá is a small cave in the Lake Mývatn area, and was a popular bathing place at one time. However, geological activity in the period 1975-1984, caused the temperature of the water to rise to such a degree that it has not been possible to bathe there since.Geothermal cave pool, too hot these days to bathe in due to the Krafla eruptions of 1975-84.
