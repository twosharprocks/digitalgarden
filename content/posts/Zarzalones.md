# Additional Notes

Access is difficult – a 200m long walk down a 100m high cliff. No entrance fee. The nearest place to park the car is on a private road, and a key is required to open the gates to get there.

The first 20 or so meters of cave which leads one down to a huge habitat that the folks from GAE (who actively explore this system) have set up, has 3 Ts; indeed a pity because it would be a great C1 cave albeit the hard work needed to move the gear. The mail tunnel is huge and drops quickly down from the "Sala GEA" where the habitat is located to 30m in 100m or so of cave. There are a number of smaller and larger (and deeper) circuits that can be enjoyed.

Cueva de Zarzalones, 2020

(Translated from Spanish) 
It is located in the municipality of Yunquera, north of the Sierra de las Nieves, near the source of the Rio Grande. It is the largest siphon in Andalusia and is still being explored by cave divers. The cavity has two entrances that siphon a few meters from the entrance. The tracers have contrasted the interconnection of the underground course of the GESM Sima with the Zarzalones Upwelling, which would give the system a difference in level of more than 1,300 m.

Caving requires special knowledge, equipment and permits, so it should only be practiced when these abilities and resources are available, always in the company of qualified people, and scrupulously respecting the fragile environment that is visited.

GEOLOGICAL DESCRIPTION

Geologically it is located in the Las Nieves Unit (Frontal Units), in the central zone of the karstic plateau of Las Nieves. In general, tectonic episodes and climatic variations have caused large episodes of karstification which, in this case, have modeled the cave on Jurassic flint limestone, terminal Trias dark limestone and Trias dolomites.

# Notes

- Zarzalones Cave, Yunquera (Near Malaga)
	- The Zarzalones spring, in the Sierra de las Nieves, is located 3 km from Yunquera (Málaga), near the San Pascual power plant. Access is through private properties, so the appropriate permits are required. It can be accessed on all terrain up to about 400 meters from the spring, which involves a 10-minute walk on foot to get there.
	- It is a spectacular and labyrinthine cave full of galleries with four branches. In one of these branches, the Galería de la Punta, a depth of -63m has been reached. Others do it up to -42m. It is a siphon with many unevenness. Precisely these slopes make the cave a place with a medium-high difficulty for experienced cave divers because you can enter decompression easily. About 40 meters from the entrance is the only dry gallery, about 200m long.
