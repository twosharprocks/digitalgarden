# Ingredients
- Ground beef - 500g
- Rice - 2 cups cooked
- Corn - 1/2 cup
- Beans - 1 can
- Cheese - 1/2 cup
- Salsa - 1/2 cup
- Seasoning
	- Taco seasoning

# Directions
1. Cook beef with seasoning
2. Prepare rice and vegetables
3. Assemble bowl with all ingredients
4. Top with cheese and salsa
