# About
Best time to go is May (Rubens)
Palau offers **year-round diving**, but the **dry season** from **October to May** is considered the best time to visit. During this period, divers can expect calmer seas, better visibility (often exceeding 30 meters), and a higher likelihood of encountering diverse marine life.

Palau's **Rock Islands**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, are characterized by **uplifted coral limestone formations** that have been sculpted over millions of years by natural processes such as erosion, tidal action, and sea-level changes. This has resulted in a stunning karst topography featuring **mushroom-shaped islands**, **caves**, **sinkholes**, and **underground channels**. These formations are not only geologically significant but also contribute to the rich biodiversity and unique ecosystems found in the region
