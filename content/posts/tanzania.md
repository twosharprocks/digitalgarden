# About

[Zanzibar Archipelago](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jM4sQe2BBDRaYxfs9)
- **Karst Type**: Coral limestone base.
- **Cave Diving**: Limited underwater cave diving, but **inland freshwater caves** like **[Kuza Cave]({{< relref "posts/Kuza Cave.md" >}})** and **[Mangapwani Cave]({{< relref "posts/Mangapwani Cave.md" >}})** offer snorkeling and could present sumps.
- **Potential**: Possibly unexplored submerged systems, especially around **Pemba Island**.
