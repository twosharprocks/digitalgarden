# Other Notes
***Travel***
- Costs: ~$500AUD/10,000TBH per day
- ALWAYS use cash whenever possible
	- Best exchange is at the airport - when you land, visit a green cash exchange and get out as much as you expect to use for the whole trip. 
	- Have cash before leaving airport for driver & food/drink
	- Local ATM's charge 250TBH (~$12AUD) per transaction with a 20,000TBH limit per transaction
	- Keep 500-1000TBH (~$25-50AUD) per person for any costs during the journey back to and within the airport.
- Immigration & Airport Security
	- Before you fly: Complete a [Thailand Digital Arrival Card](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/arrival-card/#/home) with details of Mekkiri Resort
	- Expect to have all fingers & both thumbs scanned while passing immigration into Thailand
	- Rebreathers: Print a sheet explaining in multiple languages what your rebreather is (eg. "SCUBA diving equipment for breathing underwater" in English, Thai, French, Arabic, Chinese, ect). Keep the printed sheet in your bag on top of your rebreather so airport security read it when they inevitably check your bag. 
- Food & Drink
	- Lots of rice, pork, and chicken. 
	- Soups are common (generally chicken or prawns)
	- Spicy food is common: if you don't like spicy food, then request "no spicy" early and often. Most places will offer food without spice or chilli, allowing guests to add their own from a spice/sauce tray
	- Beer ("Leo" ) is easily organised through resort staff, and costs ~60TBH per can
	- Hotels offer instant coffee, goo brewed coffee can be bought from Amazon Cafe (coffee chain), and "Coffee in a can" is commonly available from 7-11s
- Clothes
	- Bring a jumper, mosquito repellant, and hat/sunglasses
	- Mornings can be surprisingly cool
	- Mosquitos can be intense at dawn/dusk
	- Afternoons are often warm & bright, so wear a hat/sunglasses
- Language
	- Before you fly: Install Google Translate with Thai language pack
	- English is rare, but you'll mostly get by with non-verbal communication (eg. Hand signals, pointing at a menu, reading a price on a screen, ect)
	- Bow a lot, try to learn Thai for "Hello" & "Thank You"
	- For anything remotely complex or if there's doubt, use Google Translate

***Diving***
- Mine Certification is REQUIRED
	- Crossover from SSI/TDI Full Cave is easy, but you will  need to demonstrate skills in-water and be mine certified regardless of your existing cave certification.
	- Non-WRSTC cave certifications (eg. CDAA) are ***not recognised***.
- Drysuits are REQUIRED
	- Water temperature is ~23degC, but  potentially contains heavy metals & other toxins. 
	- Minimise direct skin contact wherever possible and don't drink it. 
	- Wear a base layer (eg. Thermal underwear) with a light overlayer (eg. Tracksuit top & pants)
- Helmets are REQUIRED
	- Rockfall is likely in newer/unexplored mine areas, so helmets are required and mask replacement (with helmet removal) is a required in-water skill
	- Backup light/s on your helmet is strongly encouraged
	- Backup mask on the back of your helmet is convenient & encouraged
- All cylinders are aluminium (S80s) 
	- Use waist D-rings on sidemount wings (not back rails) 
	- Bringing rigging is preferable but not essential
	- Bringing cylinder bands/bungee is encouraged
	- Bringing a flat-tip screwdriver (for individual rigging adjustment) is encouraged 
- Rebreathers
	- Sorb: 500TBH (~25AUD) per kg
	- Bringing O2 cylinder rigging (to fit 2L AL) is preferable but not essential
	- Bring spares of any essential parts that might break (eg. Spare cells, unit-specific o-rings, ect)
