Lenny (left) sitting with his newly discovered half-brother Lando

The joys of life aren’t regular features on the Mighty Ginge,but every now and again we like to embrace the lighter side of things here. Today is one such case,where I can proudly announce in our 100th post the latest addition to the Mighty Ginge family –the long lost brother Lando Kapcas.

I brought Lando home from the [Central London Lemur Hospital](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133759/http://www.nhm.ac.uk/) on Thursday and I can proudly say he’s a healthy 550 grams. He does seem to have a confused,mildly retarded look in his eyes,but I’m sure all new born lemurs look that way…

Anyway,if you’re looking for a fuller understanding of how young Lando fits into the Ginge family tree,I’ve included it below –enjoy.
