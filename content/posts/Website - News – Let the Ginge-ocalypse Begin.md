EARTHLINGS! It is I, the Mighty Ginge, returning from my adventures in Europe with a shiny new (metaphoric) hat. In what has quickly become an annual tradition, I lost steam with my web presence toward the end of last year, leaving this page and my Mars Project videos on YouTube in disarray as things in my personal life turned a little crazy. And things did get crazy.

But in another annual tradition, I’m back claiming not to let it happen again (*Editor’s Note: it probably will*). For those of you who don’t like the whole “reading” thing, the return of the Mars Project videos is below;

[Watch archived video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MNOj_cN2uYE)The short story is the return to Stroud hit me for six, and I foolishly locked myself away in a small room crying “I have to **write! WRIIIIIIIIIIITE!**” for 6 hours a day… not writing. For nearly 2 months. Then I packed up the last of my meagre possessions, threw them on my back and hoofed off to Holland for two weeks. This was also supposed to be an opportunity to write, and it also failed.

What I’ve come to realise is that most of my inspiration for writing comes from interacting with other people, and becoming some sort of anti-social hermit inhibits that. I’m not saying I’m about to become a socialite, attending all of the local sexy parties. Nor am I saying I have a tendency to turn a little “Jack Nicholson from The Shining” if it’s all work and no play. It’s just that this **is** supposed to be fun, and I should re-read what I’ve done and laugh. And staring at a laptop trying to force writing produces the opposite of that.

Wait, what was I talking about?

ANYWAY! I’m back in Perth! It’s hot enough to fry eggs outside, my pasty white thighs are a rosy red colour after slapping on sunscreen **everywhere else** yesterday before a bike ride, and now I’m inside with the air conditioning on at midday contemplating making a vegan quinoa chilli. That’s right - **VEGAN!** **AND SOMETHING THAT LOOKS LIKE “QUINN-OAH” BUT IS ACTUALLY PRONOUNCED “KEEN-WAH”. ALSO, BOLDED CAPITALS!**

Because until recently I hadn’t quite realised my standard breakfast of eggs, tinned spaghetti and half a pan-fried pig might *not*be a feasible diet to maintain when you’re locked in a spacecraft capsule hurtling towards Mars for 7 months. Nor am I aware of any break-through in technology that allows us to grow pigs hydroponically once we’ve landed on Mars.

As such, I am broadening my comfort zone and *attempting* to learn vegan recipes, the first of which I’ll be cooking & filming for YouTube this afternoon. Luckily there’s a heap of left-over curried sausages from last night’s dinner if it all goes horribly, horribly wrong.

***Horribly*** *wrong*

In other news: Mars One’s [Astronaut Application Newsletter](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108032240/http://mars-one.us5.list-manage1.com/subscribe?u=24d8ce153d9cbd2546aca36de&id=68735e115d "Astronaut Application Updates") has just sent out its first update, requesting feedback from subscribers on “Why Would You Like To Go To Mars?” in 100-1000 words. The idea is to pass on to the media what motivates people to leave Earth, risking life and limb on a 7 month journey to start a colony on another planet without any foreseeable chance to return. My response took about a week to formulate fully, and I’m hoping that what I submitted is worthy of a quote somewhere, or at least someone’s\* attention.

But in the meantime, my full 953 word answer to “Why Would You Like To Go To Mars” will be up here mid-week. It’s big, wordy, and talks about being all inspirational and the like. Which I am – I’m downright majestic. I just like to play being sarcastic and flippant as a defence-mechanism from you lot.

And just to add to the excitement of 2013, [Axe/Lynx Apollo has just launched a competition](https://web.archive.org/web/20131108032240/http://www.space.com/19199-axe-apollo-space-launch-contest.html) to give 22 ordinary (but very lucky) folks one of the greatest competitive prizes ever: a sub-orbital flight on an SXC “Lynx Mark 2″ to ~100km up.

[Watch archived video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x7gu8WVQNOQ)For our American friends the competition is already closed, but for those of us in sunny Australia the competition opens on March 1st and will give 4 Aussies the chance to touch space (provided you get through the training). So keep an eye out for deodorant-based space mischief in the coming weeks!
