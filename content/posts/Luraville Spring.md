# References

Portions of the following information were taken from Florida Geological Survey Bulletin 66.

From the junction of SR 51 and Luraville Road in Luraville, drive east on on Luraville Road to Dive Oupost 0.25 miles on the right. The spring is on private property (owned by Cathy Lesh) to the right and

behind the dive shop. It is about 600 feet southeast of the junction of CR 51 and Luraville Road.

Description - Luraville Spring has an oval-shaped spring pool that is approximately 10 ft wide

and 20 ft long. It discharges from the base of an overhanging limestone bank that

is about 8.0 ft high. The spring depth at the vent is 23.1 ft, but the rest of the

spring pool averages about 0.5 ft deep. The water is clear and bluish.  The spring

bottom is exposed sand with a thin layer of algae. No spring boil is observed.  The

spring is surrounded by a privately-owned, densely-forested land. Limestone and sand

banks directly above the vent rise to about 20 ft above the spring. Luraville Spring

is reported to be connected to the Peacock Spring underwater cave system and Telford

Spring, which has been mapped for several miles underground.
