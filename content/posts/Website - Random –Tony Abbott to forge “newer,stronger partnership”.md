[![](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133246im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/christmas-island.jpg "christmas island")](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133246/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/christmas-island.jpg)

Australian Opposition leader Tony Abbott has announced the formation of a new partnership to stem illegal immigration,as part of his on-going campaign to “Stop the Boats ™”,signing a partnership agreement with the cliffs of Christmas Island. Under the new agreement,the cliffs will continue to [crush and drown any refugees attempting to make landfall](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133246/http://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-1338730/Christmas-Island-shipwreck-27-refugees-die-rocks-tragedy.html) in the rough seas around Christmas Island,while Abbott will strive to use [future incidents to strengthen his campaign against so-called “boat people”](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530133246/http://www.couriermail.com.au/news/national/tony-abbott-calls-for-tougher-laws-to-prevent-further-asylum-seeker-boat-tragedies/story-e6freooo-1225973580725).

Abbott was quoted at the signing “This historic agreement between party and geology is a perfect example of how politics should be done. Today we are here to form a newer,stronger partnership since the Nationals became extinct. These cliffs may have only formed 6,000 years ago with the rest of the world,but I hope they’re around for another 6,000  when I’m elected Prime-Minister. After that we’ll just bomb any boats we find. With bibles”

The cliffs of Christmas Island were unavailable for comment
