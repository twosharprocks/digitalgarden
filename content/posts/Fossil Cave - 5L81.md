# From CaveDB
Fossil cave is named for the fossils found there so care must be taken not to disturb these. It is a doline collapse that has east and west dives. The west side is a shortish cave level dive that has many nooks and cranies to explore. The west is bigger and rated cave for the most part but there is a restriction at 13m that you must be cave rated to pass and enters the tank cave system.

Prior to diving you must book [here](https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/parks/piccaninnie-ponds-conservation-park/booking/143656)

You will also need to complete an indemnity form available [here](https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/parks/piccaninnie-ponds-conservation-park/diving-indemnity-form)

**NOTE:** Divers must not dive beyond the restriction to enter the phreatic passage unless the divers are qualified to the Advanced Cave Diver level.
This restriction is indicated by a depth of 13m.
