# Ingredients
- 1 tortilla wrap
- 4 tbsp bolognese
- 1 fresh lasagne sheet, or a dried sheet cooked for 5 minutes
- 2 tbsp béchamel sauce
- Handful of grated cheese and mozzarella

# Directions
1. Place the wrap flat and layer 2 tbsp bolognese, half the lasagne sheet, 1 tbsp béchamel and cheese.
2. Repeat the layers once.
3. Fold into a burrito and wrap it in foil.
4. Air-fry at 180°C for 10 minutes.
