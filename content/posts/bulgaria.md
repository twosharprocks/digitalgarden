# About
[Rhodope Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodope_Mountains)
- **Why:** Part of the same karst belt as the Balkans; caves like Duhlata are among the longest in Eastern Europe.
- **Status:** Extensive dry cave exploration but fewer cave diving ventures than Western Europe.
- **Potential:** High-value survey targets with minimal diving records.
- **Best Season:** June to September.
