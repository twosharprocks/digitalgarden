News – US Relations Tour Days 21, 26 & 29

[![IMAG0267 179x300 News

US Relations Tour Days 21, 26 & 29](https://web.archive.org/web/20141101041028im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/IMAG0267-179x300.jpg "Portland")](https://web.archive.org/web/20141101041028/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/IMAG0267.jpg)

Well I’m in Portland! Things have been pretty wild so far, but at least I can take a breath and sort things while I’m here. I’ve decided to skip San Fransisco – simply won’t have time to enjoy it, and would have spent most of my time carrying my packs around instead of doing anything. So today I’m off to practice harmonica and play Radiohead’s “Creep” on ukulele in the middle of Portland’s university district, and tomorrow I’m off to Santa Barbara to see the Silversun Pickups!

And if you haven’t heard the news from the Day 29 video or [Twitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20141101041028/https://twitter.com/#!/Mighty_Ginge "Twitter @Mighty_Ginge") – Keith the Anger Management Koala will be the opening act for “Koalafied Komedy” at The Mint in West Hollywood on June 10th. Inexplicably the organisers have decided I should start their Aussie showcase show, featuring some of Australia’s best comics such as Jackie Loeb, Sally Kimpton, Rove McManus and Wil Anderson; and hosted by the amazing Monty Franklin. Apparently a Chlamydia-riddled koala playing ukulele and screaming about sexual assault is just what their night needs.
