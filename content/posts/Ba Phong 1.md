# Ba Phong 1

Cave reported from Belgian–Vietnamese expeditions in northern Vietnam. The saved lead says only ‘Ba Phong’; whether its Facebook post depicts Ba Phong 1 is unverified.

## Research notes

The expedition overview lists Ba Phong 1 among caves surveyed during work in Son La and Lai Chau provinces and reports 900 m at the time. Do not attach that figure to a different ‘Ba Phong’ cave without checking the original post.
