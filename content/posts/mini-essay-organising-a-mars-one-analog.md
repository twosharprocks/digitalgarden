An unexpected idea that appeared recently is to organise a Mars analog with a bunch of Mars One candidates in Wadi Rum. I'm not sure why something like this wasn't organised previously, but the contact from DIrk through LinkedIn has triggered a cascade of ideas around a filmed Mars analogue featuring folks who volunteered for Mars One. 

Thinking about it, it hasn't taken much to put together a list of candidates to approach, or to pick a potential time to go - Jan/Feb 2027. There may be climate issues with a simulation in Wadi Rum then, but that's all information you can research and put together before reaching out to potential analog astronauts. It's also possible that 2027 is too soon, but I need to pick something to aim for, and it can always be adjusted later. 

What I really need to ask is why I or anyone else should want to do this? Is it just about the experience? I think there's more to it than that, but my gut reaction is "Because it's there" and that is honestly reason enough. I'd also love to finally see Mars One candidates doing something together after all these years - to share what brought us all together in the first place, and to be in one place doing a little of the thing that excited us all so much. If we can do a little research and communicate some science too, then that's a huge bonus. But I think what I'd love most is to tell the story of the Mars One project, to ask why so many of us were willing to sign up for something so unique and challenging, and to share how it changed each of us in the process. 

Sharing that experience could take a range of different formats, and I'll need to figure out what will work best based on what and who is available. A documentary would be incredible, and I think it's certainly possible if I put together a solid pitch, but I also recognise that it might not be the best use of resources to make something like this happen. A podcast is certainly something to seriously consider too - it's been done before with the Habitat, but that's no reason why you couldn't do something short form. You could also easily write a book about it and about humans in space and space analogues, although I'm not convinced that a book is what you want to do in this case - you have already written about Mars One with "Cosmic Nomad" and you have other books you want to write instead. 

Ultimately this is about storytelling and experience, and your first impulse is to organise a Mars analogue because it would just be an amazing experience for everyone involved. What it evolves into is yet to be determined, but I'd love to be storytelling around Mars One again while also sharing who we've become since it shutdown. So don't get too hung up on a documentary crew or a podcast or anything else - organise this because you want to share an incredible experience with a team of Mars One candidates. 

Make the candidates and their stories the focus here, and seek out others who would like to contribute to our story rather than seeking out supporters who it might later rely on. The candidates have always been the most vital part of Mars One, so don't lose sight of that chasing support or endorsement from anyone else - the only people who need to support this are the Mars 100. If other candidates want to contribute, then offer them ways to do that - mission support, social media, podcast interviews, ect. But you must be willing to organise this without the support of anyone except the 3 Mars One candidates who join you in the habitat.
