McCormick Sink connects to a small, silty, cave passage approximately 1000 feet in length depth of 145 feet deep. To date, explorers have been unsuccessful in linking this cave to nearby Rose Creek Cave System. Samplers were located at a depth of 125 feet deep, about 300 feet into the cave.
