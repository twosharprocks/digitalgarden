Mighty Ginge Radio returned from a self-imposed hiatus for a Christmas Eve special on last Saturday, and I have to admit it was a lot of fun. Martin and I caused utter chaos in the station for the first time in months, playing all kinds of questionable Christmas music, singing Chipmunk songs after sucking helium, and generally acting like irresponsible 4 year olds that had somehow broken into a radio station for an hour. As those of you who listened to StroudFM last Saturday night (clearly after some sort of stroke) will already know, it was also a proper farewell to Mighty Ginge Radio as we know it as I move into producing other shows (namely Martin’s own technology show in the same timeslot). I’m continuing to put radio specials together that will be periodically released through the year though, so stay tuned for announcements.

And for those that missed it (or just can’t get enough of our unique form of Christmas spirit), the MGR Christmas Eve Special is available [right here](https://web.archive.org/web/20120323153424/http://themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/Podcasts/MGR%237%20December%2024%20(Xmas%20Eve%20Special).mp3 "MGR #7 - Xmas Eve Special"); or you can download it along with all the previous Mighty Ginge Radio shows from the [Media page](https://web.archive.org/web/20120323153424/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/media/ "Mighty Ginge - Media").

I should probably encourage you all to buy/order one of StroudFM’s [“Uncovered” 2012 calendars](https://web.archive.org/web/20120323153424/http://www.stroudfm.co.uk/index.php/news/44-naked-radio-on-stroud-fm "StroudFM Naked calendar") – yours truly nuded up for it and wound up being “Mr March”. Alternatively – since I know you’ll all just be buying it so my pasty white ass can light up your bedrooms – you could just print out the image below (click for the full-size version). I won’t judge you – after all, I’m the one who had to stand naked with my hand inside a baboon puppet while a middle-aged American woman took photos…

[![FAP FOR GREAT JUSTICE!](https://web.archive.org/web/20120323153424im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/smallSTROUDFM-001.jpg "StroudFM Naked calendar - March")](https://web.archive.org/web/20120323153424/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/smallSTROUDFM-001.jpg)

Finally, the Mighty Ginge is returning with a vengeance in the New Year. Probably against my better judgement I’ve set myself some pretty high goals for writing website content and stand up material, while creating yet another terrifying comedy entity – Keith the Koala – **and** touring Australia and the US. So if things get a little weird on here, it’s the result of me **NOT SLEEPING BECAUSE THERE’S TOO MUCH COMEDY TO WRITE**

Christ, I’m writing this bullshit at 2:30 in the morning. Looking forward to a particularly weird 2012 Ginge fans – lets just hope the Mayans missed a zero somewhere…
