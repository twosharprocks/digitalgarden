# Toh Wang

Flooded cave in Satun Province, southern Thailand. The vault owner's saved Satun post refers to this cave, also called Tham To Wang.

## Exploration

Mikko Paasi's first-person account describes exploration of submerged passages in 2023. The project team and its sponsor report more than a kilometre of passage explored at the time and continuing work in the wider karst area. Possible connections to nearby sinkholes are exploration leads, not established links.
