"The origin of the name is unknown, certainly the cave that opens up between the light rocks is black, but the water is completely normal. It is thought to derive from the color of the bottom, covered in dark moss, on which the underground stream flows out before flowing into the Senaiga. Unlike today, it was once always well fed even in periods of drought, raising questions about its origin. In the thirties it was proven, with the introduction of dyes, that the cave is in communication with the Castel Tesino Caves, located at 1000 meters above sea level and a few kilometers away as the crow flies. The fine sand rich in granite crystals sedimented inside the cave indicates a supply of water much further away, coming from the Cima d'Asta Group. This suggests that there is a vast and intricate system of communicating caves inside the mountains."

"...exploration began only in the 70s by a Speleo diving group from Padua and continued in recent years by a Speleological Group from Valstagna (VI), 1600 meters have been explored overcoming three challenging siphons and many difficult passages."
