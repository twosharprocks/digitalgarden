# About

## 🗺️ Site Overview

The site features a vertical solution tube approximately 1 meter in diameter, descending around 16 meters to reach the water. Divers can swim to a clean rock in waist-deep water, which serves as a stable platform for donning or doffing dive gear.

The dive itself includes a spacious underwater main chamber that connects to a tunnel reaching a maximum depth of 33 meters.

## 🚪 Access Requirements

To access the site, divers must:

- Hold a minimum Cave diving certification.
- Be prepared for entry and exit via an overhanging cliff.
- Possess the strength and skill to climb a 15-meter free-hanging wire rope ladder. If this is not feasible, contact the Site Access Officer at least one week in advance to arrange alternative ingress/egress.
- Ensure at least one team member has prior experience accessing and diving this site, and can guide the team onto and off the property.

## 📋 Site Rules & Conditions

- Smoking is strictly prohibited.
- No compressors are allowed on site.
- One team member must remain topside at all times.
- Entry to the property is permitted after 8:00 AM; all divers must depart by 2:00 PM.
- Maintain sanitary standards and minimize visual impact. Use the designated dairy shed toilet located near the lunchroom/office.

## 🛠️ Access Notes

Refer to the Bakers Cave Site Access Protocols in the CDAA Regulations Manual (pg. 41).

To use the toilet:
- Press the green button to activate the pump before entering.
- After use, press the red button to turn it off.
- Before leaving, ensure the pump has not been left running.

The instrument panel is located on the left-hand side wall next to the office. The toilet is around the left-hand corner from the office.
