# Ingredients
- Hash browns (frozen or pre-cooked) - 400g
- Halloumi - 200g (cubed)
- Chorizo - 150g (sliced)
- Eggs - 3–4
- Onion - 1/2 (diced)
- Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
- Oil - 1 tbsp

# Seasoning
- Salt
- Pepper
- Smoked paprika
- Chilli flakes (optional)

# Directions
1. Cook hash browns in a large pan until crispy and golden, then set aside
2. In the same pan, cook chorizo until it releases oils and crisps
3. Add onion and garlic, cooking until softened
4. Add halloumi and cook until golden on all sides
5. Return hash browns to the pan and mix everything together
6. Create small wells and crack eggs into the pan
7. Cover and cook until eggs reach desired doneness
8. Serve hot
