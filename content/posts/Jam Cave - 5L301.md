# From CaveDB
BC visited 04/23: 70 cm hole descending straight down. Need ropes to enter exit, ideally an ascender as it is a hard climb out. Opens in 5m cavern area with big mound in the center that looks like accumulation or someone trying to fill it. Area at the bottom of the pile has fresh water in it.  Digging is hard and there will be a lot of material to remove for any hope of progressing.
