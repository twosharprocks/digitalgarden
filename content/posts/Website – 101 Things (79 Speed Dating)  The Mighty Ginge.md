College Of Awesome – 101 Things (#79 Speed Dating)  ![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jcNZttHjPmE)  As much as it pains me to admit it – it’s Valentine’s Day tomorrow. And while last week’s [Ron Burgundy Day](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/2012/01/11/college-of-awesome-ron-burgundy-day/ "COA: Ron Burgundy Day") tried to get the sex back into sexual harassment, there’s still plenty of people out there turning to Valentine’s Day for some old-fashioned commercialised romance. So instead of raging any further about the dubious history of St. Valentine’s Day, I’d like to suggest an alternative for those of you that failed to get laid from yelling “I WANT TO BE ON YOU” last week – speed dating.  [![](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/cupid-uzi-300x262.jpg "cupid uzi (300x262)")](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/cupid-uzi-300x262.jpg)  Now speed dating has a fair nasty stigma – dozens of guys and girls having “mini-dates” of 3-5 minutes, then ticking a secret ballot paper to say who they’d like to share contact details with – **could** be seen as slightly sleazy. But as someone who went speed dating at university (and had a damn good time doing it), I discovered it’s all in how you approach it. Most people went trying to meet the love of their life – I went to mess with other single people, and met a girl I lived with for 2 years.  So if you are considering going speed dating to help numb the loneliness tomorrow, I have a few suggestions to turn what could be an uncomfortable and ultimately disappointing experience (and that’s just the cry-wank you have when you get home), into a few hours of random/memorable hilarity. Hell, you might even get laid out of it…  **Go For A Laugh, Not A Lay**  Sure, it’s got “dating” on the end of it – but who said speed dating has to be about romance? I went because A) The girl organising it is an old friend, B) She’d had someone drop out last-minute and need even numbers of guys to girls, and C) I thought it would be hilarious trying to convince every girl I met that I was born in the Andes and raised by mountain goats till I was 5.  ![](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVpDTyelBJo)  I had **no** intention of trying to meet someone – I’d just started my last year of university, was president of the most [infamous social club](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123/http://iap.curtin.edu.au/students/physsed.cfm "Oh PHYSSED, how I miss thee...") on campus, and my project supervisor had just  *vaporised* without warning over the summer to another university. This was purely to help out a friend and have a laugh at the expense of others – the signs of a true gentleman.  Go speed dating to meet crazy people, or just people in general. Go and see if you can convince someone you have a psychic link to spider monkeys (or lemurs in my case). Take a friend and dare each other to tell more and more elaborate lies. Just don’t go planning to meet the Captain of the SS Loveboat during a date shorter than most songs. Unless of course you’re telling people you **are** the Captain of the SS Loveboat, in which case please continue. I’m sure it’ll help you to…  **Be Memorable**  [![](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/green-clown-258x300.jpg "green clown")](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/green-clown.jpg)  You’re about to meet more than a dozen members of the opposite sex, and you have 3-5 minutes to make a decision if you’d want them to call you. This is some high-pressure decision-making – fuck up and you could wind up inviting the girl who eats her toenails around for dinner with your parents, or sleeping with the guy who collects your hair for his extensive home-made doll collection.  So you want to make a fast and **memorable** impression. But because you’re *not* trying to get them to home with you, you don’t need to stress about making a *good* impression. Of the 20 girls there, I remember just 4 of them;  - The crazy blonde chick, who grabbed my ballot paper the moment I sat down and drew a cartoon elephant yelling “WEE-OR!” - The hockey-worshipping Canadian - The American who’d grown up in Indonesia and come to support the Canadian - The cranky brunette who I met last, spilt my drink on, and about 3 seconds before the night ended discovered had a shared interest in hiking.  I have no idea if these were the most attractive/sweetest/most appropriate potential partners out of all the women there – all I know is they were the most interesting and memorable. The Canadian and American had it relatively easy because obviously being foreign alone made them more distinctive/memorable. And the cranky brunette was memorable simply because she was **such a sour bitch.** Sure, it was the end of the night and everyone had kind of had enough. And yes, I’d drunk enough to tranquillise a bull elephant by that stage. But scowling at someone on a 3 minute date? If I wanted to get judgemental glares from attractive women while I was drunk, I’d have started turning up to my psychology tutorials again.  No, the really interesting one here is the blonde that drew the elephant. Now it’s not deliberate discrimination, but I have to admit I’ve never really been attracted to blonde women – my mum is blonde, and most of my friends in school were blonde girls and have always stayed friends. I’ve met a few “hot blondes” since leaving high school, but it was really the brunettes and redheads that kick-started this puberty party.   [![](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/gingers-no-soul-great-tits1-221x300.jpg "no soul, great tits")](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/gingers-no-soul-great-tits1.jpg)[   ](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/gingers-no-soul-great-tits.jpg)So cartoon-elephant-drawing blonde girl was already heading into the friend zone before I’d sat down – that non-existent friend-zone I was using because I was just there for my own twisted amusement. But I distinctly remember her: even though she was one of the girls I met early in the night **six years ago**, and I’ve never had any contact with her again; I remember her because grabbing someone’s ballot paper and drawing a yelling cartoon elephant on it is a **completely mental thing to do.** I want to point out of course the *elephant* was the one yelling “WEE-OR!”, not her. But in retrospect I probably would have shared my details with her if she  **had** yelled “WEE-OR!” during our 3 minute date, because god knows that one would have been a keeper.  **Get Your Drunk On**  [![](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/morgan-300x227.jpg "Too much Cap'n")](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608104123/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/morgan.jpg)  Alcohol – the cause and solution to all of the world’s problems. Sure, cranky-hiking-brunette didn’t appreciate me cheekily slurring and spilling my precious Southern Comfort everywhere – but if I hadn’t been drunk when I met her, I probably would have told her how absurdly dull she was. And I sure as shit wouldn’t have remembered her. So in this case alcohol stopped me from being rude to someone **and** helped me remember who they were.  I’d highly recommend having a few drinks through the night, but maybe pace yourself. Remember you’re looking at 2-3 hours of social lubrication here. So when you have a break “to go to the bar” at the hour mark, maybe order a soft drink instead of the round of tequila shots I did. Maybe **don’t** start drinking 3 hours before “for courage”. And maybe don’t start stealing the drinks of your dates to “check for poison” because you’re not being a modern-day gentleman, your paving the way to rehab.  Other than that, go out and have fun! Speed dating is a real blast – you’ll meet some fascinating people and have lots of laughs. And make sure you have lots of laughs. Because there’s no **way** you’ll be laughing when you wake up on the morning of the 15th, missing patches of hair and surrounded by your new boyfriend’s hand-made dolls.
