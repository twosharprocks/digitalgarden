# References

Joe's Sink is a series of solution tubes and a small passage in the southeast

corner that is said to connect to [Mary's Sink]({{< relref "posts/Mary's Sink.md" >}}).

Both sinks are located on Highway 50 in Hernando County.  Additionally, this sink

is in the area of [Nursery Sink]({{< relref "posts/Nursery Sink.md" >}}),

Diepoler II and

III

as well as Lost 40.

The average temperature in this system is 68°.  The surface pool affords several solution tubes with no going passage.

Be somewhat careful here as it is a local hangout for teenagers using this

system as a swimming hole.  I have personally pulled a Ford Expedition out of

this sink as the owner thought he could drive through the middle of it.
