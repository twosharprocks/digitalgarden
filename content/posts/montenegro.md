# Existing Notes
# About
Durmitor Karst Plateau
- **Why:** Deep karst pockets, alpine caves, and seasonal lakes; hydrologically active.
- **Status:** Documented caves like Ice Cave and others, but little underwater work.
- **Potential:** High-altitude flooded sumps, glacial karst interaction zones.
- **Best Season:** July to September.

# Caves
Vruja Spring, 43.0345, 19.7936, 
- Deep karst spring near Bijelo Polje; explored to over 70m depth. 
Oko Skakavice, 42.4962, 19.8194, 
- Spectacular resurgence in Prokletije; partially dived, unexplored depth.
Obod Spring, 42.3674, 19.0262, 
- Karst spring near Rijeka Crnojevića; potential link to Lipa Cave system.
Karst Spring at Plužine, 43.1583, 18.8442, 
- Emergent spring from vertical cliff face above Piva Lake; promising but unmapped.  
Grlja Spring, 42.5451, 19.9484, 
- Powerful resurgence in limestone gorge near Gusinje; unknown submerged extent.
