For awhile now I've been thinking about the emotional baggage I'm still carrying, and the overwhelming desire to shed it. As far as I can tell, it really centers on two different perceptions: a sense of hurt or harm from the things that happened in the past, and the sense that I've invested so much previously and feel drained by the lack of return. 

On the "hurt" front, I guess a big part of me still feels broken by 2020, and even by things that happened in 2019. There's no question that the experience of Georgie's crash and then the final collapse of Mars One really broke me, but I also know that I've recovered from worse. It's also nearly 6 years since all of that happened - another year and all of the cells in my body will have changed, and I'll literally be a new person. The bigger issue isn't the loss or the hurt, it's that it's not been replaced with something that was bigger or better. You've always sought adventure, and then used the valleys between to develop your stories, but after being involved in something like Mars One a lot of what' s to come is going to feel like valleys. You will continue to be drawn into extraordinary adventures, and you'll plan and develop your own adventures too, but there is really too much story inside you right now and it's making it difficult to enjoy the daily joys. You wrote Cosmic Nomad as a way of letting go of Mars One, and you started your Australian cave diving adventure a year before - now is the time to write the book about your cave diving beginnings and let them go too. You're ready to level up what you're doing, and it really feels like you have until ADEX to get all of that sorted - to publish and talk about your Book - Cave Diving at ADEX. Everything else you're doing is amazing, but the real strength for you is in storytelling about an incredible sport that you love. So let go of that hurt by telling the story of how you got to where you are today. 

The investment element seems very small now compared to the opportunity you have to release that hurt through a book. When you look honestly at you "investment" it's really just the things you've tried to cover the wound and distract yourself with. You should absolutely work on things like a podcast, about improving your storytelling opportunities, and about converting your ideas into amazing works of art. But you also know that many of them are simple distractions from the thing you know will produce the best results - writing your next book. You have so much invested in the next 5 years and you've done your best to develop and entertain a wide range of ideas... But you know that your next steps should be focused on writing books. All the answers you need can be found within yourself, and you simply need to focus in on writing to bring them out onto the page. 

You've just tried to distract yourself with all the different plans you had for 2026, and yet you've now stopped and reflected on what you can do in this moment... And it's really just about [Writing]({{< relref "posts/writing.md" >}}) [Mini Essays]({{< relref "posts/Mini Essays.md" >}}) and continuing to develop that Storytelling element that you know is so important. So use the notes you have, but start to condense them as you generate articles, blog posts, and so many other outputs that you can be proud of. Take what you have and turn it into great art - that's all there is to it.
