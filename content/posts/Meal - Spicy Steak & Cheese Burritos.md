# Ingredients
- 16 low-carb wraps
- 900g lean steak
- Reduced-fat cheese
- 800g cooked rice
- Salt and black pepper
- Paprika, garlic powder and cumin
- Coriander
- Onion and capsicum

## Sauce
- 300g fat-free Greek yoghurt
- 70g light sour cream
- 50g chipotle in adobo
- Juice of ½ lime

# Directions
1. Season and cook the steak, then slice or chop it.
2. Sauté the onion and capsicum until tender.
3. Blend or stir together the yoghurt, sour cream, chipotle and lime.
4. Divide rice, steak, vegetables, cheese and sauce between wraps.
5. Roll tightly and toast, or wrap and freeze for meal prep.
