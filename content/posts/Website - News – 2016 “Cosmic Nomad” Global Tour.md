## Josh Richards – 2016 “Cosmic Nomad” Global Tour

**![Cosmic Nomad](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/received_605394344788-1024x766.jpeg)**After spending a week [locked in a box to promote the DVD release of The Martian](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/2016/02/04/martian/), I’ve escaped and can proudly announce that throughout 2016 I’ll be touring my new comedy show “Cosmic Nomad” globally – first around Australia before South Africa, the UK, Ireland, and throughout the US!

Cosmic Nomad is both my most personal and funniest show yet – looking at about how my life has changed since I was [shortlisted to the Mars 100](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927/http://www.mars-one.com/news/press-releases/the-mars-100-mars-one-announces-round-three-astronaut-candidates), and I can’t wait to [premiere it at the Royal Institute’s Science Exchange for the Adelaide Fringe next week!](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927/https://www.adelaidefringe.com.au/fringetix/cosmic-nomad/86630991-a968-4bed-a9b7-a5263027113f)

I’m also proud to say that I’ll be heading straight from Los Angeles to Haifa (Israel) to be a Teaching Associate at the [International Space University’s 2016 Space Studies Program (SSP16)!](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927/http://www.isunet.edu/about/space-studies-program/ssp16-technion-institute-israel/265) Apparently my work as a TA at this year’s [Southern Hemisphere Space Studies Program](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927/http://unisa.edu.au/spaceprogram) in Adelaide – along with my ability to pretend to be Mark Watney in Sydney – left enough of an impression that they’re getting me to help out on the 9 week program too 😉

![Haifa](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Haifa.jpg)

It’s just as well that SSP16 finishes on September 1st, because [Mars One’s final astronaut selection phase starts on September 3rd](https://web.archive.org/web/20160530015927/https://community.mars-one.com/blog/astronaut-selection-process-qa-with-dr.-norbert-kraft). If I get through I’ll be one of 24 (selected from the remaining 100 astronaut candidates) to start full-time training in 2017 ahead of the scheduled launch in 2026.

Like I said – it’s going to be one hell of a year.
