The Garden - the web as topology, web as space. 
- Web as arrangement and rearrangement, relational
- Multi-linear approaches - every walk through the garden is different every time
- “Did the bridge come after these trees” in a well-designed garden is meaningless historical trivia.

The Stream - Jump in and let it flow past
- Stream replaces topology with serialisation (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram)
- Single, time-ordered path with our experience (and only our experience) at the centre
- Stream is self-assertive (Garden is integrative)

"As We May Think" by Vannevar Bush - Essay was inspiration for the pioneers of hypertext
- People think the web works like the "Memex" in the essay, but it does not
	- Memex is a tool to think with, not a tool to publish with.
	- Link, annotate, change, summarise, copy, and share (Gardening verbs)
	- Memex library contains original materials of you and others
	- Links are associative - they remind you of the questions you need to ask, of connections that are not obvious
		- The majority of links are made by readers, not writers
