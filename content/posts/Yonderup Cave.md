# Additional Notes

Yonderup Cave is one of the adventure caves that are available by booking only. Experience an underground wonderland full of natural cave decorations such as stalagmites, helictites, columns and shawls throughout this 'stream cave'.

A popular place for birthday parties and team building exercises.

During the guided walk you will discover the cave’s history, geology and the ecosystem that lies within. Yonderup Cave’s constant temperature makes this tour a comfortable all-year-round experience.

Participants are required to wear closed-in shoes. Minimum age 8 years old.

Group bookings can be made by phoning (08) 9303 7772 or email bookingsynp@dbca.wa.gov.au

Closed on public holidays.
