# Ingredients
- Chicken breast - 500g
- Pasta - 300g
- Garlic - 3 cloves
- Cream - 200ml
- Parmesan - 1/2 cup
- Spinach - 1 cup
- Seasoning
	- Salt
	- Pepper

# Directions
1. Cook pasta and set aside
2. Cook chicken and slice
3. Sauté garlic, add cream and parmesan
4. Add pasta, chicken and spinach
5. Stir and serve
