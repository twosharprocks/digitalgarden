# Additional Notes

This karst feature is a fairly bland sort of thing; really nothing more than an almost-circular and degraded collapse doline with no known caves leading off. It is around 90 metres in approximate diameter and some 7-8 metres deep in the centre, but it doesn't quite reach the water table and there is only slight rock exposure in the soil-covered
slopes. It is adjacent to 5L157.
