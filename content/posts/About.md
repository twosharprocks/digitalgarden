Welcome to the [Digital Garden](/posts/digital-garden/) of [Josh Richards](https://joshrichards.com.au/).

This is a working notebook made public: part personal wiki, part field notebook, part archive, and part long-running attempt to connect ideas before they have settled into something polished.

You may be wondering what a digital garden is, or why this not-quite-blog looks like it belongs in the 90s. Maggie Appleton's article on the [history and ethos of digital gardens](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history) is a good explanation, but the short version is that this place is meant to grow in public rather than arrive fully finished.

Most notes here are connected with `[[wiki links]]`. Some are tiny seeds, some are more developed, and some are old posts or references kept because they still point somewhere useful.

## Start Here

- **[Writing](/posts/writing/)** - Mini Essays, articles, book ideas, older website posts, and notes about making words behave.
- **[Diving](/posts/diving/)** - Dive sites, cave regions, trip planning, gear, photogrammetry, exploration, and field notes.
- **[Cyber Security](/posts/cyber-security/)** - Study notes, cheat sheets, threat modelling, OSCP/CISSP material, and security references.
- **[Physics](/posts/physics/)** - Cosmology, quantum ideas, information theory, and other deep-space rabbit holes.
- **[Personal](/posts/personal/)** - Goals, bucket lists, media logs, reflections, life admin, and experiments in being a person.
- **[Books](/posts/books/)** - Books, reading notes, and source material that keeps resurfacing.
- **[Trips](/posts/trips/)** - Trip plans, expedition ideas, travel logistics, and place-based notes.
