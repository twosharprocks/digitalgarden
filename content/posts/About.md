Welcome to the [Digital Garden]({{< relref "posts/Digital Garden.md" >}}) of [Josh Richards](https://joshrichards.com.au/).

This is a working notebook made public: part personal wiki, part field notebook, part archive, and part long-running attempt to connect ideas before they have settled into something polished.

You may be wondering what a digital garden is, or why this not-quite-blog looks like it belongs in the 90s. Maggie Appleton's article on the [history and ethos of digital gardens](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history) is a good explanation, but the short version is that this place is meant to grow in public rather than arrive fully finished.

Most notes here are connected with `wiki links`. Some are tiny seeds, some are more developed, and some are old posts or references kept because they still point somewhere useful.
