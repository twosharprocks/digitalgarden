Welcome to the [Digital Garden](/posts/digital-garden/) of [Josh Richards](https://joshrichards.com.au/)

You may be wondering what a "digital garden" is, or why this "not-quite-blog" looks like it belongs in the 90's. There's a great article from [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/) that perfectly sums up the [history and ethos of the digital garden](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history), but put simply this is my way of cultivating ideas and writing without the pressure of hitting "publish" on a blog post.

Just like a real garden, everything here is constantly growing and changing... and probably requires weeding. You'll find links that connect to other parts of the garden too, and almost nothing will be "finished". 

You'll see tags for #seed, #tree, and #evergreen - this is shorthand for how much I've developed an idea. A #seed is little more than a fleeting thought, a #tree is something I've spent a some time cultivating, and an #evergreen has been developed into something substantial that likely won't change much (but is still tended to). 

Occasionally you'll see #reference or #published - these are things I no longer think about, but I'm sharing them because they may help or interest others. You'll also see text inside double square brackets (eg. `[[Some crap I wrote]]`) - these are private posts that haven't been shared but might be in the future. 

Like any good garden, there are a thousand ways around and through it. But to help you get started, the broad topics are;

- ***[Writing](/posts/writing/)*** ✍️ - [Mini Essays](/posts/mini-essays/), [Books](/posts/books/) I'm writing, published [Articles](/posts/articles/), and all sorts of random ideas and notes.

- ***[Diving](/posts/diving/)*** 🤿 - [Cave Regions](/posts/cave-regions/) [Diving - Gear](/posts/diving-gear/), [Diving - Ideas](/posts/diving-ideas/), [Photogrammetry](/posts/photogrammetry/), [Caving](/posts/caving/), and anything [Exploration](/posts/exploration/) related

- ***[Cyber Security](/posts/cyber-security/)*** 💻 - Study notes for [OSCP](/posts/oscp/) and [CISSP](/posts/cissp/), [Cybersecurity Resources](/posts/cybersecurity-resources/) like [Cheat Sheet - Windows](/posts/cheat-sheet-windows/) & [Cheat Sheet - Linux](/posts/cheat-sheet-linux/), as well as an evolving guide to [Threat Modelling](/posts/threat-modelling/) & [Threat Actors](/posts/threat-actors/)

- ***[Physics](/posts/physics/)*** 🚀 - Cosmology, Quantum Computing, Information Theory and figuring out how to talk to ET

- ***Personal*** - Things like my [Bucket List](/posts/bucket-list/), Personal, and everything I've read/watched/listened to in 2025 - Media
