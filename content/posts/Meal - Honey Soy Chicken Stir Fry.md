# Ingredients
- Chicken breast - 400g
- Mixed vegetables - 2 cups
- Soy sauce - 2 tbsp
- Honey - 1 tbsp
- Garlic - 2 cloves
- Rice - 2 cups cooked

# Directions
1. Cook chicken until browned
2. Add vegetables and garlic
3. Stir in soy sauce and honey
4. Serve over rice
