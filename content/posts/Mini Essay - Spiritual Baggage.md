For awhile now I've been asking myself what kind of spiritual baggage I'm carrying and how I can shed it. I also want to separate the spiritual from the emotional, because I think my lack of focus on spiritual wellbeing is often what leads me to being surrounded by people and things that do real emotional damage too. Letting go of the frameworks and gestalt that try to bind me to the every day has been one of my deep priorities, but it's often been overwhelmed by the daily mediocrity that constantly pulls me towards the thoughts and actions of the crowd. I've always known that I don't fit the norm, and I've often revelled in that as I've put my heart and soul into things that I know are important. But when circumstances blow me off course and I lose that strong sense of direction, then I start to drift and start to pickup emotional and spiritual baggage - enough that I have repeatedly wound up in death spirals that threaten to destroy me.

I've been here before, and it's often even more upsetting to recognise that I still haven't learned to avoid these traps. But ultimately all the answers I need are within myself, and in Spiritual I have a document full of concepts that I know build and support my spiritual wellbeing. One of the things that is worth doing is going through that document and distilling much of it into something I know will act as a guide when I feel I've lost my way. Just reading through it helps remind me of what is important, and putting time into my four quadrants (Physical, Mental, Emotional and Spiritual) means making my spiritual wellbeing as important as anything else. Where I've repeatedly fallen down is not allocating as much time or energy into it as I have into everything else, and having that cascade outwards. Spirit guides everything else you do, and not developing that sense of spirit and self is even worse than not exercising, not learning, and not being around supportive & positive people. 

There is no doubt that I need to go through my spiritual baggage and decide what I still want to carry. Unlike the other areas of my life, there is less focus on cleansing *everything* out. But there is an opportunity to go through all you have and clean it up - to remind yourself what is really important to you and acts as your stabilising ballast, because it's that stuff that will allow you to weather the storm when the other areas are falling apart. 

Reduce, refine, and refactor everything you have, and know that the spiritual baggage you carry with you is essential armour when shit goes wrong - it's the thing that will keep you working towards what you know is important when you suffer losses, and will keep steering you towards your goals when bright and shiny things might lure you away. Know who you are and want to be, and use that innate sense of spirit and adventure to light your path in the darkness.
