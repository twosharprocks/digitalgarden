# Note-taking

Understanding and remembering more
Combining ideas for more creative ideas

Two steps
- Literature notes - Things that stood out or interested you (in your own words)
	- Punchy quotes, how you felt, 
	- **Write in your own words**
		- Forces you to think - properly process your thoughts
		- Check yourself - Do you really get it? Do you know what you feel about it? Can you explain it in your own words? Do you get the main idea?
		- Remember more of what you read - Learning is helped by friction (req. effort so it's an active process)
		  
- Atomic Notes - Based on Literature Notes but independent (without source context)
	- Allow idea mingling by disconnecting them from the context they were read in.
