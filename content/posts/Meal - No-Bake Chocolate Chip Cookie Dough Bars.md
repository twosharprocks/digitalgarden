# Ingredients

## Cookie Dough Base
- Butter - 1/2 cup (softened)
- Brown sugar - 3/4 cup
- Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
- Milk - 1/3 cup
- Flour (heat-treated or oat flour) - 2 cups
- Salt - pinch
- Chocolate chips - 1 cup

## Chocolate Topping
- Chocolate chips - 1/2 cup
- Butter or coconut oil - 1 tbsp

# Directions

1. In a bowl, mix softened butter and brown sugar until smooth
2. Add vanilla and milk, mixing well
3. Stir in flour and salt until a dough forms
4. Fold in chocolate chips
5. Press mixture evenly into a lined baking dish
6. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth
7. Pour chocolate over the top and spread evenly
8. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until firm
9. Slice into bars and serve
