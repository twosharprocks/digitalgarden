As mentioned in the video, the Mighty Ginge will be returning in a blaze of fury next year. Keep an ear out for the Mighty Ginge Radio Xmas Eve/Farewell special on  December 24th at 5pm GMT – check the [Facebook event](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212208/https://www.facebook.com/events/214535975293112/ "MG Xmas Eve Special") for overseas broadcast times and to listen online.

And since I’m no longer working that 9 to 5, Monday to Friday work week bullshit so many suffer; no more “Oh no, I can’t offend my sensitive arty-type workmate”; no more “Oh, I earn minimum wage doing a job for a man worth 300 million pounds I can’t talk about because of a flimsy confidentiality agreement” – I am now **gainfully unemployed**, and intend to cause as much mischief as my limited savings will allow. Also, I’ll be starting a job in February earning more money than [Darth Hirst](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212208/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Hirst "Seriously, fuck this guy") ever paid me, and where 99% of my time will be spent sitting around twiddling my thumbs – and while an idle Ginge is a dangerous Ginge, I usually get a lot more writing done too.
