# Additional Notes

Making up one of the upper levels of expansive [Wollondilly Cave]({{< relref "posts/Wollondilly Cave.md" >}}), Mulwaree Cave, accessible only during a guided tour, is one of Wombeyan’s most spectacular caves. The colour variations seen on the walls, the shining wax-like decorations, and the stalactites along the pathway are incredibly striking.

The cave itself is 200m long, and there are quite a number of steps to negotiate during the one-hour guided tour, so please be well-prepared for your visit.
