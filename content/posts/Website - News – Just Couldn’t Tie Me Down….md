Right, the final countdown has now officially started – I’m just 3 weeks out from starting my epic “US Relations Tour”, and shit is starting to get crazy again. Last weekend Keith the Anger Management Koala and I were down in Greenwich, where Keithy was a runner-up in the [“Greenwich’s Got No Talent” talent competition](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608095245/http://www.newsshopper.co.uk/leisure/latest/9626881.Reporter_turns_mermaid_competes_with_freaks_in_Greenwich_s_Got_No_Talent/?ref=fbrec). He was pretty pissed at not winning outright – especially after he mounted the judges table and serenaded “Naomi Shambles” with a hip-thrusting rendition of “Reverse Love Song/I’m a Psycho”. But in fairness, the guy who **did** win did so by snorting a line of chilli powder and throwing up on stage. So fair dues.

[![](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608095245im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/no-talent-judges-300x201.jpg "Greenwich no talent judges")](https://web.archive.org/web/20120608095245/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/no-talent-judges.jpg)

The up-shot was that Keith provided Lady Lala (centre) with a motorboat she'll never forget

So I’m now officially on my way: round-the-world tickets are booked, train schedules are organised, and ukulele’s are being re-strung. But before I head off to take over the world, we’re starting in sleepy little Stroud…

And to break things up we’ll have a special guest appearance of Gerald the Baboon to tell listeners about Revelations, I’ll be discussing the dangers of ants, and Martin will sit quietly in the corner in abject terror
