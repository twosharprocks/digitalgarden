# Tham Dan Makhia

Cave near Ban Phondou in Khammouane Province, Laos.

## Research notes

An expedition report presented at EuroSpeleo Forum 2019 gives a surveyed length of 2,093 m at that time. The cave includes an underground lake; later exploration may have changed the survey.
