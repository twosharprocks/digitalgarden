# Cenote Cuba-Checoslovaquia

Flooded karst site in the Ciénaga de Zapata area, Matanzas, Cuba; also called Casimba Cuba-Checoslovaquia.

## Research notes

The Cuban geosite declaration lists it as geosite 183 in the Sendero de las Rocas area. A Cuban diving account associates the name with Czech and Cuban cave-diving expeditions. The saved social posts still need direct review.
