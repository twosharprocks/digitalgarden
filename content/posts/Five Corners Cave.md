# About

Page 72
This cave has two entrances; a roof window and an artificial shaft (for guano mining purposes). It is one of the most extensive caves in the Mount Gambier region, being reportedly 6000 metres in length and reaching the water table at a depth of 25 metres below ground level in at least one shallow pool. The cave is a major bat roosting site, but it has reportedly been heavily polluted with dairy drainage over the years. Preliminary surveying revealed some 970 metres of confirmed passage up to 1976.
