# Karavomilos Lake Cave

Submerged karst cave entered from Karavomilos Lake on Kefalonia, Greece.

## Research notes

The Kefalonia–Ithaca Geopark describes a brackish karst spring and a surveyed siphon of about 260 m, with a maximum depth of 15 m. The spring belongs to the wider karst system connected to the Argostoli sinkholes.
