For those of you listening to last Wednesday’s Mighty Ginge Radio on StroudFM –  somewhere between my inane babble and Martin’s abortion-inducing drone – you may have heard that we’re running a competition! I currently have about 20 “Apocalypse Meow” posters left over from my shows at this year’s Edinburgh Fringe Festival, and I want the damn things out of my room – there is only so long you can stare at 20 copies of your own face screaming and hanging onto the top of a playground train before you feel the overwhelming urge to throw your neighbour’s cat into a wood chipper.

So in order to avoid potential felinicide, I’m giving away signed “Apocalypse Meow” posters! I’ll happily mail the bloody things anywhere in the world – I sent 4 of them to some of the worst hell holes around the world this morning – Afghanistan, Baton Rouge, Perth and Exeter – so geographical isolation is no excuse for not taking part.

To win yourself a signed poster, you need to do the following;

1. Snap a photo of yourself with a lemur or llama
2. Go to the [Mighty Ginge Facebook Fan](https://web.archive.org/web/20120323152924/http://www.facebook.com/pages/Josh-Richards-The-Mighty-Ginge/97584028460 "Mighty Ginge Facebook Fan Page") page
3. Become a Fan of the Mighty Ginge
4. Upload your photo onto the page’s wall
5. **Await your judgement**

Since I expect all of you to bend/break/blatantly disregard these simple instructions, posters will sent out based on the following preferences;

- Real photos of people with a living lemur/llama (preferably doing something obscene at the same time)
- Photos with the skeleton/roadkill of a lemur/llama
- Hysterical photoshops of people with a lemur/llama

If your photo with a lemur/llama is deemed suitably awesome, I’ll send you a message through Facebook asking for your mailing address so I can send you a signed Apocalypse Meow poster. If your photo makes me laugh my tits off, I’ll add a personal note on the back. If you manage to get a photo of yourself **AND** a lemur **riding** a llama, I’ll send you all my posters, a pair of soiled underwear and a marriage proposal. Get your photos in folks – the competition ends when I run out.
