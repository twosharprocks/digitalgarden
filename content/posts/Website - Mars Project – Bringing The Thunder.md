[![thunder Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/thunder.jpg "Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/thunder.jpg)So after [the bombshell in May about Kerbals in Schools, Aspiring Martian Meetings and appearing at “Profs & Pints”](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/2014/05/20/news-kerbals-in-school-profs-pints-and-aspiring-martians/), I kind of dropped off the radar a little there. Although if you look back through this website however, you’ll see a long line of apologies for being absent when crazy things start happening…

## Out of the frying pan…

Things with Mars One are now getting a little out of hand though, so writing down the absolute chaos that is about to drop in the next few months is as much for my benefit as it is for yours. First and foremost news to share though is **I’m bringing a massive new science-comedy show called “Becoming Martian” to National Science Week!** **[![becomingmartian logo wide crop Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/becomingmartian_logo-wide-crop.jpg "Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/becomingmartian_logo-wide-crop.jpg)** Some of you may have heard rumblings about this on [Facebook](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/https://www.facebook.com/joshrichardsthemightyginge) or [Twitter](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/https://twitter.com/Mighty_Ginge), but it’s official – I’ve booked the State Library Theatre for three nights on August 21, 22 and 23! The show is based on my upcoming book “Becoming Martian”, and like last year ALL the profits from National Science Week are being donated to further Mars One’s mission to colonise the red planet in 2025.

I’m incredibly excited to be taking part in National Science Week again, and being able to put together all the research and work I’ve done on Mars One together in one neat, 60 minute science-comedy package. I’m also offering some [frankly amazing perks in the Indiegogo campaign](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/https://www.indiegogo.com/projects/becoming-martian-at-national-science-week "Indiegogo - Becoming Martian") to fund it. Sure, there’s the usual **early-bird tickets**, signed **posters** and t-shirts going… but you can also pick up secure a **VIP package** with the **best seat** at the performance (not the front row, I assure you), **play Kerbal Space Program on the Cultural Centre’s 8m Super Screen** before the show, order a **DVD of the final performance**, bring your **entire school class to see the show**, or have me **visit your school instead**![![indie jpg 300x62 Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/indie-jpg-300x62.jpg "Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/https://www.indiegogo.com/projects/becoming-martian-at-national-science-week/x/3766469)

[Go to the “Becoming Martian” Indigogo campaign](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/https://www.indiegogo.com/projects/becoming-martian-at-national-science-week/x/3766469)

Oh yeah, and I almost forgot – the top perk is a chance to fly to Canberra with me after the final performance on August 23rd and to meet this guy: [![hadfield1 Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/hadfield1.jpg "Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/hadfield1.jpg)Yeah, that’s right – the top perk is a $1200 all-inclusive package to see “Becoming Martian” on August 23rd, signed posters & t-shirts, then flights and accommodation to **join me in Canberra on August 24th to meet former ISS commander & Youtube sensation Colonel Chris Hadfield**!

## … and into the fire

Oh, I’m sorry – did you think this was like last year where nothing exciting happened after National Science Week until November? **WRONG.** Not only is National Science Week packed with other events besides Becoming Martian, I’ve got a school visit on September 28th to talk to Year 6′s about Mars and space exploration then I’m flying out to Germany **the next day**. I’ve been invited to take part in a multitude of space & science communication events across Europe throughout September **before** I come back to help organise [SpaceUp & the Aspiring Martian Meeting in Adelaide](www.spaceupaustralia.org). So here’s the short-story on Europe;

- [Three days at the European Space Agency’s Astronaut Training Centre](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.esa.int/Our_Activities/Human_Spaceflight/Astronauts/The_European_Astronaut_Centre) in**Cologne (Germany)**, meeting ESA astronauts & seeing how they’re prepared for life on the International Space Station.
- Causing abject terror at the [Redhead Day Festival](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.roodharigen.nl/ "Redhead Days Festival") in **Breda (Netherlands)** again, since I missed out on flocking with my melanin-challenged kin at the festival last year.

[![redhead 300x244 Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/redhead-300x244.jpg "Mars Project   Bringing The Thunder")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/redhead.jpg)

- Visiting [ESTEC, the European Space Agency’s top technology centre](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.esa.int/About_Us/ESTEC) just outside **Amsterdam** and meeting more of the ESA’s top researchers.
- Attending the premiere in **Dublin** of [“To Space” by Dr. Niamh Shaw](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.tospace2014.com/#!niamh-shaw/cfe5), who I’ve been helping via Skype & email for the last few months as she researched the show. Niamh’s show is going to be amazing, so check out [her FundIt campaign](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://fundit.ie/project/to-space-perchance-to-dream) to help make it happen. She’s also organising other events to make the most of me being in Ireland too, so stay tuned!
- Hosting events and visiting primary schools throughout **Northern Ireland**as part of the [2014 CultureTECH Festival](culturetech.co) in Derry.

I’m also spending a week visiting friends & old work colleagues throughout Southern England (Exeter, Bristol, Stroud, Brighton & London) before I’m flying back to Oz for SpaceUp too… so be warned if I suddenly knock on your door!

## … then under the grill

Post SpaceUp, the fun continues! I’ve been invited to present a paper at the [Australian Space Research Conference](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.nssa.com.au/asrc/) (September 29 – October 2nd), then fly to the [New Young Writer’s Festival in Newcastle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.youngwritersfestival.org/) to host discussion panels and talk about the upcoming book version of “Becoming Martian” from October 2-5th. October 5th is ALSO the start of [World Space Week](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.worldspaceweek.org/), and I’ll already be in New South Wales…  so something tells me I’ll be doing my fair share of events in Sydney for World Space Week too!

But for now, the big focus is [National Science Week](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/). Get over to the**[Becoming Martian Indiegogo campaign](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920062927/https://www.indiegogo.com/projects/becoming-martian-at-national-science-week/x/3766469)** – secure an early-bird ticket, get a t-shirt, and come meet Chris Hadfield with me. Lots of work to do in the next month, but everything is falling into place perfectly. We’ll just take things slowly – one awesome day at a time.
