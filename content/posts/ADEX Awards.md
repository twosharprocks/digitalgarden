**Nomination Period** June 1 – October 1, 2025
Open to anyone working in the dive industry or dive-related industries to submit nominations across all categories

**Shortlisting Period** October 2 – October 9, 2025
Top 10 nominees per sub-category shortlisted by a select committee

**Public Voting Period** October 10, 2025 – February 10, 2026
Open to the public to vote for the Top 3 finalists in each category

**Awarding Ceremony** Saturday, April 11th 2026
Winners will be honoured at the ADEX 360 BLU Awards Ceremony on Saturday, April 11th 2026, celebrating outstanding contributions across the dive industry and community.
