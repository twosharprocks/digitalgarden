# Krem Chympe

Limestone river cave in the Jaintia Hills of Meghalaya, India. The saved Instagram post was identified by the vault owner as Krem Chympe.

## Research notes

Peer-reviewed cavefish research documents *Neolissochilus pnar* specimens from Krem Chympe and the nearby Krem Um Ladaw cave. This establishes the cave's biological significance. Published passage-length claims vary, so no length is treated as a verified current survey here.
