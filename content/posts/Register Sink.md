# References

Register Sink is in the center of a cornfield and approximately 50-75 feet in diameter. The entrance to the cave system is on the north side and feeds into a large room, through a small area into a spring-siphon. Explored several hundred feet downstream and about 800-1000 feet upstream. Operating depth is about 60 feet.
