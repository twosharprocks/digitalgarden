# References

Ward's sink is private property has been a popular diving site in the Central Florida for many years.   Previously it was used for technical training but now diving is prohibited unless diving with a family  member of the owner's.    The sink is a classic oval-shaped sink with and hourglass profile.  The debris mound is around 100' and  the edges of the sink hole can reach 200'.  The system has some unique features such as sunken boats, a  car and some intimidatingly large fish!
