# About
Area of Interest: Vayots Dzor, Syunik, Tavush
**Moderate to High Cave Potential**

- Large parts of Armenia’s landscape are **limestone and dolomite**, particularly in the **Vayots Dzor**, **Syunik**, and **Tavush** provinces.
- Features rugged terrain with **karst valleys**, **dolines**, and **numerous caves**, many of which are still actively being mapped by local and European caving groups.
### **Known Caves**
- **Mozrov Cave**, **Magili Cave**, and **Arjeri Cave** are significant dry caves.
- Armenia is known for deep fissure systems and vertical caves — some of which **contain seasonal sumps or intermittent water pools**.
### **Cave Diving Status**
- No published records of cave diving expeditions.
- Numerous karst springs, resurgences, and hydrothermal systems may offer dive potential, particularly at **mid-altitudes** where the water table is accessible.

### **Exploration Potential**
- High chance of discovering **new phreatic or resurgence caves**.
- Requires satellite scouting and geological mapping to identify high-potential karst spring zones.
- Local geology and hydrology suggest **first-descent opportunities**, especially during spring melt (April–June).
