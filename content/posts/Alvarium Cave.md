# About

Two meter drop into cave which is little awkward but not bad from people smaller than me. This leads to a belly crawl for about 5-7m that opens into a wide low chamber about 4m in diameter and 1.5m high.  The entrance pool is large and water clear. Line leads in from ceiling a couple of metres into the pool.

The nearest you can get with a car is about 700m or so from the entrance and you will need to carry in your cylinders and equipment. Ideally just take 7L tanks and be prepared to drag them through the dirt so a bag and rope are recommended.

Entry is simple and the line is obvious. You will drop through some pretty dirty areas with a lot of roots but after pushing through you will enter in a large cave opening up in front of you,
