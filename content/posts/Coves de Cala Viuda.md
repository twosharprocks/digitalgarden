# Additional Notes

"The complex consists of three single-branch caves and several tunnels of different sizes and is part of a broader cave complex in the area that is popular among recreational divers"
- **Espeleoworld:** https://espeleoworld.com/c/cova-de-cala-viuda

The cave opens at a depth of -6 meters. The opening is 10 meters wide and leads to a corridor in the W direction, (270º). After 25 meters of travel, this tunnel opens into an air chamber that extends in two branches of about 10 meters in length.
