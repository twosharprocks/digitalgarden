**Trialling Kerbal Space Program in** **Schools**  
[![ksp serious biz 1024x576 News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/ksp-serious-biz-1024x576.jpg "News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/ksp-serious-biz.jpg)  
For the last few weeks I’ve been setting up a 5-week pilot program to teach rocketry, physics & space history to Year 6-12′s using Kerbal Space Program (KSP). If you haven’t seen KSP before, essentially you design and launch your own space program, with green-skinned “Kerbals” as your fearless space explorers. The program has a full sandbox mode, allowing you to use 100′s of different parts to build your own rockets, satellites, interplanetary landers and space stations. It all sounds pretty cool & inspirational, and it is…

[Watch archived video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RkDOOsGg-9I)The folks at Squad are doing incredible things with KSP, and one of the most exciting developments is their new [KerbalEdu](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.kerbaledu.com/) mod being developed for use in schools. KerbalEdu *includes* the full retail version of Kerbal Space Program, as well as early access to the teaching aids provided by the mod too – so schools can purchase this incredible teaching tool for a radically reduced price (over 50% off retail) AND get access to the learning tools as KerbalEdu develops too.

It also means that you’ll get to laugh at your students when their precious rockets explode. And they **will** explode. **REGULARLY**. But all in the name of science.

[![science News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/science.gif "News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/science.gif)If you’re a Year 6-12 science teacher interested in having an astronaut candidate teach your kids about space exploration with a game they will love, [**get in touch**](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/contact/) – I’m offering to run pilot programs in two schools**at no cost beyond the *heavily discounted* software.** You purchase KerbalEdu (~$13 per licence) and I’ll volunteer 90 minutes a week for 5 weeks to work with you to make the magic happen –  showing your students how rockets & space stations are designed, what it takes to get into orbit, and what it takes to land on another planet, all while explaining how it links to real-world space science & the history of the space-age.

Check out the [KerbalEdu trial program flyer more information](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Josh-Richards-KerbalEdu-Schools-Flyer-Website.pdf) or get in touch [via the contact page](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/contact/) and we’ll start launching some Kerbals from *your* school!

**June 17 – Scitech’s “Profs & Pints”**[![profs Pints News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/profs-Pints.jpg "News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/profs-Pints.jpg)  
Scitech is back running “Profs & Pints” again for a 5th year at the Flying Scotsman, and they’ve invited yours truly to help stir the pot on the ethics of sending humans to Mars!

> “**GROUND CONTROL TO MAJOR TOM’: *Should we put a human on Mars?***
>
> *Have you ever wondered why so many countries, organisations and people want to go to Mars? Have you ever wondered if we should? Is it about curiosity? Is it about knowledge? Is it about ‘the BIG questions’? Is it ‘cause we can’?*

I have **NO** **IDEA** who they’re going to put me up there with/against, but I can assure you it’ll be a lively discussion. If you haven’t been to a Profs & Pints before, it’s a very similar structure to [ABC’s Q&A](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q%26A_(Australian_talk_show)), but run in a pub with scientists on the panel – guests providing a brief introduction on themselves and their field of work/research, and then answer questions from the audience and the live Twitter feed. Previous Profs & Pints have tackled things like genetic engineering & scientific censorship, and featured Nobel Prize winners.

But this won’t be the usual “Hi everyone, I’m doing science stuff on a stage for 20 minutes, look at **MEEEEEEEE!****“**. That’s right – we’re setting up a computer to play Kerbal Space Program on the Super Screen in the Perth Cultural Centre![![ldsscreen News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/ldsscreen.jpg "News   Kerbals in School, Profs & Pints, and Aspiring Martians")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/ldsscreen.jpg)

I’ll also be running a stall with KSP in the cultural centre, promoting Mars One and the **mountain** of other activities I’ve got planned – stay tuned for more updates, but this will be [100 billion hot dogs of AWESOME on the Eddie Izzard scale](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://youtu.be/0rYT0YvQ3hs).

**September 28th - Aspiring Martian Meetings in Australia & Canada**

I told you there was a lot of epic news to share… Here’s FINAL piece of incredible news I’ve been hanging onto until everything was organised.

As I mentioned in the previous post, I’ve joined the organising committee for Australia’s first SpaceUp, being hosted in Adelaide on September 28th. What I **didn’t** tell you is that I’m stepping in for two reasons: 1) Help run their social media and web content (so keep an eye out for more content both here & at [SpaceUp Australia](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.spaceupaustralia.org/)), and 2) Organise an “Aspiring Martian Meetup” for all the Mars One candidates in Australia to meet for the first time in Adelaide for the conference!

At this stage we’re expecting 15 Mars One candidates at the SpaceUp, and are currently negotiating with Mars One to have [Bas Lansdorp](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.mars-one.com/about-mars-one/team/bas-lansdorp) or [Dr. Norbert Kraft](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.mars-one.com/about-mars-one/team/norbert-kraft) videoconference in, ***or present in person,***depending on the schedule of the global candidate selection process!

I’m also coordinating with several Canadians to organise a simultaneous Aspiring Martian Meetup in Toronto at the same time – the world’s biggest space conference (the 2014 International Aeronautical Conference) starts on September 29th, so we’re hoping the team in Canada can get a mountain of Mars One candidates & space speakers and have them videoconference our SpaceUp in Adelaide at the same time.

So if you’re based in Canada (*especially* if you’re in Toronto) and want to help organising the Toronto Aspiring Martians Meetup… [get in touch](https://web.archive.org/web/20140903044531/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/contact/)!
