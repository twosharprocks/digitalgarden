# About

RESTRICTED ACCESS - PERMIT REQUIRED
Occupied by endangered Southern Bentwing Bat. 
If disturbed they consume part of their fat reserve and can die as a result. Entry into this cave between May and August inclusive will place this species at risk!

During spring and summer months the bats leave the cave for the maternity cave in the Naracoorte Caves Reserve to have and raise their young.
