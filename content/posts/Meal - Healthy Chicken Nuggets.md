# Ingredients
- Chicken breast - 500g
- Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
- Greek yogurt - 150g
- Egg - 1
- Seasoning
	- Salt
	- Pepper
	- Paprika
	- Garlic powder

# Directions
1. Cut chicken into nugget-sized pieces
2. Coat in yogurt and egg mixture
3. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
4. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
