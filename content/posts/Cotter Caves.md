# Additional Notes

There seems to be confusion here. There is the caves side and on the other side of the small valley is the mine side.
When standing at the road adjacent to the river look up the small valley. The walkway and steps to the left go to the cave which is fenced off. Above this are two more smaller caves entrances.
On the right hand side of the valley is a small track taking you through the old mullock dump and along to where the old mine is. Railway lines can still be seen going into the collapsed entrance to the adit. In the 90s this went back about 20m or so but was flooded. The mine is at the top of the valley and nowhere near the caves.
