# About

2021-Aug: MW visited on foot & photographed. Fissure type cave with large wattle tree growing over and roots running into crack. Rock dropped into fissure indicates it runs runs 5-10m down with dirt (not water) at the bottom. Well worth further investigation using wire ladder and dry caving equipment.

2023-Jan: MW, NB and SS visited on foot with ladder. Small and sculpted solution tube 6-7m deep with small brown snake at the bottom. No opportunity for development.
