[![nphicorn News   Edinburgh v2.0](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/nphicorn.png "nphicorn")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/08/nphicorn.png)Stop the presses – I’m back in the UK! Sitting in a little cafe next to the Natural History Museum at the moment – drinking massive cappuccinos, eating delicious cake, and tripping balls from having had 4 hours sleep in the last 3 days. I won’t be here long though, as I need to type this up, slip next door and claim myself a new drinking partner ([Larry 2.0](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428/http://www.themightyginge.com/2009/08/larry-the-lemur/ "I still miss him...")), then jump on a plane up to Edinburgh!

I’m back here for the [Edinburgh Fringe Festival](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428/http://www.edfringe.com/ "Ed Fringe") all over [again](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428/http://www.themightyginge.com/2009/08/news-scheduling-rocks/) - a month of comedic and artistic insanity in glorious Scotland! I’ll be doing some of the light and sound tech for the very talented [John Robertson](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428/http://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=135543926458689&index=1#!/johnrobertsoncomedy?ref=ts "John Robertson - Facebook") while he puts on 44 shows in 22 days of his amazing [“A Nifty History of Evil”](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428/http://www.edfringe.com/whats-on/comedy/john-robertson-nifty-history-of-evil-1 "Ed Fringe - A Nifty History of Evil") one-man show, as well as a mountain of open-mic spots and workshops, and obviously going to plenty of shows. I’ll be updating most days during the festival, although I am spending a week in the Lakes district climbing mountains with a gorgeous and fiesty/geeky little English girl (who henceforth will be referred to as [“Wicket”](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921212428/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Star_Wars_characters#W "Don't ask...")) over the week of my birthday (August 14th-20th).

So no real-time Ginge love for you then – you’ll just have to put up with my usual bullshit in a diluted and scheduled form. But for now, sit back and prepare for a month of me telling you how awesome I am, and how much you’re missing out on at the Festival.

Game on, my hairless bipedal friends!
