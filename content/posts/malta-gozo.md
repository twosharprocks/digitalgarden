# About
Best Season
- May: ~20°C	Calm, warming	✔ Good start
- ***June–September***: 23–27°C Warm, best visibility ✔✔ ***Best season***
- October: ~24°C	Calmer shoulder month	✔ Still excellent

Avoid
- April & November: ~20°C	Less predictable	⚠ Some limits
- Dec – March: 15–16°C	Rough, colder	✖ Less suitable
- *Many dive centers reduce operations or close entirely for winter.*
