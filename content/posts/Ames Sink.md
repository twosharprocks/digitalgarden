# Additional Notes

(Eric Flagg) Timelapse test for the Swallet-Whirlpool at Ames Sink, major sink that flows to the [Wakulla Springs]({{< relref "posts/Wakulla Springs.md" >}}) area and also input of massive amounts of treated and untreated non-point source runoff from Tallahassee. You can't see this 'whirlpool' easily without a timelapse, this was about 15-20 minutes. This footage is part of a large project, "Following the Wakulla" which will be similar to my "Following the Ichetucknee" video, tracing the entire watershed of a river, this time the Wakulla River.
