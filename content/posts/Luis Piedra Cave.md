# Luis Piedra Cave

Flooded cave near Quivicán, Cuba.

## Research notes

A Cuban cave diving federation report lists Luis Piedra among explored flooded caves near Quivicán, with approximately 700 m of installed line at the time of that report. A biological study records cavefish specimens from Luis Piedra collected in 1993. These are historical observations, not current survey figures.
