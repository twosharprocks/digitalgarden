# Ingredients
- Fries - 400g
- Chicken breast - 300g
- Cheese - 1 cup
- Sauce - 2 tbsp
- Seasoning
	- Salt
	- Pepper

# Directions
1. Cook fries until crispy
2. Cook chicken and slice
3. Layer fries, chicken and cheese
4. Melt cheese and drizzle sauce
