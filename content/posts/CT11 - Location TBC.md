# About

***Do not confuse with CT11 "La Catedral" which is between La Azohia and Cabo Tinoso***

6 known caves in the area: 
- the cave of the lake (or of the Arco), 
- La Virgen (or of Neptuno), 
- Cuevas Gemelas (with two differentiated cavities CT11 and CT12)
- Estalagmitas cave (CT17)
- Ovaza complex
