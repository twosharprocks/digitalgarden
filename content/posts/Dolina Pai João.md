# Dolina Pai João

Flooded doline and cave system in the Nobres area of Mato Grosso, Brazil. The saved ‘Brazil Exploration’ post was identified by the vault owner as this site.

## Research notes

Global Underwater Explorers' 2024 project brief reports approximately 700 m of passage explored and surveyed at that time, with continuing exploration. It places the site in Nobres and describes its cave-diving research. Survey figures should be revisited as exploration continues. No precise entrance position is recorded here.
