# About
Gobi Desert and Khangai Mountains Karst
- **Why:** Isolated karst basins in the south and west, minimal cave or diving exploration.
- **Status:** Very limited speleological work; potential aquifer-fed systems.
- **Potential:** Reconnaissance of high-latitude karst features and hydrological sinks.
- **Best Season:** June to August.
