# Ilhéus das Cabras

Marine dive area off Terceira in the Azores, Portugal. The saved lead names a canyon; it does not establish a separate named cave.

## Research notes

The Azores tourism authority identifies Gruta do Ilhéu das Cabras, also called Gruta dos Ratões, on the islets. Its official dive description also describes rocky gorges and a reef. Whether the saved photographer's ‘canyon’ is the same passage remains to be checked.
