# From CaveDB
Kilsby Sinkhole is a naturally occurring karst sinkhole located on private property 10 kilometres south-west of Mount Gambier in South Australia. The sinkhole is renowned worldwide for its crystal clear water and extensive open caverns, making it a popular destination for recreational diving. The site is accessible only via prior bookings, which can be made online or by phone.

Kilsby Sinkhole is the only site of its kind in Australia that welcomes snorkellers and Open Water certified divers, through to those with Cave or Technical dive training. Snorkellers, Freedivers and Open Water divers must be guided by an approved Dive Instructor. With a depth of almost 30 metres directly from the pontoon, the site is a perfect location for freediving and training camps are regularly held onsite.

The sinkhole has an interesting history due to its use as a top-secret space for weapons research in the 1970s, resulting in a range of infrastructure being developed at the site, some of which is still present. An onsite museum space is currently in development which tells the story of this unique site.
