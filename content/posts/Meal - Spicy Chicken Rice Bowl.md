# Ingredients
- Chicken breast - 500g
- Rice - 2 cups cooked
- Hot sauce - 2 tbsp
- Vegetables - 1 cup
- Seasoning
	- Salt
	- Pepper
	- Paprika

# Directions
1. Cook chicken with seasoning and hot sauce
2. Prepare rice and vegetables
3. Combine into bowls
4. Serve hot
