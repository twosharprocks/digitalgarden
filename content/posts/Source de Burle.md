# Source de Burle

Karst spring at Sainte-Énimie in the Gorges du Tarn, Lozère, France. It is a spring or resurgence, not an independently named cave.

## Research notes

The French geological survey describes a karst spring fed by the Causse de Sauveterre aquifer; Lozère tourism describes it as a vauclusian resurgence. A speleological bibliography documents dives into the source.
