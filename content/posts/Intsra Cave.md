# Intsra Cave

Karst cave in the Intsra River basin, Samegrelo–Zemo Svaneti, Georgia; also written Intsira Cave.

## Research notes

The regional tourism organisation describes a cave with helictites, stalactites and stalagmites in the Intsra River basin, associated with the nearby waterfall.
