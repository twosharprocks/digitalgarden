# Additional Notes

Why not consider the stunning Cabaret Cave for your next function or event. Originally used in the 1930’s to provide a secret dinner and dance location for Perth’s rich and famous.

Cabaret Cave is Perth’s only venue cave for hire. It has been beautifully modified to accommodate up to 120 guests sit-down style, or 200 guests cocktail style. The cave’s natural air conditioning makes it the perfect location all year around.

Extraordinarily unique, visually amazing and acoustically sensational, Cabaret Cave is a perfect location for your upcoming event.
