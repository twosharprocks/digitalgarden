# Packing List
## Clothing/Toiletries
- 1x quick dry shorts
- 2x canvas shorts
- 3x Long sleeve tops
- 2x t-shirts
- 1x long pants
- 1x jacket
- 2x pair of regular socks
- 2x pair of thick socks (diving)
- 1x UV-blocking neck buff
- 1x hat
- 1x sunglasses
- 1x travel towel
- Sunscreen (essential)

***Optional or Avoid***
- eSIM: Optional
	- WiFi at Grand Mine Hotel is good but mobile coverage is excellent, so an eSIM is worthwhile for flexibility.
	- Recommend Simify Data-only eSIM: Data is routed through Singapore, so be aware of Singapore's internet filtering.
- Water bottle: Optional
	- Water from taps is safe to drink
	- Snacks & drinks are always available from 7/11 on the way to/from the dive sites
- Surgical Face Mask: Optional
	- Getting sick from flights/transits is very common, so reduce the risk with a mask.
	- Recommend an [Ausair mask](https://ausair.com/products/airweave-merino-reuseable-mask) with [elastane mask strap](https://ausair.com/products/head-strap) 
- Toothbrush/paste & razors: Not required
	- Sealed complimentary kits are provided in every hotel
- Boots: Avoid
	- Only need shoes for hotel/travel/cave entrances. Shoes are generally removed when entering a building - avoid anything with laces.
	- Drysuit: ***Rock boots required for Terayama Cave*** (involves dry caving to water)  
- USB-mini cables: Avoid unless specifically required
	- "USB to USB-C" & "USB-C to USB-C" cables are much more common & useful
## Dive Gear
Expect to be diving cold water (10-12degC) using A72/A80 sidemount cylinders. 
- Gas
	- Air: Dive sites are generally shallow sumps, nitrox is *not* required. 
	- ***Prioritise pressure over ppO2***: Host/operator will often attempt to transfill DIN-valve cylinders with yoke-valve cylinders filled with 21% @ 210 bar. Do not accept low-pressure nitrox fills - pressure is more important for sump diving than O2 content.
- Hardware 
	- Sliding D's for Harness
	- 2x cylinder bands (140mm diameter), 2x clip & loop
	- 3x hose bands (Cylinders are hauled to/from the water by cavers)
- Batteries 
	- Perdix: Lithium AA
	- Transmitters: CR2
	- No need for AAA
- Helmet - ***Required for Terayama Cave***
	- 2x mounted lights
		- Argo: 2x 21700 (or 2x 18650's with sleeve), 1x Thumbscrew
		- Backup on side with elastic loops
	- Spare mask on back of helmet
- Exposure Protection
	- Drysuit: Trilaminate or Crushed Neoprene
	- Undergarments: Halo AR or BZ200
- Regs: 2x sidemount regs, 1x stage reg
- Spools: 2x jump, 1x safety.
- Cookies: Take 6-12, probably only use 1-2
- Computers: 2x Perdix with cutting tools
- Filming
	- 360: 1x X4, 1x 1Tb MicroSD, 1x float handle, 1x ball mount (threaded base), 2x 60x120mm floats, 2x lights, 1x Triple clamp, 2x ball clamps, 1x dome cover, 1x invisible selfie (surface scan), 1x X4 soft cover (for charging/data transfer)
	- GoPro: 1x Camera, 1x Dive Housing, 1x Battery, 1x MicroSD, 

***Optional or Avoid***
- Optional: 1x stage regulator with yoke adapter
	- Stage cylinders are often yoke valve - ***bring a DIN-yoke adapter***
	- Most dives are shallow with <200m penetration
	- Be aware stages make pushing into flow (eg. at Shiramizu Pond) more challenging
# Other Notes
Food
- Rice is a staple for all meals
- Breakfast is non-existent, and lunch is basic/rare 
	- "Buffet breakfast" is probably bread and a toaster
	- Get a cold, pre-packaged lunch from 7/11 on the way to the dive site
- "Combeeny" 7/11 or Lawson Convenience stores
		- Nigiri as an easy, anytime breakfast/lunch
		- Use the on-premises microwave ovens for hot meals (pizza pockets & pre-packaged)
- Steak & Fugu - Great, but very expensive
- Izakaya - Essentially a Japanese pub. Great meals, drinks, and much cheaper than steak

Drink
- Tea & Instant Coffee
	- Machine coffee is available from reception at Grand Mine, but there are kettles in the rooms so strongly recommend taking sachet coffee bags. *Milk is only available from convenience stores*
- Vending Machines are everywhere
	- Coffee, water, sports drinks, alcohol, etc. are available from vending machines in hotel lobbies, street corners, anywhere.
- Alcohol 
	- Convenience stores: Canned beer/pre-mixed spirits, bottled wine, and straight spirits are all available at any 7/11 or Lawsons
	- Bars/Restaurants/Karaoke
		- "Nama biru" (Named Beer) is tap beer (generally Asahi) in a tall pint glass
		- "High biru" (Highball) is generally Jim Beam & dry ginger ale over ice in a stien glass

Cash
- Suica Card: Pre-loaded Japanese travel card that doubles as a debit card
	- When you first arrive at NRT, get the "28-day Welcome Card" from the ATM-like machines in the NRT T2/3 train station
	- Useful for paying for trains, vending machines, luggage lockers, etc
- ATMs 
	- Cash machines can always found at the Post Office
	- Take a physical card, and be wary of it's overseas withdrawal limit (often 100,000Y)
	- Airport ATMs - Only dispense 10,000Y notes

Logistics
- Site access 
	- All access is organised through the Japanese Speleological Society - membership is a requirement for access. 
	- Self-drive with a hire car is an option but requires international drivers licence. 
	- Nearest dive shop is in Ube
- Travel: Tokyo-Ube
		- NRT to HND: "Airport Limousine Bus" buses depart regularly from NRT T2
		- HND to UBJ: Regular daily flights
		- UBE to Mine: Hire Car
- Hotel
	- 9 Hours Narita Airport: Capsule hotel with locekrs, clean and simple, but ***take earplugs***
	- Mine Grand Hotel: Excellent rooms, great staff, basic toast breakfast.
