# Škocjan Caves

Cave system in Slovenia where the Reka River sinks underground. The saved ‘ponor hole’ refers to a feature of the system, not a separate named cave.

## Research notes

The park authority records the Škocjan Caves as a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1986 and describes sinkholes and the underground canyon within the protected karst landscape.
