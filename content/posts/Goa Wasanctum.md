# Goa Wasanctum

Flooded cave on Muna Island, Southeast Sulawesi, Indonesia. The saved Sulawesi video was identified by the vault owner as Goa Wasanctum.

## Research notes

A CaveForce Expedition video describes large chambers alternating with narrow passages where sediment can quickly reduce visibility. Its caption calls the location ‘South Sulawesi’, but Muna Island is in Southeast Sulawesi Province according to the Indonesian government. No independently verified survey dimensions or precise entrance location have been added.
