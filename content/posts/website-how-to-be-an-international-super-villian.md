**Task**

Achieve global infamy and be hunted by every major government on the planet by becoming an international super villain

[![megamind College of Awesome   How to be an International Super Villain](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/megamind.jpg "megamind")](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/megamind.jpg)

**Background**

Super heroes. They’re always out there proclaiming their holier-than-thou bullshit, being an oppressive force for “good” and living in a world of black and white. They fly around making women swoon and men feel physically inferior. Let’s be honest: super heroes are jerks. And the people who _like_ super heroes are shallow, pointless and intellectually-restricted life forms.

But what if you discovered evidence that finally brought [Batman out of the closet?](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.thetoyzone.com/2008/blog/the-truth-about-batman-and-his-tight-leather-suit/ "Woah, there be mountains and broken backs beyond ye link") What if you had information that showed that [Superman’s own home planet was more segregated than South Africa under Apartheid](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://io9.com/5125026/uncovered-supermans-secret-racist-past "Keeping the darkies on their own island - GENIUS!")? Would you be brave enough to step up and reveal this for the good of people everywhere? Knowledge is power – would you share with humanity information that showed people their heroes were not who they claimed to be? And would you be ready for the reprisals from both their fans and the superheroes themselves?

Being an effective international super villain takes years of preparation, a single-mindedness to your manifesto, and the intellect to be 5 steps ahead of the modern day paladins who’s reputations you’re tarnishing. But if you are up for a life changing/threatening challenge, I encourage you to read on.

**Essential Personal Qualities of An International Super Villain**

**Be Australian –** One of the most under-rated components of any true super villain is being Australian. All of the best movie villains are either [Australians playing Americans](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QehZjjwb7-I "BOOM! And the pencil is gone!"), Australians playing [malfunctioning software](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.movievillains.com/archives/2003/11/agent_smith.html "Hugo Weaving - proof you can work in Hollywood even if you look like a rapist"), or just Australians straight out playing [evil Australians](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PKnR2mBm-wg&feature=related "Ahhh Commando - you influenced my childhood more than you should have..."). We’re all know for being easy-going psychopaths, and an effective super villain should endeavour to either become an Australian citizen or at least develop an Australian accent.

**Study Physics and Computer Science** – _Real_ super villains are almost always socially-inept geeks lashing out at the world. People who relate easily to others aren’t excessively suspicious. Socially-comfortable people don’t throw their entire existence into studying the physical world or complicated operating systems because they find human beings irrational and confusing. **Normal people** don’t try and satiate their inherent need for basic physical contact by violently pressing their genitals against others in a university common room, while screaming “YOU’VE BEEN RAVAGED!”, nor do they attempt to justify this absurd behaviour through the construction of an elaborate point-based game*.

**Develop the super villain “look” and be quirky/mysterious** – Hard to describe yet instantly recognisable to all; having the distinct super villain look is an obvious necessity. While nature may not have blessed you a cleft-lip or a parasitic twin, environment can go a long way toward making you look like a super villain. For instance, you could turn your hair a [creepy super villain white,](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://gawker.com/5611692/what-happened-to-wikileaks-founder-julian-assanges-weird-hair "Ahhh - suddenly the real topic of the post becomes clear...") by having the federal police raid your house and [being convicted of hacking a major overseas telecommunication company](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.thestar.com/news/world/article/899661--10-things-you-don-t-know-about-wikileaks-mystery-man-julian-assange "Wikileaks Julian Assange convicted") the very same year your wife leaves you with your child. Real super villains are also [fugitives](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.theage.com.au/national/keeper-of-secrets-20100521-w230.html "Wikileaks Julian Assange on the run") - running from the omnipotent powers for “good”. They’re always working and developing their plans on the run.

**Have a lair** - This may seem counter-intuitive given the super-villain’s constantly moving existence, but having an evil lair is important to provide a centralised powerbase. This provides a focus point for all of his data that is both physically and electronically secure – say by putting your servers in a [Cold-War era nuclear bunker just outside Stockholm](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://madmikesamerica.com/2010/12/wikileaks-servers-located-in-swedish-nuclear-bunker/ "Wikileaks Julian Assange swedish bunker"). Of course, a real super villain prepares for the possible destruction of his lair by spreading his resources; caching plans and intelligence all over the globe. And ofcourse, the best villains make the [public accomplices in their dastardly acts](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.wired.com/threatlevel/2010/07/wikileaks-insurance-file/ "Wikileaks Julian Assange Insurance File") too…

**Turn everyone against each other** – The best comic book villains don’t just attack the fabric of society -they turn super heroes against each other as well. You could try making up a lie about your super hero nemesis, and tell another super hero. You lie is easily checked, proven to be just that, and you wind up looking stupid. Instead, it’s always [so much](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.theaustralian.com.au/news/world/hillary-clinton-ordered-us-envoys-to-spy-wikileaks-documents/story-e6frg6so-1225962550999 "Ohhh Hillary... Wikileaks Julian Assange") [better to let](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-us-canada-11894759 "Cluster bombs - the gift that keeps giving Wikileaks Julian Assange") the [cold hard truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nTXSexxGRsg "Wikileaks Julian Assange Collateral Murder") tell [the story instead](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-us-canada-11858990 "Wikileaks Julian Assange Cablegate"). Just expect your newly embarrassed enemies to [come after you](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2010/11/29/AR2010112905973.html?sid=ST2010112906806 "Wikileaks Julian Assange Cablegate") with [everything they’ve got](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.wired.com/threatlevel/2010/11/assange-interpol/ "Wikileaks Julian Assange Cablegate - Interpol red notice") (and even [things they don’t have](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.google.com/hostednews/afp/article/ALeqM5hkddgO09cBxd0nzTJmDqKyNkejQA?docId=CNG.a710e1700c3b217beae970d667b79d7b.701 "Wikileaks Julian Assange Cablegate")) while trying to [hide the awful truth](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2010/12/01/AR2010120106809.html "Media Suppression - Wikileaks Julian Assange Cablegate") you’ve exposed…

And there you have it – some of the basic ingredients to becoming an international super villain. Don’t forget, others will quickly follow in [your footsteps then try to discredit you](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.spiegel.de/international/germany/0,1518,732212,00.html "Wikileaks Julian Assange Cablegate"). But who knows – if you work hard and strive toward true Machiavellian brilliance, one day you could have the [wicked witch of the west](https://web.archive.org/web/20141025174550/http://www.networkworld.com/news/2010/120210-sarah-palin-target-wikileaks-assange.html?hpg1=bn "Lincoln, Rabeen, King, Massoud - why do they always managed to kill the good ones...") screaming for your blood too!
