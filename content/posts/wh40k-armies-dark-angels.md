- **Deathwing**: Terminator-heavy, durable, elite army
- **Ravenwing**: Fast, mobile, hit-and-run tactics
- **Greenwing**: Balanced mix of infantry, vehicles, and characters

- Greenwing - 1995 pts (~$1300AU)
	- **Strengths**: Durable **Deathwing**, mobile **Ravenwing**, strong **firepower**.
	- **Weaknesses**: Can struggle against mass horde armies if not played carefully.
	- **Playstyle**: **Aggressive board control**, durable units, and powerful shooting.

	- **HQ (Warlords & Leaders)**
	1. **Azrael** – 145 pts (~$70)
		- Supreme Grand Master of the Dark Angels, provides excellent buffs, an invulnerable save aura, and extra CP.
	2. **Ezekiel (Librarian)** – 105 pts (~$60)
	       - Powerful psychic support with Dark Angels-specific powers.
	3. **Primaris Chaplain on Bike** – 120 pts (~$75)
	       - Provides **Ravenwing** buffs and leads bike squads for mobility.
	    - Can take **Litany of Hate** (reroll hits in melee) or **Canticle of Hate** (charge buffs).
	- **Troops (Battleline)**
	1. **5x Intercessors** – 95 pts (~$95)
	    - Standard Primaris Marines, durable and flexible.
	2. **5x Infiltrators** – 130 pts (~$95)
	    - Anti-deep strike screening, good board control.
	3. **10x Deathwing Terminators** – 435 pts (~$140/box, ~280 total)
	    - Tough as nails with **Storm Shields & Thunder Hammers / Cyclone Missile Launcher**.
	    - Can deep strike into battle.
	- **Elites (Hard-Hitters)**
	1. **5x Bladeguard Veterans** – 175 pts
	    - Durable melee fighters; work well alongside Azrael.
	2. **Redemptor Dreadnought** – 220 pts
	    - **Macro Plasma Incinerator & Onslaught Gatling Cannon** for anti-infantry and armor.
	3. **Deathwing Ancient** – 75 pts
	    - Buffs **Deathwing Terminators** with extra attacks and objective control.
	- **Fast Attack (Ravenwing Strike)**
	1. **3x Ravenwing Black Knights** – 135 pts
		- Plasma Talons & Corvus Hammers for mobile firepower & melee punch.
		- Best used with **Hit & Run tactics**.
	2. **5x Outriders (Primaris Bikes)** – 210 pts
		- Paired with Chaplain for **advance & charge** bonuses.
	- **Heavy Support**
	1. **Repulsor Executioner** – 300 pts
		- **Heavy Laser Destroyer** for anti-tank. Transports **Bladeguard Veterans** into battle.
