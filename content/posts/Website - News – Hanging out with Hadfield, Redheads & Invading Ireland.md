## Becoming Martian European Madness Tour

**September 1 - [The European Astronaut Centre](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.esa.int/Our_Activities/Human_Spaceflight/Astronauts/The_European_Astronaut_Centre)****Cologne, Germany**

Calling in to the EAC facility in Cologne to meet fellow Mars One candidates, see how European Space Agency astronauts prepare for life on the International Space Station, and to observe a centrifuge study into how blood moves through the brain under increased g-loads.

**September 2 - [Solarmax & The Aurora with Andre Kuipers](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://shop.omniversum.nl/solarmax-het-poollicht-film-en-lezing-van-andre-kuipers-p-16256.html)****Hague, The Netherlands**

[![solarmax News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/solarmax.jpg "News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/solarmax.jpg)

The Dutch Space Society, NVR, and Omniverse present “Solarmax & the Aurora”. This evening is dedicated to one of the most fascinating stars, our sun, and the Aurora Borealis. The program includes fascinating lectures from ESA astronaut André Kuipers, and Thijs Versloot, and Remco Timmermans, followed by the film “Solarmax” on the Omniverse’s IMAX screen!

**September 5 - [ISU Alumni & ESTEC Tour](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.esa.int/About_Us/ESTEC)****Noorwijk, The Netherlands**

[![ESTEC highlight std News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/ESTEC_highlight_std.jpg "News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/ESTEC_highlight_std.jpg)Meeting up with fellow alumni from the International Space University for a tour of the European Space Research & Technology Centre (European Space Agency’s peak technology & research facility) and being led by my former ISU course director & the head of ESA’s Communications Office, Juan De Dalmau!

**September 5-7 - [Redhead Days Festival](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.redheaddays.nl/)****Breda, The Netherlands**

[![10357651 670491333030243 6343116907609685329 o 1024x694 News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/10357651_670491333030243_6343116907609685329_o-1024x694.jpg "News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/10357651_670491333030243_6343116907609685329_o.jpg)

Redheads. Thousands of them. All in one city for a weekend. After missing the festival in 2013, I’ll be back in the Netherlands just in time for the Redhead Days Festival. Last time I was at the festival there was drunken ukulele playing, we broke a Guinness world record, some crazy french chick bit me, and I piggy-backed the pub crawl  leader back to her house.

This year’s theme is “Wonderland – I’m expecting things to get out of hand.

**September 9 - [Niamh Shaw’s “To Space” Premiere](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.tospace2014.com/)****Dublin, Ireland**

[![niamh News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/niamh.jpg "News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/niamh.jpg)

Off to Dublin for the premiere of Dr Niamh Shaw’s show “To Space!”. In January 2014 Niamh set herself a goal – to go to space by whatever means possible! Meeting with fellow space enthusiasts, former astronaut, space tourists and making trips to European Space Station, NASA, Niamh recounts her personal experiences and shares what she has learned along the way in this human, and informative show about the dreams we had as children, the future of Space exploration & the long-term implications of colonising new planets on our world view and society as a whole.

**September 15-18 – [Becoming Martian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://2014.culturetech.co/event/2014-09-16/becoming-martian) @ [CultureTECH Technology Festival](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://2014.culturetech.co/)****Derry, Northern Ireland**

[![becoming martian News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/becoming-martian.jpg "News   Hanging out with Hadfield, Redheads & Invading Ireland")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063654/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/becoming-martian.jpg)

I’m bringing Becoming Martian to Northern Ireland! Besides hosting some exceptional events and visiting incredible schools all around Derry, I’ve been asked to perform Becoming Martian at the Learning Pool Cottage on September 16th. AND IT IS GOING TO ROCK!
