# From CaveDB
Hell's Hole is a sinkhole approximately 45m across that extends into open water. The water level is about 38m below the water level and you will need vertical access equipment to access the water. 

You need a permit issued by [ForestrySA](https://www.forestrysa.com.au/visit-our-forests/green-triangle-forest/caving/)
