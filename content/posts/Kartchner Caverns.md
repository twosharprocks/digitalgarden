# About

Kartchner Caverns is a limestone cave system in southeastern Arizona, near Benson in the Whetstone Mountains. Groundwater dissolution formed its chambers and passages.

University of Arizona researchers have studied diverse communities of bacteria and archaea on its formations. Their 2013 research described microbes surviving on scarce nutrients carried by drip water and obtained from rock and soil chemistry.
