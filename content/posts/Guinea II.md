# About

New Guinea II is a limestone cave and rockshelter on Gunaikurnai Country in eastern Victoria. It contains archaeological evidence of Aboriginal occupation extending back more than 20,000 years and culturally important wall markings.

The cave's archaeological deposits and rock art are important to understanding the long history of Aboriginal occupation in southeastern Australia. Research and recording at the site involve Gunaikurnai Traditional Owners.
