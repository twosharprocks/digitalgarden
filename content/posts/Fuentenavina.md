# Additional Notes

From the road intersection where you leave your car, its a 150m level walk following the dirt path along the spring to the entrance (past a bath-tub !).

GAEM, the local group that explores this cave, has set up some nice benches at the entrance that remind one of the luxuries of Florida and Yucatan.

There is no real basin to speak of here, as the entrance is through a little horizontal fissure in the karst wall. One needs to slide into the cave feet first to get through the horizontal restriction, although two divers can fit side by side through this area. Inevitably the viz in this first little chamber is ruined, but as soon as you turn into the flow it opens up clear in front of you.

The line is well laid, without T (entry ends)

Emergence of FUENTENAVINA

LOCATION
The cavity is located in the Municipality of Poveda de la Sierra, to the southeast of the Province of Guadalajara and very close to the border with Cuenca (Alto Tajo Natural Park).

GENERAL
Fuentenavina is an important drainage point for part of the aquifer embedded in Cretaceous materials, located on the left bank of the Tagus River. It consists on one side of the spring, which is the one that permanently gives outlet to the waters, and on the other of a small mouth, located about 10m behind, which is the access point to the interior of the cavity. This has the particularity of being totally submerged, forming a whole set of galleries that the water has been drilling on its way to the outside. It is located at an altitude of 1,205 meters above sea level and the water temperature ranges between 10º and 11ºC.

To date, the GAEM has explored more than 1,500 meters of submerged conduits, which makes it the largest siphon in the Central Zone. At present, the work carried out by the GAEM in the upwelling is focused on exploration, both of its main gallery and of the second siphon and G. del Escalón, as well as topographic survey, study of morphology and evolution of the network, prospecting on the surface and different tasks for the maintenance of the ropes installed inside. In the same way, other works continue in nearby cavities, related to the catchment area .

GEOLOGY
Geologically, the area is made up of sedimentary deposits from the Upper Cretaceous. The cavity develops in a massive-looking dolomitic calcareous section about 90m thick, which appears well stratified in banks a few meters deep. These materials are intensely folded, highlighting a kilometer NNW-SSE syncline, as well as a notable fault of more than 4km in length, parallel to the axis of the syncline and coinciding with the location of the cavity.
This has been developed, fundamentally, in favor of stratification planes, having its current route explored a preferential southeast course, which is the same as that of the fault and the axis of the syncline. The waters that Fuentenavina emits are captured in a wide feeding area to the southeast, where some exokarstic forms are located.

DISCOVERY OF FUENTENAVINA AND HISTORY OF EXPLORATIONS (summary)

Year 1995;
In May 1995, two members of the GAEM toured the vicinity of the small Bocanegra cave , searching for possible permanent emission points that could be related to this intermittent upwelling. With the invaluable help of Mr. Hermenegildo Molina Taulero, pastor of Poveda, a total of six springs were located. Of all of them, one in particular by the name "La Navina", draws special attention due to its remarkable flow.

Although at first glance it appears as a spring that gushes out of the ground on the banks of a small stream, a detailed examination revealed behind a dense vegetation barrier, a short bed that ended in a narrow downward crevice with water, at the foot of a rock outcrop. . After removing some stones, it is possible to force the entrance, reversing bottle checkwith a bottle in hand. After going about 10 meters along a narrow gallery, you reach the head of a small hole, which definitely satisfies your timid initial curiosity.

In the following months, the recently discovered cavity was returned to, after visits to nearby Bocanegra and reusing the immersion equipment used there. In this way, it is possible to explore its first 160m, including a narrow and cloudy chimney located at 110m, through which it is possible to ascend to -6m.
__________________________________________

Year 1996;
At the beginning of the year, the topography works began up to the explored limit, but before carrying out an important work of unblocking the entrance to be able to access with greater comfort, as well as with equipment on the back. Later, in February, a new exploration point was completed somewhat prematurely, reaching 220m/-20m.

Morphologically, the cavity in its first meters is formed by an almost perfect phreatic tube, with an average diameter of 1.7m and a bottom with alternating sections of rock and others of fine, highly compacted sediment. The absence of projections and places to anchor the wire makes it difficult to install, having to resort to ballast pills to fix it.

At the end of the year, a 4-day campaign is organized with several divers and a support team, the objectives being to explore a couple of forks found at 135m and advance beyond 220m; Likewise, the topography work in the siphon and the external conditioning of access to the spring continue, after contacting the land owners.

First meters of the conduit after the entrance shaftIn the first of the explorations, it was possible to connect both galleries, including an incident, turning out to be an elbow of the main gallery: This area is baptized "route of the clueless". As for the main gallery, a new exploration is made up to 330m/-23, losing the tunnel in the distance. The zigzagging of this new section stands out, as well as the reduction, as far as section is concerned, of the duct.

Years 1997-98; 
A new point in the main gallery places the thread at 420m/-27m, keeping the siphon dimensions more or less uniform (1.7mx 1.2) but gradually increasing its depth.

This year also began to explore a narrow side gallery found at 190m/-18m, G. del Escalón (*) managing to enter it about 50m (240m) to -26 meters deep. Outdoors, intense kicking is carried out both in the vicinity of the upwelling and towards the SE, where the catchment area is located, highlighting only the discovery of a sinkhole (Sima Winchi) which is explored to -27m. An inventory and review of all the springs in the area is also carried out.

In the month of November a new advance is made in the siphon, this time by a British cave diver, where they reach 470m and -31 depth.

Although from the beginning this gallery has been considered a lateral to the main one, everything indicates that it is possibly the true main gallery, since it develops on the same stratification plane as the main duct; Thus, the right continuation of this bifurcation (190m) developed in the upper adjacent bedding plane (step) would therefore be the one that should be considered a lateral gallery.

__________________________________________ 
Year 1999; 
The first dives of the year are aimed at consolidating, relocating and signaling the installation of the guide wire, also serving as practices and recording times and consumption, with a view to future advances. In the month of October, a new exploration campaign is organized, in which depths of 570m and -34m are reached. Before several dives are necessary to leave the siphon equipped with emergency and decompression bottles (nitrox and oxygen). The tip, as on previous occasions, is done alone.
__________________________________________
Year 2000;
Several attempts to continue the scan are thwarted by computer-related issues. However, several visits are made to condition the entrance area with a view to future dives, setting up several platforms on the outside for greater comfort when equipping. A first video of the interior of the Surgencia is made.
__________________________________________
Year 2001;
In a five-day campaign, work is carried out on the exploration, on the one hand of the Galería del Escalón (230m/-26m) and on the other of the main tunnel (570m/-34m). In the first one, four conditioned points are made due to narrowness and low visibility, finally placing the line at 390m with a maximum depth of -27.5m. A narrow and cloudy passage stands out right in the deep zone.

In the main Gallery, and with the help of a small torpedo, a new point is made reaching 710m and -44m deep, in a dive that lasts about three hours. Most of the dives are carried out with nitrox, and in decompressions, complicated by the altitude, oxygen is used. The explored development of Fuentenavina exceeds one kilometer of submerged galleries.

__________________________________________
Year 2002-03;
The explorations are focused on the Galería del Escalón, beyond 390m, as the possibility of it reaching the surface is being considered. At two points it is possible to reach a depth of -4m, finally breaking the surface of the water in a third dive, the following year, after 536m, requiring a short decompression to exit. In the last 250m, several small side galleries stand out.

In the aerial area there is an ascending ramp with a water supply, which has not been explored. At the SW end of the bubble, a new siphoned section (Siphon II) is discovered, the first meters of which are examined, formed by a pronounced descending ramp .

In the Galería del Escalón, the aerial branch located at 536m is explored, resulting in impenetrable after 30m of travel; This branch, developed in favor of a high-altitude joint, contributes a small flow towards the bubble in rainy seasons.

In the Main Gallery an interesting side gallery found at 610m/-37 is explored. With the help of a torpedo, the beginning of this gallery is reached, running into a very narrow passage after a few meters, against all odds.planning

Year 2004;
In 2004, and to carry out the planned decompressions dry, a bell was placed at the entrance of the cavity at a depth of -6m, which was anchored to 4 pairs of spits installed in the rock; Likewise, the exploration of some lateral branches began, inside the Galería del Escalón.

Years 2006-2007;
A team of dir divers (dlc) after several visits to the cavity, advances 90 meters in the main tunnel, leaving the tip with a depth of -40 meters.

At the end of the year, and after a short period of inactivity in which other areas and cavities have focused attention, a new campaign is carried out in the cavity to continue with the explorations in the main gallery. This, however, was cut short, already with the siphon equipped, due to a small incident abroad that resulted in seven stitches in one finger.

In collaboration with the GAEM, two divers begin the reinstallation of the siphon with a larger diameter wire.Gearing up for The Step

Year 2008;
Work resumed on the upwelling, continuing with explorations both in the main gallery and in the Escalón gallery. Likewise, a topographic review of the cavity is started , which means a large number of dives, also continuing with the work of reinstallation and marking of the thread.

In the main gallery it is possible to reach its "end" after carrying out a new point of just over 3 hours, where the line is finally left at 870m/-43, point at which the gallery becomes impenetrable. This new area is characterized by the lack of anchor points and by having abrupt ascents and descents (roller coaster), the duct developing here in several sections in favor of vertical strata, in which poor visibility is guaranteed on the return. The fact that these strata outcrop on the surface of the ground stands out.
