TRANSCRIPT:

Dearest Justin,

I hope you are most well,embracing life and enjoying a winter break from your “My World” tour. My name is Reverend Lennard Augustus Seamus Rolmsteed,or Lenny to my parish,and I am a ruffed-lemur vicar in Chelsea,living in the grand old city of London. I write to you this fine English morning to tell you how indubitably excited I and the rest of my parish that you will be arriving in our country soon – we are big fans of both your music and yourself!

Since I first heard “One Time” on my home radiogram I have adored your music. When I was informed of the imminent release of your second album “My World 2.0”,I immediately sent the church’s bell-ringer,Gerard,to stand in the line outside of HMV for 2 days to wait for it. And I assure you,it was certainly worth the wait! Besides your wonderfully emotional songs,you look so amazing in-front of a camera too. I can only imagine how good you will look in 3D watching “Never Say Never”,and I simply fear seeing you live in concert at the O2 arena in March may be too much for my aging varecian heart. But alas I must try,as it is just after your birthday and missing such an opportunity simply because my doctor advises against it would be a terrible shame – we must not live in fear,my dearest Justin!

Although speaking of fear,I was most pleased to hear you would not be touring North Korea. I am all for a cavalier disregard for your personal health,but having visited Pyongyang over the years on missionary work I can assure you it has become a most unpleasant city. It pains me to say it but since the passing of the esteemed Kim Il-sung,the whole country has become quite a disaster. Although there is hope with Old Kimi-sung’s grandson,Kim Jong Un,set to take over and correct his father’s mistakes. And might I say he’ll look devilishly handsome while doing it too! Though he’s slightly more “homely” than I usually approve of,a finer young man to lead that wonderful nation of North Korea I am yet to see. So much gumption and potential!

I would also like to say how pleased I am to hear that Usher has taken such good care of you,given all the pressures a talented young man in your position must experience. With all the natural changes happening in a teenage boy’s body and the excitement of touring and recording,I imagine you would sometimes feel overwhelmed by it all. And while you brush it off,the horrid attacks on you and your character by those internet scoundrels could only add to the stress. So it is all too important to have the advice and comfort of an older gentleman to guide you through the tough times.

While their struggle pales in comparison,many of the boys in my parish’s choir require intensive pastoral care which I am only too happy to provide. Many a night I have had a scared and confused lad in my chamber,helping him come to terms with his faith &identity with an Earl Grey tea and a chocolate digestive. And it is always such a pleasure to see him clear-eyed and filled with the joy of life the next morning at choir practice. It is that joy of life I see in your eyes too Justin,and I thank you for being such a role-model for the young lads I valiantly try to mentor. All of my boys look up to you,and many have adopted your clothes and hairstyle when not wearing their altar boy vestments in church. They will never forgive me if I do not enquire,but what hair gel do you use and does it smell of coconut? The boys are quite partial to coconut you see,and I must admit I am also;it always reminds me of my time in Madagascar,roaming free on the sandy shores with the boys of the local tribes people,the sweat and the spray of the Indian Ocean shining off their skin in the sun.

Alas dearest Justin I must not tarry any further on this letter as Gerard has informed me that some of the parish have arrived for supper,and I mustn’t keep them waiting! Seeing strong,nubile young Christian men like yourself on the world stage is a source of inspiration for us all,and I pray that you continue doing the Lord’s work through your wonderful music,movies and television guest appearances.

God Bless You and Safe Travels,  
\*\*SIGNED\*\*  
Reverend Lennard Rolsmeet
