# Additional Notes

(Translated from German) "The entrance to the Cova des Moro is 140 meters northeast of Cala Falcó within the Cales Verges de Manacor nature reserve . The next town, S'Estany d'en Mas, is 1400 meters north, the larger bay of Cala Varques 700 meters to the south-west and the historic fortified tower of Son Fortesa 2300 meters to the west.

The cave entrance, framed by megaliths at 16 meters above sea level, is surrounded by shrubs that grow on a small rock platform on the cliffs. [2] From there, the main rooms of the Cova des Moro, which are 30 meters wide, extend about 60 meters to the west. [3] The total height difference of the cave is 16 meters. [4] Nearby other karst caves are the [Cova de Cala Falcó]({{< relref "posts/Cova de Cala Falcó.md" >}}) and the [Cova des Coloms]({{< relref "posts/Cova des Coloms.md" >}}) , just on the coast, and inland the [Cova des Pirata]({{< relref "posts/Cova des Pirata.md" >}}) and the Cova des Pont."
