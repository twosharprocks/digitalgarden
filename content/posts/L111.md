# References

- **LSECR:** Page 220
This feature appears to have originally been known as "Entrance D in the series 5L109-5L111 (refer to texts for these cave numbers), and it was described as being a 6 metre diameter, 2m deep countersunk depression which funnelled down to a one metre diameter tube before dropping some 4 metres to the floor of a north-west to south-east joint-controlled cave.
The cave floor reportedly sloped evenly down at an angle of around 30 degrees to the east-south- east, giving a drop of 8-9 metres in the length of the chamber, which was about 2.5 metres wide. The plan view map in-dicates that it terminated about 12 metres from the entrance in two very narrow fissures.
Unfortunately, this feature was reportedly completely filled by bulldozing (in preparation for pine planting) within weeks of the CEGSA visit of 7th October 1967.
