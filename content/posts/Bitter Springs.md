# References

- **Trimix Divers - https://www.trimixdivers.com/trimix5/?p=4885:** Located near the township of Mataranka NT, these springs have the same source and characteristics as the more famous Mataranka Hot Springs with several notable differences.
The water seeping out of the limestone at Bitter Springs comes from multpile point sources distributes over an area of perhaps 50m x 10m. Most are from small holes in the detritus filled floor of the spring’s waterway. None are penetrable. The water is crystal clear with a beautful blue tinge to it. It is a constant 34C and this is warmed by the limestone that holds and supplies it, not by any geothermal processes (same for Mataranka Hot Springs and [Katherine Hot Springs]({{< relref "posts/Katherine Hot Springs.md" >}})).

It is called Bitter Springs because the water actually does taste bitter ! There are no fish living in the water, but there are many turtles, both the long and short necked varieties. The turtles thrive under the vegetation mats that cover most of the upstream waterway. They are quite shy, but we found that with rebreathers we could get very close to them and Paul Boler got some excellent video footage.

There are many caves and sinkholes in the area surrounding Mataranka. They are in the same limestone that supplies water to the Bitter, Mataranka & [Katherine Hot Springs]({{< relref "posts/Katherine Hot Springs.md" >}}). It is quite possible and likely that some of these features will enable access to the (shallow) aquifer and make possible access to new cave diving sites.
