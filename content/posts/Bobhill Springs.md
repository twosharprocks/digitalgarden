# References

Portions of the following information were taken from Florida Geological Survey Bulletin 31.

Bobhill Springs is in a hammock about 7 mi S. of Weeki Wachee Springs or 5 mi N. of Hudson).  Drive

about 5 mi from Hudson on U.S. Hwy 19 to the Pasco-Hernando County line; the spring is 600 ft N. of the

county line and 1,000 ft W. of the high-way.

Description - This spring flows from a single 10-ft-diameter vent in the sand-covered limestone

bottom of a 100-by-200-ft-oval pool with concrete walls and perimeter walk. Bobhill Springs discharges

west to the Gulf through Bayou Creek and Bayou Lake. A prominent boil is reported to be continuous. Depth

at the spring vent is about 15 ft.

Utilization - The spring is privately owned. This is a traveler's trailer-tenter camp site that is

advertised to have some 250 spaces and all recreational and camping facilities.
