# About

Unusual solution tube under concrete in the Malloney/Kaczkowski's carport.
Hole searched for after report from Simon Carey (previous owner) of large cave under concrete - possibly search for steel plate with a metal detector?

# Additional Notes

"Funnily enough I had breakfast this morning with my best mate who concreted over hole in Molony's driveway. He remembers the hole and just how big the area is under Commercial St. If you follow the direction of the yellow line on your map, and extend it to the front door of Molonys...That's the direction of the cave. We dropped rocks and heard nothing, we shone torches and couldn't see anything. It was massive. If you stand in the middle of the front door and take 3 large steps heading south, your right over the hole. Under the concrete is about 2 inches of bitumen and then nothing! It's really scary! I lowered myself through the hole, shone a strong torch towards Vansittart Park, and it was just black. That probably explains why Vansittart Park dries out so quickly. You can have 2 inches of rain in the Mount and all the other ovals are under water and Vansittart Park is as dry as a bone."

"I just remembered, we actually covered the hole with a metal plate so we could pour the concrete. There's probably only 4 inches of concrete on top."

# References

- **Simon Carey:** "Years ago I used to own Molony's Brewing Company in Commercial Street. In 1980 the bitumen driveway of the Brewery at the front door, collapsed,and a hole about 2ft in diameter appeared. I stuck my head down and saw a massive hole about the size of the MCG!! I lowered myself down on a rope with a torch, and couldn't get a reflection in any direction. The cave went North, way under Vansittart Park, towards the yellow line on your map, and further South under the Brewery. I did what every responsible citizen would do and put a concrete floor straight over the hole, and made out it didn't exist!! For years I've been waiting for news of Commercial Street collapsing into the cave, but, so far so good."
