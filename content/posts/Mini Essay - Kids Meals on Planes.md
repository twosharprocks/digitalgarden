Topic: Kids Meals on Planes

I'd never realised it before, but every meal on a plane is basically a kids meal. I've just visited my relentlessly obnoxious 3 year old nephew, and on the flight back to Adelaide I've been served focaccia topped with chorizo, pesto, bell peppers & goats cheese. Seems fancy as fuck, but looking at the box it was served in I realised it's just like what you'd pull out of a Happy Meal. And that's the trick, isn't it? We're being served little boxes of snacks to keep us happy as we fly through space in an aluminium tube full of recycled farts. 

Trying to tame a toddler who's kicking off in the back of a car is impossible, but fully grown adults still need a Happy Meal to keep them from kicking off and crashing the fucking plane. Ultimately it's just a light tranquillizer for a stressful situation - you're being loaded into something our monkey brains can't process, you have limited mobility and freedom for an extended period of time which amplifies the stress, and one way of reducing that stress is through endorphin-releasing snacks (like a starchy, carb-loaded focaccia). Even the alcohol comes in those ridiculous little bottles - they're basically an adult juice box so that the 40 year old toddler in 18C doesn't try to crack a window. Sit down, be quiet, listen to the lady at the front, eat your happy meal and drink your juice box.
