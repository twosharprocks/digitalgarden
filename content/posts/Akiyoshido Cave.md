# Notes

More than 400 limestone caves have been identified in Akiyoshidai, including Akiyoshido Cave , one of Japan's three largest limestone caves and a designated special natural monument.

Underwater surveys of Higashidai, particularly those concerning Akiyoshido Cave, have progressed relatively well. The groundwater system of Higashidai is broadly divided into two parts: the "Kanoide water system" in the north and the "Akiyoshido water system" in the south, with their respective catchment areas estimated at 1,200 hectares and 1,400 hectares.

1) Intermittent surveys have been conducted both on land and underwater at Akiyoshido Cave, and as of December 2017, the total length of the surveys, including both the land and submerged sections, reached 10,700 meters.

- Akiyoshido Cave is the only cave designated as a Special Natural Monument, and therefore requires more delicate experience and environmental understanding during its investigation.

2) Akiyoshidai Science Museum, "The Cutting Edge of Akiyoshido Cave Research," 2017, https://karusuto.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/event_20171212.pdf
