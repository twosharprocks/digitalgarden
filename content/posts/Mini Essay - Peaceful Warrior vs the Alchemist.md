Having just finished two of the Peaceful Warrior books as well as the Alchemist, it feels like the perfect opportunity to compare and contrast the two in order to figure out what it is that makes them so special.

The stand out element from this most recent re-reading is that the Alchemist feels like an important reminder of the basics, whereas there are elements of the Peaceful Warrior series that seem new even though you've read it more than a dozen times. This time around rereading the Peaceful Warrior felt more important than the Alchemist, and I guess this is the opportunity to figure out why.

Finishing Peaceful Warrior felt like you'd changed for the experience - like you needed to go through that journey to wind up in exactly the same space but changed for it. If anything the Alchemist felt too short, although that's a huge power in itself - something that can remind you of the basics in just a few hours. Peaceful Warrior is very much about not being a self-indulgent victim, which might be what you need to be reminded of at the moment. As it stands it feels like you've been going through the motions with life, but you're starting to shift that as you type.

On the flipside, the Alchemist is about pursuing your dreams of adventure - something that is very close for you now - and being able to adapt and evolve to the demands of the universe without losing sight of your dreams. So both books are vital to your current journey... but this week you may have needed the shakabuku from Peaceful Warrior a little more than the adventure of the Alchemist. 

Regardless, life continues and you evolve and adapt with it. Sulawesi is a month and a half away, your CISSP exam is closer, and hearing about your application to the Explorer's Club is closer still. You are quickly developing the skills and connections you need to pursue grand adventures and break out of your current life. You could drop everything today, but you have set yourself targets which will aid your journey considerably. So keep Peaceful Warrior in mind as you work towards your goals, and lean more on the Alchemist as you journey on the adventures you're preparing for.

Above all, love the life you live - your time on Earth is short, so don't waste your time or energy on petty squabbles or being hurt by others. You have already experienced an extraordinary life, and so you need only embrace it once again. Be engaging, be alive, and above all be interesting - live with the full force of your being.
