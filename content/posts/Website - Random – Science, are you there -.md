Atheists aren’t evil, misguided, or unenlightened: we’re just normal people like the rest of you. Everywhere you look though, there’s [evidence](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://atheismisdead.blogspot.com/search/label/Adolf%20Hitler "What about all the GOOD things Hitler did?") of our [persecution](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://atheistwatch.blogspot.com/ "Atheist Watch? What kind of Orwellian nightmare is this?"). But when you start to look at what we’re up against, it’s not hard to see why.

————————————————————–

Jesus of Nazareth

Nothing I write here can possibly say can put it better – Jesus is [awesome](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://pagebuildr.com/Jesus_Christ/Snapshots/Jesus-the-badass "Seriously - what a badass")\*

As the video mentions, Jesus was an [awesome stand-up comic](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.comedyjesus.com/ "Coming to a screen near you, April 2010").

Don’t be confused by what those [filthy liberals](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.conservapedia.com/Liberal "This is the greatest wiki I've ever come across") say though – Jesus is [whiter than Rose McGowan](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://upload.moldova.org/movie/actors/r/rose_mcgowan/thumbnails/tn2_rose_mcgowan_2.jpg "Someone get me a pair of sunglasses"), grew up in Kansas, and votes Republican. He died for our sins, but then came back and walked around for a while, showing everyone how bad ass he was. But 3 days in that cave meant he was busting for an epic shit – Jesus found your garden, and started laying a bunch of “Jew eggs” in the bushes. When Mary Magdalene caught him laying an egg, he blamed it on a nearby rabbit, labelled her a whore, then bugged out back to heaven. Mary Magdalene ate a bunch of Jesus’s eggs but missed some, and they fossilised and turned into chocolate over the millennia for kids to find and eat.

And that’s the story of Easter.

Mohammad

Mohammad was just an ordinary guy… who kicked some serious ass all over the Middle East. He stomped all over those pesky Meccan tribes, laid down the Laws of Allah, and brought the whole region into a state of relative peace – which lasted all of about 30 seconds after he died. But Mohammad was totally cool with dying, because he’d already been to heaven once before, and decided to come back to keep being a bad ass on Earth for a while. One night he rode a [flying winged-horse](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buraq "I want to ride a pegasus into heaven too") into heaven with arch-angel Gabriel; prayed at the furthest mosque; hung out with Allah just chilling and listening to Sigur Rós; then he had to bail and head back to Earth to sort some shit out. How cool is that?

L. Ron Hubbard and Xenu

Just personally, I think L. Ron Hubbard was a fairly major douche. But I have to grudgingly respect a man who tells everyone exactly what he’s going to do (in [Reader’s Digest](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.skeptictank.org/readdig.htm "*facepalm*") no less), and then laughs all the way to the bank as [8 million morons](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.deseretnews.com/article/1,5143,595091823,00.html "Lets hope they can't breed") throw their money at him anyway:

> “Writing for a penny a word is ridiculous. If a man really wants to make a million dollars, the best way would be to start his own religion”

The real reason Scientology has so much going for it though, is because of this bad boy, Xenu (or is that Xemu?)

[![](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/southparkxenu-300x225.jpg "southparkxenu")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/southparkxenu.jpg)While not technically the “good” guy in [Scientology](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.xenu.net/ "Much love, Operation Clambake"), why *wouldn’t you* want to believe in a “religion” based on the idea that Xenu, evil dictator of the Galactic Confederacy, tricked his people into turning up for income-tax inspections, trapped their souls in booze and anti-freeze, flew them to Earth in DC-8′s, dumped them around volcanoes, then blew the hell out of them by dropping mecha-tons of hydrogen bombs into the volcanoes?

[![](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/shiva01-249x300.jpg "shiva01")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/shiva01.jpg)

Shiva, just chilling with his homie the Cobra

The Hindus have their own “God of ruining everything” – Shiva. And when other Hindu deities churned up the oceans to get the sweet, sugary nectar of immortality, Shiva decided to be a badass and drink the [left over poison instead](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halahala "MMMmmmm, salty"), staining his throat blue. Between being the transformer and destroyer of our entire universe, Shiva in his spare time likes long walks on the beach, throwing his trident at people who piss him off, having the Ganges river flow out from his hair, and riding around on his epic bull [“Nandi”](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nandi_%28bull%29 "Because he can")

[![](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/darwin-274x300.gif "darwin")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2009/10/darwin.gif)It wasn’t enough for Charles Darwin to undermine our entire understanding of biology (what kind of science is that anyway?), he was also a [disappointment to his father](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.conservapedia.com/Charles_Darwin#Religious_Views_of_Charles_Darwin "He jsut wanted to rape monkeys instead of altar boys"), and was called a liar by CBS news. Some claim he died from the [guilt of rejecting god](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.answersingenesis.org/creation/v17/i4/darwins_illness.asp "LOL WUT?"), others from [being a racist,](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.conservapedia.com/Charles_Darwin#Darwin.27s_Racism "I hate Australian and gorillas too Darwin") for growing a [beard too late in life and never owning a beagle](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://uk.answers.yahoo.com/question/index?qid=20090718150357AAKICCY "I couldn't agree more"). It’s also very easy to prove his theory on the big bang was [wrong too.](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://wiki.answers.com/Q/How_can_you_prove_Charles_Darwin_wrong "Just about pissed my pants reading this")

Then again, some simply think he died because [he was lactose intolerant](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Darwin%27s_illness#Other_possible_causes "People need to be more understanding of lactose") - the worst kind of intolerance.

But against all these [fantastic arguments](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.landoverbaptist.net/showthread.php?s=08d3e9e4863115ff42f081a28e02003f&t=14025 "After reading about \"Evil-ution\", now I'm a believer too!") and awesome deities, atheists push on doggedly in their belief that there is no god. Thankfully for all the *normal* people out there though, researchers have established some [causes for atheism and are currently working on a cure.](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://friendlyatheist.com/2009/01/06/common-causes-of-atheism-is-there-a-cure/)

In the mean time, I guess we’ll just have to endure the [abuse](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.topix.com/forum/religion/christian/TFVRD6T84LB3CFILD "My dunce cap fits perfectly, thank you") and [sneering remarks](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.whydarwinwaswrong.com/index.php) of the drooling masses. It’s just a burden you have to bear when you go and do something crazy, like basing your belief system on that flimsy trinity of [basic facts](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_microwave_background_radiation "NO! God PUT that their to test our FAITH!"), the [scientific method](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://www.guardian.co.uk/science/2006/apr/06/evolution.fossils "What do you know - we proposed a hypothesis, tested it, and it was right!"), and [Occam’s razor](https://web.archive.org/web/20140322164204/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occam%27s_razor "Slow development over billions of years - or an invisible boogeyman")…
