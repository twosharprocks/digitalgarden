Given that I haven’t posted since the madness of March you’ve all probably figured that the last 5 months have been even more chaotic  – you’d be right. But rather than going into the whole “moving to the other side of the country & working in job I’m now leaving” business right now, what’s way more important is that the team behind the [Helena payload](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.helenapayload.com/) are touring Australia during National Science Week![Back in February I mentioned](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/2015/02/07/news-2015-the-year-space-kicks-it-up-a-notch/) I’d been working with UWA’s motorsports team – lead by the man and legend, Andre Van Vulpen  to develop a payload for [Mars One’s 2020 lander](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVtTcklAKd8) for their [global university competition](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.mars-one.com/news/press-releases/mars-one-announces-request-for-proposals-for-2018-mars-lander-payloads). Together we designed an electrolysis unit that would produce oxygen from ice in the Martian soil we called “Helena”, named after the Shakespearean character who could “breathe life into stone”. While Helena [didn’t get selected](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.mars-one.com/news/press-releases/the-winner-of-the-mars-one-university-competition-will-bring-life-to-mars-i) to fly on the 2020 lander, [we were short-listed in the top 10](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.mars-one.com/news/press-releases/mars-one-announces-ten-potential-university-payloads-to-fly-to-mars-in-2018) and were the only short-listed entry from the southern hemisphere.

In recognition of this, the fine folks at Inspiring Australia gave the Helena team $15,000 to travel around Australia during national Science Week to visit schools and run public events to talk about the payload’s development, working on Mars missions, and pathways into a career in aerospace engineering. So watch out for the Helena team hitting a major Australian city near you –

**Hobart – August 14th & 15th**I’ll be launching National Science Week in Hobart on Friday and Saturday August 14/15, speaking to school groups about science and space exploration in a madhouse 48 hours for Tasmania’s own”Festival Of Bright Ideas”. Besides the [reception at Government house](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/reception-at-government-house/?search-location-code=7001&search-location-lat=-42.837499&search-location-lng=147.506162&search-location-name=HOBART&search-location-type=P&search-location=HOBART+7001+TAS&search-events-count=20&search-online-count=31), the festival launch and a mountain of school visits, I’m really looking forward to teaming up again with the guys from [Future Crunch on Saturday August 15th](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/future-crunch-3pm-2/?search-location-code=7001&search-location-lat=-42.837499&search-location-lng=147.506162&search-location-name=HOBART&search-location-type=P&search-location=HOBART+7001+TAS&search-events-count=20&search-online-count=31).

**Perth – August 15th**Andre, Rowan and Claire will manning the Helena stall at the Perth Science Festival – chatting to attendees about the payload design, displaying a 3D model of the payload, as well as describing their experiences working to aerospace engineering requirements.

**Adelaide – August 16th & 17th**[![CLimikkUMAAVb4_](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/CLimikkUMAAVb4_.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/CLimikkUMAAVb4_.jpg)All four of us from the Helena team are meeting up in Adelaide to present “[Helena – Breathing Life Into Stone](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/breathing-life-into-stone-putting-oxygen-art-on-mars/)” at the University of South Australia’s Mawson Lakes Campus on August 17th. Immediately after our presentation Michael Davis will be presenting a lecture on the International Space University’s upcoming [2016 Southern Hemisphere Summer Space Program](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.unisa.edu.au/it-engineering-and-the-environment/division-information-technology-engineering-and-the-environment/southern-hemisphere-summer-space-program1/).

August 17th also happens to be my 30th birthday. So on the plus side I’ll be talking about space and hanging out with ISU folks on my 30th. But at the same time I’ll be in Adelaide… so, you know…

**Sydney – August 18th**Next stop is Sydney, with another [Helena presentation at Fishburners on Tuesday August 18th](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/breathing-life-into-stone-putting-oxygen-art-on-mars-2/). The rest of the team will get a breather in Sydney, but I’ll be racing back down to Melbourne on [August 19th to see Laborastory’s National Science Week special](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/the-laborastory-national-science-week-edition-2015/?search-location-code=3000&search-location-lat=-37.814563&search-location-lng=144.970267&search-location-name=MELBOURNE+METRO&search-location-type=L&search-location=Melbourne+Metro+3000+VIC&search-date-start=Today&search-date-end=All+dates&text=laborastory&audience=All+ages&search-topic=&search-events-count=1&search-online-count=0).

**Melbourne – August 20th & 21st**Last stop on the Helena national tour is my new town of Melbourne, with school visits to University High School and Simonds Catholic College. The team’s duties will be done on Friday afternoon, but I’ll be racing down to Clayton to speak at [Monash University as they attempt to break the “World’s Largest Astronomy Lesson”](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/https://www.facebook.com/events/1452384498390666/) record set in [Kalamunda earlier this year… which I also spoke at 😀](https://web.archive.org/web/20150810163304/http://www.kalamunda.wa.gov.au/Media-Releases/March-2015/Kalamunda-Eclipses-World-Record)

Things have fallen off the wagon here the last few months – I started to lose track of what I was here in Melbourne to do. But I’ve still landed on my feet here, and now I’ve found my way I’m moving back to what I want to be doing. And I’m looking forward to telling you all about it soon.

Till then, bring on another National Science Week.
