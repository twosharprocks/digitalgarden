# Certified Information Systems Auditor

Focus: Specialises in information systems auditing—planning and performing audits, evaluating governance and controls, and reporting assurance over IT-enabled business processes and information assets. ([ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cisa "CISA® Certification | Certified Information Systems Auditor®"))

Industry: Suits organisations with strong assurance and compliance needs such as government, critical infrastructure, finance, healthcare, large enterprises, and consulting/audit firms. ([ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing/cisa "CISA® Certification | Certified Information Systems Auditor®"))

## Requirements
- Pass the CISA Exam (US$575 ISACA Member, US$760 Non-member)
    - Format: 150 multiple-choice questions, scaled score of 450 or higher (on a 200–800 scale) to pass. 
    - Duration: 4 hours.
    - Content:
        - Information System Auditing Process (18%)
        - Governance & Management of IT (18%)
        - Information Systems Acquisition, Development & Implementation (12%) 
        - Information Systems Operations and Business Resilience (26%)
        - Protection of Information Assets (26%)
- Relevant work experience
    - At least five years of professional work experience in information systems auditing, control, assurance, or security.
    - Experience must be within the 10 years preceding the application date, and you must have experience in at least 1 of the 5 CISA job practice domains.
    - You have five years after passing the exam to apply for certification. 
    - Experience waivers can reduce the required five years by up to a maximum of three years (for example, via combinations of general IS/audit experience and education waivers).
- Submit the certification application and pay the US$50 application processing fee.
- Agree to ISACA’s Code of Professional Ethics and ongoing continuing professional education requirements.
