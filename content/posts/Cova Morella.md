"La Cova Morella is one of the 30 cavities in Mallorca considered a Site of Community Interest (SCI). This cavity is so little frequented that many of the paths that will take us to it are covered by vegetation or have disappeared, so we will have to use our best intuition to reach it. We will reach its great entrance on a cliff, surrounded by ivy and ferns."
