# Gruta do Padre

Cave and part of a wider underground hydrological complex in Bahia, Brazil.

## Research notes

Brazil's geological service identifies Gruta do Padre near Santa Maria da Vitória, Bahia, within the Santo Antônio River karst system. The saved text says ‘Santana dos Brejos’; the geological record and other reports use Santana and Santa Maria da Vitória. Preserve that wording as a lead pending local confirmation.
