Hope you all had a fantastic weekend – I clearly offended some sort of deity with last week’s post. I’m still feeling a little raw from it so rather than get into it too much, here’s a few of the highlights for you all:

- Having the one material item in this world I love, my [Team Zissou hoodie](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://8074.spreadshirt.com/team-zissou-diver-hoody-A851541 "Atleast when I order another one, I can get a red beanie at the same time"), being stolen from my hostel on Friday
- Getting the wrong directions for the Hilton, spending 3 hours walking randomly around the shady docklands trying to find it, and hence missing my last chance to see friends at my last stand-up gig before I start training.
- Having a check-out chick steal my last 20 pounds from me at the counter.
- Watching “The Omen”, and then minutes later having the fire alarm go off  and having to evacuate the hotel at 1 in the morning… and no hot water or a refund the next morning
- Finding my Australian credit card has been locked again for “Overseas Use”, and having to pick 5 pence pieces off the road to buy a ticket back to Exeter.

But I managed to get back and decided to get absurdly drunk to celebrate. And with just 7 days left before I disappear, it can only improve from here. So without further ado, here’s your last weekly moments of web awesomeness.

- Materials that [kick physics in the ass](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://www.cracked.com/article_17476_7-man-made-substances-that-laugh-in-face-physics.html "Scariest... dildo...ever")
- Some [amazing slow-motion footage](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://www.viraltube.net/media/62/1_million_fps_Slow_Motion_video_of_Bullet_Impacts/) of frangible projectile impacts
- Seeing the [real Cheshire Cat](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://alexcf.com/blog/?p=204 "This thing is scary as shit") will stop you sleeping for weeks
- Improving the world by [making things fun for people](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2lXh2n0aPyw "What an incredible idea")
- Cougars gone wild on [“Who wants to be a Millionaire”](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zy_f1KOUlpA "Sure, he's an officer - but he'll always be semen to her")
- I… I really don’t know what to [say to this](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y75slWA829c "Do people do this? Is this what's funny now?")
- Turns out [Satan drops the mask occasionally](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://abcnews.go.com/Entertainment/CelebSnapshots/popup?id=3660666&contentIndex=1&page=10&start=false "Hey SAAAAAAAAAAAAATAN?") after all
- What happens when [Mario and Luigi do shrooms](https://web.archive.org/web/20140921214627/http://www.myconfinedspace.com/wp-content/uploads/2007/08/mario-s-dream-nightmare.jpg "Dude, that's fucked up")
