# Caves
- Jordbrugrotta Farm, [Plura](https://ngdf.shorthandstories.com/?page_id=421&lang=en#section-English-JQuw9Txawq) in Rana
    * [Plura Week](http://ngdf.no/?page_id=421&lang=en) (Friday Week 31 to Friday Week 32), Organised by [NGDF](https://www.facebook.com/NorskGrottedykkerforbund/) 
	    * Winter (December–March)
			- Vis ~100 meters
			- Water temp: ~2°C
		- Summer (July–August)
		- Vis ~30-50m
		- Water Temp: 4-7°C
 * [Litjåga cave](https://www.suunto.com/en-au/sports/News-Articles-container-page/Exploring-the-Litjaga-cave/)
 * Sagelva - https://vimeo.com/475236239
	 * Much of the mountain in Nordland is porous limestone, which means that Nordland has several caves. One of those that has been explored recently is the Sagelv system in Salangen municipality
* Røyrvik - https://vimeo.com/475236239
	* In Røyrvik, there are several caves that have been explored and are still being explored. The caves are shallow, and here it is nice to use side-mounted equipment.
* Dumdalen - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U1c83mvyRH8
