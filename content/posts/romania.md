# Cave Notes

Izbucul Tăuzului, 46.5117, 22.6903, 
- Deep resurgence linked to [Humpleu Cave]({{< relref "posts/Humpleu Cave.md" >}}); explored beyond 85m.   
[Izverna Cave Spring]({{< relref "posts/Izverna Cave Spring.md" >}}), 44.8462, 22.6023, 
- Long submerged entrance passage; extensively dived.  
Izbucul Cotețul Dobreștilor, 46.5089, 22.7618, 
- Karst spring in Apuseni; little known, exploratory target.
Izbucul Ponor, 46.6058, 22.7567, 
- Classic karst spring below plateau; resurgence for a large underground catchment.  
Izbucul Galbenei, 46.5543, 22.7234, 
- Scenic resurgence emerging from cliff wall; linked to deep cave system above.
