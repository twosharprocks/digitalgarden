# Qanaf Cave

Cave on the Jabal Samḥān plateau in Dhofar, Oman; also spelled Quanaf.

## Research notes

La Venta's first-person 2024 expedition report describes the entrance as a sinkhole at the end of a wadi and summarises earlier expeditions. Treat its survey dimensions as historical until a current survey is obtained.
