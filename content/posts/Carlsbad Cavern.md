# Carlsbad Cavern

Cave within Carlsbad Caverns National Park, New Mexico, United States. Distinct from nearby [Lechuguilla Cave]({{< relref "posts/Lechuguilla Cave.md" >}}).

## Research notes

The National Park Service manages visits to Carlsbad Cavern and separately restricts entry to Lechuguilla Cave to approved work.
