# About
[Katanga (Shaba) Plateau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katanga_plateau)
- **Why:** Massive, remote karst regions in southern DRC near the Zambian border.
- **Status:** Very limited cave exploration; almost no diving records.
- **Potential:** First expeditions into flooded karst systems—logistically challenging but potentially world-class.
- **Best Season:** May to August.
