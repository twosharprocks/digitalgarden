# Ingredients
- Ground pork - 1/2 lb
- Oil - 1 tbsp
- Garlic - 6 cloves (minced)
- Onion - 1/4 (finely chopped)
- Noodles - 300g
- Seasoning
	- Garlic powder - 1 tsp
	- Salt - 1/2 tsp
	- Pepper
	- Chilli flakes
- Sauce
	- Soy sauce
	- Chilli oil
	- Brown sugar

# Directions
1. Cook the noodles and set aside
2. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the ground pork until browned
3. Add the garlic and onion and cook until fragrant and softened
4. Season with garlic powder, salt, pepper and chilli flakes
5. Stir through soy sauce, chilli oil and a little brown sugar
6. Add the cooked noodles and toss well to coat
7. Serve hot
