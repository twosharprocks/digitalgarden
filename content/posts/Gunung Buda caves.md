# Gunung Buda caves

Karst and cave exploration area in Sarawak, Malaysia. Gunung Buda is a mountain and cave region, not the name of one cave.

## Research notes

A 1997 joint US–Malaysian expedition report describes surveys of multiple separate caves, including Hornbill, Loris, Babylon, Mojo, Langur, Thunder, Twilight and Fruit Bat. Keep individual caves distinct if they are later added.
