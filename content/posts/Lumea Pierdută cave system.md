# Lumea Pierdută cave system

Connected cave network in the Bihor Mountains, Romania, including Avenul Negru and Avenul Gemănata.

## Research notes

The Romanian speleological inventory records 3,322 m of development and 137 m of vertical range. These are source-specific survey figures; later surveys may differ.
