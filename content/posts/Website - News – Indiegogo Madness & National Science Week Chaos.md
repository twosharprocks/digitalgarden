## **Australian National Science Week – August 16-24, 2014**

[![nswk 2014 inline positive small1 News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/nswk_2014_inline_positive_small1.gif "News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/nswk_2014_inline_positive_small1.gif)

### **August 16 - [Perth Science Festival](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/perth-science-festival/)****10am, Perth Cultural Centre****, Free Event**

Entertain the whole family and celebrate National Science Week with the FREE Perth Science festival! Prepare for explosive fun with the Scitech science shows, experience exciting demonstrations from Universities, listen to Costa Georgiadis from Gardening Australia and be amazed by Antarctic explorer, Tim Jarvis who recreated British explorer Sir Ernest Shackleton’s survival journey!

**At 6pm Josh will be performing “Kerbal On The Super Screen” for attendees of the Perth Science Festival**

National Science Week celebrates and acknowledges the contributions of Australian Scientists to our world. It also aims to encourage an interest in science among the general public and to raise awareness of the fascinating world that we live in.

### **August 18 – [The Physicist and The Poet](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/the-physicist-the-poet/)****1pm, Perth City Library,****Free Event**

Physics meets Poetry in a Perth City Library special event for National Science Week! Join award-winning poet Bronwyn Lovell for a reading from her forthcoming verse novel “Migration” — based on Mars One’s mission to colonise Mars in 2025. Following the reading, Bronwyn will be joined by Mars One astronaut candidate Josh Richards for a Q&A session covering poetry, physics and the challenges of life, love and death in space.

### **August 19 – [Shapiro Tuesday’s Science Special](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/shapiro-tuesdays-science-special/)****8pm, Upstairs at the Brisbane Hotel, $5 on the door**

[![science 300x300 News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/science-300x300.jpg "News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/science.jpg)

An evening of science and laughter, with Perth’s premier experimental comedy room hosting a very special science-themed night.

Hosted by Perth’s best MCs and featuring 12 comedians all bringing their best stand-up on everything science to Lazy Susan’s Comedy Den (upstairs at the Brisbane Hotel), you’ll laugh while you learn more about the world in more detail than you probably needed!

### **August 20 – [Adam Peter Scott’s Science Death Match](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/adam-peter-scotts-science-death-match/)****8pm, **Upstairs at the Brisbane Hotel, Tickets $15****

[![APS Death Match 295x300 News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/APS-Death-Match-295x300.jpg "News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/APS-Death-Match.jpg)

Adam Peter Scott, comedian and entertainer, presents a show that’s part comedy, part game show, part panel and all unhinged.

In a National Science Week special featuring four different special guests (including Josh Richards) with a passion for science, APS brings them all together to discuss a mutual love (or ignorance) of science facts & science-fiction. Featuring questions about some of your favourite books, movies and TV shows, mixed in with science questions from the microscopic to cosmological.

This is unscripted. This is unpredictable. This is Science Death Match.

### **August 21st, 22nd & 23rd - [Kerbals On The Super Screen](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/kerbals-on-the-super-screen/)** **6pm, Perth Cultural Centre, Free Event**

[![ksp 300x253 News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/ksp-300x253.jpg "News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/ksp.jpg)

Have you ever dreamt of building and flying your own spaceship? Now’s your chance to try! Join physicist, comedian and Mars One astronaut candidate Josh Richards in the Perth Cultural Centre (in front of the State Library) as he invites members of the public to play the critically-acclaimed computer game Kerbal Space Program on the cultural centre’s massive 8m Super Screen.

Using accurate rocket parts and real-life physics you can build your own rockets, explore space and even attempt a landing on the Moon or Mars – all while Josh explains the out-of-this-world challenges faced by those wanting to explore the final frontier.

### **August 21st, 22nd & 23rd – [Becoming Martian](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.scienceweek.net.au/becoming-martian/)** **7pm, State Library Theatre, Tickets $20 [Available Here](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://igg.me/at/becomingmartian)**

[![becomingmartian logo wide crop News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/becomingmartian_logo-wide-crop.jpg "News   Indiegogo Madness & National Science Week Chaos")](https://web.archive.org/web/20140920063000/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/becomingmartian_logo-wide-crop.jpg)

Join physicist, comedian & Mars One astronaut candidate Josh Richards on an humorous and informative journey to the red planet, looking at how colonising Mars will change humans in body, mind and soul. 

Short-listed as a candidate to Mars One’s 2024 mission to send humans one-way to Mars, and taken from his upcoming book “Becoming Martian”, Josh blends science, engineering and ethics with comedy as he shares how colonising Mars will change us as a species.
