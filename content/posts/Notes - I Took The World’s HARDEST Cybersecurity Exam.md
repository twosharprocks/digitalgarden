4 hour exam, up to 175 questions but adaptive (can cut off at 125)
Recommend Study
- [Pete Zerger's 8 hour CISSP Exam Cram](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_nyZhYnCNLA) - 4 days watching video
	- Split up domains, watched 2 per day at 1.25x speed, took notes throughout, then practice tests on just those two domains
- [Official Study Guide ](https://www.amazon.com.au/Certified-Information-Security-Professional-Official-ebook/dp/B097NHJK9Q/)
	- Includes multiple practice tests and full length exams
Exam Questions - 3 days (~3000 questions)
- [ExSim-Max for CISSP](https://www.boson.com/practice-exam/cissp-isc2-practice-exam) Practice Exams - $75 for 700 questions
- [PocketPrep](https://www.pocketprep.com/exams/isc2-cissp/) - $25 for 1000 questions

Questions are as vague as people say 
- Practice exams are great for practicing this
- Easiest-hardest test you'll ever do - no idea at any point during exam if pass or fail
- Exam printout will show where you're weak

Requires employer endorsement
