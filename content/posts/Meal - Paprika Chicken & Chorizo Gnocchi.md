# Ingredients
- Gnocchi - 500g
- Olive oil - 1/2 tbsp
- Chorizo - 70g
- Red onion - 1 small
- Chicken breast - 350g
- Garlic - 2 cloves (crushed)
- Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
- Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
- Chicken stock - 1L
- Natural yogurt - 500g
- Parmesan - 50g (grated)
- Spinach - 1 handful
- Seasoning
	- Paprika
	- Salt
	- Pepper

# Directions
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook the chorizo until it starts to crisp
2. Add the chopped red onion and cook until softened
3. Add the chicken breast, season with paprika, salt and pepper, and cook until coloured
4. Stir through the garlic, tomato puree and chopped sun dried tomatoes
5. Pour in the chicken stock and add the gnocchi
6. Simmer until the gnocchi is tender and the sauce reduces slightly
7. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
8. Fold through the spinach until wilted
9. Serve hot
