# About

Region - Mawasangka (South)
Flow from fresh water lens to ocean
### Boats from / to Muna
Speedboats (superjet)
- **Raha to Kendari and Baubau** in [Buton](http://thespicerouteend.com/buton-travel-guide-sulawesi/). 
- Raha-Baubau: 2h, 150’000Rp
- Raha-Kendari: 3h 
- Baubau-Kendari: 185’000Rp to Kendari, price is 150’000Rp to Baubau, and Baubau Kendari is 185’000Rp.
- Daily departure from Baubau at 7AM and 12AM everyday. Should be the same from Kendari.

Ferries (operated by ASDP) 
- Passengers and vehicles. 
- To mainland Sulawesi: Board in Tampo (North Muna) to Torobulu. 
- 4 departures per day (both ways): 7AM, 10AM, 13PM, 16PM (unless on Friday only 3 departures 8AM, 13PM, 16PM). 
- The ticket is 32’000Rp per person, the trip takes 2 hours.
- Would not recommend ferry unless you have a vehicle. 
- _superjet_ costs same after transport from Raha to Tampo and then Torobulu to Kendari.

Ojek (Motorcycle Taxi)
- Raha to Tampo: 0.5hr, 50’000Rp 
- When you arrive in Torobulu, shared taxi takes passengers to Kendari for 50’000Rp (2 hours).
### Accomodation
Hotel Alia - Muna Island
- First hotel after arriving at pier. 2 single beds and fan for 150’000Rp/night (including breakfast).
- Other rooms with AC available.
- Very nice owner/staff - will put you in touch with ojek drivers in Raha who will rent their motorbike for 100’000Rp/day.
