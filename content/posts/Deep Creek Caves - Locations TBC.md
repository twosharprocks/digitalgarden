# Additional Notes

Yarrangobilly has three ‘[Deep Creek]({{< relref "posts/Deep Creek.md" >}})’ Caves: North, West, and East (no, there is no South [Deep Creek]({{< relref "posts/Deep Creek.md" >}})). West [Deep Creek]({{< relref "posts/Deep Creek.md" >}}) is the master cave for the system, but is next to impossible to access.* North [Deep Creek]({{< relref "posts/Deep Creek.md" >}}) is niiiice, but it is East [Deep Creek]({{< relref "posts/Deep Creek.md" >}}) that is undisputably the best ‘[Deep Creek]({{< relref "posts/Deep Creek.md" >}})’. All three caves are expected to resurge at Hollin Cave down on the Yarrangobilly River, along with the water from the Eagles Nest system.
