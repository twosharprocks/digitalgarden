# About
Himalayan Karst near Phobjikha Valley
- **Why:** Little-known Himalayan karst near limestone outcrops, springs, and tectonic boundaries.
- **Status:** Some hydrogeological interest but no known caving or diving expeditions.
- **Potential:** Alpine karst springs, potentially flooded systems at mid-altitude.
- **Best Season:** April to June.
