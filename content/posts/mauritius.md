# About
Chamarel Karst and Underground Streams
- **Why:** Small pockets of limestone and volcanic-limestone interface in the south of the island.
- **Status:** Some known dry caves, often shallow and inaccessible, but potential freshwater inputs remain undocumented.
- **Potential:** Small-scale tropical spring or lava-karst interfaces with pristine conditions.
- **Best Season:** May to October.
