It’s become bleedingly obvious to me that the ABC has (sadly) becoming the final bastion of quality television; Double the Fist, [awesome documentaries](https://web.archive.org/web/20131009031040/http://www.abc.net.au/tv/documentaries/stories/s2403684.htm), and highly smash-able news presenters – it really isn’t anything worth changing channel for anything else.

[![](https://web.archive.org/web/20131009031040im_/http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_2y7pNpPJmCs/SQ1lie1ReBI/AAAAAAAAAJY/HxjosoxGqtM/s400/Sky_News.jpg)](https://web.archive.org/web/20131009031040/http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_2y7pNpPJmCs/SQ1lie1ReBI/AAAAAAAAAJY/HxjosoxGqtM/s1600-h/Sky_News.jpg)Jacinta Tynan – The point ABC news peaked

Sure , I’ll admit that if my illegal download of “Heroes” or “The Mentalist” gets held up because my housemate is streaming gay porn, I might turn the tube over to Channel 7 or 9 and watch the “fast-tracked” episodes – more likely I’ll wait the extra 20 mins and watch it without ads and without having to stand up.

Thankfully though the ABC embraces people [downloading and watching their shows](https://web.archive.org/web/20131009031040/http://www.abc.net.au/tv/), and so I’ve been partaking in the great love that is government-supported television. Like some kind of internet Che Guvera, I’m out there liberating ze intarwebs of it’s precious and freely available resources while pretending to be some kind of ranga revolutionary’s revolutionary.

I really do hate all those Che-shirt wearing squeezers…

Anyway there’s a new addition to the ABC’s harem, a new character in the whorehouse of free-to-air TV

[Review with Myles Barlow](https://web.archive.org/web/20131009031040/http://www.abc.net.au/tv/review/)

David Stratton and that awful mole he  
presents with can both go to hell

It’s high time we had critical review programs informing our everyday lives, rather than analysing movies we’ll never see or books we’ll never read. Covering the full spectrum of human experience, each week Myles reviews everything from bushranging, paying for sex, to murdering the newsagent boy because he short-changed him 80 cents (change he found later in his back pocket, well after he’d buried the bodies of the victim and the two witnesses).

So watch out for Myles this Thursday night, and if you’re eager for more download the previous episodes (or in my case, download the current week’s a few hours before it screens to avoid…… all the ads…… on ABC….. whatever) or visit the Myles Barlow channel on Youtube.

Review with Myles Barlow – 5 stars
