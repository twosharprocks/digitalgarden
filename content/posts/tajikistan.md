# About
[Pamir-Alay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamir-Alay) Karst System
- **Why:** High-altitude karst with known large caves like Festivalnaya and resurgence-fed systems.
- **Status:** Explored by Russian and European dry cavers, but no known cave diving.
- **Potential:** Alpine resurgence diving and high-altitude sump exploration in untouched systems.
- **Best Season:** July to September.
