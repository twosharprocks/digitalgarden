# Cave Notes

[Vrelo Mlave]({{< relref "posts/Vrelo Mlave.md" >}}), 44.1952, 21.7864, 
- Powerful spring near Žagubica; explored to 80m depth.  
Krupajsko Vrelo, 44.2175, 21.6984, 
- Turquoise spring in Homolje region; dived to 123m depth.   
Vrelo Banje, 43.6442, 21.7228, 
- Submerged spring near Sokobanja; potential cave system.
[Resava Spring]({{< relref "posts/Resava Spring.md" >}}), 44.0708, 21.6215, 
- Karst spring below [Resavska Cave]({{< relref "posts/Resavska Cave.md" >}}); possible sump connection.  
Toplica Karst Resurgence, 43.2394, 21.3976, 
- Intermittent spring in carbonate terrain; strong seasonal flow suggests underground river.
