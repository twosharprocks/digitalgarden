# About
The **Dinaric Karst Belt** (aka **Dinaric Alps Karst** or **Dinarides Karst**) 
- One of the most extensive and well-developed karst regions in Europe, 
- Stretches along the western Balkan Peninsula. 
- Primarily associated with the **Dinaric Alps**, 

Spans the following countries:
- [Slovenia]({{< relref "posts/slovenia.md" >}})
- [Croatia]({{< relref "posts/croatia.md" >}})
- [Bosnia & Herzegovina]({{< relref "posts/Bosnia & Herzegovina.md" >}})
- [Montenegro]({{< relref "posts/montenegro.md" >}})
- [Serbia]({{< relref "posts/serbia.md" >}})
- [Albania]({{< relref "posts/albania.md" >}})
- [Kosovo]({{< relref "posts/kosovo.md" >}})
- [North Macedonia]({{< relref "posts/North Macedonia.md" >}}) - fringe southern and western zones
- [Italy]({{< relref "posts/italy.md" >}}) - northeastern edge near the Karst Plateau/Carso region, bordering Slovenia
