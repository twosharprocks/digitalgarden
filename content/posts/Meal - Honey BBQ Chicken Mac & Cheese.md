# Ingredients
- Chicken breast - 2
- Pasta - 200g
- BBQ sauce - 1/2 cup
- Honey - 1 tbsp
- Cheese - 1 cup (grated)
- Milk - 1/2 cup
- Butter - 1 tbsp
- Seasoning
	- Salt
	- Pepper
	- Paprika

# Directions
1. Cook pasta according to packet instructions
2. Season and cook chicken, then slice
3. Mix BBQ sauce and honey, coat chicken
4. Melt butter, add milk and cheese to form sauce
5. Combine pasta, sauce, and chicken
6. Serve hot
