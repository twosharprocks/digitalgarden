You may have noticed it’s been a little quiet here lately.

I spent about 10 minutes writing about how busy I’ve been. Started to explain that I’ve been working and saving hard to fly back to the UK,how I managed to convince the British High Commission in Canberra to give me a 5 year Ancestry visa,and how I was systematically selling and giving away everything I owned to reduce the evidence I ever existed in Australia.

But frankly I’d be lying if I said that I haven’t had time to write. There’s been time. And there have been things I’ve wanted to write about. It’s not that I haven’t posted because I haven’t had time –I haven’t posted because I haven’t been able to summon the willpower to create something that would mildly amuse you,when I every waking moment was filled with the knowledge that I had somehow wound stuck up YET AGAIN in the world’s biggest country town. Because I truly hoped that the whole “city”would go the way of Sodom and Gomorrah,and that the DOZEN good people leaving wouldn’t turn to salt trying to look back. And because after a 10 hour a day handling hazardous radioactive samples,analysing energy spectra to determine quantitative compositions using software designed on an [operating system](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_3.11 "Seriously,Windows 3.11 - Not cool") developed by [Homo Habilis](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://www.archaeologyinfo.com/homohabilis.htm "Fuck off Lucy - it was millions of years ago,no one cares anymore"),and being paid the same as a cleaner to do it –I DIDN’T FEEL LIKE FUCKING WRITING.

I’m still struggling now –maybe I’m out of practice,but more likely it’s the remnant fugue from the last couple of months following me here. Maybe when I’m feeling better about it all I’ll make some sort of humourous post on the last couple of months in Perth,but right now I’m just glad I’ve escaped. London is far from perfect,but it’s better than being asked by your Emu Export-drinking,singlet-wearing bogan of an uncle “Can’t think of one reason you’d want to leave Perth and this beautiful weather”. You know what Uncle Steve,because you’re family I’ll make the extra effort and give you **two** reasons –1) because it’s full of people like you who achieve [self-actualisation](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/?attachment_id=979 "Maslow makes everyone his bitch") every time they have a bowel movement,and 2) having the complexion of [Dragon](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/?attachment_id=977 "Ehhhhh....") from [The Eiger Sanction](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0072926/ "As if Clint Eastwood could ever be a professor without murdering people") means I spontenously combust every time I step outside into your “beautiful”Perth weather.

[![](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/BurningMonk-300x222.jpg "BurningMonk")](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/BurningMonk.jpg)

BUT FEAR NOT DEAR READERS! I HAVE sucessfully escaped back to the UK,and to celebrate we’re starting new (again) –a fresh site design,new features,new post types,but unfortunately for you it’s the same Mighty Ginge. And I’ve brought my newest lemur with me! Oh,did I forget to mention I have a new furry drinking buddy? Well allow me to introduce you to the newest member of the Mighty Ginge harem –Lenny.

[![Meet Lenny - he'll eat your sister... not like that](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852im_/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/09112010392-225x300.jpg "Lenny")](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/09112010392.jpg)

Sure he’s black,but here on the Ginge we choose not to discriminate based on the colour of one’s fur (unless it suits me). Right off the bat I’d like to state that Lenny is **NOT** a replacement for [Larry](https://web.archive.org/web/20110530063852/http://www.themightyginge.com/blog/2009/08/30/larry-the-lemur/ "Damn I miss that little guy...") –he’s all together too creepy for that. The photo doesn’t do it justice,but those little yellow eyes are always watching you –ALWAYS. No matter where you are in the room or what angle you put him on,if you can see one of those eyes he’s looking right at you. And if you can’t see him,chances are he’s probably still watching you. I took him to a pub for a farewell dinner just before I left,and every woman there left feeling violated and mentally undressed. And he didn’t even come out of the bag.

So while Larry was a big fan of the ladies,Lenny is more likely to cut up said ladies and store them in his freezer. And before you ask –yes,he’ll be making contributions to the Mighty Ginge.

I’m not making promises on scheduled updates,but for now lets say there will be plenty to follow. The fog is lifting people,and it turns out there was a creepy black and white lemur watching you with thermal goggles through it the whole time.
