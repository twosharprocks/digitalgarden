About 4 months ago I got a phone call from the folks at 20th Century Fox: “Can we lock you in a small, purpose-built Mars habitat in the middle of a city and have you pretend to be Mark Watney for 5 days?”

I said yes.

Now that the mini-site www.bringhimhome.com.au is live, I can finally share with you all what I’ve been working on all this time but had to keep quiet. To mark the release of “The Martian” on Blu-ray, DVD and Digital HD; from this Monday I’ll be living inside a mock Mars habitat built on Sydney’s circular quay for 5 days, surviving in isolation through a variety of science & engineering challenges.

But I’m not totally alone – you’ll all be able to help me via Twitter (@BringHimHome) and through the Facebook Page (Bringhimhome), as well as watching via the 24 hour live webcast… or if you’re in Sydney by coming down to my little glass habitat and watching what 20th Century Fox can throw at me after asking Australia and the world’s space experts what a real life Mark Watney would have to endure.

Hopefully I’ll have time before I leave my role as a Teaching Associate at SHSSP16 to write about what I’m expecting from it all, and you’re sure to be seeing it ALL over your TV’s and hearing it on the radio… but in the meantime make sure you check all the details at the website or the Timeout article.

Told you it’d be one hell of a year 😉
