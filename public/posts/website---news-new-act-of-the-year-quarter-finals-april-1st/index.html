After jumping on stage back in December to recount the horror of attending a reunion at my baptist high school,and then promoting a theory that God got bored and started making stoner monkeys on the 8th day (fuck you,sloths –any animal that moves slow enough to have moss grow on it deserves to be endangered) –in what can only be explained by some sort of administrative cock-up I’ve been mistakenly selected to compete in the quarter finals of the Laughing Horse New Act of the Year competition.

So while I’ll be proudly running around and calling myself a quarter finalist (and intend to shamelessly plaster it over all future advertising material),I’d really like to change that “quarter”to a “semi”. And giving me a semi is where you come in.

Ironically,the best way to treat an inappropriate boner is to type
“inappropriate boner”into Google with safe search off

Winners of this round are chosen by the audience –so if you find me funny,mildly amusing,confusing,vaguely threatening (or just feel tragically sorry for me that I need to feed my ego standing in front of a bunch of strangers being obnoxious) get to the Goat Tavern in Green Park this Friday (April 1st) for 8pm. I’m writing a fresh set at the moment for it and praying that everyone will be as talent-less as I am. For more information on the competition,visit the Laughing Horse website

I’ll also be announcing UK tour dates for my one-man show “Apocalypse Meow –Doomsday for Dummies with Josh Richards”leading up to two performances at this year’s Edinburgh Fringe festival on August 17th and 18th. So be prepared for an Armageddon near you soon,UK Ginge fans.
