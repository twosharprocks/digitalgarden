Additional Notes#

The entry is spectacular. Dive into this system as if it were the entrance to an Indonesian temple. The ground is covered with snow-white sand. The sidewalls run together like a pyramid. After a short swim you emerge into a huge dry cave. Like all good temples the secret passage between cavern and cave is hidden and unmarked.

This dive is especially worthwhile if you join in the tour of the dry cave.

MAX. DEPTH: 15 Meters

This system is challenging. Depths change quickly and often. Good buoyancy is required and knowledge of personal weighting is paramount to handle regular variants in water density. Sidemount systems are preferred in order to pass restrictions and easily walk from sump to sump.

EQUIPMENT CONFIGURATION: Backmount and Sidemount

GASES: Compressed Air and Nitrox

EXPLORATION: Routes were discovered during an expedition in collaboration with a speleologist group from Brunei.

  • InDepth - GUE: https://gue.com/blog/charting-sardinias-grotta-del-fico/

  • SKEP: https://protecsardinia.com/project-grotta-del-fico/?lang=en

The mountain massif called “Supramonte” is located on the east coast of Sardinia. With a summit height of 1463 m and an extension of approx. 50 square km the largest limestone area in Sardinia. Due to the strong karstification of this region, all surface water disappears in rugged mountain gorges to finally collect in the numerous underground cave systems and exit again in karst springs or flow into the sea. The cave “Grotta del Fico” is part of this subterranean system and it can be assumed that it plays an important role in the water balance of the Supramonte due to its enormous size.

Since the entrance to the cave is under water directly in front of the cliffs in the Mediterranean Sea, the entire logistics can only be carried out by water or by boat. By exploring the cave “Grotta del Fico”, this exploration can provide an important building block in the exploration of the mighty Supramonte karst system and contribute significantly to the understanding of the underwater household and the history of the formation of the “Supramonte” mountains.
