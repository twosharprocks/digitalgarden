Mini Essay - Using my folding keyboard while flying from Melbourne to Santiago

#mini-essay  #writing 

I’ve just started my journey to Antarctica, or more accurately I’ve started the first overseas step to Antarctica with the flight from Melbourne to Santiago, and I’m getting used to working on the small Bluetooth keyboard I bought to avoid my laptop. This trip will be the first major test of this, not only learning to use a small form keyboard like this, but also the experience of travelling without a laptop - something I haven’t done since I left the marines.

Already I’m seeing the benefit of shifting to somesting smaller and electronic. -There is simply no way I’d be typing on my laptop right now, as I’d be concerned about power usage, turbulence, and that lunch is likely to be served in the near future so I’d need to find somewhere to tuck the laptop during that. Likewise, there is absolutely no way I could be writing in a journal right now, as the turbulence we currently have would easily put me off as I tried to scrawl on the page.

I won’t pretend that this small form keyboard isn’t a challenge, although I seem to be getting a bit of a feel for it and I appreciate that my spellchecker is correcting the obvious typos I’m still making as I adjust. The biggest hesitation I’m currently experiencing while typing is the apostrophe as I try to type things like “aren’t” and “I’m” while being forced to use the function key instead of simply shift. I get that it’s a space-saving element that allows the L & ; keys to do double service with ’ & " alts respectively, but it’ s still awkward and a clear disruption to the flow that I can feel myself getting into when I don’t need to use an apostrophe (stumbling over “don’t” just then is another example).

The apostrophe issue aside though, I particularly love that I haven’t needed to bring a large device with me for this trip - everything I need can be handled by my phone. This keyboard has felt like a waste previously given I also like to journal, and the only time I’d like to go somewhere without a laptop is a local Cafe or travelling, but it’s exciting to consider that I’ll be doing this trip from a small mobile device instead. I’m going to Antarctica, and I don’t need a laptop, or a full size camera, or very much at all. If I weren’t diving in Ushuaia then I likely would have left the GoPro behind too, although I suspect that will do plenty of work while kayaking too.

My laptop does a wonderful job, and I have no doubt it’ll be vital for work and other trips in the future. But for a journey such as this where I don’t need to review data or anything else that requires screen real estate, then this folding keyboard is going to be perfect and it’ll encourage me to write when I otherwise might now. And anything that supports me writing more than I have in the past is something to be valued and endorsed.
