Maxwell’s Demon Strikes Back as Quantum Physics Unveils a Thermodynamic Loophole

#unread  #source  #personal  #physics 

No quantum exorcism for Maxwell’s demon (but it doesn’t need one). Credit: Reiko Matsushita

For over a century, the Maxwell’s Demon paradox has haunted physics. This thought experiment suggests that a tiny, energy-free “demon” could defy the second law of thermodynamics. Researchers put this to the test in a quantum setting, revealing that quantum mechanics allows surprising loopholes—though ultimately, thermodynamic balance always wins.

Researchers from Nagoya University in Japan and the Slovak Academy of Sciences have made a significant discovery about the relationship between quantum theory and thermodynamics. Their study, published in npj Quantum Information, reveals that while quantum theory does not strictly forbid violations of the second law of thermodynamics, quantum processes can still operate without actually breaking the law. This finding demonstrates that quantum mechanics and thermodynamics, despite being logically independent, can coexist in a complementary way.

Meal - Air Fryer Chicken Burritos

#meal  #chicken  #burrito  #air-fryer  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g
  • Rice - 2 cups cooked
  • Tortillas - 4
  • Cheese - 1 cup
  • Beans - 1 can
  • Salsa - 1/2 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook and season chicken, then shred
  2. Mix with rice, beans and salsa
  3. Fill tortillas and roll tightly
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 10–12 mins until crispy

Macros

  • Calories - 610
  • Protein - 48g
  • Carbs - 55g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - Air Fryer Nachos

#meal  #snack  #air-fryer  #quick  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips - 150–200g
  • Cheese - 1.5–2 cups (cheddar or blend)
  • Sweet corn - 1/2 cup
  • Bell peppers - 1 cup (chopped)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Jalapeños (optional)

Seasoning

  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes
  • Salt (optional)

Optional Add-ons

  • Cooked beef or chicken
  • Beans
  • Sour cream
  • Guacamole
  • Salsa

Directions

  1. Line air fryer basket with parchment paper
  2. Add a layer of tortilla chips
  3. Top with vegetables, corn, and cheese
  4. Repeat layers if needed (chips → toppings → cheese)
  5. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes
  6. Air fry at 180°C for 5–7 minutes until cheese melts
  7. Remove and add optional toppings (sour cream, salsa, etc.)
  8. Serve immediately

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 30g
