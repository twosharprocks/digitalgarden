Murra El Elevyn - 6N47

#diving  #caving 

About

  • A ~300 m main tunnel links to two phreatic networks: one half-filled and one fully flooded. The NE passage (“60 m line”) departs the north side of a large rock; it’s classed Advanced Cave. Locally named Gary’s Back Passage and a “shortcut” from the entrance lake into the main tunnel are also treated as Advanced Cave for training. (cavedivers.com.au)

  • Walls show rich scalloping/phreatic morphologies; photographers consistently report very clear water with red-to-pink limestone tones. (lizrogersphotography.com)

Nacho Beef Folded Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water
  • 4 large tortilla wraps
  • 1½ cups grated cheddar
  • 1 cup crushed nacho chips
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup diced tomatoes
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or butter

Directions

  1. Brown the beef, drain excess fat, then add taco seasoning and water and simmer until thick.
  2. Warm the tortillas and cut one slit from the centre to the edge.
  3. Divide cheese, beef, crushed chips, lettuce, tomato and sour cream between the four tortilla quarters.
  4. Fold each quarter over the next to make a layered triangle.
  5. Toast in oil or butter for 2–3 minutes per side until crisp.

Nacho Cheese Beef Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1kg lean beef mince
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 130g low-sugar ketchup
  • 150ml water
  • 230ml light evaporated milk
  • 180g Mexican cheese blend
  • 30ml hot sauce
  • 6 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with onion and capsicum.
  2. Add taco seasoning, ketchup and water, then simmer until thick.
  3. Warm evaporated milk gently, add cheese and hot sauce, and stir until smooth.
  4. Divide beef and cheese sauce between tortillas.
  5. Roll into burritos and toast seam-side down.
