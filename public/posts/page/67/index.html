Meal - No-Bake Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Bars

#meal  #dessert  #no-bake  #chocolate  #snack 

Ingredients

Cookie Dough Base

  • Butter - 1/2 cup (softened)
  • Brown sugar - 3/4 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Milk - 1/3 cup
  • Flour (heat-treated or oat flour) - 2 cups
  • Salt - pinch
  • Chocolate chips - 1 cup

Chocolate Topping

  • Chocolate chips - 1/2 cup
  • Butter or coconut oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. In a bowl, mix softened butter and brown sugar until smooth
  2. Add vanilla and milk, mixing well
  3. Stir in flour and salt until a dough forms
  4. Fold in chocolate chips
  5. Press mixture evenly into a lined baking dish
  6. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth
  7. Pour chocolate over the top and spread evenly
  8. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until firm
  9. Slice into bars and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 5g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • No-bake and egg-free → safe-to-eat cookie dough style :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
  • Chill time is essential for firm texture
  • Can substitute oat/almond flour for a healthier version

Meal - Paprika Chicken & Chorizo Gnocchi

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Olive oil - 1/2 tbsp
  • Chorizo - 70g
  • Red onion - 1 small
  • Chicken breast - 350g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (crushed)
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook the chorizo until it starts to crisp
  2. Add the chopped red onion and cook until softened
  3. Add the chicken breast, season with paprika, salt and pepper, and cook until coloured
  4. Stir through the garlic, tomato puree and chopped sun dried tomatoes
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and add the gnocchi
  6. Simmer until the gnocchi is tender and the sauce reduces slightly
  7. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  8. Fold through the spinach until wilted
  9. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 562 per serving
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 52g
  • Fat - 16g

Notes

  • Macros are estimated from the ingredient amounts shown in the reel

Meal - Pepper Jack Chicken Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #chicken  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 900g chicken breast
  • Taco seasoning
  • 200g reduced-fat cheddar
  • 200g reduced-fat mozzarella
  • 14 slices centre-cut bacon, cooked
  • 1 can diced green chillies
  • 8 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken, then chop or shred it.
  2. Combine chicken, bacon, green chillies, cheddar and mozzarella.
  3. Divide between warmed tortillas and roll into burritos.
  4. Toast seam-side down until sealed and golden.
  5. Wrap and refrigerate or freeze extras for meal prep.

Meal - Pizza Cupcakes

#meal  #snack  #pizza  #appetizer  #quick  #oven  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Biscuit dough (canned) - 1 can
  • Pizza sauce - 1/4 cup
  • Mozzarella cheese - 1/2 cup (shredded)
  • Pepperoni - 8–10 slices
  • Butter - 2 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 1 tsp (minced)
  • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Flatten biscuit dough and press into muffin tray to form cups
  2. Add pizza sauce, cheese, and pepperoni into each cup
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic and brush over dough edges
  4. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning
  5. Bake at 180°C for 12–15 minutes until golden
  6. Remove and serve warm

Macros

  • Calories - 350
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 30g
  • Fat - 20g

Notes

  • Previously misclassified as a chicken rice bowl — corrected based on reel content
  • More of a snack/appetizer than a main meal

Meal - Smash Dumpling Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g pork mince
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • Handful of coriander, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ginger paste
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp black or rice vinegar
  • Black pepper
  • 12 mini tortillas
  • Olive oil
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Dumpling dipping sauce, chilli oil and sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Combine pork, most of the spring onion and coriander, garlic, ginger, soy, sesame oil, fish sauce, vinegar and pepper.
  2. Toss cucumber with rice vinegar and salt.
  3. Spread a thin layer of pork filling over one side of each tortilla.
  4. Fry meat-side down in an oiled pan for about 4 minutes, then flip and crisp the tortilla for 1 minute.
  5. Top with cucumber, reserved herbs, sesame seeds and dipping sauce or chilli oil.
