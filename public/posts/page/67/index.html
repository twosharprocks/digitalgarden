Meal - Smash Dumpling Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g pork mince
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • Handful of coriander, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ginger paste
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp black or rice vinegar
  • Black pepper
  • 12 mini tortillas
  • Olive oil
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Dumpling dipping sauce, chilli oil and sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Combine pork, most of the spring onion and coriander, garlic, ginger, soy, sesame oil, fish sauce, vinegar and pepper.
  2. Toss cucumber with rice vinegar and salt.
  3. Spread a thin layer of pork filling over one side of each tortilla.
  4. Fry meat-side down in an oiled pan for about 4 minutes, then flip and crisp the tortilla for 1 minute.
  5. Top with cucumber, reserved herbs, sesame seeds and dipping sauce or chilli oil.

#meal  #beef  #bowl  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • 93/7 Ground beef - 75g cooked
  • Reduced fat fiesta cheese - 28g
  • Light sour cream - 30g
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Burger sauce - to taste
  • Seasoning
    • Onion powder
    • Garlic powder
    • Paprika
    • Cayenne
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Slice the potatoes into thin rounds and roast or air fry until tender and crisp
  2. Cook the ground beef and season with onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, salt and pepper
  3. Layer the smashed or roasted potatoes into a bowl
  4. Top with the cooked beef, cheese, pickles, sour cream and burger sauce
  5. Serve hot

Meal - Smokey Bacon Gnocchi

#meal  #gnocchi  #high-protein  #one-pan  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Bacon - 5–6 rashers (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef or chicken - 400–800g (optional high-protein boost)
  • Zucchini - 1–2 (diced)
  • Tomato sauce (tinned or passata) - 400–500g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Smoked paprika

Directions

  1. Fry chopped bacon in a large pan until crispy, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook garlic and ground meat (if using) until browned
  3. Add zucchini and cook until softened
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and smoked paprika
  5. Add gnocchi and simmer until tender and sauce thickens
  6. Return bacon to the pan and mix through
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 16g

Notes

  • Confirmed structure from indexed sources (gnocchi + bacon + tomato base)
  • Often presented as a high-protein, meal-prep friendly dish
  • Variations include adding cheese or baking for a crispy top

Meal - Spicy Peanut Dumplings

#meal  #spicy  #quick  #oven 

Ingredients

  • Frozen dumplings - 12 to 16
  • Spring onion - 2 sprigs
  • Peanut butter - 2 tbsp
  • Red curry paste - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 1 tsp minced
  • Ginger - 1 tsp minced
  • Soy sauce - 1 tbsp
  • Sesame oil - 1 tsp
  • Coconut milk - 1/2 cup
  • Chicken stock - 1/2 cup

Directions

  1. In a baking dish, mix together the peanut butter, red curry paste, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, coconut milk and chicken stock
  2. Add the frozen dumplings to the sauce
  3. Bake until the dumplings are cooked through and the sauce has thickened
  4. Top with sliced spring onion and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 360 per serving
  • Protein - 13g
  • Carbs - 36g
  • Fat - 18g

Notes

  • Macros are estimated from a 4-serving batch
