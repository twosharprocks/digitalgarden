Drik Drik Fault Scarp

#diving  #caving  #victoria 

Details

Identification

  • Feature type: Depression

Access and warnings

Private

Additional Notes

Locality: 7121-253912. Five kilometres south of Drik Drik. Access: Private road off the Nelson-Winnap Road. Geology/Geomorphology: In the vicinity of the Drik Drik fault scarp are a group of seven nested dolines developed in Bridgewater Formation dune limestones. These dolines are generally blind and exhibit a regular inverted cone morphology. One of the dolines has a 25 metre shaft that terminates at a stream. Other sinkholes are found nearby. Significance: State. Nested dolines are rare in the state; the only similar dolines in Victoria are found in the Buchan area.
