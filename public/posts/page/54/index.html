Meal - Chicken Alfredo Tortellini

#meal  #chicken  #tortellini  #pasta  #creamy  #one-pan  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 1 lb
  • Butter - 2 tbsp
  • Garlic - 1 tbsp minced
  • Frozen tortellini - 19 oz bag
  • Alfredo sauce - 15 oz jar
  • Mozzarella - 1 1/2 cups shredded
  • Bacon - 6 pieces
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Onion powder
    • Paprika
    • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp, then set aside
  2. Season the chicken and cook in the butter until browned and cooked through
  3. Add the garlic and cook briefly until fragrant
  4. Add the frozen tortellini and Alfredo sauce and stir to combine
  5. Simmer until the tortellini is cooked through
  6. Stir through the mozzarella until melted
  7. Crumble the bacon over the top and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 552 per serving
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 29g
  • Fat - 30g

Meal - Chicken Bacon Ranch Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #chicken  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1.3kg chicken breast
  • Taco seasoning
  • 500g low-fat cottage cheese
  • 2 tbsp ranch seasoning
  • 1 tsp chipotle in adobo
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 45ml light mayonnaise
  • 200g light mozzarella
  • 8 bacon rashers, cooked and diced
  • Chives
  • 1kg cooked rice
  • 10 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken, then dice it.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, ranch seasoning, chipotle, lime, garlic and mayonnaise until smooth.
  3. Combine chicken with the ranch sauce, mozzarella, bacon and chives.
  4. Warm each tortilla, add rice and chicken filling, then roll tightly.
  5. Toast immediately or wrap and freeze for later.

Meal - Chicken Gnocchi Soup

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #soup  #high-protein  #one-pot 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Gnocchi - 400g
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken in a pot until lightly browned, then remove and chop if needed
  2. Add the tomato puree and sun dried tomatoes and cook briefly
  3. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  4. Add the gnocchi and cook until tender
  5. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  6. Return the chicken to the pot and add the spinach
  7. Simmer gently until the spinach wilts and the soup is hot throughout
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 490 per serving
  • Protein - 52g
  • Carbs - 48g
  • Fat - 10g

Meal - Chicken Parm Baked Wraps

#meal  #wrap  #chicken 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast, diced
  • Italian seasoning
  • Garlic powder
  • Tomato passata or marinara sauce
  • Grated mozzarella
  • Grated parmesan
  • Large tortilla wraps
  • Olive oil spray

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken until browned and cooked through.
  2. Stir through enough marinara to coat the chicken.
  3. Divide the chicken between tortillas and top with mozzarella and parmesan.
  4. Fold into wraps and place seam-side down on a lined tray.
  5. Spray lightly with oil and bake until crisp and the cheese has melted.

Meal - Chicken Pesto Flatbread

#meal  #chicken  #flatbread  #quick 

Ingredients

  • Flatbread - 2
  • Chicken breast - 300g
  • Pesto - 2 tbsp
  • Mozzarella - 1/2 cup
  • Spinach - 1 cup

Directions

  1. Cook chicken and slice
  2. Spread pesto on flatbread
  3. Add chicken, cheese and spinach
  4. Bake until crispy

  • Protein - 35g
