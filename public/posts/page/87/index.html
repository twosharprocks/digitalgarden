About

  • Algarve coastal sea caves:
    Best between June and September, when visibility is highest and seas are calm. This is when marine traffic is highest too, so early or late in this period is best for quieter exploration.
  • Inland karst systems (e.g., Mira de Aire, Serras de Aire e Candeeiros):
    Access is best in spring and autumn (April–June, September–October) when groundwater levels are manageable and temperatures mild. Winter rains can flood systems, and summer can see restrictions due to fire risk or access limits.
  • Water Temperatures: Range from 14–26°C depending on depth and region, so a 5–7mm wetsuit or drysuit is advised for inland cave systems, even in summer.
  • Local Festivals & Holidays: Avoid major holiday periods (especially August) to reduce crowds and booking difficulties.
