Meal - Honey Soy Chicken Stir Fry

#meal  #chicken  #stir-fry  #quick 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 400g
  • Mixed vegetables - 2 cups
  • Soy sauce - 2 tbsp
  • Honey - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 2 cloves
  • Rice - 2 cups cooked

Directions

  1. Cook chicken until browned
  2. Add vegetables and garlic
  3. Stir in soy sauce and honey
  4. Serve over rice

Meal - Lasagne Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • 1 tortilla wrap
  • 4 tbsp bolognese
  • 1 fresh lasagne sheet, or a dried sheet cooked for 5 minutes
  • 2 tbsp béchamel sauce
  • Handful of grated cheese and mozzarella

Directions

  1. Place the wrap flat and layer 2 tbsp bolognese, half the lasagne sheet, 1 tbsp béchamel and cheese.
  2. Repeat the layers once.
  3. Fold into a burrito and wrap it in foil.
  4. Air-fry at 180°C for 10 minutes.

Meal - Loaded Breakfast Wrap

#meal  #breakfast  #wrap  #high-protein  #quick 

Ingredients

  • Large wraps - 2
  • Eggs - 4
  • Bacon - 4 rashers
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Hash browns - 2
  • Spinach or greens (optional)
  • Sauce (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook bacon until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook hash browns until golden
  3. Scramble eggs in the same pan
  4. Layer eggs, bacon, hash browns, cheese, and greens into wraps
  5. Fold tightly into burrito shape
  6. Toast in pan until golden and crispy on the outside
  7. Slice and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 32g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 26g

Meal - Nacho Beef Folded Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water
  • 4 large tortilla wraps
  • 1½ cups grated cheddar
  • 1 cup crushed nacho chips
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup diced tomatoes
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or butter

Directions

  1. Brown the beef, drain excess fat, then add taco seasoning and water and simmer until thick.
  2. Warm the tortillas and cut one slit from the centre to the edge.
  3. Divide cheese, beef, crushed chips, lettuce, tomato and sour cream between the four tortilla quarters.
  4. Fold each quarter over the next to make a layered triangle.
  5. Toast in oil or butter for 2–3 minutes per side until crisp.

Meal - Nacho Cheese Beef Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1kg lean beef mince
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 130g low-sugar ketchup
  • 150ml water
  • 230ml light evaporated milk
  • 180g Mexican cheese blend
  • 30ml hot sauce
  • 6 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with onion and capsicum.
  2. Add taco seasoning, ketchup and water, then simmer until thick.
  3. Warm evaporated milk gently, add cheese and hot sauce, and stir until smooth.
  4. Divide beef and cheese sauce between tortillas.
  5. Roll into burritos and toast seam-side down.
