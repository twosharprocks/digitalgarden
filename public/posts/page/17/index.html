Air Fryer Chicken Burritos

#meal  #chicken  #burrito  #air-fryer  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g
  • Rice - 2 cups cooked
  • Tortillas - 4
  • Cheese - 1 cup
  • Beans - 1 can
  • Salsa - 1/2 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook and season chicken, then shred
  2. Mix with rice, beans and salsa
  3. Fill tortillas and roll tightly
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 10–12 mins until crispy

Macros

  • Calories - 610
  • Protein - 48g
  • Carbs - 55g
  • Fat - 20g

Air Fryer Nachos

#meal  #snack  #nachos  #air-fryer  #quick 

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips - 150–200g
  • Cheese - 1.5–2 cups (cheddar or blend)
  • Sweet corn - 1/2 cup
  • Bell peppers - 1 cup (chopped)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Jalapeños (optional)

Seasoning

  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes
  • Salt (optional)

Optional Add-ons

  • Cooked beef or chicken
  • Beans
  • Sour cream
  • Guacamole
  • Salsa

Directions

  1. Line air fryer basket with parchment paper
  2. Add a layer of tortilla chips
  3. Top with vegetables, corn, and cheese
  4. Repeat layers if needed (chips → toppings → cheese)
  5. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes
  6. Air fry at 180°C for 5–7 minutes until cheese melts
  7. Remove and add optional toppings (sour cream, salsa, etc.)
  8. Serve immediately

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 30g

Akiyoshidai

#diving 

About

From https://tech-diving.jp/akiyoshidai

What is Akiyoshidai? Akiyoshidai is Japan’s largest karst plateau , located in the central and eastern parts of Mine City, Yamaguchi Prefecture. Its limestone distribution covers approximately 93 km², a scale unparalleled anywhere else in Japan.

More than 400 limestone caves have been identified in Akiyoshidai, including Akiyoshido Cave , one of Japan’s three largest limestone caves and a designated special natural monument.

Akiyoshidai is divided into two plateaus, Higashidai and Nishidai, by the Higashikawa River . Higashidai (Akiyoshidai in the narrow sense) was designated a national park (Akiyoshidai National Park) in 1955 and a special natural monument in 1964. In addition, individual caves are registered as natural monuments, and meticulous care is taken in their maintenance and management.
