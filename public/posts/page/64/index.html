Meal - High Protein Hot Honey Popcorn Chicken

#meal  #chicken  #high-protein  #air-fryer  #meal-prep  #spicy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (cubed)
  • Potatoes - 400–500g (diced or frozen cubes)
  • Greek yogurt - 150g
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray

Hot Honey Sauce

  • Honey - 2 tbsp
  • Hot sauce - 1 tbsp
  • Chilli flakes (optional)

Directions

  1. Coat diced chicken in Greek yogurt and seasoning
  2. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  3. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  4. Cook potatoes until golden and crispy (air fryer or oven)
  5. Mix honey and hot sauce to create hot honey glaze
  6. Toss cooked chicken in hot honey sauce
  7. Serve with crispy potatoes

Macros

  • Calories - 495
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 12g

Notes

  • Confirmed from reel caption and structure :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • High-protein “fakeaway” style meal
  • Very similar structure to KFC popcorn chicken bowls

Meal - High Protein KFC Chicken Bowl

#meal  #chicken  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (cubed)
  • Potatoes - 500g (diced or frozen potato cubes)
  • Corn - 1 cup
  • Gravy - 1/2 cup
  • Greek yogurt - 150g (for coating)
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Coat diced chicken in Greek yogurt and seasoning
  2. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  3. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  4. Cook potatoes (air fryer or oven) until golden
  5. Heat corn and gravy
  6. Assemble bowl with potatoes, corn, crispy chicken, and gravy on top
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 55g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 12g

Notes

  • Based on KFC “Famous Bowl” style structure
  • Reel explicitly frames this as a high-protein alternative (~60g protein) :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Good for meal prep and bulk cooking

Meal - High Protein Mexican Bowl

#meal  #beef  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Rice - 200g (uncooked, portioned ~50g per serve)
  • Extra lean beef mince - 500g
  • Tinned tomatoes - 400g
  • Taco seasoning - 1 packet (or ~20g)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (optional)
  • Oil spray (minimal)

Optional Add-ons

  • Greek yogurt (as sour cream substitute)
  • Lettuce
  • Cheese (light)
  • Salsa or hot sauce

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions and set aside
  2. In a pan, cook onion and garlic until softened (optional)
  3. Add lean beef mince and cook until browned
  4. Stir in taco seasoning and tinned tomatoes
  5. Simmer until thickened and well combined
  6. Portion rice into containers or bowls
  7. Top with beef mixture and optional extras
  8. Serve or store for meal prep

Macros

  • Calories - 480
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 59g
  • Fat - 9g

Notes

  • Macros confirmed from reel caption :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Designed as a high-protein, low-fat meal prep option
  • Very scalable and easy to batch cook

Meal - Honey BBQ Chicken Mac & Cheese

#meal  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 2
  • Pasta - 200g
  • BBQ sauce - 1/2 cup
  • Honey - 1 tbsp
  • Cheese - 1 cup (grated)
  • Milk - 1/2 cup
  • Butter - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook pasta according to packet instructions
  2. Season and cook chicken, then slice
  3. Mix BBQ sauce and honey, coat chicken
  4. Melt butter, add milk and cheese to form sauce
  5. Combine pasta, sauce, and chicken
  6. Serve hot

Meal - Honey Garlic Chicken Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #oven  #to-try 

Ingredients

Chicken

  • Diced boneless & skinless chicken breast - 1.05kg (raw weight)
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 35ml
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Potato starch - 100g
    • Approx. 40g adheres to chicken
  • Seasoning
    • Black pepper - 1/2 tsp
    • Onion powder - 1 tsp
    • Garlic powder - 1 tsp

Fried Rice

  • Frozen mixed vegetables - 500g
  • Eggs - 4
  • Egg whites - 200g
    • Or 5 whole eggs total
  • Spring onion / scallions (white part only) - 30g
  • Day old cooked white rice - 980g
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 40ml
  • Spring onion / scallions (green part only) - 20g

Honey Garlic Sauce

  • Garlic - 5 cloves (minced)
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 60ml
  • Water - 60ml
  • Reduced sugar ketchup - 100g
  • Rice wine vinegar - 25ml
  • Cornflour - 1 tsp
  • Water - 80ml
  • Honey - 80g

Directions

  1. Place diced chicken in a bowl and combine with 35ml soy sauce, minced garlic, black pepper, onion powder and garlic powder
  2. Coat chicken with potato starch until evenly covered
  3. Cook chicken until crispy and cooked through (oven or air fryer)
  4. Prepare honey garlic sauce by combining garlic, soy sauce, water, ketchup, rice wine vinegar, cornflour slurry and honey
  5. Heat sauce gently until thickened
  6. In a large pan or tray, cook mixed vegetables with the white parts of the spring onion
  7. Add eggs and egg whites and cook until scrambled
  8. Add day-old rice and 40ml soy sauce, mixing thoroughly
  9. Fold through crispy chicken and green spring onion
  10. Pour over honey garlic sauce and mix until evenly coated
  11. Portion into 7 servings and serve

Notes

  • Caption-faithful reconstruction from reel ingredients
  • Uses potato starch to create crispy chicken coating
  • Day-old rice improves texture and prevents clumping
  • Designed as a high-protein meal prep recipe
