Meal - Stuffed Crust Pizza

#meal  #oven 

Ingredients

  • Tortillas - 2
  • Cream cheese - 60g
  • Sausage - 100g cooked
  • Mozzarella - 120g
  • Pizza sauce - 60g
  • Pizza toppings - as desired
  • Seasoning
    • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Lightly crisp the tortillas so they hold their shape
  2. Spread cream cheese, sausage and some mozzarella around the outer edge between the two tortillas to create the stuffed crust
  3. Add pizza sauce to the centre
  4. Top with the remaining mozzarella and your preferred toppings
  5. Bake for about 20 minutes until golden and bubbling
  6. Slice and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 540 per serving
  • Protein - 29g
  • Carbs - 29g
  • Fat - 34g

Notes

  • Macros are estimated because the reel description does not provide exact weights for every topping

Mini Essay - ‘Surely You’re Joking Mr Feynman’ (Richard Feynman)

#mini-essay  #books  #writing 

During your walk this morning you finished “Surely Your Joking Mr Feynman”, and it’s been an interesting audiobook to listen to.

When I first started it, I was utterly convinced that it was absolutely brilliant - that Feynman was really connecting with that passion for the universe that I crave, that he had plenty of character and quirks that made him an engaging personality who genuinely loved learning about the universe. I’m sure that’s all true, but the further into the book I got the less I liked him - the whole thing started to feel like a collection of little stories from his life that didn’t build or develop into anything. Just snippets of experience and fun “cocktail event” type stories.

Mini Essay - Appeal of a dark, empty forest outpost

#mini-essay  #writing 

For years now there’s been something drawing you to the quiet darkness of an otherwise empty forest outpost. There are critical elements to this interest though which are non-negotiable - it they’re not present, then the entire vibe is thrown off and it doesn’t work.

First and foremost is rain sounds. You knew the moment you heard the sounds of rain as the ship passed Saturn in Interstellar that it’s the sound that relaxes you most - the sounds that drowns out the other noise, any intrusive thoughts you might have, and never distracts you in itself. Right now you’ve got the Dark Forest Ambient Focus Music from YouTube on the other screen as you type, and the ambient synths are a fraction distracting… but the rain sounds that run underneath help make up for it.
