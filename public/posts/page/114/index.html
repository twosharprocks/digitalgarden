Threat Modelling

#cyber-security  #threat-modelling 

Methologies

PASTA - Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis Focuses on aligning considerations of business objectives with technical requirements.

STRIDE - Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, DoS, Elevation Focuses on identifying what can fail in the system being modelled.

OWASP - Open Web Application Security Project Focuses on identifying possible threats, prioritizing risks, and planning mitigation strategies. It is mainly used with web and desktop applications.

  • Determine assessment scope
  • Identify threat agents.
  • Identify potential attacks.
  • Identify exploitable vulnerabilities.
  • Prioritize identified risks.
  • Mitigate risks.

Tommy Graham’s Cave - 6N56

#diving  #caving 

To Do

Diving Dry Photogrammetry

About

Description: May have more sumps (See DiverNet article)

Where it is & what it is
On the WA Nullarbor near Cocklebiddy; entrance on the lip of a doline with a long haul down to water. Expect a dry entrance slope through breakdown to a lake, then multiple sumps and at least one intervening airspace.

  • Steep entrance → main chamber → lake → ~190 m sump through a phreatic maze → large dry chamber (~80 m) with another lake at far end. Beyond, Glacier Lake reportedly sumps ~130 m to a ~30 m dia. rock-pile chamber that’s blocked ~35 m above water (suggests a collapsed dome with potential bypasses).
