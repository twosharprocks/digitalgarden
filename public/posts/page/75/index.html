I enjoyed both, but this one was certainly a lot weirder and more entertaining. It took me awhile to figure out that the journey was just an excuse to meet weird and wonderful characters along the way, but once I recognised that it was easy to settle in and see wherever the author decided to take it. Reading it wasn’t life changing, but it was certainly a fun read.
