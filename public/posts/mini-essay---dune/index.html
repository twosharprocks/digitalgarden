Mini Essay - Dune

#mini-essay  #philosophy  #writing 

For years now I’ve felt this overwhelming connection to the Dune universe, and in many ways that connection has felt as undefinable as the series itself. Some might refer to the “vibe” of Dune being appealing, but for me there’s something more than just enjoying the aesthetic - it’s the whole body understanding that if you stop moving, if you try to stay in one place, to put up defences, to block the flow of energy… then you’ll be destroyed and swept away regardless.

So much of the Dune universe is intricate and mysterious, and yet so much of it makes intuitive sense without being explained. Maybe that’s the appeal - that it’s based on intuition and feeling, but so unbelievably technologically advanced. The technology is seamlessly built-in too, and if it’s not then it’s eventually worn down and destroyed.

All I can think about it the flow of sand and water wearing down anything that doesn’t flow with it. The Holtzman shields are a classic example of pure force being deflected and destroyed, whereas subtlety and finesse will slip through hit the goal without trying. The whole thing feels and seems very Zen, like the art of archery as you do without doing.

There is no wobbling in the Dune universe - those who freeze with indecision or attempt to resist through force are soon destroyed. Like Gandalf, characters in Dune are never early or late - they arrive exactly when they need to. This isn’t clockwork, fate or predestination either - it’s knowing yourself and knowing the universe, and surfing the circumstances in such a way that you and the universe are co-creators. You don’t resist the circumstances you find yourself in, but use them to move with the flow of the universe in a direction you do desire. Be flexible in all things, but especially in your desires, because if they do not bend or break, then you will bend and break in their place.

Dune’s appeal is in the ever moving, ever evolving, intuitive merging with the universe. It’s being like water and sand - finding the lowest and most natural path, blending with the universe as a whole, using the tools you have with subtlety and grace, and working with the wind to get where you both know you should be.
