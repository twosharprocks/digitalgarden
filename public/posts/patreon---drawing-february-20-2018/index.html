Well I finally got to start on the little monsters today…

To paraphrase Ira Glass, there’s always a gap for beginners between their amazing taste and their limited ability, and the trick is to keep going and do the volume of work needed to close the gap.

https://zenpencils.com/comic/90-ira-glass-advice-for-beginners/

It’s just that days like today make it painfully clear how wide that gap is right now.

I could come up with plenty of other excuses: I was drawing in a shopping centre café, self concious of the staff and other customers who were curious to see what the weird ginger kid was doing scribbling with a Kawaii book in front of him. There’s also a lot of stressful things going on with people I care about at the moment, and I’m also feeling generally out of balance at the moment as I question my direction and struggle to find my drive. Maybe they’re just doodles and I’m just being too hard on myself.

But ultimately it’s about seeing that gap and realising I simply haven’t done the volume of work required to draw to the ability I know I’m capable of.

So today has been a reminder to keep going. It’s easy to keep going when you can see yourself improve, things work and you have a “good day”. But when it looks shit, when you know you can do so much better and didn’t? That’s when it’s even more important than ever to keep going - to keep turning up and do the work.

I’m not going to be a professional cartoonist and don’t plan to put in “10,000 hours”. I just want to be able to express myself through writing AND drawing, and I need to keep practicing the drawing part before I feel confident enough to include my doodles in a book I really care about - a book about how signing up for a one-way mission to Mars has made me a better version of myself.
