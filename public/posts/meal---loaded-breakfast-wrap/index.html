Meal - Loaded Breakfast Wrap

Ingredients#

  • Large wraps - 2
  • Eggs - 4
  • Bacon - 4 rashers
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Hash browns - 2
  • Spinach or greens (optional)
  • Sauce (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions#

  1. Cook bacon until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook hash browns until golden
  3. Scramble eggs in the same pan
  4. Layer eggs, bacon, hash browns, cheese, and greens into wraps
  5. Fold tightly into burrito shape
  6. Toast in pan until golden and crispy on the outside
  7. Slice and serve
