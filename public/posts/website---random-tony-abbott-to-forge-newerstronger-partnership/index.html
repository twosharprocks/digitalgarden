News Updates,with the Mighty Ginge

Australian Opposition leader Tony Abbott has announced the formation of a new partnership to stem illegal immigration,as part of his on-going campaign to “Stop the Boats ™”,signing a partnership agreement with the cliffs of Christmas Island. Under the new agreement,the cliffs will continue to crush and drown any refugees attempting to make landfall in the rough seas around Christmas Island,while Abbott will strive to use future incidents to strengthen his campaign against so-called “boat people”.

Abbott was quoted at the signing “This historic agreement between party and geology is a perfect example of how politics should be done. Today we are here to form a newer,stronger partnership since the Nationals became extinct. These cliffs may have only formed 6,000 years ago with the rest of the world,but I hope they’re around for another 6,000  when I’m elected Prime-Minister. After that we’ll just bomb any boats we find. With bibles”

The cliffs of Christmas Island were unavailable for comment
