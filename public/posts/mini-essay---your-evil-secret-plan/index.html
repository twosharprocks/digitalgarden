Mini Essay - Your evil secret plan

#mini-essay  #philosophy  #personal  #writing 

Ask yourself “What is your evil secret plan?”. Ultimately this is a question about how you’re going to use the system to do the things that YOU want to do and live the life you want to live. We’re not talking about blindly trying to “Live your dreams” - we’re talking about gaming the system so it looks like you’re playing along with everyone else, but you’re hacking the game so you do exactly what you want regardless of what others may expect or even demand of you. So you’re not playing by any rules, but you are faking compliance while you put all the pieces into place to subvert expectations.

For you, it’s clear you want to explore the universe and share those experiences with others through videos and books - it’s where your interest in Jacques Cousteau comes from. Under the current paradigm you need an income to live, to travel, and to share the adventures you go on - I’m not interested in trying to subvert Capitalism, but it doesn’t mean I’m not a Communist at heart. So you need to earn money, but that’s just a tool for doing what you really want to do.

My evil secret plan is to subvert the muggle world and live like a folk legend. You want folks to say “Yeah, I met Josh Richards - he was doing some ridiculous shit and then he disappeared again”.

My evil secret plan is to never let the muggles know what I’ll do next - to have layers they’ll never touch, to have connections and stories from every corner of human experience, and to leave the people I meet wondering how the fuck I’ve done all this shit in the time I’ve had, knowing they’ll never understand.

My evil secret plan is earn enough money to turn up in all sorts of weird places whenever it suits, to carry out random acts of kindness, and to disappear again. I don’t want stability or infrastructure or predictability - I want to adapt to unexpected chaos and to keep moving forward as the universe throws new and unusual things at me.

My evil secret plan is that there is no plan - I’m just tacking my way through the flow of the universe. If I feel the wind changing, then I’ll work with it. If the drops to nothing, I’ll wait. But when it picks up again, I’ll use it - to go with it, to tack into it, or to cut across it. It doesn’t matter - what does matter is that you explore the universe and you share it with others who won’t go to the places or situations that you will.
