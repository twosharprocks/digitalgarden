About#

Identified by RK as being a site of interest.

BC 24/9/2023: Simple drop into cave about 1.5m high and 6m long. Head down the main passage to water ahead that does not immediately seem to go. Yo the right is a more promising wet section (see video). There is another small hole by the entrance that on wriggling through you get to a fissure with water.
