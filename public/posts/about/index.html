#digital-garden 

Welcome to the Digital Garden of Josh Richards.

This is a working notebook made public: part personal wiki, part field notebook, part archive, and part long-running attempt to connect ideas before they have settled into something polished.

You may be wondering what a digital garden is, or why this not-quite-blog looks like it belongs in the 90s. Maggie Appleton’s article on the history and ethos of digital gardens is a good explanation, but the short version is that this place is meant to grow in public rather than arrive fully finished.

Most notes here are connected with wiki links. Some are tiny seeds, some are more developed, and some are old posts or references kept because they still point somewhere useful.

  • Writing - Mini Essays, Articles, Books & Book - Ideas, old website posts, and related notes.
  • Diving - Diving - Reading, Diving - Ideas, Cave Regions, Trips Diving - Gear, Photogrammetry, Exploration, and field notes.
  • Cyber Security - CISSP, CISA and OSCP Study notes; cheat sheets, Threat Modelling, and Cyber Security - Resources.
  • Physics - Cosmology, quantum mechanics, Information Theory, and other deep-space rabbit holes.
  • Personal - Philosophy Bucket List, reflections, life admin, and experiments in being a person.
  • Trips - Expedition Calendar, Exploration, logistics, and place-based notes.
