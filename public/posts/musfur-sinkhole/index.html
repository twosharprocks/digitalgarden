Additional Notes#

History of The Musufur Sinkhole in Qatar The Musufur Sinkhole in Qatar occurs with a geological layer called the Upper Dammam Unit. It was formed with the water created a dissolution layer of gypsum, that led to an overlaying stratum of lime-stone to collapse eventually, like any other Karst caves in the Middle East. A layer of Dolomite fell apart with the size of the chamber underneath. It continues to evolve and will in the future as well. Pieces of rock from the dolomite and limestone layers fall and huge sections of the roof could collapse one fine day.

What Can One See In The Sinkhole Musfur? Sinkhole Musfur is one of the deepest accessible caves in Qatar. Even though the bottom is about 100m deep, most scientist believe a larger cave system extended even further. People will have to adjust their eyes in the dark as the cave is quite small, however a larger chamber opening up will be seen. One can also see:

Layering of different kinds of rock and sediments Graffiti on the rocks Hundreds of nesting birds Traces of small mammals

Things To Do In Musfur Sinkhole One of the many things to do in Qatar is to scramble down into the cave. One can wear normal shoes while walking around the cave, but will expect a lot of sand in their shoes. One of the many activities people can enjoy is rock- climbing or bouldering, one of the adventure activities in Qatar. There are a few boulder issues that will keep you occupied for a while. Visitors have on various occasions used the cool environment of the sinkhole to meditate or do Yoga.

Things To Know About The Sinkhole The sinkhole has a chain- link fence around it but is open to visitors via a small gate. There aren’t any steps in the hole, but the ground is steady for a descent. Once the climb down starts, the temperature starts to drop, suggested a much-needed break from the heat of the desert. When it comes to getting away from the treaded heat, the sinkhole is your best spot. There are certain things that people should know about the Sinkhole, which include:

  1. No body of water to swim in at the bottom of the sinkhole
  2. It’s a sinkhole and not a cave, so don’t go around looking for bats hanging 3.Descending is a lot tougher than climbing up
  3. Heaven For Photographers
  4. In case your claustrophobic, don’t look down
