#mini-essay  #writing 

Where do I want to be in 5 Years time?

Dear Josh in 2031, Firstly I hope you’re still around to read this. Given the way your life has changed through the years, you’ve gone through plenty of period where you didn’t think you’d make it another 5 years. So if you’re reading this, nice work - you made it!

You’ll be 45 when you’re reading this and a about a month and a half away from your 46th birthday. Right now 46 doesn’t feel as significant at 45 does, in much the same way 40 didn’t feel as significant as I think 42 will be. You’ll have been diving for 34 years, with the last 2 of them cave diving. You’ll also have a decade of Cyber Security experience if you’re still doing it, which seems pretty uncertain right now. You’ve just started work with Alpha Echo after a bumpy 6-8 months leaving ElectraNet, going to Sulawesi and Antarctica, breaking up with Chloe, living in a Van, moving in with Simone, and asking a lot of questions about who or what you want to be.

One thing that you’ve figured out is that cyber is just a way to fund your adventures, and what you really are is an Explorer-Storyteller - someone who goes on incredible adventures, learns from them, and brings those lessons back to others through storytelling in books, speaking, comedy, video and anything else you can imagine. The most obvious way for you to push ahead right now is to put your heart and soul into writing books about things you love.

While I hope you’re doing all sorts of speaking, comedy, and even TV about exploration; the greatest hope I have by 2031 is that you’ve published at least three books - your cave diving book “517”, your anthology of Book - 101 Things, and your book Book - Calling ET. Looking at those three books, I’m simultaneously scared of how little and also how much time 5 years might be. You’re the only one who knows if I got all three done, but I also know it’s more than possible to have 5 published books before the end of my 45th year. You’ve talked about writing a book every 2 years, and yet the Book - 101 Things book has so much ready made content that you could write it much quicker if you make the commitment to. So you’ll smash out your cave diving book in 2026, put together your Book - 101 Things through 2027 before you dedicate the next 2-3 years to researching, writing, rewriting and then publishing Book - Calling ET.

Writing the Book - 101 Things will help stir you to reflect on and do more of your bucket list, and likewise I think writing Book - Calling ET will help you work through your relationship with Space Exploration too, and I hope you can find some peace when you think about the time, effort, and passion you had/have for Mars Exploration. I hope that writing these books have helped shape you into the focused and determined Explorer-Storyteller I know you are capable of being, and I hope you have been supported throughout the last 5 years to be the best and most capable version of yourself possible.

Keep being amazing and doing amazing things, and I hope you’re making us both proud with what you’re doing and where you’re going next.

Stay awesome, Josh in 2026
