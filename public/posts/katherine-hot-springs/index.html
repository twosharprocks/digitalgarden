  • Trimix Divers - https://www.trimixdivers.com/trimix5/?p=4889: This popular tourist site has 34C water flowing out of the small resurgence at a high rate. The water is crystal clear and its source is the same area of limestone as what supplies Bitter Springs and Mataranka Hot Springs further to the South. The water is geologically heated, but is warmed only by the rock in which it collects – layers of limestone which assume the average annual surface temperature. From the small entrance pool, the 3m2 cave passage descends very quickly down to 8m where it levels to horizontal and reduces in size to about 1m high by 2m wide. The effect of this reduction in passage cross sectional area is to dramatically intensify the water flow rate. The floor looks pebbly, but the rubble is cemented into fine silt and any attempt to pull on a rock simply dislodges it. The walls and ceiling are a loose, shaly type of construction and movement in this passage, against the flow is exhausting. After 20m of straight, horizontal passage, the end of the first ever penetration is found, tied off to a rock in the floor. A chromed metal clip connects the second length of guide line onto the first and the passage then slopes up to the left (SE) where a low flat restriction is encountered. Squeezing up through this restriction against very high water flow was very difficult and the second line is tied off in a wider area and small junction above the restriction.

Noting that the water was flowing from the right as well as the left hand passages, the low flow path to the right was followed and this descended through a tunnel in what seems to be denser, darker limestone. Popping through a small ‘window’ where the flow is again felt, the cave does a sharp left hand turn. 20m beyond this point, a low flat tunnel to the left was noticed that probably connects back to the junction. 10m further on and to the right, the water flows out of a low flat restriction which has not yet been penetrated. Some excavation here may be necessary. A very streamlined side mount rig with 7ltr (55cuft) tanks is recommended.
