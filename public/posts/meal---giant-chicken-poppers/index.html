Meal - Giant Chicken Poppers

Created: 2026-04-16
Updated: 2026-06-30
Status: seed
Tags: #meal #chicken #air-fryer #high-protein #meal-prep #easy
Related: Meals

Ingredients#

  • Chicken mince - 500g
  • Lite Laughing Cow Cheese - 4 wedges
  • Cornflakes - 60g
  • Greek Yogurt - 150g
  • Seasoning
    • Buffalo Chicken
    • Garlic
    • Paprika

Directions#

  1. Mix chicken mince with seasonings in a large glass bowl
  2. Cover a piece of cheese with mince mix and roll into a ball
  3. Make more balls with cheese in the middle until the mince is gone
  4. Place the balls into the freezer for 30-60 minutes
  5. Blend the cornflakes with additional seasoning
  6. Roll the chicken balls in Greek yogurt
  7. Roll the chicken balls in the cornflake and seasoning mix
  8. Spray the chicken balls with oil
  9. Cook in the air fryer for 15-20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes
