Website - Host a Bogan-free Australia Day BBQ

2012-01-23[Josh Richards]
#writing 

Task

Host a perfect Australia Day BBQ without having it spoilt by bogans.

Background

January 26th – Australia day. The day Australians collectively decide for one day of the year to mildly give a shit about anything. It’s a day we all bask in a little national pride, acknowledge some of our truly inspirational country-people, and inevitably have a BBQ. But an ever-present threat looms over every Australia Day festivity: the bogan.

This is how the world ends – not with a whimper, but with a flannel shirt

For non-Australian readers not familiar with “bogans”, they’re a sub-species of Australian that evolved from exposure to a virus that lives in the fumes produced specifically by 8 cylinder engines (known by bogan-kind as a “Vee-Aite”). Similar yet unrelated sub-species can be found in the Scotland (“Neds”), Canada (“Hosers”) the U.S. (“Rednecks”) and the U.K. (so-called “Chavs” or “B.N.P members”).

Since their initial split from the rest of humanity, bogans have developed their own culture and society – operating within the larger Australian community, but at the same time deliberately offensive to those they consider “un-Australian”. And while their herds are carefully maintained by adding chemical sterilisation agents to Victoria Bitter beer and Winfield Blue cigarettes (bogan translation: “Vee-Bee” & “Win-knee blooz”) the peak of their year-round mating season coincides with Australia Day – resulting in Australian cities being over-run by dickheads with Southern cross tattoos and wearing Australian flags as capes, that have bent a coat-hanger roughly into the shape of Australia and stuck it where their perfectly functional car’s radio antenna was.

“We’re here to make sure neither you or your tattoo reproduce, Jaydenn”

Due to their artificially boosted numbers and the bogan’s instinctual ability to sniff out a flame-grilled sausage from 2000 meters – bogan invasion is the primary threat to any Australia Day BBQ. To counter this, I’ve put together a guide to hosting the traditional Australia Day celebrations without it being spoilt by some mullet-wearing douche-bag ranting in your ear for 6 hours about “national pride” and how Pauline Hanson is a role-model for all Australian women.

To Host or Not to Host, That is the Question

Do you host your own Australia Day BBQ? If you’re not hosting, you need to ask one very important question – do you trust the actual host to screen bogans from the invite list? If you do, relax – you can just rock up with some drinks and meat to throw on the BBQ, and enjoy the day! But bogans can be incredibly sneaky at concealing their identity most of the year, with recent generations learning to infiltrate groups of friends and pass off their behaviour during the year as a “bit of harmless fun” – only to admit their true nature on Australia Day.

If your host invites anyone who;

  • Has a fresh southern cross tattoo, or has a southern cross tattoo at all

  • Arrives to the party driving any vehicle with a “Fuck off, We’re Full” sticker on it

  • Arrives to the party driving an F-Series truck or “fully-sick” ute they bought from working in the mines,

  • Uses the phrase “fully-sick”

  • Walks into the party a) with a mullet, b) wearing flannel, or c) carrying a case of “bogan beer” (eg. V.B. or XXXX)

  • Is known to rock out to AC/DC without a shred of irony

  • Is known to cry to Cold Chisel’s “Khe Sanh” or Redgum’s “I Was Only Nineteen”, even though both songs were released atleast 10 years before they were born.

  • then you may be heading to a party that’s destined to be spoilt by bogans. If the host has been known to show any of the behaviors listed above, or you pull up their address on Google Streetview only to see a car on blocks out the front – you’ve been invited to a bogan ambush.

Keep driving – Don’t look back to see if they’re following, just drive as fast as you can

If there’s any doubt about your host’s anti-bogan credentials, it’s probably safer to host it yourself. If however you apply the same criteria to yourself and discover you are a bogan, please disregard – I’d recommend turning up to a friend’s place in your flannel, trying to hit on the host with your best Chopper impersonation, getting blind drunk then driving home in your “fully-sick” V8.

Alternatively, you could stay at home with this song on repeat and re-evaluate some of your life-choices.

Hosting Essentials

So you’ve decided to host a BBQ yourself. Firstly, have you selected the correct location? If you’re considering the South Perth foreshore, I would highly suggest finding another venue – besides being the cultural Medina for all bogan-behaviour (the cultural Mecca ofcourse being Frankston, aka. Frankghanistan), you’re also liable to find yourself in the hot-zone when the fireworks barge catches fire – a spectacle I had the fortunate of witnessing in 2007 from the American Consular General’s residence in King’s Park, watching as the bogans scattered below while I ate prawns and lamb chops.

Which reminds me – serve lamb at your Australia Day BBQ, or Sam Kekovich will turn up and shoot your dog.

Provided you’ve vetted the invite list for potential bogans, picked a venue with a low bogan-hazard level, and served lamb; you’re almost there. Besides obvious things like buying all your booze and ice the week before to avoid the last-minute rush and inevitable price-hikes, your final consideration should be music. This needs to be carefully considered – playing Triple J’s Hottest 100 countdown is a national institution, but any bogan walking by your house it is likely to come bursting through the door. So instead, I’d recommend investing in a heavy-duty cricket bat – there’s a certain cruel justice in chasing a bogan out of your house with a sporting item invented by the English that bogan’s have decided to claim as their own.

Happy Australia Day Everyone
