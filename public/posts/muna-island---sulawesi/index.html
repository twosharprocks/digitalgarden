Boats from / to Muna#

Speedboats (superjet)

  • Raha to Kendari and Baubau in Buton.
  • Raha-Baubau: 2h, 150’000Rp
  • Raha-Kendari: 3h
  • Baubau-Kendari: 185’000Rp to Kendari, price is 150’000Rp to Baubau, and Baubau Kendari is 185’000Rp.
  • Daily departure from Baubau at 7AM and 12AM everyday. Should be the same from Kendari.

Ferries (operated by ASDP)

  • Passengers and vehicles.
  • To mainland Sulawesi: Board in Tampo (North Muna) to Torobulu.
  • 4 departures per day (both ways): 7AM, 10AM, 13PM, 16PM (unless on Friday only 3 departures 8AM, 13PM, 16PM).
  • The ticket is 32’000Rp per person, the trip takes 2 hours.
  • Would not recommend ferry unless you have a vehicle. 
  • superjet costs same after transport from Raha to Tampo and then Torobulu to Kendari.

Ojek (Motorcycle Taxi)

  • Raha to Tampo: 0.5hr, 50’000Rp
  • When you arrive in Torobulu, shared taxi takes passengers to Kendari for 50’000Rp (2 hours).

Accomodation#

Hotel Alia - Muna Island

  • First hotel after arriving at pier. 2 single beds and fan for 150’000Rp/night (including breakfast).
  • Other rooms with AC available.
  • Very nice owner/staff - will put you in touch with ojek drivers in Raha who will rent their motorbike for 100’000Rp/day.

Kadena Glamping Muna - Muna Island

Alam Moko Cave

  • Nearby: Danau Moko 1, Permandian Moko, & other feature La Ode Panu - Facebook & Instagram

  • Coordinates: Approximately 4°52′S, 122°37′E

  • Features: This cave has been a focal point for technical cave diving training. Divers have reported complex passageways and significant depths, making it ideal for advanced exploration. Moko Morete Cave Gua Moko Cave Danau Moko 4 - Nearby Feature

  • Waara Karst Spring, -5.3489, 122.5312, Major spring near Waara village; likely resurgence from subsurface limestone drainage.

  • Napabale Lake Underground Connection, -5.491, 122.5245, Tidal lake connected to the sea by submerged karst tunnel; highly dynamic spring and siphon system.

  • Liangkabori Cave Spring, -5.2685, 122.6267, Spring emerging near archaeological cave system; part of active karst hydrology.

  • Gua Mabolu Spring, -5.3724, 122.6503, Cave spring in dense karst area; limited documentation, strong exploration potential.

  • Lambiku Karst Resurgence, -5.3076, 122.7141, Clear resurgence near Lambiku; emerging from low limestone cliff, likely part of conduit network.

  • Goa Matano Spring, -5.4271, 122.5664, Emergent spring near cave entrance west of Matano; part of dynamic karst drainage system.

  • Goa Lakasa Spring, -5.4002, 122.5583, Cave system with submerged conduit in central Muna; possible tidal influence.

  • Goa Watu Koo, -5.2744, 122.6029, Karst cave with internal spring; flows seasonally into downstream ravine.

  • Gua Kontu Bakulu, -5.2943, 122.6847, Karst resurgence cave with pool; active flow in wet season, potential for sump passage.
