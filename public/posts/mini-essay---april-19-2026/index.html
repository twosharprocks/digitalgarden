Mini Essay - April 19 (2023)

Having just re-read Mini Essay - April 19 (2023) I realise that I don’t really know how to describe the way I feel about it anymore. That post still hits hard, and it’s a testament to how raw it was. I’m also shocked that it was published just three years ago, although I’m certain it was written years earlier. Reflecting back on things, I realise now that it’s just a date, but it’s a date that is important to me because it’s an anniversary of my choice to live.

Things could easily have been different, and I could not have experienced all the things that I’ve experienced since 2008-04-19. Becoming a diving instructor and guiding people through the Melbourne aquarium, diving the HMAS Junee, joining the Navy, joining the Royal Marines, leaving both, getting into comedy, doing the Edinburgh fringe a bundle of times, going to the Redhead festival, discovering Mars One, writing science comedy shows about going to space, writing books about going to Mars, speaking to literally hundreds of thousands of kids about space exploration. Learning to cave dive, discovering new caves and sharing them with the world, speaking at international conferences about using underwater photogrammetry in overseas caves in order to produce centimetre accurate maps on the fly, meeting Simone and feeling like I’m closer than ever to living the way I have always wanted to live.

There are so many extraordinary things that you’ve seen and done over the last 17 years, and it’s so unbelievably easy to get bogged down in the every day to lose sight of that. You are always pushing yourself, always trying to be a better version of yourself, and the reality is you’re perfect however you want to be. So many of the different things you’ve tried since 2008-04-19 have failed, and yet every step has made your life more interesting, enriched the lives of others, and weaved a tapestry of different experience together into a remarkable human being.

The greatest thing to come out of April 19 is your capacity of adventure and storytelling. You don’t want to be defined by any single thing, but you know that you have often felt your most alive when you’re on adventures and storytelling. You’re a travelling bard at the end of the day, even if you have been so many others things too. Right now I’m not entirely sure what it is I want to do with myself, or how to figure it out, but I do know that I want to be Writing every day to tell my stories, plan my next adventure, and help figure out who I want to be today.

Reflecting on what is important on days like April 19 is important, but it really is just a date - I’m not even writing this on April 19 itself, but the day before. No matter what happens though, it’s vital that I keep looking forward, keep cutting out the things that drag me down into the mud, and keep telling stories in whatever form they decide to take. You don’t need to be in the spotlight, but you do need to be creating, reviewing, editing, and polishing the stories that you tell. April 19 is just one point in your annual cycle of storytelling and adventure - the lowest point, but the point that you build up and create a new life from.
