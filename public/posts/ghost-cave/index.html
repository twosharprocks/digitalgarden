About#

Discovery#

The discovery, made during a night dive in the summer of 1994, is due to Marco Busdraghi, who then carried out the first explorations and surveys, and who disseminated knowledge of it through the press and television. Busdraghi himself later also discovered the fossil deposit of sardinian deer in the Punta Giglio cave.

Description#

The cave joint has a maximum penetration of approximately 70 meters. The different environments are characterized by an alternation of large openings and narrow passages with multiple aerial outcrops and an internal lake characterized by some “waterfalls” of very spectacular calcitic volutes. The submerged parts are particularly interesting for the modifying evolutions that occurred during the various geological eras which can be summarized in three very long distinct phases: a first phase was the typical one of the creation of caves in an aerial environment, with erosion/melting of the limestone due to the action of the waters, a second, again in an aerial environment, for the creation of numerous layers of calcite, in some parts above clay and mud deposits, and countless stalactites and stalagmites with also, thanks to the fusion of these, many joined columns, the third, more recent phase was that of “reerosion” of the cave of the submerged parts, therefore destroying all the spectacular calcite formations that have remained in the currently surface parts, both in the main lakes and in the numerous niches and outcrops. The calcite of this cave is white in color and semi-transparent, so much so that by placing a lamp in contact you obtain beautiful plays of light. In the swing line or shoreline the eroded calcite takes the resemblance of melting ice to warm water. The characteristic that makes it one of the most important caves in the Sardinia and of all the mediterranean basin in a scientific sense it is that of being a cave with Dolomite characteristics in a marine environment. Due to the particular nature of the waters, life inside, unlike almost all the other submerged caves in the area, is almost non-existent and therefore extremely clear, unless the sediment or some mud deposits scattered in various parts of the cave.
