Additional Notes#

  • Wikipedia: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cova_des_Pont

(Translated from German) “The entrance to the Cova des Pont is located 480 meters from the east coast of Mallorca at Cala Falcó within the Cales Verges de Manacor nature reserve . The next town, S’Estany d’en Mas, is 1080 meters to the north, the bay of Cala Varques 830 meters to the south and the historic fortified tower of Son Fortesa 1840 meters to the west.

The entrance to the cave, with a metal grille door, is 170 meters northwest of the path from S’Estany d’en Mas to the buildings of Can Frasquet , under tall trees amidst shrubs. From there the main room of the Cova des Pont , the great hall (Sala gran) , extends some 70 meters to the north. [4] More corridors lead east and south. The total length of the cave is 1075 meters, [5] the greatest width 40 meters. [4] About 190 meters southeast of the entrance there is a connection to the Cova des Pirata . [6] The cave entrance is 26 meters above sea level, the total height difference of the cave is 33 meters.”
