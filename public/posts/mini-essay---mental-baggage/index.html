Mini Essay - Mental Baggage

Created: 2025-05-11
Updated: 2025-10-30
Status: tree
Tags: #mini-essay #writing #knowledge-management 
Related: Mini Essays Knowledge-Management

One of my ongoing challenges is keeping track of and managing all the different things that engage my attention. This can mean my Personal, asking Mini Essay - What are your obligations, my Work, and anything else that contacts my senses and hooks my attention. While I can track a wide range of interests and passions, the real challenge for me is task-switching: being invested in one thing, but needing to keep something else in mind at the same time, or being pulled away from my focus to handle some other issue.

Recently this rapid task-switching has started to really erode my mental health, and moving to Obsidian as a Knowledge-Management system means I can keep everything in one place. Currently I still have a few other areas which I need to track, but Obsidian is quickly becoming the default for storing and organising my mental baggage. I also need to remember that I don’t need to sort it all out immediately, but I do need to contain as much here as possible. Sorting out knowledge is an ongoing process, and I really am overdue for a very long sort out which will mature into something extraordinary… but for now I’m happy with getting all my thoughts, ideas and work into one place so I’m not losing anything.

Clearing out my mental baggage is an ongoing process, and one that I really love. But for now, the most important thing is that I’m making an effort to consolidate all my knowledge into one sprawling digital garden which I can prune/cultivate/harvest as my priorities allow. Spend time collecting all your baggage in one place (eg. Obsidian) and you’ll be able to sort/shed it with time.
