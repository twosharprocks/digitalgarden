About#

Additional Notes#

(Post by Joseph Bicanic on 19-Nov-2023) I’ve set up a new pole trail at The Coombe which, when set up (line installed), mirrors the requirements for CDAA Cave and Advanced Cave Training Dives. This pole trail can also be used for Basic Cave Divers. I’m hoping this gets used and maintained so the poles have any growth removed so as to make is less likely for Divers to cut their hans on the growth. Shallowest pole is in 3 - 4 m depth in forward of the park bench (2 poles at the start - 1 at an angle) Last pole also has 2 pole, both upright. Max depth 8 - 9 m. All poles on this circuit have 50mm of orange tape almost at the top. Shallowest and Deepest poles are all orange. Others are mostly grey. Orange circles in pic indicate the poles. Green circles in pic indicate anchor points for lost line procedures. Orange curve is a restriction. I’ve placed this in the water for myself as well as anyone else wanting to practice the circuit. I’m hoping this gets a bit of use as this will reduce the build up of growth on the poles. Please help in maintain as follows: Take a scourer of some type to scrub off any growth on poles. If a pole comes out - use a weight etc to hammer it back in. If there is a problem - DM me so I can attend to it. Enjoy
