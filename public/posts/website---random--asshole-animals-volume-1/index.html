The animal kingdom is a damn diverse one, with all sorts of crazy shit flying/swimming/crawling about this blue marble we call Earth. We gape at the majesty of a soaring eagle, the grace of dolphins, or the sheer size an African elephant. But outside of these treasures of the animal kingdom, there’s a niche of animals that many find less appealing.

Because that niche is full of animals that are complete assholes.

Hyenas laugh their tits off every night because they’re letting lions do all the work, then they sneak in and steal their shit. It may be all well and good being a graceful and care-free dolphin, right up until the point a pod of Killer whales pushes you up into shallow water then leave you to die… purely for lolz.

Just like people, there are those recognised as icons of grace, beauty and power. And then there are those who like to fuck with those icons. So as part of a new series we’ll be celebrating all those animals mother nature decided to put on the naughty list, and given bad attitudes instead of fancy feathers or fur.

Honey Badgers

Watch archived videoWhat can I say that Randall from the video above hasn’t already said? Honey Badgers come in, take what they want, and to hell with anyone else. Bitten by a venomous snake? Screw it: I’ll just have a nap, sleep off the venom then eat the snake when I wake up. Swarms of angry bees protecting their hive? I can pick out the stings when I’m dead.

Honey badgers are the perfect example of an asshole animal – he doesn’t give a shit if you’re bigger, stronger, got a better mark in the maths mid-term, or if you got picked for the high school cricket team and he didn’t. Honey badger is going to come into school, punch you in the head, push your stupid mate in front of bus to make an example out him, then molest your girlfriend at morning recess. And she’s going to like it, because honey badger don’t give a shit.

Pistol Shrimp

Watch archived videoEveryone likes shrimp, right? Well these little bastard shrimp hunt other shrimps. And they do it by cocking the firearm they have on one of their claws, and shooting them with a jet of water hotter than the surface of the sun. CHK-CHK BOOM! Pistol shrimp just sit back, accuse another shrimp of sleeping with their sister, then shoot them in the head.

Slow Loris

Watch archived video

Looks adorable, doesn’t he? That cute little loris is holding onto the tiny umbrella? What they don’t tell you is that slow loris are the animal kingdom’s version of Splinter Cell – the Acehnese called them “wind monkeys”, because they can silently slip away into the shadows, and then the little bastards hang upside down for hours waiting for their prey to come along. And when it does, they reach backwards and snap their neck in an inverted neck-break just like Sam Fisher does

Haha! No, of course they don’t break their prey’s neck – instead they lick the toxic glands they have on their arms then they bite them in the fucking neck to poison their prey instead. And I don’t mean “prey” as in just insects or bugs – these things take on birds and lizards. The toxin the loris secretes is exceptionally noxious, but it’s only activated by saliva - so the sneaky little pricks can sit in the shadows waiting for a bird to doze off, before murdering it in its sleep, without the smell giving them away. But if a predator comes along and the loris can’t vaporise into the shadows like a frigging ninja for some reason, they just lick their arms and the odour is enough to scare off bears and leopards.

Not so adorable now, are they?
