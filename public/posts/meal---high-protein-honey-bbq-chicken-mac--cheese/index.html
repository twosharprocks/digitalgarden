High Protein Honey BBQ Chicken Mac & Cheese

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #mac-and-cheese  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients#

  • Chicken breast - 1kg
  • Pasta - 300g
  • Reduced fat cheese - 160g
  • Milk - 250ml
  • BBQ sauce - 120g
  • Honey - 20g
  • Butter - 10g
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions#

  1. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions and drain
  2. Season the chicken breast and cook until done, then dice or slice
  3. Combine the BBQ sauce and honey and coat the chicken
  4. In a pan, melt the butter and add the milk and cheese to make a sauce
  5. Stir the cooked pasta through the cheese sauce
  6. Fold through the honey BBQ chicken
  7. Serve hot

Macros#

  • Calories - 630 per serving
  • Protein - 58g
  • Carbs - 58g
  • Fat - 13g

Notes#

  • Macros are estimated from a high-protein version of the dish
