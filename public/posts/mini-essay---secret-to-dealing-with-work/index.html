Mini Essay - Secret to dealing with work

#mini-essay  #writing  #work 

Related:

Surviving any uncomfortable workplace always requires an evil secret plan on your part, and your current situation is no different.

Your secret is that you’re using them as a stepping stone to more suitable work, and as a way to gain useful experience from both a corporate and technical perspective. From a technical perspective you’re now working in threat intelligence for a critical infrastructure organisation, which looks incredible on your resume. Being able to string those things together means you’re well on your way to choosing the kind of work you want in industries that you feel are important.

The corporate experience is just as important though, as you work in a flexible environment but recognise that it’s not for you. Counting out leave is breaking your heart, and knowing that you can’t just up and go is crushing your sense of freedom. You can survive that for awhile knowing that it’s for a purpose, but it will become harder the longer it goes on because you know the purpose of pushing on where you are is to get to the end - to get what you want and leave.

For you, the secret to working where you are for the last 10 months, and what will get you through the final 6, is knowing that this is a massive stepping stone towards the flexibility and income you desire. You’re being sponsored to study the certification (CISSP) which you’ve known for years is the goal, and you’re gaining highly relevant experience in a vital field. The responsibility for fixing issues weighs heavily on you, and when it’s combined with the organisational bureaucracy you witness, the result is soul-crushing. But if you step back from the responsibility and the misguided sense you “need to fix it all” then it’s a very easy job to do, and it supports your end goals.

While work may have been a source of angst and depression for you over the last few months, having made the decision to leave has eased that pressure and allow you to recognise the real secret of working there - this is just a stepping stone to greater things. Finish the next 6 months, and get everything you need in terms of experience and certifications to go so much further.
