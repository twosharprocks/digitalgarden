Scitech Space Spectacular – Friday April 11th
Would you like to visit Mars? And would you still go if you couldn’t come back? In 2024 the not-for-profit Mars One foundation will send the first four humans to the planet Mars – and they are there to stay! Over 200,000 people applied to become Mars One’s first colonists, with three Perth residents being short-listed as potential “Marstronauts” and to be featured in a reality-TV style selection process throughout 2014 & 2015.

On Friday April 11th – timed to celebrate 53 years since Yuri Gagarin becoming the first man in space – this last day of term event starts with ‘Live’ presentations by Scitech in the Horizon planetarium that integrate a Mars One mission fly through and discusses space exploration. Students are then treated to a 25 minute Q&A panel in the planetarium’s dome with Perth’s own potential Mars colonists Korum Ellis, Kerry McCloud and Josh Richards.

Students will explore the science, technology and challenges of space exploration and find out why the astronaut candidates are willing to leave their friends, families and Earth behind forever to make Mars their permanent home.

General Public Session: 2.45-3.45pm

  • 10.45-11.45am
  • 12.15-1.15pm
  • 1.15-2.15pm
