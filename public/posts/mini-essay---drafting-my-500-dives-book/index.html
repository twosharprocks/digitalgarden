Mini Essay - Drafting my 500 Dives book

Created: 2025-11-05
Updated: 2026-07-17
Status: seed
Tags: #mini-essay #writing 
Related: Mini Essays Writing Book - Cave Diving

A few months ago I made the decision that I’d write a book about my first 500 cave dives, right around the time I realised I’d be hitting my 500th cave dive during Trip - Sulawesi - 2025 Aug. Since then the ideas associated with this book have rolled around my head, but I’ve not sat down to make a solid go at a draft, and if I’m honest this #mini-essay is potentially another stalling tactic, but at least I’m writing about the idea of this book - it’s a start.

There’s absolutely no way I’d write a book about every one of those 500 dives though, and limiting this to simply those very specific 500 dives would be quite limiting too, so I know that the final book needs to be broader than that - it’s where the idea of BC (Before Cave), CC (Cave Course) and AC (After Cave) or PC (Post Cave) comes from. I also have zero interest in naming the training either - I just want to define the points where I was unqualifed, qualifying, and qualified to enter full overhead environments. This isn’t an advert for any particular training or instructor - this is about telling interesting stories about my cave diving experiences and sharing them with people who may never even scuba dive.

That is what the highest priority of this book needs to be - writing something that people who don’t scuba dive (let alone cave dive) will find interesting. Something that is engaging, informative, and funny. And it needs to tell stories that I think are important too. I’m not interested in sharing the stories that other people think I should, I want to tell the stories from my diving that are memorable and made an impact on me.

Subsurface logs all my dives, so I’ll use the number it has as a quick reference. But when I think about the dives that impacted me most, I think about:

  • 175-181 - Basic cave course with Will, Dave, Matt and Curt
    • how that course taught me more in 5 days than I’d learned in anything else before,
    • being narked in One Tree,
    • the incredibly shitty stress test Rob put me through,
    • and then that dive in Kilsby at the end (when I’d qualified because of a double Goulden’s dive).
  • 183 - Matt in One Tree where we fucked up our TTS. Experience of going through the swim through (THIS is what I want to do), shivering in wetsuits on deco, wedging my helmet into the wall
  • 185 - Matt in Ela Elap with the thermocline and getting caught trailing the paralenz through the tree. A very spooky dive
  • 189 - Little Blue Lake with Curt being the spookiest dive you’d ever done and experiencing hydrogen sulfide for the first time, and how you’d experience that again later with Will when you were on your JJ.
  • 190 - Picaninnie Ponds for the first time and how unimpressed I was.
  • 191-207 - Stockton Lake diving solo in the coal mine at night - long swims on the rebreather, following line, mapping, total whiteouts
  • 208-217 - Pre course dives with Dave getting his numbers up before Cave Course.
  • 218-225 - Cave Course with Dave & Lachlan East - experience of stress test in Pines and Dave handing me a reg that was off, Rob being checked out, testing the crack and turning off all the lights in Allendale (224 - first REAL cave), Rob ripping things off (225)
  • 226 - Post Course Pines with Dave - going through the TV room, finding holes everywhere, Dave stopping (too tight)
  • 231-232 - First dives in the Shaft - still basic
  • 236 - Cathedral Caves with Dave (Tassie) - Concerns about lights and line, Dave’s later near-death experience.
  • 270 - Allendale and first attempt at the crack - Squeezing in with 12’s and getting stuck, right cylinder off and dragged to get out.
  • 279 - Fossil East & Testing the Rasp - swinging cylinders, perfect lineup, later changes
  • 280 - Fossil West and finding passages
  • 295 - Chris Dawson in Pines - showing me Wedge Room, snapping line, definitely confusing
  • 305-306 - Nettlebed & Mudhole - Graeme & Matt A, 20 min dives
  • 307/311-314/342-344 - Allendale Digging - 307 on 2x7s, 311 on 4x7s, 312 as Airbus, 342 Long Dig, 343 Survey & Long dig, 344/346 long dig
  • 310/313/335/338 - Fossil West Blue Line - 310 Install blue line, 313 blue line tangle,
  • 308 - Discovery of sump to EEX
  • 329 - Kislbys with Lachlan & Simon - new years day, pushing lead at 50m on air
  • 330-331 - The Sisters with Lachlan - So unbelievably shitty
  • 335/338/347/348 - Fossil West Digging - 335 clean up with lachlan, 338 find claypot, 347/348 digging,
  • 337 - Mudhold Digging with Matt A - Upside-down behind coffin rock with wire saw
  • 340 - EEX first push with Matt - returned in a wetsuit and camera
  • 359 - First Solo Cave Dive - Diving Fossil West to recover tools, take photos,
  • 360 - First trip to Pines CCR - Diving with lachlan
