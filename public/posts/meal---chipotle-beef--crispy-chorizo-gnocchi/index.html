Meal - Chipotle Beef & Crispy Chorizo Gnocchi

Ingredients#

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Beef mince - 400g
  • Chorizo - 100–150g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes - 400g
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Garlic - 1 tsp (or 2 cloves)
  • Chipotle paste - 1 tsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Chilli powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Cheese (optional, grated)
  • Fresh herbs (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Sugar - 1/2 tsp

Directions#

  1. Add gnocchi to air fryer, drizzle with oil and chilli powder, and cook at 190°C for ~18 minutes until crispy
  2. Dice and cook chorizo in a pan until crispy, then remove and set aside
  3. In the same pan, sauté onion and garlic until softened
  4. Add beef mince and cook for ~5–7 minutes until browned
  5. Stir in chipotle paste, chopped tomatoes, salt, pepper, and sugar
  6. Simmer sauce for several minutes until slightly thickened
  7. Add crispy gnocchi and cooked chorizo back into the pan and mix well
  8. Optional: top with grated cheese and fresh herbs before serving
