This expedition feels important for a wide variety of reasons, and not just for what we discovered or did while we were out there. First and foremost this trip feels like the end of a major phase for the Soggy Wombats as there were four of us out there, but it was always intended to be all of us. The fact that the two Matts and Simon didn’t come wasn’t a huge surprise, and I’m certainly not upset with them for not being there, but it does reflect that when a trip was organise 12 months in advance for them to make time to be there, they had other priorities. The fact that Martin said yes, but then pulled out without telling any of us, and that he replaced himself with Farzad says everything about where that relationship has now gone. There’s no need to dwell on it, but it does mean that he won’t be asked to participate in any trips again.

Corey proved himself throughout the entire experience, and Will did exactly what he said he would be able to do, and I value both of them all the more for living up to expectations and being clear about what they would and wouldn’t do. Likewise with James, Nancy and Toby - all three of them proved to be excellent additions to the trip, to be incredibly capable and resilient, and I’m really glad I got to be there alongside each of them. Chloe is an interesting one from this trip, as we managed to get through it without our personal issues blowing over, but there’s no doubt that there’s still lingering resentment there. It’s not a showstopper for her joining future trips that I organise, but it is definitely something to consider before extending an invite.

More importantly though, this trip acted as the end of a phase for me - one where I feel like I need to support and work with others when it comes to cave diving. I originally created the Soggy Wombats as cover for my own activities - to present a united team who made these discoveries and deflect some of the attention from myself. After the shitshow of the last few years where I’ve been consistently punished by the Australian cave diving community for sharing the things I’ve discovered, there is exactly zero incentive to organise things with other people in mind. I know now that I don’t owe the community anything, and what I do share deserves to be acknowledged and celebrated. Being suspended puts me solidly outside of the community, and that’s really forced my hand when it comes to trying to work with others - I really can’t give or plan to support others, because I’m expressly forbidden from it. So this trip has really served as a hard full stop on my concern for supporting the community and for what the Soggy Wombats were. What we evolve into next will be something different - something that Will, Corey and I can shape and use as a brand rather than a team.

In terms of actual caves, it was wonderful to be able to collect meaningful data from three different sites too. Finally getting back to Murra-El-Elevyn - 6N47 to collect meaningful photogrammetry data and run line into Mitch’s Maze has been nearly a year in the making, and from all accounts it was exactly what we needed to do. I’ve given Will everything he needs to turn that cave into an extraordinary 3D model, and to be able to map this new section using 360 imagery alongside the MNemo survey data too. Part of me is disappointed to have bottomed out the two branches of Mitch’s Maze, but I think the photogrammetry processing will reveal a whole range of interesting opportunities for exploration that I haven’t foreseen yet. I am also keeping in mind that there are several lakes in Murra to the north of the Crystal room that have never been dived or properly explored - there is enormous potential for exploration there if someone like me is willing to put in the effort to get there.

Getting into Tommy Graham’s Cave - 6N56 for the first time was an interesting experience too, and one that I’m very glad I took the 360 camera for. The effort to get into Tommy’s makes it prohibitive, but there is no question that I will be going back there - it has so much more to give, and we didn’t even go close to collecting all the photogrammetry data from it that I’d have liked. Losing the X4 in there is frustrating, but that’s just the nature of the beast given what we were trying to achieve, and there are clear lessons from that loss for the future too. I am definitely keen to return to Tommy’s for a dedicated push - not to just visit for the day, but to spend several days there with some strong cave divers in order to push and thoroughly explore it without carrying weaker members of the group.

And finally Cocklebiddy Cave - 6N48 was it’s own experience entirely. I’m glad that I’ve done it, and I’m glad that the people who were there have now done that trip, but I’m equally glad not to be going back. It was never about diving Cocklebiddy for me - it was about supporting a Nullarbor expedition for the Soggy Wombats and a few others to collect data from an epic cave, and I’m glad that we’ve done that. Do I ever need to go back to Cocklebiddy? I don’t think so, and I’d put places like Pannikin Plains - 6N49 well ahead of any return to Cocklebiddy. Am I glad that we collected photogrammetry data from the surface all the way through to Toad Hall? Fuck yes I am, and I have no issue with staying on the surface as support while other people collected that data either - at the end of the day it’s about producing a 3D model that gives people some sense of how truly epic these places are and how hard it is to get people safely in and out again. So I have no issue with the outcome from Cocklebiddy, and I’m glad that I went. I’m equally glad that I didn’t dive, and that the conflict we experienced out there has hammered home to me that I need to plan trips for myself rather than for others - do the things that I’m excited for, and figure out who will add positively to the experience rather than feeling like I need to invite others to make it happen. I am more than capable of travelling completely solo and collecting meaningful data that will help others, so don’t feel like you need to carry others - share the experience through storytelling, video, and writing. Don’t feel like you need to indulge others - this is a pretty selfish sport, and you absolutely do not need to share anything unless you want to.
