Meal - Beef Taco Rice Bowl

Created: 2026-04-16
Updated: 2026-04-16
Status: seed
Tags: #meal #beef #rice #bowl #meal-prep
Related: Meals

Ingredients#

  • Ground beef - 500g
  • Rice - 2 cups cooked
  • Corn - 1/2 cup
  • Beans - 1 can
  • Cheese - 1/2 cup
  • Salsa - 1/2 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Taco seasoning

Directions#

  1. Cook beef with seasoning
  2. Prepare rice and vegetables
  3. Assemble bowl with all ingredients
  4. Top with cheese and salsa

Macros#

  • Calories - 580
  • Protein - 40g
  • Carbs - 52g
  • Fat - 18g
