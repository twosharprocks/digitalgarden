Mini Essay - Procrastinating Writing the Pines Article

Created: 2025-05-30
Updated: 2025-05-30
Status: tree
Tags: #mini-essay #writing 
Related: Mini Essays

What’s the holdup on writing the article about your Crazy Wombat Tunnel discovery in Pines? You announced it all 2.5 months ago now, and yet you’ve never dedicated yourself to actually sitting down and writing something very important. Is this just procrastination, or is there something else here?

It’s easy to generate a list of excuses for not having done it yet - you’ve been sick, work has been full-on, you’ve been very focused on CISSP study, you’ve been dealing with the stress around Chloe wanting a home loan… is that it? It doesn’t feel like you’ve had many weekends to yourself, and the last weekend you had was dedicated to developing your Expedition plans (which you made excellent progress on).

This last week you’ve been recovering from illness, but you’ve also been making the most of the afternoons in order to complete the CISSP study that’s required. You’re focusing that onto the weekday afternoons though, so while you’ll continue to study CISSP for the next couple of weeks until the June 30 deadline, that’s not a barrier to you writing the article about the Crazy Wombat Tunnel.

I guess the article has fallen into the same category as the website development - something you know you should want to get on with, but it’ll take a reasonable amount of commitment and planning in order to succeed with it, and it’s been a lot easier to put other things in front of it instead. As you figure out your real priorities though, you know that you’ll feel an immense sense of relief at completing both the article and the web update.

The reality is you have so many things you’d like to do - things you know will help you in the long run, that are important to you living the life you want to live - but you get side-tracked with things that are easier or you feel you’ve “earned” the right to relax with. Rather than thinking about what you want to watch next, figure out what you’d like to write next. Breaking the cycle with TV is probably the biggest thing here, as you’re relatively good about breaking away from the computer.

Don’t be too hard on yourself, but make sure you’re prioritising the things that are truly important to you. You also need to make sure that you’re not making these priorities out to be bigger than they are. If you’re too tired to write and article, then go to bed! If you’re tired but don’t want to sleep, then read your Kindle! Doing other stuff is fine, but the habits you really want to maintain are reading books and writing books/articles. So set your life up so that the default for you is writing whenever you can, and reading when you can’t write. Read, write. RW. That’s what you’re here to do, and anything else should be a support mechanism for you to do more of those things.
