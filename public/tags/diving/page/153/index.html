Additional Notes

Location estimated from image on pg 2

  • enciclopedia.cat: https://www.enciclopedia.cat/gran-enciclopedia-catalana/font-de-ses-aiguades

“Cave and archaeological site in the bay of Alcúdia (Mallorca), near the islet of Alcanada. It is a natural cave flooded for the most part by phreatic waters, with the entrance located about 6 m above sea level and a hundred meters from the coastline. The maximum depth is 21 m, 15 m of which below the water level. In a first layer, the water is sweet, as it comes from precipitation on the earth’s surface, while the lower layer is salt water from the sea. Along its route (about 180 m in total length), it consists of several more or less dilated cavities that are called the Amphora Room, a gallery of the Typhlocirolana moraguesi (a troglodyte crustacean capable of living in either fresh or salt water , present in large quantities), gallery of the Myotragus(extinct Mallorcan endemic bovid whose remains would indicate that the cave would have emerged in an earlier period, probably during the last glaciation), north gallery and northeast gallery.”
