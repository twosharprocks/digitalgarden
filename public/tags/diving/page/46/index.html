Tommy Graham’s Cave - 6N56

#diving  #caving 

To Do

Diving Dry Photogrammetry

About

Description: May have more sumps (See DiverNet article)

Where it is & what it is
On the WA Nullarbor near Cocklebiddy; entrance on the lip of a doline with a long haul down to water. Expect a dry entrance slope through breakdown to a lake, then multiple sumps and at least one intervening airspace.

  • Steep entrance → main chamber → lake → ~190 m sump through a phreatic maze → large dry chamber (~80 m) with another lake at far end. Beyond, Glacier Lake reportedly sumps ~130 m to a ~30 m dia. rock-pile chamber that’s blocked ~35 m above water (suggests a collapsed dome with potential bypasses).

Tonga

#diving  #personal  #trips  #expeditions 

About

Best time is April to October (Dry season). Excellent visibility and the chance to encounter humpback whales during their migration (June to November).

Tonga’s geological landscape includes islands with uplifted coral limestone formations, leading to the development of karst topography characterized by caves, sinkholes, and underground drainage systems. Notably, ʻEua Island exhibits extensive limestone formations with karst features, including numerous caves formed through the dissolution of carbonate rocks.
