Additional Notes

  • Mallorcaverde.es: http://www.mallorcaverde.es/cova-des-xots.htm

(Translated from Spanish) “In the deepest part of the cave there is a kind of pond that we can access using a rope in order to avoid a possible slip since the ground is always wet and slippery. I have to say that the Cova des Xats serves as a drain for the entire sinkhole in which it is located and the water accompanied by branches usually enters t, as evidenced by the wooden trunks, some of considerable dimensions that can be observed through the cave. At the bottom of the lower room, a kind of funnel that can be accessed deposits us right in the murky water pool mentioned above and 1.5m deep, M. Trias (1977)”
