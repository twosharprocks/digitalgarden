I’d been training with them for a few months, and somewhere between the days of trudging through 6ft of soft snow and shivering through the -20°C nights, I was bitten by a deer tick. I didn’t know at the time that I’d been infected with Lymes disease, but 5 weeks later I was admitted to CTCRM’s hospital where I lost 8 kg in 5 days and nearly died.

Mini Essay - Why Antarctica is Important to you

#mini-essay  #writing  #antarctica 

As you sit in the airport at Buenos Aires after such a hefty few days of flying, it’s starting to really dawn on me that I’m about to flying to Ushuaia - the gateway to Antarctica. And in just a few days, I’ll be getting onboard an expedition ship Going to the Antarctic Peninsula!

There’s a big part of me that is simply not processing that fact - that in a few days I’ll be in Antarctica. I’m not sure if it’s the numbness you’ve experienced a lot of these last few years, but it’s not clear if my lack of excitement is because I haven’t come to terms with the reality of the trip yet, or if I’m downplaying it all in an attempt to protect my psyche from potential disappointment.
