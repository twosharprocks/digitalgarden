Meal - Air Fryer Chicken Burritos

#meal  #chicken  #burrito  #air-fryer  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g
  • Rice - 2 cups cooked
  • Tortillas - 4
  • Cheese - 1 cup
  • Beans - 1 can
  • Salsa - 1/2 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook and season chicken, then shred
  2. Mix with rice, beans and salsa
  3. Fill tortillas and roll tightly
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 10–12 mins until crispy

Macros

  • Calories - 610
  • Protein - 48g
  • Carbs - 55g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - Air Fryer Nachos

#meal  #snack  #nachos  #air-fryer  #quick  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips - 150–200g
  • Cheese - 1.5–2 cups (cheddar or blend)
  • Sweet corn - 1/2 cup
  • Bell peppers - 1 cup (chopped)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Jalapeños (optional)

Seasoning

  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes
  • Salt (optional)

Optional Add-ons

  • Cooked beef or chicken
  • Beans
  • Sour cream
  • Guacamole
  • Salsa

Directions

  1. Line air fryer basket with parchment paper
  2. Add a layer of tortilla chips
  3. Top with vegetables, corn, and cheese
  4. Repeat layers if needed (chips → toppings → cheese)
  5. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes
  6. Air fry at 180°C for 5–7 minutes until cheese melts
  7. Remove and add optional toppings (sour cream, salsa, etc.)
  8. Serve immediately

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 30g

  • Best eaten fresh — chips soften quickly after cooking :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Easily adaptable with added protein for a full meal

Meal - Giant Chicken Poppers

#meal  #chicken  #air-fryer  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken mince - 500g
  • Lite Laughing Cow Cheese - 4 wedges
  • Cornflakes - 60g
  • Greek Yogurt - 150g
  • Seasoning
    • Buffalo Chicken
    • Garlic
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Mix chicken mince with seasonings in a large glass bowl
  2. Cover a piece of cheese with mince mix and roll into a ball
  3. Make more balls with cheese in the middle until the mince is gone
  4. Place the balls into the freezer for 30-60 minutes
  5. Blend the cornflakes with additional seasoning
  6. Roll the chicken balls in Greek yogurt
  7. Roll the chicken balls in the cornflake and seasoning mix
  8. Spray the chicken balls with oil
  9. Cook in the air fryer for 15-20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes

Macros

  • Calories - 254 per serving
  • Protein - 36g
  • Carbs - 16g
  • Fat - 4g

Meal - High Protein Chicken Katsu Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #fried-rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer  #easy 

Ingredients

Chicken Katsu

  • Chicken breast - 600g (sliced or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Cornflakes or panko breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder

Fried Rice

  • Cooked rice - 3–4 cups (preferably day-old)
  • Eggs - 2
  • Garlic - 2–3 cloves (minced)
  • Soy sauce - 2–3 tbsp
  • Mixed vegetables - 1–2 cups
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. Coat chicken in egg and seasoning, then cover in crushed cornflakes or panko
  2. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  3. In a pan, scramble eggs and set aside
  4. Fry garlic in oil until fragrant
  5. Add rice, vegetables, and soy sauce, stirring well
  6. Return eggs and mix through
  7. Slice crispy chicken katsu and place over fried rice
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 560
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 12g

  • Combines Japanese-style chicken katsu with classic fried rice technique
  • Ideal for high-protein meal prep
