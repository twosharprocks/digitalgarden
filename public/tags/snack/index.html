Meal - Air Fryer Nachos

#meal  #snack  #nachos  #air-fryer  #quick  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips - 150–200g
  • Cheese - 1.5–2 cups (cheddar or blend)
  • Sweet corn - 1/2 cup
  • Bell peppers - 1 cup (chopped)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Jalapeños (optional)

Seasoning

  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes
  • Salt (optional)

Optional Add-ons

  • Cooked beef or chicken
  • Beans
  • Sour cream
  • Guacamole
  • Salsa

Directions

  1. Line air fryer basket with parchment paper
  2. Add a layer of tortilla chips
  3. Top with vegetables, corn, and cheese
  4. Repeat layers if needed (chips → toppings → cheese)
  5. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes
  6. Air fry at 180°C for 5–7 minutes until cheese melts
  7. Remove and add optional toppings (sour cream, salsa, etc.)
  8. Serve immediately

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 30g

Notes

  • Confirmed structure from reel + standard air fryer method :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
  • Best eaten fresh — chips soften quickly after cooking :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Easily adaptable with added protein for a full meal

Meal - Cheese Pull Apart Bread

#meal  #snack  #bread  #cheese  #oven  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Bread loaf (sourdough or cob) - 1
  • Mozzarella - 200g (cubed or shredded)
  • Butter - 3 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Parsley (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cut the bread in a crosshatch pattern (do not slice all the way through)
  2. Stuff mozzarella into the cuts throughout the loaf
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic and seasoning
  4. Pour or brush mixture over the bread, ensuring it gets into the gaps
  5. Wrap loosely in foil and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes
  6. Uncover and bake another 5–10 minutes until crispy and melted
  7. Serve immediately and pull apart

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 14g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • Identified based on “cheese pull” style construction
  • Designed for visual melt/stretch rather than high-protein efficiency
  • Best served immediately while cheese is hot and elastic

Meal - Garlic Butter Jalapeño Popper Croissants

#meal  #snack  #croissant  #cheese  #quick  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Jalapeños - 6 (halved, deseeded)
  • Cheddar cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Croissant dough - 1 tube
  • Butter - 2 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Parsley (optional)

Directions

  1. Slice jalapeños in half and remove seeds
  2. Fill each with shredded cheddar cheese
  3. Wrap each jalapeño with croissant dough
  4. Mix melted butter with garlic and brush over wrapped poppers
  5. Bake at 180°C for 15–20 minutes until golden
  6. Garnish with parsley and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 28g
  • Fat - 28g

Notes

  • Originally misclassified as a chicken caesar bowl — corrected based on reel content
  • High-fat, indulgent snack rather than high-protein meal

Meal - Hash Brown Feta Bites

#meal  #snack  #hash-brown  #feta  #quick  #oven  #vegetarian 

Ingredients

  • Hash browns (frozen or shredded potato) - 400g
  • Feta cheese - 150g (crumbled or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Flour or breadcrumbs - 2 tbsp (optional, for binding)
  • Oil spray or drizzle
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. If using frozen hash browns, thaw slightly
  2. Mix hash browns with egg, seasoning, and optional binder
  3. Form small portions and press feta into the centre (or mix feta through)
  4. Shape into balls or patties
  5. Place on a lined tray and lightly spray with oil
  6. Bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 340
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 30g
  • Fat - 20g

Notes

  • Confirmed as a feta + hash brown bite-style recipe
  • Likely variations include coating feta in hash browns or mixing through
  • Good as a snack, side, or brunch item

Meal - No-Bake Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Bars

#meal  #dessert  #no-bake  #chocolate  #snack 

Ingredients

Cookie Dough Base

  • Butter - 1/2 cup (softened)
  • Brown sugar - 3/4 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Milk - 1/3 cup
  • Flour (heat-treated or oat flour) - 2 cups
  • Salt - pinch
  • Chocolate chips - 1 cup

Chocolate Topping

  • Chocolate chips - 1/2 cup
  • Butter or coconut oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. In a bowl, mix softened butter and brown sugar until smooth
  2. Add vanilla and milk, mixing well
  3. Stir in flour and salt until a dough forms
  4. Fold in chocolate chips
  5. Press mixture evenly into a lined baking dish
  6. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth
  7. Pour chocolate over the top and spread evenly
  8. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until firm
  9. Slice into bars and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 5g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • No-bake and egg-free → safe-to-eat cookie dough style :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
  • Chill time is essential for firm texture
  • Can substitute oat/almond flour for a healthier version
