Meal - Air Fryer Nachos

#meal  #snack  #nachos  #air-fryer  #quick  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips - 150–200g
  • Cheese - 1.5–2 cups (cheddar or blend)
  • Sweet corn - 1/2 cup
  • Bell peppers - 1 cup (chopped)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Jalapeños (optional)

Seasoning

  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes
  • Salt (optional)

Optional Add-ons

  • Cooked beef or chicken
  • Beans
  • Sour cream
  • Guacamole
  • Salsa

Directions

  1. Line air fryer basket with parchment paper
  2. Add a layer of tortilla chips
  3. Top with vegetables, corn, and cheese
  4. Repeat layers if needed (chips → toppings → cheese)
  5. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes
  6. Air fry at 180°C for 5–7 minutes until cheese melts
  7. Remove and add optional toppings (sour cream, salsa, etc.)
  8. Serve immediately

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 30g

  • Best eaten fresh — chips soften quickly after cooking :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Easily adaptable with added protein for a full meal

Meal - Cheeseburger Bowls

#meal  #beef  #bowl  #burger  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Ground beef - 500g
  • Potatoes (or fries) - 400g
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Lettuce - 1–2 cups (shredded)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)

Burger Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or fries until crispy
  2. Cook ground beef with salt and pepper until browned
  3. Mix burger sauce ingredients in a bowl
  4. Assemble bowls with potatoes, beef, cheese, lettuce, onion, and pickles
  5. Drizzle with burger sauce and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 40g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 22g

Notes

  • Identified as cheeseburger-style bowl based on user verification
  • Typical “burger in a bowl” structure (beef + potato base + sauce)

Meal - Chipotle Beef & Crispy Chorizo Gnocchi

#meal  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Beef mince - 400g
  • Chorizo - 100–150g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes - 400g
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Garlic - 1 tsp (or 2 cloves)
  • Chipotle paste - 1 tsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Chilli powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Cheese (optional, grated)
  • Fresh herbs (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Sugar - 1/2 tsp

Directions

  1. Add gnocchi to air fryer, drizzle with oil and chilli powder, and cook at 190°C for ~18 minutes until crispy
  2. Dice and cook chorizo in a pan until crispy, then remove and set aside
  3. In the same pan, sauté onion and garlic until softened
  4. Add beef mince and cook for ~5–7 minutes until browned
  5. Stir in chipotle paste, chopped tomatoes, salt, pepper, and sugar
  6. Simmer sauce for several minutes until slightly thickened
  7. Add crispy gnocchi and cooked chorizo back into the pan and mix well
  8. Optional: top with grated cheese and fresh herbs before serving

Meal - Honey BBQ Chicken Mac & Cheese

#meal  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 2
  • Pasta - 200g
  • BBQ sauce - 1/2 cup
  • Honey - 1 tbsp
  • Cheese - 1 cup (grated)
  • Milk - 1/2 cup
  • Butter - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook pasta according to packet instructions
  2. Season and cook chicken, then slice
  3. Mix BBQ sauce and honey, coat chicken
  4. Melt butter, add milk and cheese to form sauce
  5. Combine pasta, sauce, and chicken
  6. Serve hot

Meal - Honey Garlic Chicken Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #fried-rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #oven  #to-try 

Ingredients

Chicken

  • Diced boneless & skinless chicken breast - 1.05kg (raw weight)
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 35ml
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Potato starch - 100g
    • Approx. 40g adheres to chicken
  • Seasoning
    • Black pepper - 1/2 tsp
    • Onion powder - 1 tsp
    • Garlic powder - 1 tsp

Fried Rice

  • Frozen mixed vegetables - 500g
  • Eggs - 4
  • Egg whites - 200g
    • Or 5 whole eggs total
  • Spring onion / scallions (white part only) - 30g
  • Day old cooked white rice - 980g
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 40ml
  • Spring onion / scallions (green part only) - 20g

Honey Garlic Sauce

  • Garlic - 5 cloves (minced)
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 60ml
  • Water - 60ml
  • Reduced sugar ketchup - 100g
  • Rice wine vinegar - 25ml
  • Cornflour - 1 tsp
  • Water - 80ml
  • Honey - 80g

Directions

  1. Place diced chicken in a bowl and combine with 35ml soy sauce, minced garlic, black pepper, onion powder and garlic powder
  2. Coat chicken with potato starch until evenly covered
  3. Cook chicken until crispy and cooked through (oven or air fryer)
  4. Prepare honey garlic sauce by combining garlic, soy sauce, water, ketchup, rice wine vinegar, cornflour slurry and honey
  5. Heat sauce gently until thickened
  6. In a large pan or tray, cook mixed vegetables with the white parts of the spring onion
  7. Add eggs and egg whites and cook until scrambled
  8. Add day-old rice and 40ml soy sauce, mixing thoroughly
  9. Fold through crispy chicken and green spring onion
  10. Pour over honey garlic sauce and mix until evenly coated
  11. Portion into 7 servings and serve

Notes

  • Caption-faithful reconstruction from reel ingredients
  • Uses potato starch to create crispy chicken coating
  • Day-old rice improves texture and prevents clumping
  • Designed as a high-protein meal prep recipe
