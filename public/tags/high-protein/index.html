Cheeseburger Bowls

#meal  #beef  #bowl  #burger  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Ground beef - 500g
  • Potatoes (or fries) - 400g
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Lettuce - 1–2 cups (shredded)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)

Burger Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or fries until crispy
  2. Cook ground beef with salt and pepper until browned
  3. Mix burger sauce ingredients in a bowl
  4. Assemble bowls with potatoes, beef, cheese, lettuce, onion, and pickles
  5. Drizzle with burger sauce and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 40g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 22g

Notes

  • Identified as cheeseburger-style bowl based on user verification
  • Typical “burger in a bowl” structure (beef + potato base + sauce)

Chicken & Chorizo One Pan Rice

#meal  #chicken  #chorizo  #rice  #one-pan  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (diced)
  • Chorizo - 120–150g (sliced)
  • Rice - 250–300g (uncooked, rinsed)
  • Onion - 1 (diced)
  • Red and yellow peppers - 2 (chopped)
  • Garlic - 3 cloves (minced)
  • Chicken stock - 500–700ml
  • Tomato paste - 2 tbsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Smoked paprika - 2 tsp
  • Garlic powder - 1 tsp
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Chilli (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan and cook chorizo until it releases oils
  2. Add onion, peppers, and garlic, cooking until softened
  3. Add chicken and cook until browned
  4. Stir in tomato paste and seasoning
  5. Add rice and mix to coat in flavours
  6. Pour in chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  7. Cover and cook for 15–20 minutes until rice is tender and liquid absorbed
  8. Fluff and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 600
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 18g

Notes

  • Confirmed from indexed reel + multiple matching recipes :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
  • Similar to simplified paella-style cooking (one-pan absorption method)
  • Very strong meal prep option

Chicken Gnocchi Soup

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #soup  #high-protein  #one-pot 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Gnocchi - 400g
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken in a pot until lightly browned, then remove and chop if needed
  2. Add the tomato puree and sun dried tomatoes and cook briefly
  3. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  4. Add the gnocchi and cook until tender
  5. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  6. Return the chicken to the pot and add the spinach
  7. Simmer gently until the spinach wilts and the soup is hot throughout
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 490 per serving
  • Protein - 52g
  • Carbs - 48g
  • Fat - 10g

Notes

  • Macros come from the reel caption

Chorizo Smash Breakfast Tacos

#meal  #breakfast  #chorizo  #tacos  #high-protein  #stovetop 

Ingredients

  • Chorizo - 300g
  • Small tortillas - 6
  • Eggs - 3
  • Cheddar cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Bacon - 3–4 rashers (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (caramelised)

Sauce (optional)

  • Mayonnaise
  • Hot sauce
  • Mustard or similar tangy sauce

Directions

  1. Press chorizo directly onto tortillas in a thin layer
  2. Cook meat-side down in a hot pan until crispy
  3. Flip tortillas and crack egg on top (or cook eggs separately)
  4. Add cheese and allow to melt
  5. Top with bacon and caramelised onion
  6. Fold and serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 620
  • Protein - 32g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 38g

Notes

  • Confirmed from indexed reel description :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
  • “Smash taco” method = meat pressed into tortilla and cooked together
  • Breakfast-focused variation of viral smash taco trend

Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #creamy  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Pasta - 300g
  • Garlic - 3 cloves
  • Cream - 200ml
  • Parmesan - 1/2 cup
  • Spinach - 1 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta and set aside
  2. Cook chicken and slice
  3. Sauté garlic, add cream and parmesan
  4. Add pasta, chicken and spinach
  5. Stir and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 590
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 22g
