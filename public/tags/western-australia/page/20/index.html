River Cave

#diving  #caving  #western-australia 

About

Location from Arrowsmith Hydrogen Project Assessment paper

Details

Identification

  • Feature type: Cave

Additional Notes

“Arramall Cave is thought to be approximately 2 km in length and extends north of Brand Highway (under the road). It is incompletely mapped but contains the larger chambers than River Cave. These chambers were formed by roof collapses (Western Australian Speleological Group 1990). River Cave has a length of about 1.8 km. The floors of both caves are sandy as a result of caves largely filling with sand when Lake Arramall overflows into them.”
