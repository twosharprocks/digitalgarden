Picanninnie Ponds - 5L72

#diving  #caving  #mount-gambier  #south-australia 

About

Piccaninnie Ponds is a karst sinkhole located in the Limestone Coast region of South Australia, approximately 32 kilometres southeast of Mount Gambier. The site is recognized as a wetland of international importance and is listed under the Ramsar Convention. The ponds are renowned for their crystal-clear water, which supports aquatic vegetation up to 15 metres below the surface.

Piccaninnie Ponds is a popular destination for recreational diving, attracting both snorkelers and cave divers. The site contains three main features of interest to cave divers: The First Pond, an open depression about 10 metres deep with a silt floor and much aquatic life; The Chasm, a sinkhole over 100 metres deep; and The Cathedral, a large underwater cavern with majestic white walls of sculptured and scalloped limestone.
