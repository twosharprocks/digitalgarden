Meal - Cheese Pull Apart Bread

#meal  #snack  #bread  #cheese  #oven  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Bread loaf (sourdough or cob) - 1
  • Mozzarella - 200g (cubed or shredded)
  • Butter - 3 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Parsley (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cut the bread in a crosshatch pattern (do not slice all the way through)
  2. Stuff mozzarella into the cuts throughout the loaf
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic and seasoning
  4. Pour or brush mixture over the bread, ensuring it gets into the gaps
  5. Wrap loosely in foil and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes
  6. Uncover and bake another 5–10 minutes until crispy and melted
  7. Serve immediately and pull apart

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 14g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • Identified based on “cheese pull” style construction
  • Designed for visual melt/stretch rather than high-protein efficiency
  • Best served immediately while cheese is hot and elastic

Meal - Hash Brown Feta Bites

#meal  #snack  #feta  #quick  #oven  #vegetarian 

Ingredients

  • Hash browns (frozen or shredded potato) - 400g
  • Feta cheese - 150g (crumbled or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Flour or breadcrumbs - 2 tbsp (optional, for binding)
  • Oil spray or drizzle
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. If using frozen hash browns, thaw slightly
  2. Mix hash browns with egg, seasoning, and optional binder
  3. Form small portions and press feta into the centre (or mix feta through)
  4. Shape into balls or patties
  5. Place on a lined tray and lightly spray with oil
  6. Bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 340
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 30g
  • Fat - 20g

Notes

  • Confirmed as a feta + hash brown bite-style recipe
  • Likely variations include coating feta in hash browns or mixing through
  • Good as a snack, side, or brunch item

Meal - Honey Garlic Chicken Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #fried-rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #oven  #to-try 

Ingredients

Chicken

  • Diced boneless & skinless chicken breast - 1.05kg (raw weight)
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 35ml
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Potato starch - 100g
    • Approx. 40g adheres to chicken
  • Seasoning
    • Black pepper - 1/2 tsp
    • Onion powder - 1 tsp
    • Garlic powder - 1 tsp

Fried Rice

  • Frozen mixed vegetables - 500g
  • Eggs - 4
  • Egg whites - 200g
    • Or 5 whole eggs total
  • Spring onion / scallions (white part only) - 30g
  • Day old cooked white rice - 980g
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 40ml
  • Spring onion / scallions (green part only) - 20g

Honey Garlic Sauce

  • Garlic - 5 cloves (minced)
  • Salt reduced soy sauce - 60ml
  • Water - 60ml
  • Reduced sugar ketchup - 100g
  • Rice wine vinegar - 25ml
  • Cornflour - 1 tsp
  • Water - 80ml
  • Honey - 80g

Directions

  1. Place diced chicken in a bowl and combine with 35ml soy sauce, minced garlic, black pepper, onion powder and garlic powder
  2. Coat chicken with potato starch until evenly covered
  3. Cook chicken until crispy and cooked through (oven or air fryer)
  4. Prepare honey garlic sauce by combining garlic, soy sauce, water, ketchup, rice wine vinegar, cornflour slurry and honey
  5. Heat sauce gently until thickened
  6. In a large pan or tray, cook mixed vegetables with the white parts of the spring onion
  7. Add eggs and egg whites and cook until scrambled
  8. Add day-old rice and 40ml soy sauce, mixing thoroughly
  9. Fold through crispy chicken and green spring onion
  10. Pour over honey garlic sauce and mix until evenly coated
  11. Portion into 7 servings and serve

Notes

  • Caption-faithful reconstruction from reel ingredients
  • Uses potato starch to create crispy chicken coating
  • Day-old rice improves texture and prevents clumping
  • Designed as a high-protein meal prep recipe

Meal - Pizza Cupcakes

#meal  #snack  #pizza  #appetizer  #quick  #oven  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Biscuit dough (canned) - 1 can
  • Pizza sauce - 1/4 cup
  • Mozzarella cheese - 1/2 cup (shredded)
  • Pepperoni - 8–10 slices
  • Butter - 2 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 1 tsp (minced)
  • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Flatten biscuit dough and press into muffin tray to form cups
  2. Add pizza sauce, cheese, and pepperoni into each cup
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic and brush over dough edges
  4. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning
  5. Bake at 180°C for 12–15 minutes until golden
  6. Remove and serve warm

Macros

  • Calories - 350
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 30g
  • Fat - 20g

Notes

  • Previously misclassified as a chicken rice bowl — corrected based on reel content
  • More of a snack/appetizer than a main meal

Meal - Spicy Peanut Dumplings

#meal  #dumplings  #peanut  #spicy  #quick  #oven 

Ingredients

  • Frozen dumplings - 12 to 16
  • Spring onion - 2 sprigs
  • Peanut butter - 2 tbsp
  • Red curry paste - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 1 tsp minced
  • Ginger - 1 tsp minced
  • Soy sauce - 1 tbsp
  • Sesame oil - 1 tsp
  • Coconut milk - 1/2 cup
  • Chicken stock - 1/2 cup

Directions

  1. In a baking dish, mix together the peanut butter, red curry paste, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, coconut milk and chicken stock
  2. Add the frozen dumplings to the sauce
  3. Bake until the dumplings are cooked through and the sauce has thickened
  4. Top with sliced spring onion and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 360 per serving
  • Protein - 13g
  • Carbs - 36g
  • Fat - 18g

Notes

  • Macros are estimated from a 4-serving batch
