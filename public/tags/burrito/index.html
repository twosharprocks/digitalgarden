Meal - Air Fryer Chicken Burritos

#meal  #chicken  #burrito  #air-fryer  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g
  • Rice - 2 cups cooked
  • Tortillas - 4
  • Cheese - 1 cup
  • Beans - 1 can
  • Salsa - 1/2 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook and season chicken, then shred
  2. Mix with rice, beans and salsa
  3. Fill tortillas and roll tightly
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 10–12 mins until crispy

Macros

  • Calories - 610
  • Protein - 48g
  • Carbs - 55g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - Beef Bulgogi Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 500g steak, very thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 Asian pear, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2cm ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 large burrito wraps
  • Cooked rice
  • Kimchi
  • Sliced spring onion
  • Kewpie mayonnaise

Directions

  1. Mix beef with onion, pear, soy, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, gochujang and sesame seeds.
  2. Marinate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in batches over high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Layer rice, kimchi, beef, mayonnaise and spring onion on each wrap.
  5. Roll tightly and toast in a dry pan or sandwich press.

Meal - Chicken Bacon Ranch Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #chicken  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1.3kg chicken breast
  • Taco seasoning
  • 500g low-fat cottage cheese
  • 2 tbsp ranch seasoning
  • 1 tsp chipotle in adobo
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 45ml light mayonnaise
  • 200g light mozzarella
  • 8 bacon rashers, cooked and diced
  • Chives
  • 1kg cooked rice
  • 10 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken, then dice it.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, ranch seasoning, chipotle, lime, garlic and mayonnaise until smooth.
  3. Combine chicken with the ranch sauce, mozzarella, bacon and chives.
  4. Warm each tortilla, add rice and chicken filling, then roll tightly.
  5. Toast immediately or wrap and freeze for later.

Meal - Grilled Cheese Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • Taco seasoning
  • 1 cup cooked seasoned rice
  • 1 cup grated cheddar, plus extra for grilling
  • 1 cup queso or nacho cheese sauce
  • ¼ cup chipotle sauce
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • Crushed tortilla strips
  • 8 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown the beef, add taco seasoning and cook until the sauce has reduced.
  2. Layer beef, rice, queso, cheddar, chipotle sauce, sour cream and tortilla strips on each wrap.
  3. Roll tightly into burritos.
  4. Scatter cheese in a hot non-stick pan and place each burrito seam-side down over it.
  5. Turn once the cheese forms a golden crust and toast the other side.

Meal - High Protein Breakfast Burritos

#meal  #breakfast  #burrito  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Large tortillas - 10
  • Eggs - 12–14
  • Lean ground meat (beef/turkey/chicken) - 800g–1kg
  • Potatoes or hash browns - 600–800g
  • Cheese - 2 cups (shredded)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Paprika
  • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or hash browns until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook lean meat with seasoning until browned
  3. Scramble eggs in a large pan
  4. Combine eggs, meat, and potatoes
  5. Lay out tortillas and evenly distribute filling
  6. Add cheese on top
  7. Roll tightly into burritos
  8. Optional: toast in pan or wrap for meal prep storage

Macros

  • Calories - 550
  • Protein - 59g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 18g
