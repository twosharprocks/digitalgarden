Meal - Chili Garlic Noodles

#meal  #noodles  #pork  #spicy  #quick  #stovetop 

Ingredients

  • Ground pork - 1/2 lb
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 6 cloves (minced)
  • Onion - 1/4 (finely chopped)
  • Noodles - 300g
  • Seasoning
    • Garlic powder - 1 tsp
    • Salt - 1/2 tsp
    • Pepper
    • Chilli flakes
  • Sauce
    • Soy sauce
    • Chilli oil
    • Brown sugar

Directions

  1. Cook the noodles and set aside
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the ground pork until browned
  3. Add the garlic and onion and cook until fragrant and softened
  4. Season with garlic powder, salt, pepper and chilli flakes
  5. Stir through soy sauce, chilli oil and a little brown sugar
  6. Add the cooked noodles and toss well to coat
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 430 per serving
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 44g
  • Fat - 15g

Meal - Chili Mac and Cheese

#meal  #beef  #pasta  #chili  #one-pot  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Olive oil - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Onion - 1 (finely chopped)
  • Red bell pepper - 1 (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef - 500g
  • Diced tomatoes - 1 can
  • Pasta or macaroni - 250g
  • Cheese - 1 cup shredded
  • Stock or water - as needed
  • Seasoning
    • Chilli powder
    • Cumin
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and cook the garlic, onion and red bell pepper until softened
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned
  3. Stir through the chilli powder, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper
  4. Add the diced tomatoes and pasta
  5. Pour in enough stock or water to cook the pasta and simmer until tender
  6. Stir through the shredded cheese until melted
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 510 per serving
  • Protein - 30g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - Chipotle Beef & Crispy Chorizo Gnocchi

#meal  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Beef mince - 400g
  • Chorizo - 100–150g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes - 400g
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Garlic - 1 tsp (or 2 cloves)
  • Chipotle paste - 1 tsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Chilli powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Cheese (optional, grated)
  • Fresh herbs (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Sugar - 1/2 tsp

Directions

  1. Add gnocchi to air fryer, drizzle with oil and chilli powder, and cook at 190°C for ~18 minutes until crispy
  2. Dice and cook chorizo in a pan until crispy, then remove and set aside
  3. In the same pan, sauté onion and garlic until softened
  4. Add beef mince and cook for ~5–7 minutes until browned
  5. Stir in chipotle paste, chopped tomatoes, salt, pepper, and sugar
  6. Simmer sauce for several minutes until slightly thickened
  7. Add crispy gnocchi and cooked chorizo back into the pan and mix well
  8. Optional: top with grated cheese and fresh herbs before serving

Meal - Chorizo Smash Breakfast Tacos

#meal  #breakfast  #chorizo  #tacos  #high-protein  #stovetop 

Ingredients

  • Chorizo - 300g
  • Small tortillas - 6
  • Eggs - 3
  • Cheddar cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Bacon - 3–4 rashers (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (caramelised)

Sauce (optional)

  • Mayonnaise
  • Hot sauce
  • Mustard or similar tangy sauce

Directions

  1. Press chorizo directly onto tortillas in a thin layer
  2. Cook meat-side down in a hot pan until crispy
  3. Flip tortillas and crack egg on top (or cook eggs separately)
  4. Add cheese and allow to melt
  5. Top with bacon and caramelised onion
  6. Fold and serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 620
  • Protein - 32g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 38g

  • “Smash taco” method = meat pressed into tortilla and cooked together
  • Breakfast-focused variation of viral smash taco trend
