Meal - Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Pasta - 300g
  • Garlic - 3 cloves
  • Cream - 200ml
  • Parmesan - 1/2 cup
  • Spinach - 1 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta and set aside
  2. Cook chicken and slice
  3. Sauté garlic, add cream and parmesan
  4. Add pasta, chicken and spinach
  5. Stir and serve

Meal - Feta Fried Egg Tortilla

#meal  #wrap  #quick  #breakfast 

Ingredients

  • Egg - 1
  • Feta cheese - 2 tbsp (crumbled)
  • Tortilla - 1 small
  • Avocado - 1/4 (sliced)
  • Chilli oil - drizzle (optional)

Directions

  1. Sprinkle feta in a circle in a hot pan
  2. Once it starts melting, crack the egg into the centre
  3. Place tortilla on top and press down gently
  4. Cook until the egg sets and feta crisps
  5. Flip so tortilla side is down and toast briefly
  6. Remove, top with avocado and chilli oil
  7. Fold and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 380
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 28g
  • Fat - 22g

  • Very fast, high-flavour breakfast or snack
  • Texture comes from crispy feta + soft egg contrast

Meal - Garlic Butter Jalapeño Popper Croissants

#meal  #snack  #quick  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Jalapeños - 6 (halved, deseeded)
  • Cheddar cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Croissant dough - 1 tube
  • Butter - 2 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Parsley (optional)

Directions

  1. Slice jalapeños in half and remove seeds
  2. Fill each with shredded cheddar cheese
  3. Wrap each jalapeño with croissant dough
  4. Mix melted butter with garlic and brush over wrapped poppers
  5. Bake at 180°C for 15–20 minutes until golden
  6. Garnish with parsley and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 28g
  • Fat - 28g

Notes

  • Originally misclassified as a chicken caesar bowl — corrected based on reel content
  • High-fat, indulgent snack rather than high-protein meal

Meal - Garlic Honey BBQ Pulled Chicken Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos  #chicken 

Ingredients

  • 700g boneless chicken breast
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp each onion powder and smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Coleslaw mix
  • Chopped coriander
  • Lime wedges

Directions

  1. Coat the chicken with BBQ sauce, honey, garlic, onion powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook covered on low until tender, then shred and mix it back through the sauce.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill with pulled chicken and coleslaw.
  4. Finish with coriander and lime.
