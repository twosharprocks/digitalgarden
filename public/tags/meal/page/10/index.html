Nacho Beef Folded Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water
  • 4 large tortilla wraps
  • 1½ cups grated cheddar
  • 1 cup crushed nacho chips
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup diced tomatoes
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or butter

Directions

  1. Brown the beef, drain excess fat, then add taco seasoning and water and simmer until thick.
  2. Warm the tortillas and cut one slit from the centre to the edge.
  3. Divide cheese, beef, crushed chips, lettuce, tomato and sour cream between the four tortilla quarters.
  4. Fold each quarter over the next to make a layered triangle.
  5. Toast in oil or butter for 2–3 minutes per side until crisp.

Nacho Cheese Beef Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1kg lean beef mince
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 130g low-sugar ketchup
  • 150ml water
  • 230ml light evaporated milk
  • 180g Mexican cheese blend
  • 30ml hot sauce
  • 6 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with onion and capsicum.
  2. Add taco seasoning, ketchup and water, then simmer until thick.
  3. Warm evaporated milk gently, add cheese and hot sauce, and stir until smooth.
  4. Divide beef and cheese sauce between tortillas.
  5. Roll into burritos and toast seam-side down.

No-Bake Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Bars

#meal  #dessert  #no-bake  #chocolate  #snack 

Ingredients

Cookie Dough Base

  • Butter - 1/2 cup (softened)
  • Brown sugar - 3/4 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Milk - 1/3 cup
  • Flour (heat-treated or oat flour) - 2 cups
  • Salt - pinch
  • Chocolate chips - 1 cup

Chocolate Topping

  • Chocolate chips - 1/2 cup
  • Butter or coconut oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. In a bowl, mix softened butter and brown sugar until smooth
  2. Add vanilla and milk, mixing well
  3. Stir in flour and salt until a dough forms
  4. Fold in chocolate chips
  5. Press mixture evenly into a lined baking dish
  6. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth
  7. Pour chocolate over the top and spread evenly
  8. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until firm
  9. Slice into bars and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 5g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • No-bake and egg-free → safe-to-eat cookie dough style :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
  • Chill time is essential for firm texture
  • Can substitute oat/almond flour for a healthier version

Paprika Chicken & Chorizo Gnocchi

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Olive oil - 1/2 tbsp
  • Chorizo - 70g
  • Red onion - 1 small
  • Chicken breast - 350g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (crushed)
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook the chorizo until it starts to crisp
  2. Add the chopped red onion and cook until softened
  3. Add the chicken breast, season with paprika, salt and pepper, and cook until coloured
  4. Stir through the garlic, tomato puree and chopped sun dried tomatoes
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and add the gnocchi
  6. Simmer until the gnocchi is tender and the sauce reduces slightly
  7. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  8. Fold through the spinach until wilted
  9. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 562 per serving
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 52g
  • Fat - 16g

Pepper Jack Chicken Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #chicken  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 900g chicken breast
  • Taco seasoning
  • 200g reduced-fat cheddar
  • 200g reduced-fat mozzarella
  • 14 slices centre-cut bacon, cooked
  • 1 can diced green chillies
  • 8 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken, then chop or shred it.
  2. Combine chicken, bacon, green chillies, cheddar and mozzarella.
  3. Divide between warmed tortillas and roll into burritos.
  4. Toast seam-side down until sealed and golden.
  5. Wrap and refrigerate or freeze extras for meal prep.
