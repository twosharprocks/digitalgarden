Meal - Chili Mac and Cheese

#meal  #beef  #pasta  #chili  #one-pot  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Olive oil - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Onion - 1 (finely chopped)
  • Red bell pepper - 1 (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef - 500g
  • Diced tomatoes - 1 can
  • Pasta or macaroni - 250g
  • Cheese - 1 cup shredded
  • Stock or water - as needed
  • Seasoning
    • Chilli powder
    • Cumin
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and cook the garlic, onion and red bell pepper until softened
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned
  3. Stir through the chilli powder, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper
  4. Add the diced tomatoes and pasta
  5. Pour in enough stock or water to cook the pasta and simmer until tender
  6. Stir through the shredded cheese until melted
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 510 per serving
  • Protein - 30g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - Grilled Cheese Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • Taco seasoning
  • 1 cup cooked seasoned rice
  • 1 cup grated cheddar, plus extra for grilling
  • 1 cup queso or nacho cheese sauce
  • ¼ cup chipotle sauce
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • Crushed tortilla strips
  • 8 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown the beef, add taco seasoning and cook until the sauce has reduced.
  2. Layer beef, rice, queso, cheddar, chipotle sauce, sour cream and tortilla strips on each wrap.
  3. Roll tightly into burritos.
  4. Scatter cheese in a hot non-stick pan and place each burrito seam-side down over it.
  5. Turn once the cheese forms a golden crust and toast the other side.

Meal - High Protein Grilled Cheese Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 900g extra-lean beef mince
  • ½ white onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • Taco seasoning
  • 400g canned diced tomatoes
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 100g light cheddar
  • 600g cooked rice
  • Coriander
  • 6 large tortillas
  • 1 tbsp nacho cheese sauce per burrito
  • 20g light mozzarella per burrito

Directions

  1. Brown onion, garlic and beef, then add taco seasoning, tomatoes and stock.
  2. Simmer until the mixture is thick, then stir through cheddar and coriander.
  3. Divide rice, beef filling and nacho cheese sauce between tortillas and roll tightly.
  4. Place mozzarella in a hot non-stick pan and set the burrito on top.
  5. Cook until the cheese forms a crust, then turn and toast the other side.

Meal - High Protein Mexican Bowl

#meal  #beef  #rice  #mexican  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Rice - 200g (uncooked, portioned ~50g per serve)
  • Extra lean beef mince - 500g
  • Tinned tomatoes - 400g
  • Taco seasoning - 1 packet (or ~20g)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (optional)
  • Oil spray (minimal)

Optional Add-ons

  • Greek yogurt (as sour cream substitute)
  • Lettuce
  • Cheese (light)
  • Salsa or hot sauce

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions and set aside
  2. In a pan, cook onion and garlic until softened (optional)
  3. Add lean beef mince and cook until browned
  4. Stir in taco seasoning and tinned tomatoes
  5. Simmer until thickened and well combined
  6. Portion rice into containers or bowls
  7. Top with beef mixture and optional extras
  8. Serve or store for meal prep

Macros

  • Calories - 480
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 59g
  • Fat - 9g

Meal - Nacho Beef Folded Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water
  • 4 large tortilla wraps
  • 1½ cups grated cheddar
  • 1 cup crushed nacho chips
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup diced tomatoes
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or butter

Directions

  1. Brown the beef, drain excess fat, then add taco seasoning and water and simmer until thick.
  2. Warm the tortillas and cut one slit from the centre to the edge.
  3. Divide cheese, beef, crushed chips, lettuce, tomato and sour cream between the four tortilla quarters.
  4. Fold each quarter over the next to make a layered triangle.
  5. Toast in oil or butter for 2–3 minutes per side until crisp.
