Nacho Cheese Beef Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1kg lean beef mince
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 130g low-sugar ketchup
  • 150ml water
  • 230ml light evaporated milk
  • 180g Mexican cheese blend
  • 30ml hot sauce
  • 6 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with onion and capsicum.
  2. Add taco seasoning, ketchup and water, then simmer until thick.
  3. Warm evaporated milk gently, add cheese and hot sauce, and stir until smooth.
  4. Divide beef and cheese sauce between tortillas.
  5. Roll into burritos and toast seam-side down.

Smashed Potato Cheeseburger Bowls

#meal  #beef  #potatoes  #burger  #bowl  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Potatoes - 200g
  • 93/7 Ground beef - 75g cooked
  • Reduced fat fiesta cheese - 28g
  • Light sour cream - 30g
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Burger sauce - to taste
  • Seasoning
    • Onion powder
    • Garlic powder
    • Paprika
    • Cayenne
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Slice the potatoes into thin rounds and roast or air fry until tender and crisp
  2. Cook the ground beef and season with onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, salt and pepper
  3. Layer the smashed or roasted potatoes into a bowl
  4. Top with the cooked beef, cheese, pickles, sour cream and burger sauce
  5. Serve hot

  • Protein - 28g

Spicy Steak & Cheese Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 16 low-carb wraps
  • 900g lean steak
  • Reduced-fat cheese
  • 800g cooked rice
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Paprika, garlic powder and cumin
  • Coriander
  • Onion and capsicum

Sauce

  • 300g fat-free Greek yoghurt
  • 70g light sour cream
  • 50g chipotle in adobo
  • Juice of ½ lime

Directions

  1. Season and cook the steak, then slice or chop it.
  2. Sauté the onion and capsicum until tender.
  3. Blend or stir together the yoghurt, sour cream, chipotle and lime.
  4. Divide rice, steak, vegetables, cheese and sauce between wraps.
  5. Roll tightly and toast, or wrap and freeze for meal prep.
