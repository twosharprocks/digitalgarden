Meal - Bolognese Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp each oregano, thyme, basil and garlic powder
  • 2 tsp each sweet paprika and dried parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1½ cups tomato passata
  • ⅓ cup water
  • ½ vegetable stock cube
  • Grated cheddar
  • 5–6 mini tortilla wraps
  • ½ tbsp olive oil

Directions

  1. Soften the onion in olive oil, add the beef and brown over high heat for 5–6 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, carrot, passata, tomato purée, seasonings, stock and water.
  3. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Dip each tortilla in the sauce, place it in a hot pan, and layer with cheese and 2–3 tbsp of filling.
  5. Fold and grill both sides until browned and crisp.

Meal - Chorizo Smash Breakfast Tacos

#meal  #breakfast  #chorizo  #tacos  #high-protein  #stovetop 

Ingredients

  • Chorizo - 300g
  • Small tortillas - 6
  • Eggs - 3
  • Cheddar cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Bacon - 3–4 rashers (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (caramelised)

Sauce (optional)

  • Mayonnaise
  • Hot sauce
  • Mustard or similar tangy sauce

Directions

  1. Press chorizo directly onto tortillas in a thin layer
  2. Cook meat-side down in a hot pan until crispy
  3. Flip tortillas and crack egg on top (or cook eggs separately)
  4. Add cheese and allow to melt
  5. Top with bacon and caramelised onion
  6. Fold and serve hot

  • Fat - 38g

Meal - Garlic Honey BBQ Pulled Chicken Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos  #chicken 

Ingredients

  • 700g boneless chicken breast
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp each onion powder and smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Coleslaw mix
  • Chopped coriander
  • Lime wedges

Directions

  1. Coat the chicken with BBQ sauce, honey, garlic, onion powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook covered on low until tender, then shred and mix it back through the sauce.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill with pulled chicken and coleslaw.
  4. Finish with coriander and lime.

Meal - Smash Dumpling Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g pork mince
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • Handful of coriander, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ginger paste
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp black or rice vinegar
  • Black pepper
  • 12 mini tortillas
  • Olive oil
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Dumpling dipping sauce, chilli oil and sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Combine pork, most of the spring onion and coriander, garlic, ginger, soy, sesame oil, fish sauce, vinegar and pepper.
  2. Toss cucumber with rice vinegar and salt.
  3. Spread a thin layer of pork filling over one side of each tortilla.
  4. Fry meat-side down in an oiled pan for about 4 minutes, then flip and crisp the tortilla for 1 minute.
  5. Top with cucumber, reserved herbs, sesame seeds and dipping sauce or chilli oil.
