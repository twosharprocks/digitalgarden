Meal - Chili Mac and Cheese

#meal  #beef  #pasta  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Olive oil - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Onion - 1 (finely chopped)
  • Red bell pepper - 1 (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef - 500g
  • Diced tomatoes - 1 can
  • Pasta or macaroni - 250g
  • Cheese - 1 cup shredded
  • Stock or water - as needed
  • Seasoning
    • Chilli powder
    • Cumin
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and cook the garlic, onion and red bell pepper until softened
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned
  3. Stir through the chilli powder, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper
  4. Add the diced tomatoes and pasta
  5. Pour in enough stock or water to cook the pasta and simmer until tender
  6. Stir through the shredded cheese until melted
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 510 per serving
  • Protein - 30g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - Giant Chicken Poppers

#meal  #chicken  #air-fryer  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken mince - 500g
  • Lite Laughing Cow Cheese - 4 wedges
  • Cornflakes - 60g
  • Greek Yogurt - 150g
  • Seasoning
    • Buffalo Chicken
    • Garlic
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Mix chicken mince with seasonings in a large glass bowl
  2. Cover a piece of cheese with mince mix and roll into a ball
  3. Make more balls with cheese in the middle until the mince is gone
  4. Place the balls into the freezer for 30-60 minutes
  5. Blend the cornflakes with additional seasoning
  6. Roll the chicken balls in Greek yogurt
  7. Roll the chicken balls in the cornflake and seasoning mix
  8. Spray the chicken balls with oil
  9. Cook in the air fryer for 15-20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes

Macros

  • Calories - 254 per serving
  • Protein - 36g
  • Carbs - 16g
  • Fat - 4g

  • Macros are estimated from the ingredients used in the original template

Meal - Healthy Chicken Nuggets

#meal  #chicken  #air-fryer  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Greek yogurt - 150g
  • Egg - 1
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cut chicken into nugget-sized pieces
  2. Coat in yogurt and egg mixture
  3. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy

Meal - High Protein Breakfast Burritos

#meal  #breakfast  #burrito  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Large tortillas - 10
  • Eggs - 12–14
  • Lean ground meat (beef/turkey/chicken) - 800g–1kg
  • Potatoes or hash browns - 600–800g
  • Cheese - 2 cups (shredded)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Paprika
  • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or hash browns until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook lean meat with seasoning until browned
  3. Scramble eggs in a large pan
  4. Combine eggs, meat, and potatoes
  5. Lay out tortillas and evenly distribute filling
  6. Add cheese on top
  7. Roll tightly into burritos
  8. Optional: toast in pan or wrap for meal prep storage

Macros

  • Calories - 550
  • Protein - 59g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 18g

Meal - High Protein Chicken Katsu Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer  #easy 

Ingredients

Chicken Katsu

  • Chicken breast - 600g (sliced or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Cornflakes or panko breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder

Fried Rice

  • Cooked rice - 3–4 cups (preferably day-old)
  • Eggs - 2
  • Garlic - 2–3 cloves (minced)
  • Soy sauce - 2–3 tbsp
  • Mixed vegetables - 1–2 cups
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. Coat chicken in egg and seasoning, then cover in crushed cornflakes or panko
  2. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  3. In a pan, scramble eggs and set aside
  4. Fry garlic in oil until fragrant
  5. Add rice, vegetables, and soy sauce, stirring well
  6. Return eggs and mix through
  7. Slice crispy chicken katsu and place over fried rice
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 560
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 12g

  • Confirmed from reel caption and structure :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Combines Japanese-style chicken katsu with classic fried rice technique
  • Ideal for high-protein meal prep
