Meal - Chicken Gnocchi Soup

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #soup  #high-protein  #one-pot 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Gnocchi - 400g
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken in a pot until lightly browned, then remove and chop if needed
  2. Add the tomato puree and sun dried tomatoes and cook briefly
  3. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  4. Add the gnocchi and cook until tender
  5. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  6. Return the chicken to the pot and add the spinach
  7. Simmer gently until the spinach wilts and the soup is hot throughout
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 490 per serving
  • Protein - 52g
  • Carbs - 48g
  • Fat - 10g

Meal - Chili Mac and Cheese

#meal  #beef  #pasta  #chili  #one-pot  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Olive oil - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Onion - 1 (finely chopped)
  • Red bell pepper - 1 (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef - 500g
  • Diced tomatoes - 1 can
  • Pasta or macaroni - 250g
  • Cheese - 1 cup shredded
  • Stock or water - as needed
  • Seasoning
    • Chilli powder
    • Cumin
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and cook the garlic, onion and red bell pepper until softened
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned
  3. Stir through the chilli powder, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper
  4. Add the diced tomatoes and pasta
  5. Pour in enough stock or water to cook the pasta and simmer until tender
  6. Stir through the shredded cheese until melted
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 510 per serving
  • Protein - 30g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 20g

  • The reel title was not fully visible from public snippets, but the ingredient pattern matches a chili mac and cheese style dish
