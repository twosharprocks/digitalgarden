Mini Essay - Not Fade Away (Jim Dodge)

#mini-essay  #books  #writing 

“Not Fade Away” turned out to be quite an interesting read after the initial confusion around the book by Ben Torres-Fong of the same name (accidentally read first before realising the author was different>. There’s no way I would have read it unless it had been on the 101 Things - Complete List list, but that’s the case with so many of those books anyway.

The narrative within a narrative was interesting, and was something that was easy to forget when I was in the thick of George’s story and not realising what I was reading was being told by George to someone else. Besides “Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance” I’ve not read an “on the road” book like it before, and a lot of it felt like going places just to go there, not to actually do anything. Even the idea of burning the Big Bopper’s car seemed arbitrary - it really was just about the journey in the end, and the people you meet along the way.

In early January 2010, I was on Dartmoor doing a survival exercise with the Royal Marine Commandos. I’d been training with them for a few months, and somewhere between the days of trudging through 6ft of soft snow and shivering through the -20°C nights, I was bitten by a deer tick. I didn’t know at the time that I’d been infected with Lymes disease, but 5 weeks later I was admitted to CTCRM’s hospital where I lost 8 kg in 5 days and nearly died.

Mini Essay - Organising a Mars One Analog

#mini-essay  #writing 

An unexpected idea that appeared recently is to organise a Mars analog with a bunch of Mars One candidates in Wadi Rum. I’m not sure why something like this wasn’t organised previously, but the contact from DIrk through LinkedIn has triggered a cascade of ideas around a filmed Mars analogue featuring folks who volunteered for Mars One.

Thinking about it, it hasn’t taken much to put together a list of candidates to approach, or to pick a potential time to go - Jan/Feb 2027. There may be climate issues with a simulation in Wadi Rum then, but that’s all information you can research and put together before reaching out to potential analog astronauts. It’s also possible that 2027 is too soon, but I need to pick something to aim for, and it can always be adjusted later.

The stand out element from this most recent re-reading is that the Alchemist feels like an important reminder of the basics, whereas there are elements of the Peaceful Warrior series that seem new even though you’ve read it more than a dozen times.
