#writing 
College Of Awesome – 101 Things (#79 Speed Dating) As much as it pains me to admit it – it’s Valentine’s Day tomorrow. And while last week’s Ron Burgundy Day tried to get the sex back into sexual harassment, there’s still plenty of people out there turning to Valentine’s Day for some old-fashioned commercialised romance. So instead of raging any further about the dubious history of St. Valentine’s Day, I’d like to suggest an alternative for those of you that failed to get laid from yelling “I WANT TO BE ON YOU” last week – speed dating. Now speed dating has a fair nasty stigma – dozens of guys and girls having “mini-dates” of 3-5 minutes, then ticking a secret ballot paper to say who they’d like to share contact details with – could be seen as slightly sleazy. But as someone who went speed dating at university (and had a damn good time doing it), I discovered it’s all in how you approach it. Most people went trying to meet the love of their life – I went to mess with other single people, and met a girl I lived with for 2 years. So if you are considering going speed dating to help numb the loneliness tomorrow, I have a few suggestions to turn what could be an uncomfortable and ultimately disappointing experience (and that’s just the cry-wank you have when you get home), into a few hours of random/memorable hilarity. Hell, you might even get laid out of it… Go For A Laugh, Not A Lay Sure, it’s got “dating” on the end of it – but who said speed dating has to be about romance? I went because A) The girl organising it is an old friend, B) She’d had someone drop out last-minute and need even numbers of guys to girls, and C) I thought it would be hilarious trying to convince every girl I met that I was born in the Andes and raised by mountain goats till I was 5. I had no intention of trying to meet someone – I’d just started my last year of university, was president of the most infamous social club on campus, and my project supervisor had just  vaporised without warning over the summer to another university. This was purely to help out a friend and have a laugh at the expense of others – the signs of a true gentleman. Go speed dating to meet crazy people, or just people in general. Go and see if you can convince someone you have a psychic link to spider monkeys (or lemurs in my case). Take a friend and dare each other to tell more and more elaborate lies. Just don’t go planning to meet the Captain of the SS Loveboat during a date shorter than most songs. Unless of course you’re telling people you are the Captain of the SS Loveboat, in which case please continue. I’m sure it’ll help you to… Be Memorable You’re about to meet more than a dozen members of the opposite sex, and you have 3-5 minutes to make a decision if you’d want them to call you. This is some high-pressure decision-making – fuck up and you could wind up inviting the girl who eats her toenails around for dinner with your parents, or sleeping with the guy who collects your hair for his extensive home-made doll collection. So you want to make a fast and memorable impression. But because you’re not trying to get them to home with you, you don’t need to stress about making a good impression. Of the 20 girls there, I remember just 4 of them; - The crazy blonde chick, who grabbed my ballot paper the moment I sat down and drew a cartoon elephant yelling “WEE-OR!” - The hockey-worshipping Canadian - The American who’d grown up in Indonesia and come to support the Canadian - The cranky brunette who I met last, spilt my drink on, and about 3 seconds before the night ended discovered had a shared interest in hiking. I have no idea if these were the most attractive/sweetest/most appropriate potential partners out of all the women there – all I know is they were the most interesting and memorable. The Canadian and American had it relatively easy because obviously being foreign alone made them more distinctive/memorable. And the cranky brunette was memorable simply because she was such a sour bitch. Sure, it was the end of the night and everyone had kind of had enough. And yes, I’d drunk enough to tranquillise a bull elephant by that stage. But scowling at someone on a 3 minute date? If I wanted to get judgemental glares from attractive women while I was drunk, I’d have started turning up to my psychology tutorials again. No, the really interesting one here is the blonde that drew the elephant. Now it’s not deliberate discrimination, but I have to admit I’ve never really been attracted to blonde women – my mum is blonde, and most of my friends in school were blonde girls and have always stayed friends. I’ve met a few “hot blondes” since leaving high school, but it was really the brunettes and redheads that kick-started this puberty party. So cartoon-elephant-drawing blonde girl was already heading into the friend zone before I’d sat down – that non-existent friend-zone I was using because I was just there for my own twisted amusement. But I distinctly remember her: even though she was one of the girls I met early in the night six years ago, and I’ve never had any contact with her again; I remember her because grabbing someone’s ballot paper and drawing a yelling cartoon elephant on it is a completely mental thing to do. I want to point out of course the elephant was the one yelling “WEE-OR!”, not her. But in retrospect I probably would have shared my details with her if she  had yelled “WEE-OR!” during our 3 minute date, because god knows that one would have been a keeper. Get Your Drunk On Alcohol – the cause and solution to all of the world’s problems. Sure, cranky-hiking-brunette didn’t appreciate me cheekily slurring and spilling my precious Southern Comfort everywhere – but if I hadn’t been drunk when I met her, I probably would have told her how absurdly dull she was. And I sure as shit wouldn’t have remembered her. So in this case alcohol stopped me from being rude to someone and helped me remember who they were. I’d highly recommend having a few drinks through the night, but maybe pace yourself. Remember you’re looking at 2-3 hours of social lubrication here. So when you have a break “to go to the bar” at the hour mark, maybe order a soft drink instead of the round of tequila shots I did. Maybe don’t start drinking 3 hours before “for courage”. And maybe don’t start stealing the drinks of your dates to “check for poison” because you’re not being a modern-day gentleman, your paving the way to rehab. Other than that, go out and have fun! Speed dating is a real blast – you’ll meet some fascinating people and have lots of laughs. And make sure you have lots of laughs. Website – How to Ruin Christmas

#writing 
This is the final post of the year. The Mighty Ginge will be on break until January 17th,2011 –looking forward to seeing you all next year with new site features and new posts. Merry Christmas,and try to avoid killing yourselves over the New Year. The Mighty Ginge Task Ruin the annual yule-tide festivities through a series of increasingly disrespectful and socially-misguided acts. Background Christmas –a time to share with friends,family and reindeer. It’s the season for togetherness,appreciating those close to you,for generosity and empathy. It’s also the time of the year when people go batshit insane. Shopping,dinners,cards,letters –people are travelling and sending shit everywhere. They get together with family because “they have to”,put up with Grandad’s violent,racist and mysoginistic outbursts for the sake of “harmony”,send out bulk emails to people they don’t even talk to,break the bank buying gifts for kids who play with the box instead,and then take them to sit on the knee of some red-suited paedophile who talks about the goodies in his sack that he’ll give them if they’re good. So in response to this insanity,I’ve compiled a variety of ways you can strike back at the spirit of Saint Nic. These are merely suggestions and guidelines based on previous experiences however –I strongly encourage you all to create or recount your own misadventures,and post them in the comments below. Hitting the Fat Man where it hurts Disclaimer:As this is an entry level experience,I recommend starting out here for those looking to ruin their first Christmas. If at the end you feel any emotion other than malicious joy,I would recommend avoiding any of the other techniques and simply continuing to be passive aggressive at Christmas dinner when passing the salt. “Hitting the Fat Man”requires a high visibility and very large inflatable Santa Claus. Ideally the giant Santa will be located somewhere with limited physical access,but with massive visual impact –for example on the top of a shopping centre facing a freeway. You will need; - A lightweight ladder (or mad ninja skills) - A large,plain white sheet - Black spraypaint - Two bricks - Several meters of string. On Christmas eve,approach your target Santa from low ground below the sight line of any security cameras/roving patrols,and get access to the roof. This can either be by lifting yourself on the edge of a doorframe in a loading bay,hanging off the external grate of a nearby airvent and then swinging your legs onto the roof to pull yourself up backwards (eg. Generally being Sam Fisher);or bringing a ladder with you. Ensure though that you can pull the ladder onto the roof with you if you do use it though,least it be spotted by a roaming security guard. Once on the roof,tie the string to the corners of the sheet and use the bricks to hold the string in place,spreading the sheet out along the wall facing the freeway under Santa’s feet. Now spraypaint “SANTA ISN’T REAL –MERRY XMAS”in foot tall letters on the white sheet,climb back down the ladder/airvent,and make your escape. Chances are it will stay up there for 3 days,because the shopping centre needs to hire an elevated work platform to legally access that part of the roof. The Facade Breaker Extended family:you can’t pick them,but you can ensure they never speak to you again. Christmas with big families is usually a fairly stressful event for everyone,so save yourself the trouble of false pre tense and feigned respect –tell them what you think of them. For example if you’re 8 and having Christmas at your Grandad’s,playing with a water hose and enjoying a hot Australian summer,and he suddenly comes out of the kitchen with a carving knife in his hand screaming that you’ll kink the hose –tell your parents you’re scared of him (like the rest of the family),and that you won’t come out of a spider-infested caravan until he dies or they take you home. Alternatively,inform your parents every year that you’d rather be raped by a thousand angry Squirrel monkeys that see extended family for Christmas. When they finally do convince you that 5 days in a holiday home with them is survivable,give it a real chance. Stay quiet when the pointed questions about your career start;play the game when plans get changed at the last minute to suit the selfish needs of a few;bite your tongue when your the relatives start getting overly religious –it’s okay,your time will come. Because on Christmas night –when everyone is gathered around having a roast and your god-bothering Aunty steps up her week long campaign of bitchiness by accusing your Mum of trying to steal her children away –you get to step up and tell her to “stop acting like such a fucking child”,and that “everyone is sick of your shit”. When she demands you respect her because she’s your aunty,inform her that you respect people who deserve it (eg. not middle aged women who act like children),then storm out of the house. If your not sure that will get you cut off from family Christmases,make sure you rub salt in the wound by apologising to your aunt the next morning for the language you used,but make a point that you still think she’s acts like child and that you don’t care if she thinks it’s disrespectful,she can still fuck off. Seriously,let that shit out –a little work early on will save you a lifetime of awkward Christmas conversations and annual attempts to justify yourself to people you don’t like just because you share genetic material. Spread the Malice Just because you’ve been banned from any future family yule-tide gatherings doesn’t mean you can’t spread your own unique brand of Christmas cheer to other families. Ofcourse not being part of the family makes it more difficult to expose their insecurities:why not expose your own? A favourite of mine is to run away from your long term girlfriend to another state “just for Summer”when she needs you most,and break up with her two week before Christmas from the other side of the country. Then it’s important to get absurdly drunk at the Christmas dinner of the family friends you’re staying with,spill hard liquor on their grandmother,and sing out of tune Disney classics on Singstar with their nieces. To top it off,make sure you then sleep with one of the nieces on Boxing Day and get a photo of her sitting on your lap posted on facebook for your still-grieving ex to see. Not only will your parents be personally horrified,but you’ll have spoiled Christmas for two other families too. The company of strangers Congratulations of getting this far –you’ve worked hard. Now that you can’t go home for Christmas and friends won’t invite you around because you’re a liability,the final stage is to ruin Christmas with people you’ve just met! Book a room in a youth hostel,get drunk and fuck some hideous French girl who doesn’t know enough English to say no! Go back the next year and remind the hostel staff that none of them have moved on their lives! Start fights with the homeless! If you’re in a foreign country,abuse the good intentions of others by tagging along with a friend who you haven’t seen since school,then wage a week long war against their cat. The world is your oyster –people will welcome you into their homes out of charity,and shoo you back out less certain of man’s inherent good nature. Just don’t do anything I wouldn’t do.
