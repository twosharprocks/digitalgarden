Meal - Garlic Honey BBQ Pulled Chicken Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos  #chicken 

Ingredients

  • 700g boneless chicken breast
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp each onion powder and smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Coleslaw mix
  • Chopped coriander
  • Lime wedges

Directions

  1. Coat the chicken with BBQ sauce, honey, garlic, onion powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook covered on low until tender, then shred and mix it back through the sauce.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill with pulled chicken and coleslaw.
  4. Finish with coriander and lime.

Meal - Loaded Breakfast Wrap

#meal  #breakfast  #wrap  #high-protein  #quick 

Ingredients

  • Large wraps - 2
  • Eggs - 4
  • Bacon - 4 rashers
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Hash browns - 2
  • Spinach or greens (optional)
  • Sauce (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook bacon until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook hash browns until golden
  3. Scramble eggs in the same pan
  4. Layer eggs, bacon, hash browns, cheese, and greens into wraps
  5. Fold tightly into burrito shape
  6. Toast in pan until golden and crispy on the outside
  7. Slice and serve

  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 26g

Meal - Nacho Beef Folded Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water
  • 4 large tortilla wraps
  • 1½ cups grated cheddar
  • 1 cup crushed nacho chips
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup diced tomatoes
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or butter

Directions

  1. Brown the beef, drain excess fat, then add taco seasoning and water and simmer until thick.
  2. Warm the tortillas and cut one slit from the centre to the edge.
  3. Divide cheese, beef, crushed chips, lettuce, tomato and sour cream between the four tortilla quarters.
  4. Fold each quarter over the next to make a layered triangle.
  5. Toast in oil or butter for 2–3 minutes per side until crisp.

Meal - Smash Dumpling Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g pork mince
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • Handful of coriander, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp ginger paste
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp black or rice vinegar
  • Black pepper
  • 12 mini tortillas
  • Olive oil
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Dumpling dipping sauce, chilli oil and sesame seeds

Directions

  1. Combine pork, most of the spring onion and coriander, garlic, ginger, soy, sesame oil, fish sauce, vinegar and pepper.
  2. Toss cucumber with rice vinegar and salt.
  3. Spread a thin layer of pork filling over one side of each tortilla.
  4. Fry meat-side down in an oiled pan for about 4 minutes, then flip and crisp the tortilla for 1 minute.
  5. Top with cucumber, reserved herbs, sesame seeds and dipping sauce or chilli oil.
