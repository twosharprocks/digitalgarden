Meal - Chicken Alfredo Tortellini

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #one-pan  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 1 lb
  • Butter - 2 tbsp
  • Garlic - 1 tbsp minced
  • Frozen tortellini - 19 oz bag
  • Alfredo sauce - 15 oz jar
  • Mozzarella - 1 1/2 cups shredded
  • Bacon - 6 pieces
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Onion powder
    • Paprika
    • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp, then set aside
  2. Season the chicken and cook in the butter until browned and cooked through
  3. Add the garlic and cook briefly until fragrant
  4. Add the frozen tortellini and Alfredo sauce and stir to combine
  5. Simmer until the tortellini is cooked through
  6. Stir through the mozzarella until melted
  7. Crumble the bacon over the top and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 552 per serving
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 29g
  • Fat - 30g

Meal - Giant Chicken Poppers

#meal  #chicken  #air-fryer  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken mince - 500g
  • Lite Laughing Cow Cheese - 4 wedges
  • Cornflakes - 60g
  • Greek Yogurt - 150g
  • Seasoning
    • Buffalo Chicken
    • Garlic
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Mix chicken mince with seasonings in a large glass bowl
  2. Cover a piece of cheese with mince mix and roll into a ball
  3. Make more balls with cheese in the middle until the mince is gone
  4. Place the balls into the freezer for 30-60 minutes
  5. Blend the cornflakes with additional seasoning
  6. Roll the chicken balls in Greek yogurt
  7. Roll the chicken balls in the cornflake and seasoning mix
  8. Spray the chicken balls with oil
  9. Cook in the air fryer for 15-20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes

Macros

  • Calories - 254 per serving
  • Protein - 36g
  • Carbs - 16g
  • Fat - 4g

Meal - High Protein Chicken Katsu Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer  #easy 

Ingredients

Chicken Katsu

  • Chicken breast - 600g (sliced or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Cornflakes or panko breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder

Fried Rice

  • Cooked rice - 3–4 cups (preferably day-old)
  • Eggs - 2
  • Garlic - 2–3 cloves (minced)
  • Soy sauce - 2–3 tbsp
  • Mixed vegetables - 1–2 cups
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. Coat chicken in egg and seasoning, then cover in crushed cornflakes or panko
  2. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  3. In a pan, scramble eggs and set aside
  4. Fry garlic in oil until fragrant
  5. Add rice, vegetables, and soy sauce, stirring well
  6. Return eggs and mix through
  7. Slice crispy chicken katsu and place over fried rice
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 560
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 12g

  • Combines Japanese-style chicken katsu with classic fried rice technique
  • Ideal for high-protein meal prep

Meal - High Protein Mexican Bowl

#meal  #beef  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Rice - 200g (uncooked, portioned ~50g per serve)
  • Extra lean beef mince - 500g
  • Tinned tomatoes - 400g
  • Taco seasoning - 1 packet (or ~20g)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (optional)
  • Oil spray (minimal)

Optional Add-ons

  • Greek yogurt (as sour cream substitute)
  • Lettuce
  • Cheese (light)
  • Salsa or hot sauce

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions and set aside
  2. In a pan, cook onion and garlic until softened (optional)
  3. Add lean beef mince and cook until browned
  4. Stir in taco seasoning and tinned tomatoes
  5. Simmer until thickened and well combined
  6. Portion rice into containers or bowls
  7. Top with beef mixture and optional extras
  8. Serve or store for meal prep

Macros

  • Calories - 480
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 59g
  • Fat - 9g

  • Designed as a high-protein, low-fat meal prep option
  • Very scalable and easy to batch cook

Meal - Smokey Bacon Gnocchi

#meal  #gnocchi  #high-protein  #one-pan  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Bacon - 5–6 rashers (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef or chicken - 400–800g (optional high-protein boost)
  • Zucchini - 1–2 (diced)
  • Tomato sauce (tinned or passata) - 400–500g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Smoked paprika

Directions

  1. Fry chopped bacon in a large pan until crispy, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook garlic and ground meat (if using) until browned
  3. Add zucchini and cook until softened
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and smoked paprika
  5. Add gnocchi and simmer until tender and sauce thickens
  6. Return bacon to the pan and mix through
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 16g

Notes

  • Confirmed structure from indexed sources (gnocchi + bacon + tomato base)
  • Often presented as a high-protein, meal-prep friendly dish
  • Variations include adding cheese or baking for a crispy top
