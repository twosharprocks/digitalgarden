Meal - Cheesy Bacon Jalapeño Popper Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #bacon 

Ingredients

  • Large tortilla wraps
  • Cream cheese, softened
  • Grated cheddar
  • Grated mozzarella
  • Cooked bacon, crumbled
  • Jalapeños, grilled and sliced

Directions

  1. Mix cream cheese, cheddar, mozzarella, bacon and jalapeños.
  2. Spread the filling over the centre of each tortilla.
  3. Fold the sides inward and roll tightly.
  4. Toast seam-side down in a dry pan, then turn until golden and the cheese has melted.

Meal - Smokey Bacon Gnocchi

#meal  #gnocchi  #bacon  #high-protein  #one-pan  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Bacon - 5–6 rashers (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef or chicken - 400–800g (optional high-protein boost)
  • Zucchini - 1–2 (diced)
  • Tomato sauce (tinned or passata) - 400–500g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Smoked paprika

Directions

  1. Fry chopped bacon in a large pan until crispy, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook garlic and ground meat (if using) until browned
  3. Add zucchini and cook until softened
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and smoked paprika
  5. Add gnocchi and simmer until tender and sauce thickens
  6. Return bacon to the pan and mix through
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 16g

Notes

  • Confirmed structure from indexed sources (gnocchi + bacon + tomato base)
  • Often presented as a high-protein, meal-prep friendly dish
  • Variations include adding cheese or baking for a crispy top
