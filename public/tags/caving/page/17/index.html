The Shaft - 5L158

#diving  #caving  #mount-gambier 

From CaveDB

The Shaft is a sinkhole located near Mount Gambier in South Australia. It is a popular destination for cave divers, known for its clear water and interesting internal structure.

The Shaft is the site of a tragic scuba diving incident that occurred on May 28, 1973, which claimed the lives of four recreational scuba divers. The incident led to the formation of the Cave Divers Association of Australia and the development of the South Australian Police Underwater Recovery Squad .

Tommy Graham’s Cave - 6N56

#diving  #caving  #nullarbor 

Other Descriptions

About

The entrance to the cave is a large doline 50m in diameter. The obvious route into the doline leads to short cave below the Northwest cliff line.

The main cave has been described as a “wombat- sized hole” which is found in the Southeast end of the doline, about 3m down from the level of the plain. There is a visitor book on a rock shelf in the entrance. The way on is not obvious and goes through a rock squeeze at the end of the low entrance chamber.
