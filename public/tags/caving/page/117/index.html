Three “Deep Creek” caves to locate: North, West, and East

Details

Identification

  • Feature type: Cave
  • Source: NUCC

Cave and exploration data

  • Exploration potential: Medium
  • Tourism potential: Low

Additional Notes

Yarrangobilly has three ‘Deep Creek’ Caves: North, West, and East (no, there is no South Deep Creek). West Deep Creek is the master cave for the system, but is next to impossible to access.* North Deep Creek is niiiice, but it is East Deep Creek that is undisputably the best ‘Deep Creek’. All three caves are expected to resurge at Hollin Cave down on the Yarrangobilly River, along with the water from the Eagles Nest system.
