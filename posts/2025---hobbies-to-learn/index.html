Mental

  • Reading - Provides other perspectives, makes you more interesting Physical
  • Need to do something physical that keeps you active well into your future years
  • Gym, Running, Group Sport, Martial Arts, ect
    • Gym - Even just a small, shitty space. Pullup bar, dumb-bells
    • Running - Cardio Social
  • Getting out to talk to people - find common interests
  • Find other weirdos and maintain connections Creative
  • Writing - Something to slow down, shifting to creation over consumption
  • Inspire, engage
  • Videos - Sharing things you’re interested in
  • Feel free to keep it as a private hobby
  • Try lots of things!
    • Cooking, Film making, photography, design, painting miniatures, music, ham radio
    • Look at recommended online hobbies
    • Plenty of dead-ends, but you’ll find what fits

Hobbies do not need to be allocated to just one category

  • Boxing - Physical & Social
  • Coding - Intellectual & Creative
