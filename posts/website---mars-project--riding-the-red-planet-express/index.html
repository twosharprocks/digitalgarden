Somewhere between memory transplants and three-breasted women, Arnie managed to tell himself  ”Get your ass to Mars”. Well, I’ve been busting my ass trying to do exactly that – spreading the word about Mars One through every conceivable medium known to man. Every medium except through my long-suffering website it seems…

So to help rectify this gross communicative short-coming, here’s the latest updates on Mars!

  • Mars One hit 78,000 applicants in the first 2 weeks – new application numbers haven’t been released since the start of May, so expect this number to keep pushing toward the expected 500,000 before application close August 31st
  • The Aspiring Martians Group on Facebook has exploded with activity in the last few weeks after it was mentioned in Mars One’s monthly applicant email. The Aspiring Martians Wiki is growing equally rapidly, providing all the other answers you could possibly need about the Mars One mission. Have a look to see what the buzz is about – I’m a regular contributor to both.
  • Results from Mars rover Curiosity’s radiation sensor have been released, and show that anyone doing a round-trip to Mars is likely to fractionally exceed NASA’s exposure limits. Just as well we’re only going one-way
  • The folks over at The Verge have been covering Mars One fairly consistently – highly recommend having a read of Katie Drummond’s article on wearable sensors titled “How To Prevent An Astronaut Bloodbath”.

And my own little press bonanza!

  • Talking Mars One with ABC Darwin 105.7 (10 minutes – phone interview)
  • Two-part chat with The Big Couch, Mix 94.5 - [Part 1] and [Part 2] (8 minutes total – studio interview)
  • Lovely little mention in Mary-Rose MacColl’s Queensland Courier-Mail column (June 1, 2013)

Plenty of other interviews and mischief afoot – I’ll share my radio interview with RTRFM, and my interview with The Verge, when they both become available. In the meantime keep rocking ginge fans – many, MANY thanks for all the support by continuing to rate my Mars One application, all of the wonderful support on the Facebook event and the Mighty Ginge fanpage, and the wealth of support on Twitter.
