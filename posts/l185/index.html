Additional Notes#

This unnamed (and justifiably so!), relatively insignificant karst feature is nothing more than a 1.3 x 0.5-metre wide rectangular hole which drops 2m to several extremely narrow joint fissures about 4.5 metres long. At least it had ONE endearing quality in the form of some pretty ferns which were growing around the entrance back in 1979!
