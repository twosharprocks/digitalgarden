The last two and a half years have been pretty odd.

In September 2012 I started researching how to send people one-way to Mars for a comedy show, and found an organisation aiming to do it for real.
In 2013 I was one of 202,586 people who applied to be on the first crew to go, and one of 1058 short-listed for it.
In 2014 I was one of 705 that passed the medical selection, and one of 660 offered an interview.

Today, I’m one of 100 people worldwide progressing to Mars One’s third round of astronaut selection.

Today I can tell you I had my selection interview in early December, and that I’ve waited to tell you about it for the last two and a half months because of a non-disclosure agreement with Mars One. And today I can finally tell you the knot that’s been sitting in my stomach for the last two weeks started to unwrap on Friday when I found out I’d made it through, that it’s unwrapping a little further sharing this with you now… but that it won’t fully clear until this all really sinks in Wednesday morning.

This afternoon I’ll be speaking to:

  • 2UE in Sydney minutes after this is published (14:07 in Perth, 17:07 in Sydney),
  • 6PR in Perth at 14:35 Perth Time
  • Michael Smyth on Drive with ABC 891 Adelaide at 15:05 Perth time (17:35 in Adelaide),
  • Barry Nicholls on Drive with ABC Regional (WA) 20 minutes later (15:15),
  • Going national with Triple J’s Hack at 17:45.
  • Tuesday morning I’ll be Channel 7’s Sunrise, and RTRFM’s Breakfast with Caitlin shortly after that.

With the embargo lifted expect to hear a lot more of Mars One in the media this week. All 100 remaining candidates are expected to undergo group testing (likely in the US or Europe) before Mars One selects 24 to 40 of us to start full-time training in June/July. But right now I don’t know much about what the next few months will involve.

I’m grateful to have so many incredible people believe in what we’re doing – so many incredible people who believe in me. Some of you I’ve known for years, some I’ve really only just met. But all of you are vital to this journey – thank you so much.

I don’t know what happens next… but I’m really glad you’re here.
