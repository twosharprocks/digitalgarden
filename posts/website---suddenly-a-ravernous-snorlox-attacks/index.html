Holy hoverboards Ginge fans, you’d be forgiven for thinking I was dead. Alas, it is not the case – I’m still here, but things have kicked off so massively that I’ve fallen well behind on the website upkeep. So here’s the big stuff…

  • Mars One has officially launched their astronaut applications. Timed to be exactly 10 years before the planned landing of the first Martian colonists in April 2023, Mars One launched an updated website with it’s new applicant section where people of any nationality can apply to become the first Mars colonists, and where others can view and rate their application videos. Within a week there had been over 78,000 applicants, and that number is expected to grow to 500,000 before applications close on August 31, 2013.

  • The press conference launching the application program confirmed some previously suspected details of the mission as well. After first round filtering and second round medical testing/regional selection, 20 men and 20 women will join the Mars One astronaut corp to begin training. From those 40 individuals, teams composed of two men and two women will be formed to test applicant compatibility and suitability. And over 8 years of training, these teams will be whittled down to a final team selected to become the first people to travel to and colonise another planet. Homo Sapiens will have become an inter-planetary species.

  • My own application was submitted within 5 days of the launch, and has maintained a consistantly high rating thanks to the AMAZING outpouring of support from family, friends and fans. The encouragement through my facebook fanpage, the “Send Josh to Mars… Forever!” event , and messages through twitter have just been mindblowing. I’m deeply grateful to all of you so very much.

  • In the same vein, Mars One has asked me to work as an “entertainment ambasador” for them here in Australia – spreading the word of Mars One’s incredible mission throughout the country. It’s an amazing honour to be asked to act as a representative of an organisation with such an inspiring objective, and I only hope I’m able to inspire others to the way Mars One has inspired me. To date I’ve had interviews for national radio and newspapers, as well as an hour long discussion as part of a major American stand-up’s podcast – I can’t wait to share them (and the many others currently being organised) with you all when they’re officially published!

***UPDATE*** The “major American stand-up” was Eddie Ifft, and he’s published his latest “Talkin Shit with Eddie” podcasts, and episode 233 is the 90 minute recording I did with the Pajama Men. The first 34 minutes are Eddie chatting to the guys, and then I join them to discuss my application to go to Mars for the final hour. You can download it through iTunes here or listen online.

And in some other (still pretty amazing) news,

  • The Perth International Comedy Festival is into it’s final week, and I’ve been having a ball driving around all the wonderful comics who have made the long trip out here to put on such amazing shows. It’s been quite inspiring learning so much from folks who live their lives travelling the world to make others laugh, and it’s been truly heartwarming to hear their enthusiasm for Mars One (even if they all think I’m crazy). So now I’m looking forward to the Festival Finale on May 19th so I can get on with taking my Mars mission further!

  • There won’t be much of a break after the festival finale though – the comedy festival’s director Jo Marsh has asked me to be a bridesmaid for her, so there will be PLENTY to do between the end of the festival and the wedding just three weeks later!

  • AXE/Lynx deodorant have finally begun advertising their “Ticket to Space” competition here in Australia. In the meantime, AXE/Lynx’s ambassador Buzz Aldrin has released a new book “Mission To Mars”, outlining his vision to put people on the red planet. I’ve just finished reading it, and look forward to posting a review in the coming week.

  • Dutch airline KLM ran an international competition to predict where a weather balloon in Nevada would burst, with the closest guess winning a sub-orbital flight aboard an XCOR “Lynx” to 103km up. I was close at 31.5 km, but didn’t win the ticket. Pedro in Brazil won the ticket.

Dammit Pedro.

  • And finally, I’m currently waiting anxiously to hear if I’ve been approved for $2000 worth of funding for my National Science Week show. I won’t know if I’ve got the support until May 27th, but in the meantime I CAN tell you that it’s called “Martian Rock Band”, combines the human exploration of Mars with classic rock music on ukuleles & kazoos in a highly interactive stage show, and will rock the living shit out of everyone who sees it. Watch out for it in cities all around Australia in the coming months, and don’t miss it when it hits Perth for National Science Week August 10-18.

So stay tuned for more regular updates, and watch out for some major renovations to the Mighty Ginge website in the coming months to reflect the change in direction. Exciting times ahead.
