Mini Essay - New Tattoo

Created: 2025-11-03
Updated: 2025-11-03
Status: tree
Tags: #mini-essay #writing 
Related: Mini Essays Writing Tattoo - Ideas

Getting a new tattoo has been a consideration for several years now, however there’s been some hesitation over what, where, and why.

The design that I’ve circled back to repeatedly is from the Zen Pencils comic for George Mallory’s “Because It’s There” quote, specifically from the cell where the Inventor is climbing with the text “We eat and make money to be able to live”. So much of what I’ve done for the last 15 years (and even earlier if I’m honest) has been about doing things that make me feel alive, and doing them regardless of whether other people understand the motivation.

Not only is the sepia image of the climbing Inventor important to me as it reflects that drive to climb and push beyond what others will, but the text captures something very special too - a definition for the meaning of life that resounds more fully with what I believe than anything else I’ve ever heard. The image is perfect, and the idea that what we get from these adventures is just sheer joy is what pulls me back to this comic over and over again. It’s why I have an A0 poster of the original comic directly above my screen as I type this.

Turning that cell into a tattoo feels important, but the greatest hesitation comes from where on my body it might go. I will inevitably forget that I have it until I look in the mirror, just as I often forget my shoulder tattoos and even more regularly forget the Bender tattoo on my right ass cheek. I’ve thought about my right breast, but I’ve always felt uncomfortable about my chest and would hesitate to put anything there. On one of my calves has always been an option too, although there’s a big part of me that wants to be able to cover my tatts - my existing tattoos were all placed while keeping in mind how much I wear shirts and shorts.

Looking at myself in the mirror just now, there’s plenty of real estate on my shoulders, upper chest, or even on the side of my chest. What’s clear though is that I don’t feel comfortable in my body at the moment - that I’m carrying more weight than I like, and part of my hesitation comes from the idea I’d be getting skin tattooed which has more fat under it than I want. I’m taking steps to change that right now, and there’s no question my habits are improving and I’ve already made significant progress, but I’m still carrying 5-7 kilos more than I’m comfortable with.

The extra weight is really a reflection that I’m not living the life I want to be living too. While I’m regularly out doing adventurous things, it feels like the baseline I come back to is unhealthy and unsupportive - the longer I’m away the better things feel, and the more weight I lose, but I’m still trying to find a balance point where I can do the daily, non-adventurous things in a manageable way while still preparing (and not losing sight of) the adventurous things I live to do.

I’m not sure what I want to do with this tattoo idea - the “what” of the design is easy and obvious, just as my first “five-leaf clover” design was easy and obvious 10 years ago. The why is equally clear. But the where of that design being placed on my body remains to be decided, and I know that I need to feel more comfortable in my skin before I put any more ink into it.
