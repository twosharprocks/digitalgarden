Meal - High Protein Chicken Sausage Breakfast Burritos

Created: 2026-04-16
Updated: 2026-04-16
Status: seed
Tags: #meal #breakfast #burrito #high-protein #meal-prep
Related: Meals

Ingredients#

  • Chicken sausage - 4 links (sliced)
  • Eggs - 6
  • Egg whites - 500ml (approx 16 oz)
  • Low-fat cottage cheese - 1/2 cup
  • Large tortillas - 6
  • Cheese - 1–1.5 cups (shredded)
  • Oil spray

Seasoning#

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Paprika

Directions#

  1. Cook chicken sausage in a pan until browned
  2. In a bowl, whisk eggs, egg whites, and cottage cheese together
  3. Pour mixture into pan and scramble until fully cooked
  4. Combine eggs with cooked sausage
  5. Lay out tortillas and evenly distribute filling
  6. Add shredded cheese on top
  7. Roll tightly into burritos
  8. Optional: toast lightly or store for meal prep

Macros#

  • Calories - 500
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 40g
  • Fat - 14g

  • Ideal for batch cooking and freezing
