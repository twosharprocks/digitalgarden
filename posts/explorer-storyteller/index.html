Mission#

Explore the unknown, then return to tell stories that inspire other people to become explorers themselves.

Not just caves. Not just space. Not just science. The universe.

I am not just

  • a Cyber Security consultant
  • a cave diver
  • a Mars-One candidate
  • an author
  • a keynote speaker

I am an Explorer, and everything else supports that identity.

Financial Engine vs Meaning Engine#

Financial Engine - Cyber Security funds exploration. Consulting on GRC, Threat Intelligence, Auditing, and Training are just ways to make money

Meaning Engine - Work I want to be remembered for.

  • Exploration
  • Trips - Ideas
  • Writing
  • Storytelling
  • Science Communication
  • Speaking

Everything I create should ultimately contribute to one lifelong conversation:

What happens when humans choose to explore the unknown?

  • caves
  • space
  • mapping
  • team leadership
  • curiosity
  • hostile environments
  • science

Nothing should skip my Exploration process.

Experience -> Capture -> Reflect -> Connect ideas -> Digital Garden -> Essays -> Talks -> Books -> Legacy

Digital Garden#

My Digital Garden is not a blog - it is my lifelong research laboratory and everything begins here.

Don’t build a career - build a body of work that still matters in thirty years.

The structure should revolve around enduring ideas instead of projects.

Top-level structure:

  • Exploration
    • Space
    • Cave Diving
  • Storytelling
    • Writing
    • Speaking
    • Comedy
    • Questions, Wonder & Curiosity
  • Data Steward
    • Expedition Notes & Mapping
    • Risk & Technology
    • Scientific Publication

Every note should answer “Why does this matter to exploration, storytelling, or being a data steward?”

Explorer’s Index#

The Explorer’s Index becomes the foundation of everything. It is not just for one book. It becomes the archive of my life. Every important experience eventually enters it.

Each dive contains:

  • Story
  • Lesson
  • Humour
  • Wonder
  • Links

Over decades this becomes books, talks, documentaries, essays, & presentations.

517 Book#

This is not a dive log. It is the story of becoming an explorer. The chronology matters, and the themes emerge naturally.

The protagonist is not the caves. The protagonist is the gradual transformation of Josh Richards into an explorer capable of answering the call to Laos.

***Writing Style

  • Funny. Honest. Self-deprecating.
  • Scientific without becoming academic.
  • Technically accurate without becoming technical.
  • Write for someone who has never worn scuba gear.
  • Teach without lecturing.

Decision Filter#

Does this help me become a better explorer-storyteller?

If yes: Do it. If it only earns money: Use it to fund the mission. If it does neither: Decline. If it's not a hell yes, it's a hell no

I will not teach exploration from a classroom - I chose to be someone who explores, then comes back to tell the story.

Everything in your life needs to be aligned with going somewhere unknown and return with a story that sparks curiosity in others.
