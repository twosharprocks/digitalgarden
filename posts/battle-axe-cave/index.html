2019-Dec: MW walked around forest checking clear spots identified from satellite imagery. Forestry sign showing “L254” with significant cave found with some water. Further investgation required (dry caving)

2023-July: MW, BC & NB revisited with caving gear. Interesting but relatively small dry cave with low probability of leads

References#

  • LSECR: Page 496 Like nearby Witches Cave (5L253), Battle-Axe Cave is a 15 x 10 metre wide, 8m deep sinkhole with sheer sides and lots of old domestic rubbish. Some interesting large “columns” and stalactitic decoration (“like battle-axes” - again refer to Kevin Mott!) can also be seen on its northern side. This cave has also not yet been fully explored, but it is reportedly quite large underground and probably connects with Witches Cave in some manner.
