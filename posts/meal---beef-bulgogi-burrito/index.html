Meal - Beef Bulgogi Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #meal-prep 

Ingredients#

  • 500g steak, very thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 Asian pear, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2cm ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 large burrito wraps
  • Cooked rice
  • Kimchi
  • Sliced spring onion
  • Kewpie mayonnaise

Directions#

  1. Mix beef with onion, pear, soy, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, gochujang and sesame seeds.
  2. Marinate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in batches over high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Layer rice, kimchi, beef, mayonnaise and spring onion on each wrap.
  5. Roll tightly and toast in a dry pan or sandwich press.
