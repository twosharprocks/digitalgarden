First things first…


This wonderful bit of news rolled in last Wednesday night, just minutes after I’d been talking to a journalist from The Universe about how the Mars One candidates were all eagerly awaiting the next update. There wasn’t any real surprises here – the 352 who dropped out either did so willingly or were told 2 months ago that they weren’t medically fit enough to continue, and everyone who passed their medical has been invited to an interview. But it certainly becomes far more real every step of the way, and dropping from 1058 candidates to 706 has been no different.

As the email says, next stage of selection is the regional interviews. No idea on wherethe regional interviews for Australia will be held, but a likely option is Sydney – New South Wales & Queensland have the highest number of candidates (8 candidates in each), however it’s a direct flight from Sydney to Los Angeles where the Mars One selection committee is primarily based. But we’ll see, they could come to Perth via Melbourne for all I know

Although it’s pretty unlikely…

Where ever it ends up being, I’m committed to being there for it. I’d had grand plans of heading overseas a bit this year, including doing a course in Costa Rica on sustainability, but everywhere I look at the moment there seems to be more & more things I can do to promote Mars One in Australia, and that’s ultimately what I left Europe to do. I’ve locked down plenty of school visits in the next few months to talk to Year 6-12′s about sending people one-way to Mars, submitted an application to run 4 different events at National Science Week in August (more info after their approval in a few weeks), and I’m running workshops at the Extreme Science Experience on May 22nd.

But the best bit of news to share is that I’ve joined the organising committee for Australia’s very first SpaceUp conference!


SpaceUp is a series of space-based **un-**conferences around the world – essentially a free-form conference, where people negotiate session times to present specifically on topics the other attendees want to hear about (and not just what the organisers think they want to hear about). SpaceUp Australia will be the first of it’s kind hosted at Flinders University, running from 9am till 6pm on September 28th. We’re looking for interesting space-peeps to present/skype so if you’re interested in being involved, get in touch!

Very cool indeed – looking forward to being able to tell you all more about it in a few weeks time. Till then, stay awesome.
