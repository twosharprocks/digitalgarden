Mini Essay - Where do I want to be in 2028

Created: 2026-01-22
Updated: 2026-06-19
Status: tree
Tags: #mini-essay #writing 
Related: Mini Essays Writing

There’s no denying that the last few months have been pretty turbulent, and I have a whole series of fairly significant life changes heading my way in the next month too. My perspective on life has massively shifted since turning 40 in August, leaving ElectraNet in September, and especially since Trip - Antarctica - 2025 Nov so it’s natural to look ahead and wonder where I’m trying to go next.

While everyone seems to put a lot of focus on turning 40, having drinks with Cairo last night reminded me that I’m still a massive Htchhikers Guide to the Galaxy nerd so I’ve always been more curious about what my life would look like at age 42. I’ll hit that milestone in August 2027, so I’m really curious about what my life is going to look like 4 months after that in 2028.

Between now and then I’ll be finally embracing my desire to live in the VW Transporter; I’ll have the experience and certifications to do any kind of Cyber Security work I want (realistically I’m already at that point, but even more so with my plans for 2026), and I’ll have achieved a lifelong dream of diving at Bikini Atoll as part of Trip - Bikini Atoll - 2028 June. So the real question is what I’d like to do after all of that? And what do I want to do to prepare for the life I want to live at age 42?

Working in Antarctica is the most obvious option right now. After the experience of Trip - Antarctica - 2025 Nov I know that I absolutely the incrediblely stark and beautiful nature of the place, but I also know I don’t want to go back as a tourist or really as a tour guide. I’d love to do that in the Arctic, but there’s a huge part of me that desperately wants to truly live and work in that environment for an extended period of time. I’m keenly aware that it’d disconnect me from #diving for an extended period, but it’d also disconnect me from much of my current life too, and that has a lot of appeal. It’s not that I want to disappear from the world, but I’d like to turn the noise down and be pulled in fewer directions at once. In the same way I love organising a pet/housesit so I can work on a book, I love the idea of extended disconnection from the outside world so I can work on the things that I truly care about.

The idea of studying for a masters in Slovenia is also present, but the more I reflect on it the more I realise that I’ve been down this path before - my physical degree is still at a university in the Netherlands because I was deadset about enrolling in a masters there, then realised that a return to uni would be interesting but wouldn’t actually move me forward very much. Anything that I find interesting, I could (and likely should) write a book about instead. There are so many books I’d like to write, and the ideas clog up the longer they go untapped, so studying for something that doesn’t have a clear outcome (like studying for a CISA certification as a pathway to becoming an IRAP Assessor) doesn’ make a whole lot of sense, but I know I get wrapped up in the enthusiasm of how cool it’d be to live in another country and study something I’m passionate about. Do you need to do that within a university framework? No, but that doesn’t mean you need to reject the idea outright - just be very wary of allowing the fantasy override the practicalities of investing in the idea.

The are many other things on your Bucket List and 101 Things - To Do which I want to put energy into, but they don’t leap to mind right now without looking at them. One thing that I might also look at doing is a Long Solo - getting on a bike, kayak, or even on foot to cover a significant distance. Much like the idea of Study - Masters though, there kind of needs to be a reason to get to the end of it. Is it about Writing Books? There are far too many other things you want to write about more than what it’s like being on the move for months. The one thing that you know you can do endlessly is write, so having the achievement of a Long Solo would be nice, there are plenty of other things in my past to be proud of already, and I’d rather write about them first.

So what do I want to be doing in 2028 after turning 42? I want to be a travelling author. I might be in Antarctica, I might be on a boat travelling the world, I might be living in a wood cabin by a lake in a pine forest… But whatever it is I’m doing, I want to be working on writing Books. Everything else is a way of experiencing the world so I can create stories in my Writing. So keep doing the things you’re doing and working towards what you’ve made a priority, but remember that in two years you want to be doing things that directly support writing more books, rather than hindering or distracting me from the goal of writing 10 books in your life.
