Josh Richards – 2016 “Cosmic Nomad” Global Tour#

****After spending a week locked in a box to promote the DVD release of The Martian, I’ve escaped and can proudly announce that throughout 2016 I’ll be touring my new comedy show “Cosmic Nomad” globally – first around Australia before South Africa, the UK, Ireland, and throughout the US!

Cosmic Nomad is both my most personal and funniest show yet – looking at about how my life has changed since I was shortlisted to the Mars 100, and I can’t wait to premiere it at the Royal Institute’s Science Exchange for the Adelaide Fringe next week!

I’m also proud to say that I’ll be heading straight from Los Angeles to Haifa (Israel) to be a Teaching Associate at the International Space University’s 2016 Space Studies Program (SSP16)! Apparently my work as a TA at this year’s Southern Hemisphere Space Studies Program in Adelaide – along with my ability to pretend to be Mark Watney in Sydney – left enough of an impression that they’re getting me to help out on the 9 week program too 😉

It’s just as well that SSP16 finishes on September 1st, because Mars One’s final astronaut selection phase starts on September 3rd. If I get through I’ll be one of 24 (selected from the remaining 100 astronaut candidates) to start full-time training in 2017 ahead of the scheduled launch in 2026.

Like I said – it’s going to be one hell of a year.
