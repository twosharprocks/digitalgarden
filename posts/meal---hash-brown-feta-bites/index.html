Meal - Hash Brown Feta Bites

Created: 2026-04-16
Updated: 2026-07-08
Status: seed
Tags: #meal #snack #feta #quick #oven #vegetarian 
Related: Meals

Ingredients#

  • Hash browns (frozen or shredded potato) - 400g
  • Feta cheese - 150g (crumbled or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Flour or breadcrumbs - 2 tbsp (optional, for binding)
  • Oil spray or drizzle
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder
    • Paprika

Directions#

  1. If using frozen hash browns, thaw slightly
  2. Mix hash browns with egg, seasoning, and optional binder
  3. Form small portions and press feta into the centre (or mix feta through)
  4. Shape into balls or patties
  5. Place on a lined tray and lightly spray with oil
  6. Bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy
  7. Serve hot

Macros#

  • Calories - 340
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 30g
  • Fat - 20g

Notes#

  • Confirmed as a feta + hash brown bite-style recipe
  • Likely variations include coating feta in hash browns or mixing through
  • Good as a snack, side, or brunch item
