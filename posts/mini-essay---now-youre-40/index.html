Mini Essay - Now You’re 40

Created: 2025-11-06
Updated: 2025-11-06
Status: tree
Tags: #mini-essay #writing 
Related: Mini Essays Writing Philosophy

Turning 40 hasn’t had any obvious impact on me so far, at least not in the way that turning 30 did as I discussed in Mini Essay - Why you didn’t think you’d make it to 30. I still joke about not living particularly long because of the stuff I do while cave diving, but the last 10 years haven’t had the same edge to them that the previous 10 did. As I’ve written elsewhere, I think being involved with Mars One made me think about where I’d be in 10 years time (eg. on my way to mars) rather than where I’d be when I turned a particular age.

What’s interesting now though is I’ve turned a specific age (40) and I’m no longer involved in something that would see me leaving Earth to “die” after a specific period of time. In a way I feel like I’m floating directionless, but it’s more than I’m untethered from any external sense of time - I can do things that I care about and it doesn’t matter as much when they happen. I fear that I’m slipping into that “normie” sense that subconsciously I’m acting like I’ll live forever, which I absolutely know I will not, but it’s easy to act that way when you don’t have any kind of deadline ahead of you.

Part of me wants to impose a 10 year deadline and ask myself what I’d do before I turn 50, but I’ll tackle that in Mini Essay - Before You’re 50. For now I’m really just asking what “40” means to me, and the reality is “not much”. It was nice having lunch with Chloe and Mum, and I enjoyed Trip - Sulawesi - 2025 Aug as a kind of birthday present to myself, but turning 40 didn’t feel important in any way. It was just another birthday, and it didn’t even feel like a time for reflection. I flew back from Sulawesi, saw my Mum, and then went to work the next day. I did MC an event later that week which also marked my last 4 weeks at ENet… but turning 40 just kind of happened among all the other things that were happening at the same time.

In many ways I think turning 42 will be a whole lot more important than turning 40. It’s not just the obvious allusion to the Hitchhiker’s Guide to the Galaxy, but also the timeline that I’ve sketched out for the next 18-20 months. I’ve earned a series of serious cyber certifications over the last few years (capped by CISSP in June) and it now looks like I’m about to land a fully remote role doing work I really enjoy (or a different but super flexible hybrid role doing stuff I’m good at), so I’m in a position to do comfortable work that doesn’t break me the way trying to study while going into an offices has.

I’ve also broken away from the small-time cave diving politics now, and I’m developing a global reputation for cave exploration which is very exciting. So if I can maintain a stable income doing good, meaningful and flexible remote work while organising world-class cave expeditions, and I can maintain that in the 18-20 months leading to my Trip - Bikini Atoll - 2028 June, then who knows what else I’m capable of! Better yet, who knows what I’ll do after that! There’s no question I want to write books, and pulling all my previous articles together today is going to help with that enormously, just as writing these daily Mini Essays is helping strengthen that writing muscle you’ve let atrophy over the last 5 years.

By all means figure out a list of things to do in the 10 years before I turn 50, but what’s more important is stabilising my home, work, writing, and diving activities over the next 18 months so I can do more of the things I love and do them well. Sort out a new job that suits, make sure that I’m comfortable with my living arrangements, keep asking myself what I’m going to write tomorrow, and figure out what diving trips you need and want to do in order to be ready for Bikini in June 2027. That is MORE than enough to maintain, and I’d be kicking some serious goals if I just maintained those four things as my priorities for the next 18 months.
