Akiyoshidai

#diving 

About

From https://tech-diving.jp/akiyoshidai

What is Akiyoshidai? Akiyoshidai is Japan’s largest karst plateau , located in the central and eastern parts of Mine City, Yamaguchi Prefecture. Its limestone distribution covers approximately 93 km², a scale unparalleled anywhere else in Japan.

More than 400 limestone caves have been identified in Akiyoshidai, including Akiyoshido Cave , one of Japan’s three largest limestone caves and a designated special natural monument.

Akiyoshidai is divided into two plateaus, Higashidai and Nishidai, by the Higashikawa River . Higashidai (Akiyoshidai in the narrow sense) was designated a national park (Akiyoshidai National Park) in 1955 and a special natural monument in 1964. In addition, individual caves are registered as natural monuments, and meticulous care is taken in their maintenance and management.
