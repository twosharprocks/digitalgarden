Chipotle Beef & Crispy Chorizo Gnocchi

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Beef mince - 400g
  • Chorizo - 100–150g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes - 400g
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Garlic - 1 tsp (or 2 cloves)
  • Chipotle paste - 1 tsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Chilli powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Cheese (optional, grated)
  • Fresh herbs (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Sugar - 1/2 tsp

Directions

  1. Add gnocchi to air fryer, drizzle with oil and chilli powder, and cook at 190°C for ~18 minutes until crispy
  2. Dice and cook chorizo in a pan until crispy, then remove and set aside
  3. In the same pan, sauté onion and garlic until softened
  4. Add beef mince and cook for ~5–7 minutes until browned
  5. Stir in chipotle paste, chopped tomatoes, salt, pepper, and sugar
  6. Simmer sauce for several minutes until slightly thickened
  7. Add crispy gnocchi and cooked chorizo back into the pan and mix well
  8. Optional: top with grated cheese and fresh herbs before serving

ChOptima - Post-Dive Checklist

#diving  #rebreather 

Immediately Post-Dive

  • Close DSV
  • Set point to 0.19
  • Check battery voltages
  • Check O2 remaining
  • Check scrubber remaining
  • Close valves, bleed with MAVs
  • Dry HUD contacts
  • Turn HUD & Controller OFF

End of Day

  • Rinse CCR in fresh water
  • Discard/Seal scrubber
  • Disassemble loop - DSV, loop, counterlungs, end cap
  • Flush loop components with warm water + steramine
  • Air dry electronics head
  • Rinse loop components with water
  • Hang hoses & counterlungs to dry (use blower)

Chorizo Smash Breakfast Tacos

#meal  #breakfast  #chorizo  #tacos  #high-protein  #stovetop 

Ingredients

  • Chorizo - 300g
  • Small tortillas - 6
  • Eggs - 3
  • Cheddar cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Bacon - 3–4 rashers (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (caramelised)

Sauce (optional)

  • Mayonnaise
  • Hot sauce
  • Mustard or similar tangy sauce

Directions

  1. Press chorizo directly onto tortillas in a thin layer
  2. Cook meat-side down in a hot pan until crispy
  3. Flip tortillas and crack egg on top (or cook eggs separately)
  4. Add cheese and allow to melt
  5. Top with bacon and caramelised onion
  6. Fold and serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 620
  • Protein - 32g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 38g

  • “Smash taco” method = meat pressed into tortilla and cooked together
  • Breakfast-focused variation of viral smash taco trend
