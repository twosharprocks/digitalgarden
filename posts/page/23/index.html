Bulgaria

#diving 

About

Rhodope Mountains

  • Why: Part of the same karst belt as the Balkans; caves like Duhlata are among the longest in Eastern Europe.
  • Status: Extensive dry cave exploration but fewer cave diving ventures than Western Europe.
  • Potential: High-value survey targets with minimal diving records.
  • Best Season: June to September.

Cameroon

#diving 

About

Mandara Mountains Karst (CLOSE TO NIGERIA - DO NOT TRAVEL)

  • Why: Unique tropical karst topography near the Nigerian border with surface limestone exposures.
  • Status: Negligible caving or diving work; area is geologically promising.
  • Potential: Spring and cave pool diving in little-documented karst regions.
  • Best Season: December to February.
