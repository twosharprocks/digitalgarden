Murra-El-Elevyn - 6N47

#diving  #caving  #nullarbor 

About

Also known as Cocklebiddy 3 Mile Cave, the entrance is a small overhanging doline 20m in diameter. A 20m pitch leads to a loose talus and silt slope which becomes a boulder floored passage after the doline. The passage continues to a small lake at 89m. There are wetas near the entrance of the cave. There was a thylacine carcase found on early trips to the cave. Uphill of the lake leads to further boulder floored chambers with bats. There are 300m of low maze passages beyond the lake, which contains gypsum flowers and needles. The cave breathes in maze section. Beyond the lake underwater, the cave extends into a series of passages and boulder floored chambers with an average depth of 10m. There is a section of silt floor and parts of the cave passages which have a colouration similar to “coffee and cream”. There are three cave passages which have small air chambers.

Musanze Caves

#caving  #diving 

Notes

Rwanda is not currently a mainstream cave diving destination, but it does have significant volcanic and karst features — especially around the Northwest (Musanze) and Western regions (near Lake Kivu and Nyungwe Forest). Several lava tube caves like Musanze Caves exist, and while most are explored on foot, they may include submerged sections not yet fully mapped.

  • Musanze Caves: Approximately 2 km long with 31 known entrances, these caves are part of the lava basaltic layers and are the most frequently visited in Rwanda.

Nettlebed - 5L290

#diving  #caving  #mount-gambier 

About

Nettlebed Cave is home to a range of interesting features for cave divers to explore. The wall-scratchings which can seen once inside “The Gallery” are probable evidence of megafauna, while entrenched seashells provide insight into the cave’s Ice Age inhabitants.

Beyond The Gallery, the cave drops deeper and breaks into additional rooms containing many large, dark silt-covered boulders, black walls and white-clay floored passages.

The cave finishes in a chamber at 28m, but with an actual penetration distance from the surface of 80m.
