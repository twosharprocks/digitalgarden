Caves

Whip Cave? Runaway Cave?

Lost Lake Cave

  • Location: Western plateau, inland from Flying Fish Cove
  • Type: Partially flooded limestone cave
  • Details: Known to contain an underground lake with significant depth. The full extent of the submerged section has never been explored, but it is considered one of the most promising inland sites for potential cave diving.
  • Exploration Status: Dry portions mapped; no documented dive attempts.

Daniel Roux Cave

  • Location: Near the eastern coast, northeast of South Point
  • Type: Dry limestone cave with multiple chambers
  • Details: While primarily dry, reports mention water seepage and active flow in the wet season. Potential exists for deeper sections to be partially flooded.
  • Exploration Status: Surveyed, but no recorded underwater exploration.

The Dales

  • Location: Western coast, near Hugh’s Dale Waterfall
  • Type: Karst spring and stream system
  • Details: Features spring-fed water emerging from the base of limestone cliffs. The area around Hugh’s Dale and Anderson Dale may be connected to subterranean water systems—possible resurgence cave diving target.
  • Exploration Status: Surface features documented; no known underwater exploration.

Hosnies Spring Cave

  • Location: North coast, near Hosnies Spring
  • Type: Coastal karst feature with permanent spring
  • Details: This area features a freshwater spring discharging into a brackish mangrove environment, suggesting a potential coastal cave system or resurgence beneath.
  • Exploration Status: Caves reported nearby but not explored underwater.

Waterfall Cave / Runoff Sites (Unnamed)

  • Location: North-west coast cliffs between Martin Point and Ethel Beach
  • Type: Sea caves and freshwater outflows
  • Details: Rainfall channels sometimes discharge clear freshwater through cliffs, indicating potential connections to karst drainage systems that could be explored from the sea side via cave diving.
  • Exploration Status: Not documented or surveyed for cave access, but satellite imagery shows several sea-level notches.

Dolly Beach & Greta Beach Karst System

  • Location: South-east coast
  • Type: Cliff-edge karst with marine influence
  • Details: Both beaches are bordered by steep limestone with visible dissolution features. No documented caves, but the geological setting is favourable for sea cave development, possibly with partially submerged access.
  • Exploration Status: No known surveys, but suitable for scouting.

CISA

Certified Information Systems Auditor

Focus: Specialises in information systems auditing—planning and performing audits, evaluating governance and controls, and reporting assurance over IT-enabled business processes and information assets. (ISACA)

Industry: Suits organisations with strong assurance and compliance needs such as government, critical infrastructure, finance, healthcare, large enterprises, and consulting/audit firms. (ISACA)

Requirements

  • Pass the CISA Exam (US$575 ISACA Member, US$760 Non-member)
    • Format: 150 multiple-choice questions, scaled score of 450 or higher (on a 200–800 scale) to pass.
    • Duration: 4 hours.
    • Content:
      • Information System Auditing Process (18%)
      • Governance & Management of IT (18%)
      • Information Systems Acquisition, Development & Implementation (12%)
      • Information Systems Operations and Business Resilience (26%)
      • Protection of Information Assets (26%)
  • Relevant work experience
    • At least five years of professional work experience in information systems auditing, control, assurance, or security.
    • Experience must be within the 10 years preceding the application date, and you must have experience in at least 1 of the 5 CISA job practice domains.
    • You have five years after passing the exam to apply for certification.
    • Experience waivers can reduce the required five years by up to a maximum of three years (for example, via combinations of general IS/audit experience and education waivers).
  • Submit the certification application and pay the US$50 application processing fee.
  • Agree to ISACA’s Code of Professional Ethics and ongoing continuing professional education requirements.

CISSP - 1 - Security & Risk Management

#cyber-security  #cissp 

Reference: https://cissprep.net/domain-1-security-and-risk-management/

Import and Export Controls

  • Wassenaar Arrangement
    • 42 countries agreeing not to distribute weapons & cryptographic tools
    • Some states have outright bans on the import of cryptographic technologies (often about preventing challenges to government authority)

Program Effectiveness Evaluation

  • Due care requires effective education, training, and awareness for employees
  • Due diligence requires evaluation of the effectiveness of that training
  • Ask the right questions: Purpose of an awareness program is to enable and empower all organisation members to achieve safer, more secure and more compliant actions when using information
    • Do your workers have the tools, processes, and procedures to do this?
    • Do they know how to use then effectively
    • Do they have the resource have have what they need to deal with the unexpected
  • User Participation
    • List of training outcomes, then test participants, audits, spot checks
  • Social Engineering response
    • Mock attack attempts to determine if personnel respond appropriately
  • Log Reviews
    • Review user activity to determine if it’s compliant with policies

CISSP - 2 - Asset Security

#cyber-security  #cissp 

Information Asset Security

Learning Objectives

  • Identify, classify, and categorize information assets.
  • Explain the importance of treating information as an asset.
  • Differentiate the IT asset management lifecycle from the data security lifecycle.​
  • Relate the data states of in use, in transit, and at rest to the data lifecycle.​
  • Relate the different roles that people and organizations have with respect to data.​
  • Describe the different security control types and categories. ​
  • Explain the use of data security standards and baselines to meet organizational compliance requirements.

Key Topics

  • Provision Information & Assets Securely
  • Information & Asset Handling
  • Manage Data Life Cycle
  • Appropriate Asset Retention
  • Data Security Controls & Compliance
