Meal - Arayes

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef or lamb mince
  • 1 onion, grated and drained
  • 1 jalapeño, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, grated
  • ¼ cup parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp baharat
  • ½ tsp each cumin, paprika, salt and black pepper
  • 4 pita breads, halved
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Mix the mince with onion, jalapeño, garlic, parsley and spices.
  2. Spread a thin, even layer of filling inside each pita half.
  3. Brush the outside lightly with olive oil.
  4. Grill or pan-fry over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side, then cook the open meat edge until the filling is cooked through.
  5. Serve hot with yoghurt or tahini sauce.

Meal - Beef Bulgogi Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 500g steak, very thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 Asian pear, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2cm ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 large burrito wraps
  • Cooked rice
  • Kimchi
  • Sliced spring onion
  • Kewpie mayonnaise

Directions

  1. Mix beef with onion, pear, soy, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, gochujang and sesame seeds.
  2. Marinate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in batches over high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Layer rice, kimchi, beef, mayonnaise and spring onion on each wrap.
  5. Roll tightly and toast in a dry pan or sandwich press.

Meal - Big Mac Toastie

#meal  #beef  #sandwich  #toastie  #quick  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Bread slices - 4
  • Ground beef - 300g
  • Cheese - 4 slices
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Onion - 1/4 (finely diced)
  • Lettuce - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Butter - 1 tbsp

Big Mac Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp
  • Paprika - pinch

Directions

  1. Cook ground beef in a pan, breaking it up and seasoning with salt and pepper
  2. Mix all Big Mac sauce ingredients in a small bowl
  3. Butter the outside of the bread slices
  4. On the inside, layer beef, cheese, pickles, onion, lettuce and sauce
  5. Close sandwich and cook in a pan or sandwich press until golden and crispy
  6. Slice and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 680
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 42g
  • Fat - 38g

  • Corrected from previous misclassification (was incorrectly labelled as a chicken wrap)
  • Classic fast-food style toastie — high calorie, high fat

Meal - Bolognese Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp each oregano, thyme, basil and garlic powder
  • 2 tsp each sweet paprika and dried parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1½ cups tomato passata
  • ⅓ cup water
  • ½ vegetable stock cube
  • Grated cheddar
  • 5–6 mini tortilla wraps
  • ½ tbsp olive oil

Directions

  1. Soften the onion in olive oil, add the beef and brown over high heat for 5–6 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, carrot, passata, tomato purée, seasonings, stock and water.
  3. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Dip each tortilla in the sauce, place it in a hot pan, and layer with cheese and 2–3 tbsp of filling.
  5. Fold and grill both sides until browned and crisp.
