The Shaft - 5L158

#diving  #caving  #mount-gambier 

About

The Shaft is a sinkhole located near Mount Gambier in South Australia. It is a popular destination for cave divers, known for its clear water and interesting internal structure.

The Shaft is the site of a tragic scuba diving incident that occurred on May 28, 1973, which claimed the lives of four recreational scuba divers. The incident led to the formation of the Cave Divers Association of Australia and the development of the South Australian Police Underwater Recovery Squad .
