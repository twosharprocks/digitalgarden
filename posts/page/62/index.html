Meal - Hash Brown Feta Bites

#meal  #snack  #quick  #oven 

Ingredients

  • Hash browns (frozen or shredded potato) - 400g
  • Feta cheese - 150g (crumbled or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Flour or breadcrumbs - 2 tbsp (optional, for binding)
  • Oil spray or drizzle
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. If using frozen hash browns, thaw slightly
  2. Mix hash browns with egg, seasoning, and optional binder
  3. Form small portions and press feta into the centre (or mix feta through)
  4. Shape into balls or patties
  5. Place on a lined tray and lightly spray with oil
  6. Bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 340
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 30g
  • Fat - 20g

Notes

  • Confirmed as a feta + hash brown bite-style recipe
  • Likely variations include coating feta in hash browns or mixing through
  • Good as a snack, side, or brunch item

Meal - Hash Brown, Halloumi & Chorizo Hash

#meal  #breakfast  #chorizo  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Hash browns (frozen or pre-cooked) - 400g
  • Halloumi - 200g (cubed)
  • Chorizo - 150g (sliced)
  • Eggs - 3–4
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Smoked paprika
  • Chilli flakes (optional)

Directions

  1. Cook hash browns in a large pan until crispy and golden, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook chorizo until it releases oils and crisps
  3. Add onion and garlic, cooking until softened
  4. Add halloumi and cook until golden on all sides
  5. Return hash browns to the pan and mix everything together
  6. Create small wells and crack eggs into the pan
  7. Cover and cook until eggs reach desired doneness
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 620
  • Protein - 32g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 40g

Notes

  • Rich, high-fat breakfast or brunch dish
  • Can be adapted with veggies (spinach, peppers) for balance

Meal - Healthy Chicken Nuggets

#meal  #chicken  #air-fryer  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Greek yogurt - 150g
  • Egg - 1
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cut chicken into nugget-sized pieces
  2. Coat in yogurt and egg mixture
  3. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy

Meal - High Protein Breakfast Burritos

#meal  #breakfast  #burrito  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Large tortillas - 10
  • Eggs - 12–14
  • Lean ground meat (beef/turkey/chicken) - 800g–1kg
  • Potatoes or hash browns - 600–800g
  • Cheese - 2 cups (shredded)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Paprika
  • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or hash browns until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook lean meat with seasoning until browned
  3. Scramble eggs in a large pan
  4. Combine eggs, meat, and potatoes
  5. Lay out tortillas and evenly distribute filling
  6. Add cheese on top
  7. Roll tightly into burritos
  8. Optional: toast in pan or wrap for meal prep storage

Macros

  • Calories - 550
  • Protein - 59g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 18g

Notes

  • Confirmed from reel caption (≈59g protein per burrito) :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Designed for bulk prep (≈10 servings)
  • Freezes well and reheats easily
