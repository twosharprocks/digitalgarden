Meal - Bolognese Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp each oregano, thyme, basil and garlic powder
  • 2 tsp each sweet paprika and dried parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1½ cups tomato passata
  • ⅓ cup water
  • ½ vegetable stock cube
  • Grated cheddar
  • 5–6 mini tortilla wraps
  • ½ tbsp olive oil

Directions

  1. Soften the onion in olive oil, add the beef and brown over high heat for 5–6 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, carrot, passata, tomato purée, seasonings, stock and water.
  3. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Dip each tortilla in the sauce, place it in a hot pan, and layer with cheese and 2–3 tbsp of filling.
  5. Fold and grill both sides until browned and crisp.

Meal - Cheese Pull Apart Bread

#meal  #snack  #bread  #cheese  #oven  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Bread loaf (sourdough or cob) - 1
  • Mozzarella - 200g (cubed or shredded)
  • Butter - 3 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Parsley (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cut the bread in a crosshatch pattern (do not slice all the way through)
  2. Stuff mozzarella into the cuts throughout the loaf
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic and seasoning
  4. Pour or brush mixture over the bread, ensuring it gets into the gaps
  5. Wrap loosely in foil and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes
  6. Uncover and bake another 5–10 minutes until crispy and melted
  7. Serve immediately and pull apart

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 14g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • Identified based on “cheese pull” style construction
  • Designed for visual melt/stretch rather than high-protein efficiency
  • Best served immediately while cheese is hot and elastic

Meal - Cheeseburger Bowls

#meal  #beef  #bowl  #burger  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Ground beef - 500g
  • Potatoes (or fries) - 400g
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Lettuce - 1–2 cups (shredded)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)

Burger Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or fries until crispy
  2. Cook ground beef with salt and pepper until browned
  3. Mix burger sauce ingredients in a bowl
  4. Assemble bowls with potatoes, beef, cheese, lettuce, onion, and pickles
  5. Drizzle with burger sauce and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 40g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 22g

Notes

  • Identified as cheeseburger-style bowl based on user verification
  • Typical “burger in a bowl” structure (beef + potato base + sauce)

Meal - Cheesy Bacon Jalapeño Popper Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #bacon 

Ingredients

  • Large tortilla wraps
  • Cream cheese, softened
  • Grated cheddar
  • Grated mozzarella
  • Cooked bacon, crumbled
  • Jalapeños, grilled and sliced

Directions

  1. Mix cream cheese, cheddar, mozzarella, bacon and jalapeños.
  2. Spread the filling over the centre of each tortilla.
  3. Fold the sides inward and roll tightly.
  4. Toast seam-side down in a dry pan, then turn until golden and the cheese has melted.

Meal - Chicken & Chorizo One Pan Rice

#meal  #chicken  #chorizo  #rice  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (diced)
  • Chorizo - 120–150g (sliced)
  • Rice - 250–300g (uncooked, rinsed)
  • Onion - 1 (diced)
  • Red and yellow peppers - 2 (chopped)
  • Garlic - 3 cloves (minced)
  • Chicken stock - 500–700ml
  • Tomato paste - 2 tbsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Smoked paprika - 2 tsp
  • Garlic powder - 1 tsp
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Chilli (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan and cook chorizo until it releases oils
  2. Add onion, peppers, and garlic, cooking until softened
  3. Add chicken and cook until browned
  4. Stir in tomato paste and seasoning
  5. Add rice and mix to coat in flavours
  6. Pour in chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  7. Cover and cook for 15–20 minutes until rice is tender and liquid absorbed
  8. Fluff and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 600
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 18g
