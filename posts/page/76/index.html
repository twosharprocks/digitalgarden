As you sit in the airport at Buenos Aires after such a hefty few days of flying, it’s starting to really dawn on me that I’m about to flying to Ushuaia - the gateway to Antarctica. And in just a few days, I’ll be getting onboard an expedition ship Going to the Antarctic Peninsula!

There’s a big part of me that is simply not processing that fact - that in a few days I’ll be in Antarctica. I’m not sure if it’s the numbness you’ve experienced a lot of these last few years, but it’s not clear if my lack of excitement is because I haven’t come to terms with the reality of the trip yet, or if I’m downplaying it all in an attempt to protect my psyche from potential disappointment.

Mini Essay - Why you didn’t think you’d make it to 30

#mini-essay  #personal  #101-things  #philosophy  #writing 

Throughout most of my 20’s I was certain I wouldn’t be alive to turn 30. It’s not like I can pinpoint exactly where that idea came from, but there’s no question it was a recurring belief that shaped the way I thought and planned my life. If I try to trace it back, I suspect I’ll wind up back at April 19th, 2008. I made a choice that night to live, and it was certainly a close-call, but I’ve used that night as a reference for most of my life since - no matter what awful shit I’ve been through since then, I know that I will never allow myself to be trapped in that mindset ever again, where suicide felt like the only way out.
