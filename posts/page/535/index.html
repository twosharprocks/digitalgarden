  • Source: Google

Cave and exploration data

  • Cave rating: Basic
  • Vertical access: No
  • Tourism potential: Medium
  • Goes to water: Yes

References

  • Google Review: (Norm M - Local Guide) “What an adventure into the Abaco back country! There is a sign on a dirt road off Bootle Highway just South of the Treasure Cay Airport. It points to the direction of the Blue Hole. Once on the dirt road, travel about four miles. After going around a curve to the left you’ll see a short fairly worn dirt road leading into the woods. At the end of that road (about a quarter mile) you’ll find the blue hole in a stand of pine trees. The hole is clear fresh water and about 100’ across. Easy to enter and great for swimming and snorkeling. Rocks (limestone) around the edges is a bit slippery. Scuba diving (certified cave divers only) can and has occurred. Hole is about 254’ deep. There is a layer of hydrogen sulfide at about 60’ that should not be entered due to toxicity but waters above are fine. There are two home made rope swings on the edge of the hole to the right. This is a remote location- go prepared and not alone.”
