Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #creamy  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Pasta - 300g
  • Garlic - 3 cloves
  • Cream - 200ml
  • Parmesan - 1/2 cup
  • Spinach - 1 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta and set aside
  2. Cook chicken and slice
  3. Sauté garlic, add cream and parmesan
  4. Add pasta, chicken and spinach
  5. Stir and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 590
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 22g

About

Speleological Club of Crete (S.P.O.K.) - Runs expeditions

Crete, the largest of the Greek islands, offers significant caving and some limited but promising cave diving opportunities, thanks to its complex geology and extensive karst systems. The island is dominated by limestone and dolomite formations, especially in its mountainous interior, which has led to the development of thousands of caves—some still unexplored or only partially mapped.

CRISC

#cyber-security  #study 

Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control

Focus: Specialises in enterprise IT risk management and control. Ideal for those identifying, assessing and mitigating information systems risks.

Industry: Suits industries with strong risk management requirements like insurance, fintech, healthcare and government institutions.

Requirements

Requirements for CRISC certification - Pass the CRISC Exam (US$575 ISACA Member, US$760 Non-member) - Format: 150 multiple-choice questions, scaled score of >450 out of 800 to pass. - Duration: 4 hours. - **Content: - Governance (26%): IT risk governance, frameworks, policies, and organizational structures. - IT Risk Assessment (20%): Risk identification, evaluation, and analysis. - Risk Response and Reporting (32%): Mitigating risk, implementing controls, and communicating results. - Information Technology and Security (22%): Supporting risk management through IT controls and solutions. - Relevant Work Experience - At least three years of cumulative, professional-level work experience in at least two of the four CRISC domains. - At least one year of experience in either IT Risk Identification or IT Risk Assessment - Experience must be verified and within the 10 years preceding the certification application or within five years after passing the exam.

Crispy Chicken Loaded Fries

Ingredients
