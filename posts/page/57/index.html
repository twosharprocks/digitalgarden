Meal - Air Fryer Nachos

#meal  #snack  #air-fryer  #quick  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips - 150–200g
  • Cheese - 1.5–2 cups (cheddar or blend)
  • Sweet corn - 1/2 cup
  • Bell peppers - 1 cup (chopped)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Jalapeños (optional)

Seasoning

  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes
  • Salt (optional)

Optional Add-ons

  • Cooked beef or chicken
  • Beans
  • Sour cream
  • Guacamole
  • Salsa

Directions

  1. Line air fryer basket with parchment paper
  2. Add a layer of tortilla chips
  3. Top with vegetables, corn, and cheese
  4. Repeat layers if needed (chips → toppings → cheese)
  5. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes
  6. Air fry at 180°C for 5–7 minutes until cheese melts
  7. Remove and add optional toppings (sour cream, salsa, etc.)
  8. Serve immediately

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 18g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 30g

Meal - Arayes

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef or lamb mince
  • 1 onion, grated and drained
  • 1 jalapeño, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, grated
  • ¼ cup parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp baharat
  • ½ tsp each cumin, paprika, salt and black pepper
  • 4 pita breads, halved
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Mix the mince with onion, jalapeño, garlic, parsley and spices.
  2. Spread a thin, even layer of filling inside each pita half.
  3. Brush the outside lightly with olive oil.
  4. Grill or pan-fry over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side, then cook the open meat edge until the filling is cooked through.
  5. Serve hot with yoghurt or tahini sauce.

Meal - Beef Bulgogi Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 500g steak, very thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 Asian pear, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2cm ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 large burrito wraps
  • Cooked rice
  • Kimchi
  • Sliced spring onion
  • Kewpie mayonnaise

Directions

  1. Mix beef with onion, pear, soy, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, gochujang and sesame seeds.
  2. Marinate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in batches over high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Layer rice, kimchi, beef, mayonnaise and spring onion on each wrap.
  5. Roll tightly and toast in a dry pan or sandwich press.

Meal - Big Mac Toastie

#meal  #beef  #quick  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Bread slices - 4
  • Ground beef - 300g
  • Cheese - 4 slices
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Onion - 1/4 (finely diced)
  • Lettuce - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Butter - 1 tbsp

Big Mac Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp
  • Paprika - pinch

Directions

  1. Cook ground beef in a pan, breaking it up and seasoning with salt and pepper
  2. Mix all Big Mac sauce ingredients in a small bowl
  3. Butter the outside of the bread slices
  4. On the inside, layer beef, cheese, pickles, onion, lettuce and sauce
  5. Close sandwich and cook in a pan or sandwich press until golden and crispy
  6. Slice and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 680
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 42g
  • Fat - 38g
