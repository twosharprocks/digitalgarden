Notes - Too Many Interests

#knowledge-management  #interests 

Reference: https://youtu.be/lQFOeC3maeI?si=NVyRDJ759R7_Uhl2

Too Many Interests

You can’t follow your interests if you have no energy

  • Working all day, overstimulation,
  • Unfair to expect all to be able to come home and jump straight in
  • More about energy rather than time
    • How do you enjoy your hobby with a fresh mind and happy outlook
  • Save the heavy stuff for the morning when you’re fresh
    • Probably start to hate the hobby
    • Tired? Focus on relaxed aspects
    • Fresh? Focus on heavy
    • Split hobby elements into “Relaxing” and “Demanding”
  • Look at your energy levels and where you can give best (for me: Mornings)
  • Woodworking vs Camping - Split your hobbies into Relaxing vs Demanding
    • Woodworking you can roll straight into (Relaxing & Rejuvinating)
    • Camping requires more setup & investment (More Demanding)
  • Money
    • Must meet basic needs to fund hobbies
    • Expensive hobbies
    • Money can buy time to embrace hobbies
  • Take a break when you get home
    • Take half an hour to relax and disconnect from work
    • Get back in creative mood
    • Figure out how to have a better break
  • Carve out space for your hobbys/interests
    • Don’t be completely selfless
    • Express that it’s important to you, and an important part of who you are
  • Say no to some of your hobbies (or atleast say no to new ones)
    • The more you do, the more you dilute
    • Kill your darlings
    • Cut it down to 2-3 main hobbies - Intellectual, Physical, Social
  • Hobby Seasons
    • Focus hard on one hobby for several months, then steer away to something else
    • You’ll have muscle memory of the hobby (so you can come back)
    • You’re free to return to any hobby whenever you want
      • If you miss something after you stop, that’s important - you may stick with it your whole life
    • You can’t do everything
  • Busy with other priorities? Find ways to do a little of something you love
    • Let the big thing go, but find small ways to do it instead
    • Reduce the time
  • Quality over Quantity - focused practice
    • Have a plan, set goals, reflect after each session (+ buy quality with coaches)
