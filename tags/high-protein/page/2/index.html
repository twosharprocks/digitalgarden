Meal - Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #creamy  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Pasta - 300g
  • Garlic - 3 cloves
  • Cream - 200ml
  • Parmesan - 1/2 cup
  • Spinach - 1 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta and set aside
  2. Cook chicken and slice
  3. Sauté garlic, add cream and parmesan
  4. Add pasta, chicken and spinach
  5. Stir and serve

Meal - Giant Chicken Poppers

#meal  #chicken  #air-fryer  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken mince - 500g
  • Lite Laughing Cow Cheese - 4 wedges
  • Cornflakes - 60g
  • Greek Yogurt - 150g
  • Seasoning
    • Buffalo Chicken
    • Garlic
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Mix chicken mince with seasonings in a large glass bowl
  2. Cover a piece of cheese with mince mix and roll into a ball
  3. Make more balls with cheese in the middle until the mince is gone
  4. Place the balls into the freezer for 30-60 minutes
  5. Blend the cornflakes with additional seasoning
  6. Roll the chicken balls in Greek yogurt
  7. Roll the chicken balls in the cornflake and seasoning mix
  8. Spray the chicken balls with oil
  9. Cook in the air fryer for 15-20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes

Macros

  • Calories - 254 per serving
  • Protein - 36g
  • Carbs - 16g
  • Fat - 4g

  • Macros are estimated from the ingredients used in the original template

Meal - Hash Brown, Halloumi & Chorizo Hash

#meal  #breakfast  #hash  #chorizo  #halloumi  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Hash browns (frozen or pre-cooked) - 400g
  • Halloumi - 200g (cubed)
  • Chorizo - 150g (sliced)
  • Eggs - 3–4
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Smoked paprika
  • Chilli flakes (optional)

Directions

  1. Cook hash browns in a large pan until crispy and golden, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook chorizo until it releases oils and crisps
  3. Add onion and garlic, cooking until softened
  4. Add halloumi and cook until golden on all sides
  5. Return hash browns to the pan and mix everything together
  6. Create small wells and crack eggs into the pan
  7. Cover and cook until eggs reach desired doneness
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 620
  • Protein - 32g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 40g

  • Can be adapted with veggies (spinach, peppers) for balance

Meal - Healthy Chicken Nuggets

#meal  #chicken  #air-fryer  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Greek yogurt - 150g
  • Egg - 1
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cut chicken into nugget-sized pieces
  2. Coat in yogurt and egg mixture
  3. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
