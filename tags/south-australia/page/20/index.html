The entrance takes the form of a low slide-down under a 2 metre high limestone ledge at the southern edge of an indistinct degraded doline, and it quickly opens up into a small muddy cham- ber about a metre high, a few metres wide and 12 metres long. The water-table barely intersects the cave, flooding the flat floor during the September visit and completely drying out during a subsequent visit some 4 months later, at the height of the local summer season.
