Chicken & Chorizo One Pan Rice

#meal  #chicken  #chorizo  #rice  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (diced)
  • Chorizo - 120–150g (sliced)
  • Rice - 250–300g (uncooked, rinsed)
  • Onion - 1 (diced)
  • Red and yellow peppers - 2 (chopped)
  • Garlic - 3 cloves (minced)
  • Chicken stock - 500–700ml
  • Tomato paste - 2 tbsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Smoked paprika - 2 tsp
  • Garlic powder - 1 tsp
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Chilli (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan and cook chorizo until it releases oils
  2. Add onion, peppers, and garlic, cooking until softened
  3. Add chicken and cook until browned
  4. Stir in tomato paste and seasoning
  5. Add rice and mix to coat in flavours
  6. Pour in chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  7. Cover and cook for 15–20 minutes until rice is tender and liquid absorbed
  8. Fluff and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 600
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 18g

  • Similar to simplified paella-style cooking (one-pan absorption method)
  • Very strong meal prep option

Chicken Alfredo Tortellini

#meal  #chicken  #tortellini  #pasta  #creamy  #one-pan  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 1 lb
  • Butter - 2 tbsp
  • Garlic - 1 tbsp minced
  • Frozen tortellini - 19 oz bag
  • Alfredo sauce - 15 oz jar
  • Mozzarella - 1 1/2 cups shredded
  • Bacon - 6 pieces
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Onion powder
    • Paprika
    • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp, then set aside
  2. Season the chicken and cook in the butter until browned and cooked through
  3. Add the garlic and cook briefly until fragrant
  4. Add the frozen tortellini and Alfredo sauce and stir to combine
  5. Simmer until the tortellini is cooked through
  6. Stir through the mozzarella until melted
  7. Crumble the bacon over the top and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 552 per serving
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 29g
  • Fat - 30g

Hash Brown, Halloumi & Chorizo Hash

#meal  #breakfast  #hash  #chorizo  #halloumi  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Hash browns (frozen or pre-cooked) - 400g
  • Halloumi - 200g (cubed)
  • Chorizo - 150g (sliced)
  • Eggs - 3–4
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Smoked paprika
  • Chilli flakes (optional)

Directions

  1. Cook hash browns in a large pan until crispy and golden, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook chorizo until it releases oils and crisps
  3. Add onion and garlic, cooking until softened
  4. Add halloumi and cook until golden on all sides
  5. Return hash browns to the pan and mix everything together
  6. Create small wells and crack eggs into the pan
  7. Cover and cook until eggs reach desired doneness
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 620
  • Protein - 32g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 40g

  • Confirmed from reel caption (halloumi + chorizo + hash brown combination) :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Rich, high-fat breakfast or brunch dish
  • Can be adapted with veggies (spinach, peppers) for balance

Paprika Chicken & Chorizo Gnocchi

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Olive oil - 1/2 tbsp
  • Chorizo - 70g
  • Red onion - 1 small
  • Chicken breast - 350g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (crushed)
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook the chorizo until it starts to crisp
  2. Add the chopped red onion and cook until softened
  3. Add the chicken breast, season with paprika, salt and pepper, and cook until coloured
  4. Stir through the garlic, tomato puree and chopped sun dried tomatoes
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and add the gnocchi
  6. Simmer until the gnocchi is tender and the sauce reduces slightly
  7. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  8. Fold through the spinach until wilted
  9. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 562 per serving
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 52g
  • Fat - 16g

Smokey Bacon Gnocchi

#meal  #gnocchi  #bacon  #high-protein  #one-pan  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Bacon - 5–6 rashers (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef or chicken - 400–800g (optional high-protein boost)
  • Zucchini - 1–2 (diced)
  • Tomato sauce (tinned or passata) - 400–500g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Smoked paprika

Directions

  1. Fry chopped bacon in a large pan until crispy, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook garlic and ground meat (if using) until browned
  3. Add zucchini and cook until softened
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and smoked paprika
  5. Add gnocchi and simmer until tender and sauce thickens
  6. Return bacon to the pan and mix through
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 16g

Notes

  • Confirmed structure from indexed sources (gnocchi + bacon + tomato base)
  • Often presented as a high-protein, meal-prep friendly dish
  • Variations include adding cheese or baking for a crispy top
