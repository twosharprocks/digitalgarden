High Protein Mexican Bowl

#meal  #beef  #rice  #mexican  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Rice - 200g (uncooked, portioned ~50g per serve)
  • Extra lean beef mince - 500g
  • Tinned tomatoes - 400g
  • Taco seasoning - 1 packet (or ~20g)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (optional)
  • Oil spray (minimal)

Optional Add-ons

  • Greek yogurt (as sour cream substitute)
  • Lettuce
  • Cheese (light)
  • Salsa or hot sauce

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions and set aside
  2. In a pan, cook onion and garlic until softened (optional)
  3. Add lean beef mince and cook until browned
  4. Stir in taco seasoning and tinned tomatoes
  5. Simmer until thickened and well combined
  6. Portion rice into containers or bowls
  7. Top with beef mixture and optional extras
  8. Serve or store for meal prep

Macros

  • Calories - 480
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 59g
  • Fat - 9g

Notes

  • Macros confirmed from reel caption :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Designed as a high-protein, low-fat meal prep option
  • Very scalable and easy to batch cook

Loaded Breakfast Wrap

#meal  #breakfast  #wrap  #high-protein  #quick 

Ingredients

  • Large wraps - 2
  • Eggs - 4
  • Bacon - 4 rashers
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Hash browns - 2
  • Spinach or greens (optional)
  • Sauce (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook bacon until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook hash browns until golden
  3. Scramble eggs in the same pan
  4. Layer eggs, bacon, hash browns, cheese, and greens into wraps
  5. Fold tightly into burrito shape
  6. Toast in pan until golden and crispy on the outside
  7. Slice and serve

  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 26g

No-Bake Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Bars

#meal  #dessert  #no-bake  #chocolate  #snack 

Ingredients

Cookie Dough Base

  • Butter - 1/2 cup (softened)
  • Brown sugar - 3/4 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Milk - 1/3 cup
  • Flour (heat-treated or oat flour) - 2 cups
  • Salt - pinch
  • Chocolate chips - 1 cup

Chocolate Topping

  • Chocolate chips - 1/2 cup
  • Butter or coconut oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. In a bowl, mix softened butter and brown sugar until smooth
  2. Add vanilla and milk, mixing well
  3. Stir in flour and salt until a dough forms
  4. Fold in chocolate chips
  5. Press mixture evenly into a lined baking dish
  6. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth
  7. Pour chocolate over the top and spread evenly
  8. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until firm
  9. Slice into bars and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 5g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • No-bake and egg-free → safe-to-eat cookie dough style :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
  • Chill time is essential for firm texture
  • Can substitute oat/almond flour for a healthier version

Paprika Chicken & Chorizo Gnocchi

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Olive oil - 1/2 tbsp
  • Chorizo - 70g
  • Red onion - 1 small
  • Chicken breast - 350g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (crushed)
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook the chorizo until it starts to crisp
  2. Add the chopped red onion and cook until softened
  3. Add the chicken breast, season with paprika, salt and pepper, and cook until coloured
  4. Stir through the garlic, tomato puree and chopped sun dried tomatoes
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and add the gnocchi
  6. Simmer until the gnocchi is tender and the sauce reduces slightly
  7. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  8. Fold through the spinach until wilted
  9. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 562 per serving
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 52g
  • Fat - 16g
