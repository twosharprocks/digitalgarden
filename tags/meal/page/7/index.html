Meal - High Protein Breakfast Burritos

#meal  #breakfast  #burrito  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Large tortillas - 10
  • Eggs - 12–14
  • Lean ground meat (beef/turkey/chicken) - 800g–1kg
  • Potatoes or hash browns - 600–800g
  • Cheese - 2 cups (shredded)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Paprika
  • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or hash browns until crispy, then set aside
  2. Cook lean meat with seasoning until browned
  3. Scramble eggs in a large pan
  4. Combine eggs, meat, and potatoes
  5. Lay out tortillas and evenly distribute filling
  6. Add cheese on top
  7. Roll tightly into burritos
  8. Optional: toast in pan or wrap for meal prep storage

Macros

  • Calories - 550
  • Protein - 59g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 18g

  • Designed for bulk prep (≈10 servings)
  • Freezes well and reheats easily

Meal - High Protein Chicken Katsu Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer  #easy 

Ingredients

Chicken Katsu

  • Chicken breast - 600g (sliced or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Cornflakes or panko breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder

Fried Rice

  • Cooked rice - 3–4 cups (preferably day-old)
  • Eggs - 2
  • Garlic - 2–3 cloves (minced)
  • Soy sauce - 2–3 tbsp
  • Mixed vegetables - 1–2 cups
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. Coat chicken in egg and seasoning, then cover in crushed cornflakes or panko
  2. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  3. In a pan, scramble eggs and set aside
  4. Fry garlic in oil until fragrant
  5. Add rice, vegetables, and soy sauce, stirring well
  6. Return eggs and mix through
  7. Slice crispy chicken katsu and place over fried rice
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 560
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 12g

Notes

  • Confirmed from reel caption and structure :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Combines Japanese-style chicken katsu with classic fried rice technique
  • Ideal for high-protein meal prep

Meal - High Protein Chicken Sausage Breakfast Burritos

#meal  #breakfast  #burrito  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken sausage - 4 links (sliced)
  • Eggs - 6
  • Egg whites - 500ml (approx 16 oz)
  • Low-fat cottage cheese - 1/2 cup
  • Large tortillas - 6
  • Cheese - 1–1.5 cups (shredded)
  • Oil spray

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook chicken sausage in a pan until browned
  2. In a bowl, whisk eggs, egg whites, and cottage cheese together
  3. Pour mixture into pan and scramble until fully cooked
  4. Combine eggs with cooked sausage
  5. Lay out tortillas and evenly distribute filling
  6. Add shredded cheese on top
  7. Roll tightly into burritos
  8. Optional: toast lightly or store for meal prep

Macros

  • Calories - 500
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 40g
  • Fat - 14g

Notes

  • Confirmed from indexed ingredient list (chicken sausage + egg whites + cottage cheese) :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Leaner, higher-protein version of breakfast burritos
  • Ideal for batch cooking and freezing

Meal - High Protein Crispy Honey Garlic Chicken Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • Diced chicken breast - 1.05kg
  • Rice - 700g cooked
  • Garlic - 35ml minced
  • Honey - 60ml
  • Soy sauce - 60ml
  • Eggs - 7
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook or air fry the diced chicken until browned and crispy
  2. Scramble the eggs in a large pan and set aside
  3. Fry the garlic in a little oil until fragrant
  4. Add the rice and soy sauce and toss well
  5. Stir through the honey and return the cooked chicken to the pan
  6. Fold through the scrambled egg
  7. Portion and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 546 per serving
  • Protein - 51g
  • Carbs - 67g
  • Fat - 7g
