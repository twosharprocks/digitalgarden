Meal - Spicy Peanut Dumplings

#meal  #dumplings  #peanut  #spicy  #quick  #oven 

Ingredients

  • Frozen dumplings - 12 to 16
  • Spring onion - 2 sprigs
  • Peanut butter - 2 tbsp
  • Red curry paste - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 1 tsp minced
  • Ginger - 1 tsp minced
  • Soy sauce - 1 tbsp
  • Sesame oil - 1 tsp
  • Coconut milk - 1/2 cup
  • Chicken stock - 1/2 cup

Directions

  1. In a baking dish, mix together the peanut butter, red curry paste, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, coconut milk and chicken stock
  2. Add the frozen dumplings to the sauce
  3. Bake until the dumplings are cooked through and the sauce has thickened
  4. Top with sliced spring onion and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 360 per serving
  • Protein - 13g
  • Carbs - 36g
  • Fat - 18g

Notes

  • Macros are estimated from a 4-serving batch

Meal - Spicy Steak & Cheese Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 16 low-carb wraps
  • 900g lean steak
  • Reduced-fat cheese
  • 800g cooked rice
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Paprika, garlic powder and cumin
  • Coriander
  • Onion and capsicum

Sauce

  • 300g fat-free Greek yoghurt
  • 70g light sour cream
  • 50g chipotle in adobo
  • Juice of ½ lime

Directions

  1. Season and cook the steak, then slice or chop it.
  2. Sauté the onion and capsicum until tender.
  3. Blend or stir together the yoghurt, sour cream, chipotle and lime.
  4. Divide rice, steak, vegetables, cheese and sauce between wraps.
  5. Roll tightly and toast, or wrap and freeze for meal prep.

Meal - Stuffed Crust Pizza

#meal  #pizza  #tortillas  #low-carb  #oven 

Ingredients

  • Tortillas - 2
  • Cream cheese - 60g
  • Sausage - 100g cooked
  • Mozzarella - 120g
  • Pizza sauce - 60g
  • Pizza toppings - as desired
  • Seasoning
    • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Lightly crisp the tortillas so they hold their shape
  2. Spread cream cheese, sausage and some mozzarella around the outer edge between the two tortillas to create the stuffed crust
  3. Add pizza sauce to the centre
  4. Top with the remaining mozzarella and your preferred toppings
  5. Bake for about 20 minutes until golden and bubbling
  6. Slice and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 540 per serving
  • Protein - 29g
  • Carbs - 29g
  • Fat - 34g

Notes

  • Macros are estimated because the reel description does not provide exact weights for every topping
