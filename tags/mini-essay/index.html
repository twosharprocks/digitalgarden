When I first started it, I was utterly convinced that it was absolutely brilliant - that Feynman was really connecting with that passion for the universe that I crave, that he had plenty of character and quirks that made him an engaging personality who genuinely loved learning about the universe. I’m sure that’s all true, but the further into the book I got the less I liked him - the whole thing started to feel like a collection of little stories from his life that didn’t build or develop into anything. Mini Essay - 15000 days

#writing  #mini-essay 

On Friday I hit the 15,000 day mark - 15,000 days that I’ve been alive and living on this planet. It’s strange to think that I started tracking my days alive when I first discovered Mars One, and stranger still to discover that I hadn’t created a markdown file for Mars One until just now. It still feels like it’s a thing that I’m trying to forget or let go, and yet it was one of the things that lit me up the most in the world. I think the hurt I experienced with Mars One and the criticism it (and I in turn) received really left a major mark on my psyche, and in many ways I’m really just recovering from it and finding my feet again all these years after the program officially shutdown. I love that I track my days on Earth, and especially enjoyed that I might one day be able to countdown the number of days I’d have left - not because I was dying, but because I was leaving for another world. Seeing my time on Earth as being finite and measurable provided an enormous sense of purpose - that days are never to be wasted, and that I am capable of doing so very much with what I have that each day on Earth is an opportunity to make things better for myself and for others.

Mini Essay - Appeal of a dark, empty forest outpost

#mini-essay  #writing 

For years now there’s been something drawing you to the quiet darkness of an otherwise empty forest outpost. There are critical elements to this interest though which are non-negotiable - it they’re not present, then the entire vibe is thrown off and it doesn’t work.

First and foremost is rain sounds. You knew the moment you heard the sounds of rain as the ship passed Saturn in Interstellar that it’s the sound that relaxes you most - the sounds that drowns out the other noise, any intrusive thoughts you might have, and never distracts you in itself. Right now you’ve got the Dark Forest Ambient Focus Music from YouTube on the other screen as you type, and the ambient synths are a fraction distracting… but the rain sounds that run underneath help make up for it.
