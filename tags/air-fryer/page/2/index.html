Meal - High Protein Crispy Honey Garlic Chicken Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • Diced chicken breast - 1.05kg
  • Rice - 700g cooked
  • Garlic - 35ml minced
  • Honey - 60ml
  • Soy sauce - 60ml
  • Eggs - 7
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook or air fry the diced chicken until browned and crispy
  2. Scramble the eggs in a large pan and set aside
  3. Fry the garlic in a little oil until fragrant
  4. Add the rice and soy sauce and toss well
  5. Stir through the honey and return the cooked chicken to the pan
  6. Fold through the scrambled egg
  7. Portion and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 546 per serving
  • Protein - 51g
  • Carbs - 67g
  • Fat - 7g

Meal - High Protein Hot Honey Popcorn Chicken

#meal  #chicken  #high-protein  #air-fryer  #meal-prep  #spicy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (cubed)
  • Potatoes - 400–500g (diced or frozen cubes)
  • Greek yogurt - 150g
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray

Hot Honey Sauce

  • Honey - 2 tbsp
  • Hot sauce - 1 tbsp
  • Chilli flakes (optional)

Directions

  1. Coat diced chicken in Greek yogurt and seasoning
  2. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  3. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  4. Cook potatoes until golden and crispy (air fryer or oven)
  5. Mix honey and hot sauce to create hot honey glaze
  6. Toss cooked chicken in hot honey sauce
  7. Serve with crispy potatoes

Macros

  • Calories - 495
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 12g

Notes

  • Confirmed from reel caption and structure :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • High-protein “fakeaway” style meal
  • Very similar structure to KFC popcorn chicken bowls

Meal - High Protein KFC Chicken Bowl

#meal  #chicken  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (cubed)
  • Potatoes - 500g (diced or frozen potato cubes)
  • Corn - 1 cup
  • Gravy - 1/2 cup
  • Greek yogurt - 150g (for coating)
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Coat diced chicken in Greek yogurt and seasoning
  2. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  3. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  4. Cook potatoes (air fryer or oven) until golden
  5. Heat corn and gravy
  6. Assemble bowl with potatoes, corn, crispy chicken, and gravy on top
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 55g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 12g

Notes

  • Based on KFC “Famous Bowl” style structure
  • Reel explicitly frames this as a high-protein alternative (~60g protein) :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Good for meal prep and bulk cooking

Meal - Lasagne Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • 1 tortilla wrap
  • 4 tbsp bolognese
  • 1 fresh lasagne sheet, or a dried sheet cooked for 5 minutes
  • 2 tbsp béchamel sauce
  • Handful of grated cheese and mozzarella

Directions

  1. Place the wrap flat and layer 2 tbsp bolognese, half the lasagne sheet, 1 tbsp béchamel and cheese.
  2. Repeat the layers once.
  3. Fold into a burrito and wrap it in foil.
  4. Air-fry at 180°C for 10 minutes.
