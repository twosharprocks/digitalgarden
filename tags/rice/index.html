Meal - Chicken & Chorizo One Pan Rice

#meal  #chicken  #chorizo  #rice  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (diced)
  • Chorizo - 120–150g (sliced)
  • Rice - 250–300g (uncooked, rinsed)
  • Onion - 1 (diced)
  • Red and yellow peppers - 2 (chopped)
  • Garlic - 3 cloves (minced)
  • Chicken stock - 500–700ml
  • Tomato paste - 2 tbsp
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Seasoning

  • Smoked paprika - 2 tsp
  • Garlic powder - 1 tsp
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Chilli (optional)

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan and cook chorizo until it releases oils
  2. Add onion, peppers, and garlic, cooking until softened
  3. Add chicken and cook until browned
  4. Stir in tomato paste and seasoning
  5. Add rice and mix to coat in flavours
  6. Pour in chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  7. Cover and cook for 15–20 minutes until rice is tender and liquid absorbed
  8. Fluff and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 600
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 18g

Notes

  • Confirmed from indexed reel + multiple matching recipes :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
  • Similar to simplified paella-style cooking (one-pan absorption method)
  • Very strong meal prep option

Meal - High Protein Chicken Katsu Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer  #easy 

Ingredients

Chicken Katsu

  • Chicken breast - 600g (sliced or cubed)
  • Egg - 1
  • Cornflakes or panko breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Garlic powder

Fried Rice

  • Cooked rice - 3–4 cups (preferably day-old)
  • Eggs - 2
  • Garlic - 2–3 cloves (minced)
  • Soy sauce - 2–3 tbsp
  • Mixed vegetables - 1–2 cups
  • Oil - 1 tbsp

Directions

  1. Coat chicken in egg and seasoning, then cover in crushed cornflakes or panko
  2. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  3. In a pan, scramble eggs and set aside
  4. Fry garlic in oil until fragrant
  5. Add rice, vegetables, and soy sauce, stirring well
  6. Return eggs and mix through
  7. Slice crispy chicken katsu and place over fried rice
  8. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 560
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 60g
  • Fat - 12g

Notes

  • Confirmed from reel caption and structure :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Combines Japanese-style chicken katsu with classic fried rice technique
  • Ideal for high-protein meal prep

Meal - High Protein Crispy Honey Garlic Chicken Fried Rice

#meal  #chicken  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • Diced chicken breast - 1.05kg
  • Rice - 700g cooked
  • Garlic - 35ml minced
  • Honey - 60ml
  • Soy sauce - 60ml
  • Eggs - 7
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook or air fry the diced chicken until browned and crispy
  2. Scramble the eggs in a large pan and set aside
  3. Fry the garlic in a little oil until fragrant
  4. Add the rice and soy sauce and toss well
  5. Stir through the honey and return the cooked chicken to the pan
  6. Fold through the scrambled egg
  7. Portion and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 546 per serving
  • Protein - 51g
  • Carbs - 67g
  • Fat - 7g

Meal - High Protein Mexican Bowl

#meal  #beef  #rice  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Rice - 200g (uncooked, portioned ~50g per serve)
  • Extra lean beef mince - 500g
  • Tinned tomatoes - 400g
  • Taco seasoning - 1 packet (or ~20g)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (optional)
  • Onion - 1 (optional)
  • Oil spray (minimal)

Optional Add-ons

  • Greek yogurt (as sour cream substitute)
  • Lettuce
  • Cheese (light)
  • Salsa or hot sauce

Directions

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions and set aside
  2. In a pan, cook onion and garlic until softened (optional)
  3. Add lean beef mince and cook until browned
  4. Stir in taco seasoning and tinned tomatoes
  5. Simmer until thickened and well combined
  6. Portion rice into containers or bowls
  7. Top with beef mixture and optional extras
  8. Serve or store for meal prep

Macros

  • Calories - 480
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 59g
  • Fat - 9g

Notes

  • Macros confirmed from reel caption :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Designed as a high-protein, low-fat meal prep option
  • Very scalable and easy to batch cook
