Meal - Chicken Alfredo Tortellini

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #one-pan  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 1 lb
  • Butter - 2 tbsp
  • Garlic - 1 tbsp minced
  • Frozen tortellini - 19 oz bag
  • Alfredo sauce - 15 oz jar
  • Mozzarella - 1 1/2 cups shredded
  • Bacon - 6 pieces
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Onion powder
    • Paprika
    • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp, then set aside
  2. Season the chicken and cook in the butter until browned and cooked through
  3. Add the garlic and cook briefly until fragrant
  4. Add the frozen tortellini and Alfredo sauce and stir to combine
  5. Simmer until the tortellini is cooked through
  6. Stir through the mozzarella until melted
  7. Crumble the bacon over the top and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 552 per serving
  • Protein - 44g
  • Carbs - 29g
  • Fat - 30g

Meal - Chili Mac and Cheese

#meal  #beef  #pasta  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Olive oil - 1 tbsp
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Onion - 1 (finely chopped)
  • Red bell pepper - 1 (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef - 500g
  • Diced tomatoes - 1 can
  • Pasta or macaroni - 250g
  • Cheese - 1 cup shredded
  • Stock or water - as needed
  • Seasoning
    • Chilli powder
    • Cumin
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and cook the garlic, onion and red bell pepper until softened
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned
  3. Stir through the chilli powder, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper
  4. Add the diced tomatoes and pasta
  5. Pour in enough stock or water to cook the pasta and simmer until tender
  6. Stir through the shredded cheese until melted
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 510 per serving
  • Protein - 30g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - High Protein Honey BBQ Chicken Mac & Cheese

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 1kg
  • Pasta - 300g
  • Reduced fat cheese - 160g
  • Milk - 250ml
  • BBQ sauce - 120g
  • Honey - 20g
  • Butter - 10g
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions and drain
  2. Season the chicken breast and cook until done, then dice or slice
  3. Combine the BBQ sauce and honey and coat the chicken
  4. In a pan, melt the butter and add the milk and cheese to make a sauce
  5. Stir the cooked pasta through the cheese sauce
  6. Fold through the honey BBQ chicken
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 630 per serving
  • Protein - 58g
  • Carbs - 58g
  • Fat - 13g

  • Macros are estimated from a high-protein version of the dish
