Stockton Colliery - Location Approximate

#diving  #caving  #western-australia 

About

Multiple entrances. Yellow marker buoy deployed from main entrance in March 2019 and suspended just below surface (marker still in place Aug 2019)

Location of main entrance estimated by triangulation using compass bearings taken at night:

  • Flashing transmission tower = 080 deg (tower @ -33.37938, 116.23889)
  • Illuminated house (MX track) = 225 deg
  • Large tree on edge of shore (MX track) = 250 deg

Details

Identification

  • Feature type: Mine

Cave and exploration data

  • Cave rating: Cave
  • Vertical access: No
  • Exploration potential: Medium
  • Goes to water: Yes

Access and warnings

CCR only - highly fragile mine workings not safe for open circuit. No access through motorcross track, so significant swim from boat ramp

About

Details

  • Feature type: Cave

  • Source: TrailsWA

  • Exploration potential: Low
  • Tourism potential: Medium
  • Disabled access potential: Low

Additional Notes

  • Explore Parks: https://exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au/park/stockyard-gully-reserve

Discover an area rich in limestone and caves although, the only cave open to visitors is the Stockyard Tunnel.

In Spring, the park is rich in wildflowers and colour including the rare Queen of Sheba orchid (Thelymitra variegate) and other Banksia species.
