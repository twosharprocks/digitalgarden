Fuente de la Cueva

#diving  #caving  #spain 

About

Spring for Mundilla River, emerging out of small ridge in limestone cliff

Details

Identification

  • Feature type: Cave
  • Source: GAEM

Cave and exploration data

  • Exploration potential: High
  • Goes to water: Yes

Additional Notes

(Translated from Spanish)

LOCATION :Parrots of Valdivia and Alta Villaescobedo (Burgos)

GENERAL INFORMATION Fuente de la Cueva is a permanent spring that gives birth to the Mundilla River . This source opens under a small ridge with a clear retreat of the limestone cliff, the waters forming a beautiful succession of waterfalls and cascades adorned with some tuff deposits. The emergency point, captured for the drinking water supply of several towns, is a narrow horizontal crack that appears impenetrable. Due to the use of the waters that flow from this cavity, it is advisable to have the corresponding authorization to access it.
