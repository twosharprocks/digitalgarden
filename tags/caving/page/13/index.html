Nettlebed - 5L290

#diving  #caving  #mount-gambier 

From CaveDB

Nettlebed Cave is home to a range of interesting features for cave divers to explore. The wall-scratchings which can seen once inside “The Gallery” are probable evidence of megafauna, while entrenched seashells provide insight into the cave’s Ice Age inhabitants.

Beyond The Gallery, the cave drops deeper and breaks into additional rooms containing many large, dark silt-covered boulders, black walls and white-clay floored passages.

The cave finishes in a chamber at 28m, but with an actual penetration distance from the surface of 80m.

Nurina Cave

#diving  #caving  #roe-plains 

From CaveDB

Situated to the east of Madura Pass, approximately 1400km from Perth, you’ll find a fascinating cave on the Roe Plain. Here, the limestone surface is in close proximity to the water table. This underwater cave is a complex network of intersecting fissures, with a generally small breakdown passage. It features distinct bedding planes at depths of 3, 5, and 7 meters, which house stunning horizontal elliptical phreatic passages as well as low flat passages, all interconnected. The deepest point is a 7m bedding plane, and so far, 410m of underwater passage has been explored (as of February 2004).

Olwolgin - 6N

#diving  #caving  #roe-plains 

From CaveDB

Overview

Located in the vast expanse of the Nullarbor desert, over 2500 km from Melbourne, Australia, lies a hidden gem known as the Olwolgin Cave. This underwater cave system, named after a nearby bluff on the Roe Plain escarpment, is a fascinating destination for adventurous divers and explorers.

Getting there

The journey to Olwolgin Cave is an adventure in itself. It begins with meticulous packing for a road trip to the middle of nowhere. The route takes you through major multilane highways, rolling hills, and farmed greenery, before the landscape gives way to the single-lane highway snaking along the southern coastline of the continent.
