Cova de Cala Falcó

#diving  #caving  #spain 

Details

Identification

  • Feature type: Cave
  • Source: Mindat.org

Cave and exploration data

  • Cave rating: Advanced
  • Exploration potential: Medium
  • Tourism potential: Medium
  • Disabled access potential: Low
  • Goes to water: Yes

References

  • Mindat.org: https://www.mindat.org/loc-290943.html
  • Wikipedia: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cala_Falc%C3%B3

(Translated from German) “In the immediate vicinity of Cala Falcó there are several cave systems that are partially accessible and have underground lakes. These include, to the west and north of the bay, the Cova de sa Piqueta , the Cova des Pont , the Cova des Xots and the Cova des Pirata . On the coast to the northeast are the Cova des Moroand southeast the Cova des Coloms, accessible only by water . Directly on the north side of the bay is the entrance to the Cova de Cala Falcó , a karst cave that is no longer illuminated a few meters behind the entrance.”
