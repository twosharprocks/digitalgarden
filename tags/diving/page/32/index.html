Matka Canyon Caves - Facebook

North Macedonia offers some intriguing caving opportunities, particularly for dry cave exploration, with limited—but potentially promising—cave diving potential. The country lies within the Dinaric karst region, which spans much of the western Balkans and is known for extensive limestone formations, karst plateaus, sinkholes, and underground rivers.
