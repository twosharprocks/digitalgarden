Additional Notes

Natural site in the municipality of Gibara , in the province of Holguín , Cuba , proposed to be considered a national monument. It takes its name from a sinkhole or cenote (a flooded cavern whose roof has collapsed and resembles a large swimming pool) that gives access to the largest flooded cavern discovered on the island. Its main scenic attraction is the access to the cavern itself through a sinkhole or cenote 30 meters long by 15 meters wide and 7 meters deep with transparent waters and blue reflections that give rise to its name.
