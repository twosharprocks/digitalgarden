About

Speleological Club of Crete (S.P.O.K.) - Runs expeditions

Crete, the largest of the Greek islands, offers significant caving and some limited but promising cave diving opportunities, thanks to its complex geology and extensive karst systems. The island is dominated by limestone and dolomite formations, especially in its mountainous interior, which has led to the development of thousands of caves—some still unexplored or only partially mapped.

Izvor Une, 44.7542, 15.7746,

  • Deepest underwater cave in Croatia; dived to 248m
    Izvor Cetine, 43.9203, 16.494,
  • Iconic karst spring; narrow shaft dived to 155m.
    Izvor Gacka (Pećina Vrelo), 44.842, 15.3104,
  • Multiple spring heads feeding Gacka River; partially explored. Izvor Rumin, 43.8852, 16.6798,
  • Submerged karst spring in Cetina basin; partially explored.
    Izvor Slunjčica, 45.1150, 15.5953,
  • Spring forming Slunjčica River; extensive karst drainage, largely unmapped underwater.
