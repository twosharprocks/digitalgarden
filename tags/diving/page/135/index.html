Portions of the following information were taken from www.protecsardinia.com This entrance is spectacular. One dives into this system as if you were entering a Indonesian Temple. The bottom is covered with snow white sand and the inner walls get together in pyramid like shape. After a short time one ascents to the surface and finds him self in a massive dry cave. Depending on the water level, take off the fins and get to the actual entry. The entry is hard to find as the water is right on top of it and the cave narrows to a restriction. A very appealing cave with rapidly changes depths, it is recommended to dive with a Nitrox blend. After a short while one approaches the main line, further through a narrow tunnel where the cave shows a side tunnel short after which leads to the right in to the deep. Further on the mainline to a opening in the bottom where the line suddenly falls down from 8 meters deep to 23 meters deep. One has to be careful not to disturb the sediment as the walls are covered with the finest cave dust. Without perfect neutral buoyancy there is a risk to the disturb the fine sediment resulting in a complete silt out. This cave system is complex in its shape. Shafts leading from 25 meters deep into sumps make this system very interesting, but only for the experienced cave diver. Equipment configuration: Back mount and Sidemount.

  • latracciaescursioniemontagna.com: https://latracciaescursioniemontagna.com/2022/03/12/676-grotta-dellacqua-nera/ “The origin of the name is unknown, certainly the cave that opens up between the light rocks is black, but the water is completely normal. It is thought to derive from the color of the bottom, covered in dark moss, on which the underground stream flows out before flowing into the Senaiga. Unlike today, it was once always well fed even in periods of drought, raising questions about its origin. In the thirties it was proven, with the introduction of dyes, that the cave is in communication with the Castel Tesino Caves, located at 1000 meters above sea level and a few kilometers away as the crow flies. The fine sand rich in granite crystals sedimented inside the cave indicates a supply of water much further away, coming from the Cima d’Asta Group. This suggests that there is a vast and intricate system of communicating caves inside the mountains.”

Grotta dell’Orso di Ponte di Nava

#diving  #caving  #italy 

About

The cave was discovered by chance in October 1886 by the owner of the land while he was excavating for the construction of a wall. The cavity was explored by the owner of the land who found the bone remains of Ursus spelaeus. Hence the name given to the cave. The first systematic exploration was by Capello in 1952, the GSP passed the first siphon upstream in the sixties reaching the next tunnel. In 1991 the CSARI group from Brussels passed a series of siphons reaching a fourth impassable siphon.
