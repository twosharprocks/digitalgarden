About

Katanga (Shaba) Plateau

  • Why: Massive, remote karst regions in southern DRC near the Zambian border.
  • Status: Very limited cave exploration; almost no diving records.
  • Potential: First expeditions into flooded karst systems—logistically challenging but potentially world-class.
  • Best Season: May to August.

Dinaric Karst Belt

#diving  #interests  #trips  #expeditions 

About

The Dinaric Karst Belt (aka Dinaric Alps Karst or Dinarides Karst)

  • One of the most extensive and well-developed karst regions in Europe,
  • Stretches along the western Balkan Peninsula.
  • Primarily associated with the Dinaric Alps,

Spans the following countries:

  • Slovenia
  • Croatia
  • Bosnia & Herzegovina
  • Montenegro
  • Serbia
  • Albania
  • Kosovo
  • North Macedonia - fringe southern and western zones
  • Italy - northeastern edge near the Karst Plateau/Carso region, bordering Slovenia
