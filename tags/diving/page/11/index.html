ChOptima - Post-Dive Checklist

#diving  #rebreather 

Immediately Post-Dive

  • Close DSV
  • Set point to 0.19
  • Check battery voltages
  • Check O2 remaining
  • Check scrubber remaining
  • Close valves, bleed with MAVs
  • Dry HUD contacts
  • Turn HUD & Controller OFF

End of Day

  • Rinse CCR in fresh water
  • Discard/Seal scrubber
  • Disassemble loop - DSV, loop, counterlungs, end cap
  • Flush loop components with warm water + steramine
  • Air dry electronics head
  • Rinse loop components with water
  • Hang hoses & counterlungs to dry (use blower)
