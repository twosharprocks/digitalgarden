Meal - Chicken Parm Baked Wraps

#meal  #wrap  #chicken 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast, diced
  • Italian seasoning
  • Garlic powder
  • Tomato passata or marinara sauce
  • Grated mozzarella
  • Grated parmesan
  • Large tortilla wraps
  • Olive oil spray

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken until browned and cooked through.
  2. Stir through enough marinara to coat the chicken.
  3. Divide the chicken between tortillas and top with mozzarella and parmesan.
  4. Fold into wraps and place seam-side down on a lined tray.
  5. Spray lightly with oil and bake until crisp and the cheese has melted.

Meal - Chicken Pesto Flatbread

#meal  #chicken  #flatbread  #quick 

Ingredients

  • Flatbread - 2
  • Chicken breast - 300g
  • Pesto - 2 tbsp

Directions

Meal - Creamy Garlic Chicken Pasta

#meal  #chicken  #pasta  #creamy  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Pasta - 300g
  • Garlic - 3 cloves
  • Cream - 200ml
  • Parmesan - 1/2 cup

    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook pasta and set aside
  2. Cook chicken and slice
  3. Sauté garlic, add cream and parmesan
  4. Add pasta, chicken and spinach

Meal - Garlic Honey BBQ Pulled Chicken Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos  #chicken 

Ingredients

  • 700g boneless chicken breast
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp each onion powder and smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Coleslaw mix
  • Chopped coriander
  • Lime wedges

Directions

  1. Coat the chicken with BBQ sauce, honey, garlic, onion powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook covered on low until tender, then shred and mix it back through the sauce.
  3. Warm the tortillas and fill with pulled chicken and coleslaw.
  4. Finish with coriander and lime.
