Meal - Chicken Gnocchi Soup

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 500g
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Gnocchi - 400g
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken in a pot until lightly browned, then remove and chop if needed
  2. Add the tomato puree and sun dried tomatoes and cook briefly
  3. Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a simmer
  4. Add the gnocchi and cook until tender
  5. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  6. Return the chicken to the pot and add the spinach
  7. Simmer gently until the spinach wilts and the soup is hot throughout
  8. Serve hot

  • Calories - 490 per serving
  • Protein - 52g
  • Carbs - 48g
  • Fat - 10g

Meal - Paprika Chicken & Chorizo Gnocchi

#meal  #chicken  #gnocchi  #one-pan  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Olive oil - 1/2 tbsp
  • Chorizo - 70g
  • Red onion - 1 small
  • Chicken breast - 350g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (crushed)
  • Tomato puree - 3 tbsp
  • Sun dried tomatoes - 6 (chopped)
  • Chicken stock - 1L
  • Natural yogurt - 500g
  • Parmesan - 50g (grated)
  • Spinach - 1 handful
  • Seasoning
    • Paprika
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook the chorizo until it starts to crisp
  2. Add the chopped red onion and cook until softened
  3. Add the chicken breast, season with paprika, salt and pepper, and cook until coloured
  4. Stir through the garlic, tomato puree and chopped sun dried tomatoes
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and add the gnocchi
  6. Simmer until the gnocchi is tender and the sauce reduces slightly
  7. Lower the heat and stir through the yogurt and Parmesan
  8. Fold through the spinach until wilted
  9. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 562 per serving
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 52g
  • Fat - 16g

Meal - Smokey Bacon Gnocchi

#meal  #gnocchi  #high-protein  #one-pan  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Bacon - 5–6 rashers (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef or chicken - 400–800g (optional high-protein boost)
  • Zucchini - 1–2 (diced)
  • Tomato sauce (tinned or passata) - 400–500g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Smoked paprika

Directions

  1. Fry chopped bacon in a large pan until crispy, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook garlic and ground meat (if using) until browned
  3. Add zucchini and cook until softened
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and smoked paprika
  5. Add gnocchi and simmer until tender and sauce thickens
  6. Return bacon to the pan and mix through
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 16g

Notes

  • Confirmed structure from indexed sources (gnocchi + bacon + tomato base)
  • Often presented as a high-protein, meal-prep friendly dish
  • Variations include adding cheese or baking for a crispy top
