Meal - Smokey Bacon Gnocchi

#meal  #gnocchi  #high-protein  #one-pan  #meal-prep  #easy 

Ingredients

  • Gnocchi - 500g
  • Bacon - 5–6 rashers (chopped)
  • Lean ground beef or chicken - 400–800g (optional high-protein boost)
  • Zucchini - 1–2 (diced)
  • Tomato sauce (tinned or passata) - 400–500g
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Oil - 1 tbsp
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Smoked paprika

Directions

  1. Fry chopped bacon in a large pan until crispy, then set aside
  2. In the same pan, cook garlic and ground meat (if using) until browned
  3. Add zucchini and cook until softened
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and smoked paprika
  5. Add gnocchi and simmer until tender and sauce thickens
  6. Return bacon to the pan and mix through
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 45g
  • Carbs - 50g
  • Fat - 16g

Notes

  • Confirmed structure from indexed sources (gnocchi + bacon + tomato base)
  • Often presented as a high-protein, meal-prep friendly dish
  • Variations include adding cheese or baking for a crispy top

Meal - Spicy Steak & Cheese Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 16 low-carb wraps
  • 900g lean steak
  • Reduced-fat cheese
  • 800g cooked rice
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Paprika, garlic powder and cumin
  • Coriander
  • Onion and capsicum

Sauce

  • 300g fat-free Greek yoghurt
  • 70g light sour cream
  • 50g chipotle in adobo
  • Juice of ½ lime

Directions

  1. Season and cook the steak, then slice or chop it.
  2. Sauté the onion and capsicum until tender.
  3. Blend or stir together the yoghurt, sour cream, chipotle and lime.
  4. Divide rice, steak, vegetables, cheese and sauce between wraps.
  5. Roll tightly and toast, or wrap and freeze for meal prep.
