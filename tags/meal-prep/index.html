Meal - Air Fryer Chicken Burritos

#meal  #chicken  #burrito  #air-fryer  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g
  • Rice - 2 cups cooked
  • Tortillas - 4
  • Cheese - 1 cup
  • Beans - 1 can
  • Salsa - 1/2 cup
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook and season chicken, then shred
  2. Mix with rice, beans and salsa
  3. Fill tortillas and roll tightly
  4. Air fry at 190°C for 10–12 mins until crispy

Macros

  • Calories - 610
  • Protein - 48g
  • Carbs - 55g
  • Fat - 20g

Meal - Beef Bulgogi Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 500g steak, very thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 Asian pear, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2cm ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 4 large burrito wraps
  • Cooked rice
  • Kimchi
  • Sliced spring onion
  • Kewpie mayonnaise

Directions

  1. Mix beef with onion, pear, soy, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, gochujang and sesame seeds.
  2. Marinate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Stir-fry the beef in batches over high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Layer rice, kimchi, beef, mayonnaise and spring onion on each wrap.
  5. Roll tightly and toast in a dry pan or sandwich press.

Meal - Cheeseburger Bowls

#meal  #beef  #bowl  #burger  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Ground beef - 500g
  • Potatoes (or fries) - 400g
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Lettuce - 1–2 cups (shredded)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)

Burger Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or fries until crispy
  2. Cook ground beef with salt and pepper until browned
  3. Mix burger sauce ingredients in a bowl
  4. Assemble bowls with potatoes, beef, cheese, lettuce, onion, and pickles
  5. Drizzle with burger sauce and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 40g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 22g

Notes

  • Identified as cheeseburger-style bowl based on user verification
  • Typical “burger in a bowl” structure (beef + potato base + sauce)

Meal - Chicken Bacon Ranch Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #chicken  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1.3kg chicken breast
  • Taco seasoning
  • 500g low-fat cottage cheese
  • 2 tbsp ranch seasoning
  • 1 tsp chipotle in adobo
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 45ml light mayonnaise
  • 200g light mozzarella
  • 8 bacon rashers, cooked and diced
  • Chives
  • 1kg cooked rice
  • 10 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken, then dice it.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, ranch seasoning, chipotle, lime, garlic and mayonnaise until smooth.
  3. Combine chicken with the ranch sauce, mozzarella, bacon and chives.
  4. Warm each tortilla, add rice and chicken filling, then roll tightly.
  5. Toast immediately or wrap and freeze for later.
