Meal - Arayes

#meal  #wrap  #beef 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef or lamb mince
  • 1 onion, grated and drained
  • 1 jalapeño, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, grated
  • ¼ cup parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp baharat
  • ½ tsp each cumin, paprika, salt and black pepper
  • 4 pita breads, halved
  • Olive oil

Directions

  1. Mix the mince with onion, jalapeño, garlic, parsley and spices.
  2. Spread a thin, even layer of filling inside each pita half.
  3. Brush the outside lightly with olive oil.
  4. Grill or pan-fry over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side, then cook the open meat edge until the filling is cooked through.
  5. Serve hot with yoghurt or tahini sauce.

Meal - Bolognese Tacos

#meal  #wrap  #tacos 

Ingredients

  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 medium carrot, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp each oregano, thyme, basil and garlic powder
  • 2 tsp each sweet paprika and dried parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1½ cups tomato passata
  • ⅓ cup water
  • ½ vegetable stock cube
  • Grated cheddar
  • 5–6 mini tortilla wraps
  • ½ tbsp olive oil

Directions

  1. Soften the onion in olive oil, add the beef and brown over high heat for 5–6 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, carrot, passata, tomato purée, seasonings, stock and water.
  3. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Dip each tortilla in the sauce, place it in a hot pan, and layer with cheese and 2–3 tbsp of filling.
  5. Fold and grill both sides until browned and crisp.

Meal - Cheesy Bacon Jalapeño Popper Wrap

#meal  #wrap  #bacon 

Ingredients

  • Large tortilla wraps
  • Cream cheese, softened
  • Grated cheddar
  • Grated mozzarella
  • Cooked bacon, crumbled
  • Jalapeños, grilled and sliced

Directions

  1. Mix cream cheese, cheddar, mozzarella, bacon and jalapeños.
  2. Spread the filling over the centre of each tortilla.
  3. Fold the sides inward and roll tightly.
  4. Toast seam-side down in a dry pan, then turn until golden and the cheese has melted.

Meal - Chicken Parm Baked Wraps

#meal  #wrap  #chicken 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast, diced
  • Italian seasoning
  • Garlic powder
  • Tomato passata or marinara sauce
  • Grated mozzarella
  • Grated parmesan
  • Large tortilla wraps
  • Olive oil spray

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken until browned and cooked through.
  2. Stir through enough marinara to coat the chicken.
  3. Divide the chicken between tortillas and top with mozzarella and parmesan.
  4. Fold into wraps and place seam-side down on a lined tray.
  5. Spray lightly with oil and bake until crisp and the cheese has melted.

Meal - Feta Fried Egg Tortilla

#meal  #egg  #feta  #wrap  #quick  #breakfast 

Ingredients

  • Egg - 1
  • Feta cheese - 2 tbsp (crumbled)
  • Tortilla - 1 small
  • Avocado - 1/4 (sliced)
  • Chilli oil - drizzle (optional)

Directions

  1. Sprinkle feta in a circle in a hot pan
  2. Once it starts melting, crack the egg into the centre
  3. Place tortilla on top and press down gently
  4. Cook until the egg sets and feta crisps
  5. Flip so tortilla side is down and toast briefly
  6. Remove, top with avocado and chilli oil
  7. Fold and serve
