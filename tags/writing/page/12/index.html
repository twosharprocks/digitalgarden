Sitting in a little cafe next to the Natural History Museum at the moment – drinking massive cappuccinos, eating delicious cake, and tripping balls from having had 4 hours sleep in the last 3 days. I won’t be here long though, as I need to type this up, slip next door and claim myself a new drinking partner (Larry 2.0), then jump on a plane up to Edinburgh! I’m back here for the Edinburgh Fringe Festival all over again – a month of comedic and artistic insanity in glorious Scotland! I’ll be doing some of the light and sound tech for the very talented John Robertson while he puts on 44 shows in 22 days of his amazing “A Nifty History of Evil” one-man show, as well as a mountain of open-mic spots and workshops, and obviously going to plenty of shows. So no real-time Ginge love for you then – you’ll just have to put up with my usual bullshit in a diluted and scheduled form.
