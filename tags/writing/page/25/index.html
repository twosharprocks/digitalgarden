Website – How to Ruin Christmas

#writing 
This is the final post of the year. The Mighty Ginge will be on break until January 17th,2011 –looking forward to seeing you all next year with new site features and new posts. Merry Christmas,and try to avoid killing yourselves over the New Year. The Mighty Ginge Task Ruin the annual yule-tide festivities through a series of increasingly disrespectful and socially-misguided acts. Background Christmas –a time to share with friends,family and reindeer. It’s the season for togetherness,appreciating those close to you,for generosity and empathy. It’s also the time of the year when people go batshit insane. Shopping,dinners,cards,letters –people are travelling and sending shit everywhere. They get together with family because “they have to”,put up with Grandad’s violent,racist and mysoginistic outbursts for the sake of “harmony”,send out bulk emails to people they don’t even talk to,break the bank buying gifts for kids who play with the box instead,and then take them to sit on the knee of some red-suited paedophile who talks about the goodies in his sack that he’ll give them if they’re good. So in response to this insanity,I’ve compiled a variety of ways you can strike back at the spirit of Saint Nic. These are merely suggestions and guidelines based on previous experiences however –I strongly encourage you all to create or recount your own misadventures,and post them in the comments below. Hitting the Fat Man where it hurts Disclaimer:As this is an entry level experience,I recommend starting out here for those looking to ruin their first Christmas. If at the end you feel any emotion other than malicious joy,I would recommend avoiding any of the other techniques and simply continuing to be passive aggressive at Christmas dinner when passing the salt. “Hitting the Fat Man”requires a high visibility and very large inflatable Santa Claus. Ideally the giant Santa will be located somewhere with limited physical access,but with massive visual impact –for example on the top of a shopping centre facing a freeway. You will need; - A lightweight ladder (or mad ninja skills) - A large,plain white sheet - Black spraypaint - Two bricks - Several meters of string. On Christmas eve,approach your target Santa from low ground below the sight line of any security cameras/roving patrols,and get access to the roof. This can either be by lifting yourself on the edge of a doorframe in a loading bay,hanging off the external grate of a nearby airvent and then swinging your legs onto the roof to pull yourself up backwards (eg. Generally being Sam Fisher);or bringing a ladder with you. Ensure though that you can pull the ladder onto the roof with you if you do use it though,least it be spotted by a roaming security guard. Once on the roof,tie the string to the corners of the sheet and use the bricks to hold the string in place,spreading the sheet out along the wall facing the freeway under Santa’s feet. Now spraypaint “SANTA ISN’T REAL –MERRY XMAS”in foot tall letters on the white sheet,climb back down the ladder/airvent,and make your escape. Chances are it will stay up there for 3 days,because the shopping centre needs to hire an elevated work platform to legally access that part of the roof. The Facade Breaker Extended family:you can’t pick them,but you can ensure they never speak to you again. Christmas with big families is usually a fairly stressful event for everyone,so save yourself the trouble of false pre tense and feigned respect –tell them what you think of them. For example if you’re 8 and having Christmas at your Grandad’s,playing with a water hose and enjoying a hot Australian summer,and he suddenly comes out of the kitchen with a carving knife in his hand screaming that you’ll kink the hose –tell your parents you’re scared of him (like the rest of the family),and that you won’t come out of a spider-infested caravan until he dies or they take you home. Alternatively,inform your parents every year that you’d rather be raped by a thousand angry Squirrel monkeys that see extended family for Christmas. When they finally do convince you that 5 days in a holiday home with them is survivable,give it a real chance. Stay quiet when the pointed questions about your career start;play the game when plans get changed at the last minute to suit the selfish needs of a few;bite your tongue when your the relatives start getting overly religious –it’s okay,your time will come. Because on Christmas night –when everyone is gathered around having a roast and your god-bothering Aunty steps up her week long campaign of bitchiness by accusing your Mum of trying to steal her children away –you get to step up and tell her to “stop acting like such a fucking child”,and that “everyone is sick of your shit”. When she demands you respect her because she’s your aunty,inform her that you respect people who deserve it (eg. not middle aged women who act like children),then storm out of the house. If your not sure that will get you cut off from family Christmases,make sure you rub salt in the wound by apologising to your aunt the next morning for the language you used,but make a point that you still think she’s acts like child and that you don’t care if she thinks it’s disrespectful,she can still fuck off. Seriously,let that shit out –a little work early on will save you a lifetime of awkward Christmas conversations and annual attempts to justify yourself to people you don’t like just because you share genetic material. Spread the Malice Just because you’ve been banned from any future family yule-tide gatherings doesn’t mean you can’t spread your own unique brand of Christmas cheer to other families. Ofcourse not being part of the family makes it more difficult to expose their insecurities:why not expose your own? A favourite of mine is to run away from your long term girlfriend to another state “just for Summer”when she needs you most,and break up with her two week before Christmas from the other side of the country. Then it’s important to get absurdly drunk at the Christmas dinner of the family friends you’re staying with,spill hard liquor on their grandmother,and sing out of tune Disney classics on Singstar with their nieces. To top it off,make sure you then sleep with one of the nieces on Boxing Day and get a photo of her sitting on your lap posted on facebook for your still-grieving ex to see. Not only will your parents be personally horrified,but you’ll have spoiled Christmas for two other families too. The company of strangers Congratulations of getting this far –you’ve worked hard. Now that you can’t go home for Christmas and friends won’t invite you around because you’re a liability,the final stage is to ruin Christmas with people you’ve just met! Book a room in a youth hostel,get drunk and fuck some hideous French girl who doesn’t know enough English to say no! Go back the next year and remind the hostel staff that none of them have moved on their lives! Start fights with the homeless! If you’re in a foreign country,abuse the good intentions of others by tagging along with a friend who you haven’t seen since school,then wage a week long war against their cat. The world is your oyster –people will welcome you into their homes out of charity,and shoo you back out less certain of man’s inherent good nature. Just don’t do anything I wouldn’t do. I like kids and some of them are still innocent,so I wouldn’t want to ruin THEIR Christmas. But,yeah,I have no problem ruining the Christmas of adults that should know better. Although I’m sure I’ll continue to be passive agressive…
