Meal - Cheeseburger Bowls

#meal  #beef  #bowl  #burger  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #to-try 

Ingredients

  • Ground beef - 500g
  • Potatoes (or fries) - 400g
  • Cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Lettuce - 1–2 cups (shredded)
  • Onion - 1/2 (diced)

Burger Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp

Directions

  1. Cook potatoes or fries until crispy
  2. Cook ground beef with salt and pepper until browned
  3. Mix burger sauce ingredients in a bowl
  4. Assemble bowls with potatoes, beef, cheese, lettuce, onion, and pickles
  5. Drizzle with burger sauce and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 40g
  • Carbs - 35g
  • Fat - 22g

Notes

  • Identified as cheeseburger-style bowl based on user verification
  • Typical “burger in a bowl” structure (beef + potato base + sauce)

Meal - High Protein KFC Chicken Bowl

#meal  #chicken  #bowl  #high-protein  #meal-prep  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast - 600g (cubed)
  • Potatoes - 500g (diced or frozen potato cubes)
  • Corn - 1 cup
  • Gravy - 1/2 cup
  • Greek yogurt - 150g (for coating)
  • Cornflakes or breadcrumbs - 80g
  • Oil spray
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper
    • Paprika
    • Garlic powder

Directions

  1. Coat diced chicken in Greek yogurt and seasoning
  2. Roll in crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs
  3. Air fry at 190°C for 12–15 minutes until crispy
  4. Cook potatoes (air fryer or oven) until golden
  5. Heat corn and gravy
  6. Assemble bowl with potatoes, corn, crispy chicken, and gravy on top
  7. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 520
  • Protein - 55g
  • Carbs - 45g
  • Fat - 12g

Notes

  • Based on KFC “Famous Bowl” style structure
  • Reel explicitly frames this as a high-protein alternative (~60g protein) :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
  • Good for meal prep and bulk cooking

Meal - Smashed Potato Cheeseburger Bowls

#meal  #beef  #potatoes  #burger  #bowl  #high-protein 

Ingredients

  • Potatoes - 200g
  • 93/7 Ground beef - 75g cooked
  • Reduced fat fiesta cheese - 28g
  • Light sour cream - 30g
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Burger sauce - to taste
  • Seasoning
    • Onion powder
    • Garlic powder
    • Paprika
    • Cayenne
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Slice the potatoes into thin rounds and roast or air fry until tender and crisp
  2. Cook the ground beef and season with onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, salt and pepper
  3. Layer the smashed or roasted potatoes into a bowl
  4. Top with the cooked beef, cheese, pickles, sour cream and burger sauce
  5. Serve hot

Macros

  • Calories - 430 per serving
  • Protein - 28g
  • Carbs - 32g
  • Fat - 20g

  • Built from the single-serving ingredient amounts shown in the reel
