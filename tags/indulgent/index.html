Meal - Big Mac Toastie

#meal  #beef  #quick  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Bread slices - 4
  • Ground beef - 300g
  • Cheese - 4 slices
  • Pickles - to taste
  • Onion - 1/4 (finely diced)
  • Lettuce - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Butter - 1 tbsp

Big Mac Sauce

  • Mayonnaise - 3 tbsp
  • Ketchup - 1 tbsp
  • Mustard - 1 tsp
  • Pickle juice - 1 tsp
  • Paprika - pinch

Directions

  1. Cook ground beef in a pan, breaking it up and seasoning with salt and pepper
  2. Mix all Big Mac sauce ingredients in a small bowl
  3. Butter the outside of the bread slices
  4. On the inside, layer beef, cheese, pickles, onion, lettuce and sauce
  5. Close sandwich and cook in a pan or sandwich press until golden and crispy
  6. Slice and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 680
  • Protein - 38g
  • Carbs - 42g
  • Fat - 38g

Meal - Cheese Pull Apart Bread

#meal  #snack  #oven  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Bread loaf (sourdough or cob) - 1
  • Mozzarella - 200g (cubed or shredded)
  • Butter - 3 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Parsley (optional)
  • Seasoning
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions

  1. Cut the bread in a crosshatch pattern (do not slice all the way through)
  2. Stuff mozzarella into the cuts throughout the loaf
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic and seasoning
  4. Pour or brush mixture over the bread, ensuring it gets into the gaps
  5. Wrap loosely in foil and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes
  6. Uncover and bake another 5–10 minutes until crispy and melted
  7. Serve immediately and pull apart

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 14g
  • Carbs - 38g
  • Fat - 24g

Notes

  • Identified based on “cheese pull” style construction
  • Designed for visual melt/stretch rather than high-protein efficiency
  • Best served immediately while cheese is hot and elastic

Meal - Garlic Butter Jalapeño Popper Croissants

#meal  #snack  #quick  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Jalapeños - 6 (halved, deseeded)
  • Cheddar cheese - 1 cup (shredded)
  • Croissant dough - 1 tube
  • Butter - 2 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 2 cloves (minced)
  • Parsley (optional)

Directions

  1. Slice jalapeños in half and remove seeds
  2. Fill each with shredded cheddar cheese
  3. Wrap each jalapeño with croissant dough
  4. Mix melted butter with garlic and brush over wrapped poppers
  5. Bake at 180°C for 15–20 minutes until golden
  6. Garnish with parsley and serve

Macros

  • Calories - 420
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 28g
  • Fat - 28g

Notes

  • Originally misclassified as a chicken caesar bowl — corrected based on reel content
  • High-fat, indulgent snack rather than high-protein meal

Meal - Pizza Cupcakes

#meal  #snack  #quick  #oven  #indulgent 

Ingredients

  • Biscuit dough (canned) - 1 can
  • Pizza sauce - 1/4 cup
  • Mozzarella cheese - 1/2 cup (shredded)
  • Pepperoni - 8–10 slices
  • Butter - 2 tbsp (melted)
  • Garlic - 1 tsp (minced)
  • Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Flatten biscuit dough and press into muffin tray to form cups
  2. Add pizza sauce, cheese, and pepperoni into each cup
  3. Mix melted butter with garlic and brush over dough edges
  4. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning
  5. Bake at 180°C for 12–15 minutes until golden
  6. Remove and serve warm

Macros

  • Calories - 350
  • Protein - 12g
  • Carbs - 30g
  • Fat - 20g

Notes

  • Previously misclassified as a chicken rice bowl — corrected based on reel content
  • More of a snack/appetizer than a main meal
