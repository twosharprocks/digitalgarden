High Protein Chicken Sausage Breakfast Burritos

#meal  #breakfast  #burrito  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • Chicken sausage - 4 links (sliced)
  • Eggs - 6
  • Egg whites - 500ml (approx 16 oz)
  • Low-fat cottage cheese - 1/2 cup
  • Large tortillas - 6
  • Cheese - 1–1.5 cups (shredded)
  • Oil spray

Seasoning

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Paprika

Directions

  1. Cook chicken sausage in a pan until browned
  2. In a bowl, whisk eggs, egg whites, and cottage cheese together
  3. Pour mixture into pan and scramble until fully cooked
  4. Combine eggs with cooked sausage
  5. Lay out tortillas and evenly distribute filling
  6. Add shredded cheese on top
  7. Roll tightly into burritos
  8. Optional: toast lightly or store for meal prep

Macros

  • Calories - 500
  • Protein - 50g
  • Carbs - 40g
  • Fat - 14g

  • Confirmed from indexed ingredient list (chicken sausage + egg whites + cottage cheese) :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
  • Leaner, higher-protein version of breakfast burritos
  • Ideal for batch cooking and freezing

High Protein Grilled Cheese Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 900g extra-lean beef mince
  • ½ white onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • Taco seasoning
  • 400g canned diced tomatoes
  • 250ml chicken stock
  • 100g light cheddar
  • 600g cooked rice
  • Coriander
  • 6 large tortillas
  • 1 tbsp nacho cheese sauce per burrito
  • 20g light mozzarella per burrito

Directions

  1. Brown onion, garlic and beef, then add taco seasoning, tomatoes and stock.
  2. Simmer until the mixture is thick, then stir through cheddar and coriander.
  3. Divide rice, beef filling and nacho cheese sauce between tortillas and roll tightly.
  4. Place mozzarella in a hot non-stick pan and set the burrito on top.
  5. Cook until the cheese forms a crust, then turn and toast the other side.

Lasagne Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #air-fryer 

Ingredients

  • 1 tortilla wrap
  • 4 tbsp bolognese
  • 1 fresh lasagne sheet, or a dried sheet cooked for 5 minutes
  • 2 tbsp béchamel sauce
  • Handful of grated cheese and mozzarella

Directions

  1. Place the wrap flat and layer 2 tbsp bolognese, half the lasagne sheet, 1 tbsp béchamel and cheese.
  2. Repeat the layers once.
  3. Fold into a burrito and wrap it in foil.
  4. Air-fry at 180°C for 10 minutes.

Nacho Cheese Beef Burritos

#meal  #burrito  #beef  #high-protein  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 1kg lean beef mince
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 130g low-sugar ketchup
  • 150ml water
  • 230ml light evaporated milk
  • 180g Mexican cheese blend
  • 30ml hot sauce
  • 6 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Brown the beef with onion and capsicum.
  2. Add taco seasoning, ketchup and water, then simmer until thick.
  3. Warm evaporated milk gently, add cheese and hot sauce, and stir until smooth.
  4. Divide beef and cheese sauce between tortillas.
  5. Roll into burritos and toast seam-side down.

Pepper Jack Chicken Burrito

#meal  #burrito  #chicken  #meal-prep 

Ingredients

  • 900g chicken breast
  • Taco seasoning
  • 200g reduced-fat cheddar
  • 200g reduced-fat mozzarella
  • 14 slices centre-cut bacon, cooked
  • 1 can diced green chillies
  • 8 large tortillas

Directions

  1. Season and cook the chicken, then chop or shred it.
  2. Combine chicken, bacon, green chillies, cheddar and mozzarella.
  3. Divide between warmed tortillas and roll into burritos.
  4. Toast seam-side down until sealed and golden.
  5. Wrap and refrigerate or freeze extras for meal prep.
